The Amber Teardrop That Seems Lit From Behind

A horizontal abstract of glowing magenta, violet and amber fields, crisp bubbles and one dark-flecked patch, available as a print on Fine Art America.

Start with the amber teardrop, right of center. It glows as if a light were behind it. Around it, magenta and pink shapes curve across the frame, and deep violet fills the lower and right areas.

Then look right. Round bubbles of different sizes trail down the edge in a loose chain, each one crisp against the soft color. This is Amber Teardrop Glow, Violet and Magenta Abstract.

What the eye finds, one stop at a time

I'm not going to give you a backstory for this piece. What I can give you is the route your eye takes, because that route is the experience. It begins at the upper left and follows the long diagonal arcs down to the lower right.

Along the way there are places to rest. A pale olive oval and a patch of green sit on the left, above streaks of vivid red. Near the top, inside an orange shape, is a small patch of dark specks. It is the only fleck-filled spot in the image, and it is easy to miss until you notice it.

Thin bright edges trace the curves, and the shadows stay soft. The surfaces look smooth, glossy and glass-like, as if you were looking through layers of something liquid. There is no horizon and no single subject, only color, curve and glow. That is enough reason to want it.

What you'll see in the print

  • Color that looks backlit, with soft luminous fields and thin bright edges along the curves. The piece reads as glowing rather than flat.
  • Magenta-pink, deep violet and burnt amber, accented by olive-chartreuse green and flashes of vivid red. This gives you a warm, rich palette with a few sharp notes.
  • A large amber teardrop at center right and a pale olive oval on the left. These give the composition two anchors without a single dominant subject.
  • Long diagonal arcs from upper left to lower right. They move your eye across the whole width of the image.
  • Crisp round bubbles of different sizes and one dark-flecked patch. They add fine detail that rewards a closer look.
  • Smooth, glossy, glass-like surfaces. They give the colors a liquid depth.

Where it belongs

This is a wide horizontal piece, so it suits the wall above a sofa, bed, console or desk. The long diagonal sweep fills a wide space naturally, and with no horizon or central subject it works as a color-led statement rather than a picture of something.

The warm amber and magenta against deep purple can set the palette for a room, or add a rich accent to neutral walls. The mood is warm, vivid and dreamlike, with a calm, fluid energy.
