Happy Thanksgiving Week!

It’s a great time of year to be thankful, merry, and stay warm! LOL… This week also kicks off the holiday gift and shopping season.

This one is a lot of fun for me because it means that I get to share my Holiday Gift Guide with you!

There are many ways to share art with friends, family, and loved ones. Not just on the wall but in every area of daily life!

Most people are familiar with print on demand products all the way from greeting cards, to mugs, coasters, puzzles and more. These items all make great gifts!

The quality is amazing and the designs are unique and memorable. Most retail stores have mass produced designs which can often times feel very generic.
