The Peacock Fan That Glows Against Dark Foliage

Emerald Pageant, Three Peacocks is a digitally enhanced photograph of one huge open fan, a second bird striding past below it, and a third perched above.

Look at the middle of the frame first. A male peacock has opened his tail into a huge semicircle, and every feather carries a blue-and-green eyespot. Against the dark forest-green foliage behind him, the fan looks lit from within.

Then let your eye drop. A second peacock walks left across straw-covered ground, its long green train trailing behind. A third bird sits high at the upper right. One portrait has become a small scene.

What the eye finds, one step at a time

The fan comes first. It spreads in a wide arc from the central bird's blue neck, and the eyespots, with their touches of white, repeat outward like a pattern that never quite ends.

Next comes the walking peacock. Its step and trailing train sweep diagonally to the left and pull you across the lower part of the picture. The perched bird at the upper right does the opposite. It is a calm vertical note that settles the movement below.

Then come the details. Fine feather barbs radiate like threads. The straw and leaves share the same swirling, brushed texture, so the whole surface feels combed and alive. The chain-link fencing and foliage stay darker, and that darkness is what makes the emerald, peacock blue and straw gold glow.

  • Emerald green, deep peacock blue and straw gold sit against dark forest green, and the colors glow instead of blending into the background.
  • Bright, even daylight and strong contrast give the fan a luminous look, which keeps it vivid even in a room with plenty of other things going on.
  • Fine radiating barbs and combed, flowing straw give a swirling, brushed surface that rewards a closer look.
  • A diagonal step at lower left and a quiet perch at upper right balance the fan, so the eye travels around the picture and keeps finding something new.

Where it belongs

The wide horizontal format is made for a wall that can stretch out, such as above a sofa, a bed, a console or a dining table. The fan spreads across the space the way it spreads across the frame.

The mood is bold and lively, with no hush about it. The deep greens and blues hold their own against dark woods and neutral walls, so the piece suits a room that can carry strong color.
