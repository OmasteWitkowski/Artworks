A Cobalt Sky, Doubled in Still Water

Cobalt Mirror is a painterly lake landscape in which a deep blue sky and its exact reflection meet a tangle of warm, dry grass.

Start with the blue. It fills the upper half of the picture, deep and saturated, streaked with thin white clouds that sweep diagonally toward the center.

Then look down. The lake holds that same sky almost exactly, so the blue appears twice, once above the far shore and once below it. This is Cobalt Mirror.

What the eye finds, in the order it finds it

I can't give you a memory of where this lake is or when it was made, and I won't invent one. What I can give you is what the picture holds, and that is plenty to look at.

First comes the doubled sky. Then the eye drops to the foreground, where pale, wiry stalks of dry grass and patches of fresh green lean in different directions. They lead you toward the shoreline and out across the water.

On the far side sit a line of dark evergreens, a few small buildings and soft blue ridges low on the horizon. A single bare tree stands at the right edge. The clouds move, the water doesn't, and the whole surface carries a fine painterly texture that gives the bright scene a brushed, canvas-like feel.

What to look for when you get close

  • Saturated cobalt and sky blue, repeated in the water. The color reads clearly from across a room and makes the lake feel bright and open.
  • Warm straw tan, rusty brown and olive green in the foreground. They balance all that blue and keep it from feeling cold.
  • Layered horizontal bands of sky, treeline, mirrored water and grassy bank. The picture settles into a calm, orderly rhythm.
  • A tangle of dry, pale grass across the bottom edge. It adds small, scattered movement and draws your eye into the lake.
  • A fine painterly texture over the whole image, with feathery cloud streaks. It rewards a closer look and gives the scene a hand-worked surface.
  • Clear, even daylight with soft shadows. The mood is spacious and still, not dramatic.

Where it belongs

This is a wide, horizontal picture with strong blue color. It suits a wide wall: above a sofa, a console, a bed or a desk, or along a hallway. The layered bands read clearly from across the room, and the grass texture is there for anyone who steps closer.

The warm grasses tie the blue to wood, neutral and earth-toned rooms. In a living room, bedroom or office where you want a calm, open outdoor scene, it brings a lot of color without any busyness.
