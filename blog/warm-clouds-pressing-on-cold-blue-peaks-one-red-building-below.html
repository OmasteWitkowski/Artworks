Warm Clouds Pressing on Cold Blue Peaks, One Red Building Below

The Quiet Weight of Clouds is a painterly winter mountain landscape where cream-and-peach sky meets slate-blue snow, and one rust-red building sets the scale.

Look at the top of the picture first. Thick clouds pile up above the mountains, and they glow in warm cream and peach. Sunlight breaks through the cloud banks at the upper right.

Now look at what they sit on. The mountain slopes below are deep slate blue and cool, and the warm sky bears down on them. That contrast between warm and cold is why I made this piece the way I did, and why it holds your eye.

What your eye finds, in the order it finds it

I can't give you a tale about where this valley is or how it came to be, so I'll tell you what is actually in the image. It is built in three layers. Rolling clouds fill the top, a band of jagged ridgelines steps back diagonally toward the right, and an open snowy plain runs across the foreground.

Then there is the building. In the lower left, a red-brown wooden building with a brown metal roof sits alone in the snow. Its angled roof points toward the mountains and pulls your eye along with it. It is the only warm, saturated color in a palette of taupe-gray, slate blue and snow white, and it is small enough that the peaks feel enormous.

Bare shrubs and a small evergreen stand at the lower right, finely detailed against the softer snow. Dark conifers dot the valley and foothills. The surface is painterly and slightly oily, with rippled, brush-like patterning in the sky and snow. It sits somewhere between photograph and painting, and I've listed it as digital art.

Where it belongs on your wall

The horizontal format suits a wide wall, such as above a sofa, a bed, a console or a desk. The three layers spread across the width and make a calm band of landscape.

The taupe, slate and cream palette sits quietly in a neutral room, and the rust-red building gives the space one small warm accent. The mood is still, remote and expansive, and it suits cold-weather or lodge-style spaces as well.
