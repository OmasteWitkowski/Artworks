The Glowing Diamond That Keeps Your Eye Circling

Ember Totem is a mirrored digital abstract with an amber diamond glowing against deep cobalt blue, available as a print on Fine Art America.

Look at the center first. A diamond of orange and amber glows there, ringed by green coils and small circular spirals. Around it, deep cobalt blue fills all four corners.

Nothing in the picture is lit from a direction, and nothing casts a shadow. The glow comes from color alone: saturated orange and gold set against dark blue. That is the piece I'm asking you to look at.

What Your Eye Finds When It Stays a Little Longer

The whole design is mirrored left to right and top to bottom. That is why the center reads like a symmetrical, totem-like figure made of liquid swirls. A vertical column of orange shapes runs through the middle, and teal arrows point inward from both sides.

Then the lines take over. Curling forms spiral and fold inward and outward from the center, and the symmetry keeps all that motion in balance. Your eye travels around the central form and keeps finding small spirals it missed the first time.

The surfaces are smooth and glossy, like marbled or melted paint, with fine swirling grain in the blue areas. Teal, aqua and yellow-green scrolls curl between the corners and along the edges. Small touches of violet, lavender and rust sit among them. A thin dark border and a yellow band frame the image, so it looks finished before it is ever framed.

Where Ember Totem Belongs

This is a horizontal, high-saturation piece with a clear center, so it can carry a wall. Think of the space over a sofa, a console or a bed, or use it as the color anchor of a room. The cobalt corners and bright middle give a plain wall a strong focal point. Because the design is so balanced, it can also sit in a quieter spot like an entry or above a desk.

The mood is lively and ornamental, with an underlying sense of order. It suits a living room, studio, entry or office that wants a bold, pattern-rich piece. You choose the size when you order, so you can fit it to your wall.
