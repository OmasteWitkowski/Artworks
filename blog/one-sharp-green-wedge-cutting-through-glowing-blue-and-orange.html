The Library · Omaste Witkowski

One Sharp Green Wedge Cutting Through Glowing Blue and Orange

Emerald Wedge is a luminous abstract close-up of translucent, glass-like facets in emerald, cobalt, burnt orange and golden yellow.

Look at the upper left first. A pointed green wedge, sharp as a blade, cuts across the frame. Past it, cobalt blue glows at the top and warm orange, yellow and dusty rose fill the bottom.

There is no object to name and no figure to find. What you get is color that seems lit from within, and your eye has plenty of places to go.

What Your Eye Finds, Piece by Piece

I won't invent a backstory for this piece. The image tells its own, so I'll point out what I see in it.

The green wedge sets the direction. It pulls your eye across and down the frame, then the blue and orange bands on the right carry it onward in a long, flowing sweep.

Then the light takes over. White highlights flash along the edges and creases, while deeper greens and blues pool in the folds. The planes look wet and polished, and tiny bubbles or flecks sit in the yellow and clear areas.

Some edges are crisp and some zones dissolve into soft color. That contrast keeps this from becoming one blurry wash of brightness. Your eye keeps finding a new place to rest.

Where It Belongs on Your Wall

This is a horizontal, full-bleed image, so it suits a wide wall such as the space over a sofa, a console or a desk. The cool top and warm bottom make it a strong focal point in a neutral room.

It also works as part of a bold, gallery-style group. The mood is vivid, energetic and lively, and the saturated color is reason enough to hang it.
