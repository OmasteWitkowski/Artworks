A Heart That Is Only Light and Color, Never Drawn

Ember Heart is an abstract macro photograph of a glowing red and purple heart-like form, floating in soft, translucent color.

Look at the center of the frame. Two rounded lobes sit above a pointed base, and the shape reads as a heart. But nothing is outlined. The heart is made from overlapping planes of deep violet and saturated red, so you see it as an impression, not a symbol.

Around it, amber, blush pink, dusty blue, peach and cream melt into one another, out of focus. The red and purple form seems to float in the middle of them, glowing.

What the eye finds when it stays a while

From across a room, you see a warm, saturated shape in a calm field of color. The red and violet planes lean diagonally toward the point at the bottom, so your eye is drawn down and into the center.

Come closer and the light begins to matter. It looks as if it is passing through colored glass. Thin bright highlights trace the curving edges at the top and lower right, and pale areas glow at the left and lower right.

Closer still, there are small details. A few tiny bubble-like droplets sit near the top corners. Fine dark specks show in the upper purple. These small things give the smooth color some depth.

The shallow, out-of-focus depth is what keeps the heart from becoming a greeting-card image. It stays soft, a little dreamlike, and open to however you read it.

Where Ember Heart belongs

The horizontal format and the centered red and violet form suit the wall above a bed, a sofa or a console. The soft blurred fields keep it calm from across the room, while the saturated center adds a clear point of warmth, even to a neutral space.

The mood is romance and tenderness. It suits a bedroom or living room, or a gift for someone who would like a heart on the wall that feels like light and color.
