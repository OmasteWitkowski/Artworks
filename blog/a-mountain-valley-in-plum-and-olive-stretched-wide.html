A Mountain Valley in Plum and Olive, Stretched Wide

Plum Ridges Panorama is a painterly digital landscape: a long, quiet view over a violet-washed valley, available as a print on Fine Art America.

Most mountain pictures hand you bright blue sky, crisp green slopes and sharp detail. This one does the opposite. The ridges are dusky violet and plum, the sky is pale lavender fading to blue, and everything is softly brushed.

The view is very wide and very low, like looking out from a hillside at the end of the day. Dark evergreens frame the bottom edge, and the valley opens out below them.

What the eye finds when it starts to travel

I won't tell you a made-up story about where this view is. What I can tell you is what happens when you look at it. You start at the dark conifers in the foreground, then drop into the valley, where pale roads wind between fields and stands of dark forest.

Follow a road and your eye is led back toward the first ridge. Behind it comes another, then another. Each range is a little paler and hazier than the one before, so the mountains read as bands of atmosphere rather than literal rock.

Above them the streaked clouds sweep left to right, with a band of dusty blue at the upper right. A little warm tan touches the valley slopes, just enough to warm the cool violets and olive.

The surface is smooth and painterly, with softened edges and faint brush-like swirls in the sky and hills. It is digital art with an oil-paint softness, and the calm comes from that softness together with the wide view.
