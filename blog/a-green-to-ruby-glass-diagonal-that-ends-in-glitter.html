A Green-to-Ruby Glass Diagonal That Ends in Glitter

Ruby Ember Current is a glowing glass macro abstract: one bold diagonal of green, red and amber crossing a warm orange field.

Picture a warm orange field, soft and out of focus at the edges. Across it runs a single diagonal form. It starts green at the top, turns ruby and crimson through the middle, and deepens to amber near the bottom.

The layers look lit from within, and bright white highlights sit on the glossy curves of the red. That is the first thing you see in Ruby Ember Current. The longer you look, the more it holds.

Follow the diagonal with your eye

I'll tell you what happens when you look at this piece, because the image does the work. Your eye enters at the green top and slides down through the red. The red is the brightest, glossiest part, and it is where the light seems to pass through.

The amber section ends at a rough, speckled pad in the lower right corner. After all that smooth, liquid-looking surface, the texture changes, and your eye stops there. That corner patch holds the whole diagonal in place.

Around the form, small bubbles and droplets drift through the orange. A round red drop is cropped at the left edge, which gives the frame a sense of depth. At the bottom there are fine ridges and thin curling lines, and cool violet and blue edges set off the warmth.

What you will find in the image

  • A bold diagonal from upper center to lower right gives the horizontal frame direction and energy.
  • Green, ruby and amber shift along one form, so the color changes as your eye moves.
  • Light seems to glow through the translucent layers, which makes the colors look lit from within.
  • Smooth, glassy surfaces meet a rough, sparkling granular patch, so the eye has a place to land.
  • Tiny bubbles, droplets and fine curling ridges reward a closer look.
  • Shallow depth of field blurs the edges and leaves the central form crisp, like a macro study of color.

Where it belongs

The palette is warm and saturated: orange, ruby and amber, with emerald, violet and blue at the edges. It suits a living room, studio or office that needs a strong color presence. The horizontal format and diagonal movement work over a sofa, console or desk.

The mood is vivid and energetic, with a jewel-like glow. The appeal here is the color and the light.

  • This is for you if you like a strong diagonal in a horizontal composition.
