One Gold Star, Held Still Inside a Swirling Blue Diamond

Jewel Box Star is a symmetrical abstract in which a glowing golden star sits calmly at the center of liquid cobalt, red-orange and turquoise.

Start at the center. A large golden star with eight long points glows against dark chocolate brown, with four smaller stars placed near the corners of its square. Everything around it is perfectly balanced, left to right and top to bottom.

Then look outward. That brown square rests on a tilted cobalt-blue diamond filled with swirling red-orange, magenta-purple, turquoise and olive green. The center is still. The color around it is not.

What your eye finds, one layer at a time

I built this piece as nested squares: an upright frame, a tilted diamond, and a central panel. A thin orange line runs around each layer with a soft halo, so every edge looks lit from within.

The diamond is where the piece moves. Its marbled blue swirls curl toward the corners, and the red-orange forms there mirror each other. Against that, the star's points radiate evenly outward and hold perfectly still.

Look closer at the gold and you will see a faint mottled, flame-like grain inside the stars. The dark brown and gold read as a night sky, and the saturated color around them adds a festive, jewel-like richness.

What you'll see in this piece

  • A large golden eight-pointed star at the center. It gives the whole image one clear point of focus.
  • Four smaller golden stars near the corners of the brown panel. They echo the main star and keep the center feeling full and balanced.
  • Strict four-way symmetry in nested squares. The composition reads as ordered and mandala-like from across a room.
  • Thin glowing orange lines with a soft halo around each layer. They make the stars and edges look lit from within against the dark brown.
  • A marbled, fluid swirl of blue, red-orange, purple and turquoise in the diamond. It adds slow movement against the rigid geometry.
  • A patterned blue, purple and turquoise border. It frames the image and carries the cool colors to the outer edge.

Where it belongs on your wall

The square, centered, symmetrical design works as a focal piece on a single wall. Think above a sofa, a bed or a console, or at the end of a hallway.

The deep brown and gold sit well in warm, moody rooms. The cobalt and turquoise add a cooler contrast, so the piece feels rich without feeling heavy.
