One Green Lens Pulls Every Color Toward It

Green Lens, Golden Arc is an abstract color study: a dark-rimmed round opening, like an eye, set in swirling green, yellow and teal bands.

Look left of center. There is a round opening with a dark rim, and inside it blue-violet and gold. It reads like an eye or a lens, and once you have seen it, it is hard to look anywhere else.

Around it, bands of vivid green, golden yellow and teal curve in wave-like arcs, as if the whole surface were being drawn toward that one circle. Over the top, a golden-brown band sweeps toward the upper right.

What your eye finds when it stays a while

I'll tell you what is in the image, because that is the honest story of this piece. Most colorful abstracts spread their energy evenly across the frame. Here the energy has a destination. Every band bends around the circle, and your eye gets somewhere to land.

Then the golden arc takes over. It curves diagonally from the lower center to the upper right, carrying your gaze across the opening and out of the frame. Stay a little longer and the details begin to separate. Small bright highlights sit along the edge of the opening, cyan glows against amber depths, and a line of tiny sparkling specks runs down the left side.

The surface looks glossy and translucent, with fine streaks, ripples and small bubble-like specks. The color seems lit from within, with glowing yellows and cyans set against darker blue-violet and brown pockets. There is no horizon and no background, only close, absorbing color.

What you'll see in Green Lens, Golden Arc

  • A dark-rimmed round opening with a blue-violet and gold interior. It gives the piece one clear focal point that draws people in.
  • Concentric green, yellow and teal bands curving around the circle. They make the whole frame feel pulled together and in motion.
  • A golden-brown arc sweeping from the lower center to the upper right. It leads the eye across the circle and out to the right.
  • Translucent color with bright highlights and deeper blue-violet and brown pockets. It looks lit from within and adds depth to a flat wall.
  • A glossy, glass-like look with fine streaks, ripples and tiny bubble-like specks. Up close there is more to discover each time you look.
  • Accents of deep cobalt, violet, amber, turquoise and pale aqua. They add richness without breaking up the green and gold.

Where it belongs

This is a horizontal, full-bleed piece with saturated color and a strong off-center focal point. It suits a wall that needs a confident color statement, such as above a sofa, a bed, a console or a desk. It is bold enough to be the main color note in the room.

The mood is energetic and vivid. It does not try to be quiet. It is meant to hold attention, and it works simply because it is striking to look at.
