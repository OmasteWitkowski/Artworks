A Dark Mask-Like Core Inside a Blaze of Mirrored Orange

Ember Medallion is a symmetrical orange abstract with a brown and olive center, glowing gold swirls and red forms at the top and bottom.

Start at the middle. A dark brown and olive medallion sits dead center, and it can look a little like a mask or a face. Around it, smooth, translucent swirls of saturated orange and golden yellow spread outward.

Then notice that nothing is random. The left side mirrors the right, and the top mirrors the bottom. Red petal-like shapes anchor the top and bottom edges, and the whole horizontal image balances around a single point.

What your eye finds when it stays a while

The first thing you see is the glow. The yellow and orange areas near the edges look luminous and translucent, while the center stays darker and more muted. That contrast pulls your eye inward to the medallion.

Once you reach the center, the curves carry you back out. They repeat outward and curl into spirals at the corners and sides, like layered ribbons of smoke or molten glass. The motion is rhythmic and flame-like.

Up close, the surfaces are smooth and liquid-looking, with fine wispy lines and ripples. Small speckled red details sit near the center. The mask-like impression is only that, an impression, and I'd rather you decide for yourself what the medallion is.

Most orange abstracts feel loose or chaotic. This one is ornamental and ordered, so the heat stays under control.

Where this piece belongs

Ember Medallion is a horizontal piece with a bold central form and saturated warm color. It suits a statement wall, such as above a sofa, a bed, a console or a desk, where you want warmth and a clear focal point.

The mirrored symmetry also suits formal or ornamental rooms. The mood is warm, energetic and bold, but orderly rather than wild.
