A Magenta Diamond Glows at the Exact Center of This Golden Oval

Mirrored Lantern is a tall, symmetrical abstract in golden yellow, teal and olive, built around one glowing pink diamond.

Look at the middle first. A hot magenta-pink diamond sits at the exact center of a tall golden-yellow oval, and it is the brightest point in the whole piece.

Everything else is arranged around it. Teal and turquoise curls gather on either side, and ribbons of yellow, amber and chartreuse swirl outward in mirrored pairs.

What Your Eye Finds, Step by Step

The first thing you see is the glow. The layers of the oval are translucent and overlap, so the whole shape looks lit from behind. A faint pinkish halo traces its edge.

Then your eye starts to travel. It lands on the pink center and follows the curls up and down toward the points of the oval. Orange-red fan shapes close it at the top and bottom.

The longer you stay, the more you notice. The muted olive-green border carries fainter echoes of the same swirls. Up close, the surface is smooth and liquid, with soft transparent overlaps and fine pale lines, and no visible brushwork.

I'm not going to hand you a backstory for this one. What I can tell you is what is on the surface: warm color, steady balance, and one bright point that holds the attention.

What You'll See in Mirrored Lantern

  • A magenta diamond at the exact center. It gives the eye one clear place to land.
  • Strict left-right mirror symmetry inside a tall oval. This makes the piece calm and ordered, and easy to center on a wall.
  • Translucent, overlapping layers with a faint pink halo. The oval looks backlit, so the warm colors seem to glow.
  • Golden yellow, amber and chartreuse set against teal, turquoise and hot magenta. The warm and cool colors make each other look brighter.
  • Swirling ribbons in mirrored pairs. They carry the eye from the center toward the top and bottom points.
  • A smooth, liquid surface with fine pale lines. It reads as clean and ornamental, not busy or muddy.

Where It Belongs

This is a vertical piece with a single focal point, so it suits a hallway, an entry, a narrow wall, or the space above a console or a bed. The strict symmetry makes it simple to center, and it would pair well with a second piece.

The golden warmth sits comfortably beside neutral or olive-toned rooms. The teal and pink add a bright accent without crowding the space.
