One White Summit, Half a Frame of Deep Blue Sky

"Quiet Summit, Snowy Peak Over Evergreen Ridges" is my landscape photograph of a bright snow-capped peak framed by dark conifer ridges under a cloudless sky.

Start with three colors: a saturated sky blue, a near-black evergreen and a snow white that looks lit from within. This photograph is built almost entirely from them.

A single snow-covered peak sits slightly right of center. Dark forested ridges frame it on both sides, and roughly the top half of the picture is open, cloudless sky.

What the Eye Finds, in Order

I'm Omaste Witkowski. I'd rather tell you what is in this image than invent a story around it, so here is what I see. The eye lands on the white summit first. Against a flat, deep blue sky it is the brightest thing in the frame.

Then the ridgelines take over. They slope diagonally down toward a V-shaped valley in the middle, and your eye follows them back up to the peak. Everything else stays still, which is why the scene feels quiet.

Look closer and the textures separate. The conifer slopes are finely grained. The grassy hillsides and bare rust-brown patches are smoother. Dark rock shows through the snow near the top, and the farthest ridge fades to slate blue. The long, compressed perspective stacks these layers one behind another.
