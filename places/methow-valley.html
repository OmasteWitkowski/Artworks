Methow Valley Art

The Methow Valley, in north-central Washington, is where I spend part of my time. These pieces are of places in and around the valley.

There are 66 pieces here. Most are horizontal. The colours that come up most are green, blue and brown. They suit rooms like the living room, the home office and the entry & hallway. Narrow them by colour and shape below, and see any of them on a wall at its real size.
