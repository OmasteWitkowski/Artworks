Winthrop Art

Winthrop, in the Methow Valley, is where my friend Garth Mudge blows the glass I photograph, at Glassworks, and where my work hangs at the Hotel Rio Vista.

There are 11 pieces here. Most are horizontal. The colours that come up most are blue, green and brown. Narrow them by colour and shape below, and see any of them on a wall at its real size.
