Kitchen Wall Art

Kitchens have less wall than you think, and the wall they have is seen every day. These pieces work on a short run of wall or above a counter.

Hanging tip. Keep art in a kitchen away from the cooktop and sink, and favour a print you can wipe, such as metal or acrylic. Press “See it in this room” on any piece below to hang it in the room above, at real size.
