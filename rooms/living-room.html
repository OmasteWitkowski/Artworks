Living Room Wall Art

The wall over the sofa is the one everyone sees first, and often the one that has stayed bare the longest. These pieces are wide enough, or bold enough, to hold it.

Hanging tip. A simple rule: choose art about two-thirds the width of the sofa, and hang it 8 to 10 inches above the back. Press “See it in this room” on any piece to try it at real size.
