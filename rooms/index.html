Sizing, the way a designer would

The rules I use, room by room.

  • A long wallOver a seven-foot sofa, art should be about 56 to 63 inches wide: one large piece, or two or three hung 2 to 3 inches apart so they read as one.
  • Living RoomA simple rule: choose art about two-thirds the width of the sofa, and hang it 8 to 10 inches above the back.
  • BedroomOver a queen bed, a piece 40 to 48 inches wide sits well. Hang it 8 to 12 inches above the headboard.
  • KitchenKeep art in a kitchen away from the cooktop and sink, and favour a print you can wipe, such as metal or acrylic.
  • Home OfficeOver a desk, hang the centre of the art at about eye level when you are standing, around 57 to 60 inches from the floor.
  • Entry & HallwayA tall, narrow piece suits the wall beside a door; a panorama or a row of pieces suits a long hallway.
