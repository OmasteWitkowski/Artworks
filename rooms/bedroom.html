Bedroom Wall Art

The wall over the bed is the last thing you see at night and the first in the morning. These pieces suit a room you want to come back to.

Hanging tip. Over a queen bed, a piece 40 to 48 inches wide sits well. Hang it 8 to 12 inches above the headboard. Press “See it in this room” on any piece to try it at real size.
