Ember Drift, Red and Gold Fluid Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Swirling masses of deep crimson, burnt orange, amber gold and olive green flow across a horizontal field, broken by one pale grey-white area at the right. The colors melt into one another along jagged, feathery edges, and fine rippling lines run through the surface like liquid.

The piece stands out for its light. The inner image glows as if lit from within, with bright yellow highlights near the top center and a thin golden edge against a wide dark maroon border. That one cool, pale patch gives the hot colors a counterweight and keeps the whole from reading as a single block of red.

Look longer and the movement becomes clear. Serrated edges point diagonally across the frame and the colors seem to drift toward the lower right, while large red shapes anchor the lower left and center. The dark border carries a faint embossed pattern of circles and curls. The mood is warm and dramatic, with the intensity of embers or autumn color.

The dark surround and warm glow suit a living room, bedroom, den or office wall, where the horizontal format works over a sofa, bed or console. It pairs well with deep neutrals, wood tones and rooms that already lean toward rich color.
