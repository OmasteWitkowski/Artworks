Hear Me Now

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A dark, close-up abstract image of glowing, ribbed forms in white, purple and magenta sweeping in curved lines across the centre. Warm orange reflections fill the upper left and blurred pink lights glow at the right. The surface looks glassy and streaked, with sharp highlights against deep black shadow.

Luminous white, violet and magenta ridges sweep across a dark ground like a glowing shell or wing, with warm orange and pink glints at the edges, so it works as a bold, high-contrast abstract.
