Cobalt Starbloom, Blue Abstract on Glowing Gold

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A deep cobalt blue form sits at the center of a glowing field of gold and orange. Its angular points radiate outward like a star or the petals of a flower. Green tendrils, aqua streaks and small spiral swirls curl around it on both sides, and red-orange warms the lower corners. Rippled blue lines run along the bottom edge.

The piece is memorable for its contrast and its sense of flow. The cool, dark blue center is set against warm yellow and orange, and pale highlights on the inner edges of the blue form add depth. The surfaces look smooth, glossy and poured, as if the whole scene is melting and stretching outward from the middle.

Take your time with it. The central form may read as a star, a flower or neither, and the rippling lines at the base can suggest water or a reflection. The mirrored tendrils and spirals on the left and right create a steady rhythm around the blue center, while the dripping, curling shapes keep the eye moving.

The horizontal format and strong focal point suit a wall that needs a bold, colorful statement, such as above a sofa, a bed, a console or a desk. Its vivid blue and gold palette will energize a neutral room or pick up warm and cool accents already in the space.
