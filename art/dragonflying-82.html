Ruby Ember Current, Glowing Glass Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A bold diagonal of glossy, translucent color crosses a warm orange field. It begins green at the top, turns ruby and crimson through the middle, and deepens to amber before ending in a speckled, sandy-looking patch at the lower right. Small bubbles and droplets float around it, and a round red drop is cropped at the left edge.

The color and the light make this piece distinctive. The layered areas glow as if lit from within, and bright white highlights sit on the curves of the red form. Smooth, liquid-looking surfaces meet the rough, sparkling patch, so the eye travels the full length of the diagonal and settles in the corner.

Look closer and you find fine ridges and thin curling lines at the bottom, tiny pinpoint specks across the orange, and cool violet and blue edges that set off the warmth. The shallow depth of field softens the surroundings and leaves the central form crisp. The curves give a sense of slow movement.

The saturated warm palette suits a living room, studio, office or any space that needs a strong color presence. The horizontal format and diagonal energy work well over a sofa, console or desk.
