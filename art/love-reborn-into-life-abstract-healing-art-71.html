Emerald Ember Windows: Mirrored Abstract in Green and Orange

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A central upright oval holds a deep green ovoid shape, flanked by two mirrored rectangular panels. Each panel shows a large glossy green form with small spiral curls, set against swirls of burnt orange, fiery red and golden yellow. Layered maroon rectangles step inward from the edges, with touches of peach, lavender and olive in the details.

The design rests on strict bilateral symmetry. The same green form repeats in the center and in both panels, glowing against the molten color around it. The nested frames create depth like a tunnel or a row of stacked windows, and they draw the eye toward the central oval.

Look longer and the details start to move. Mirrored swirls and small spirals radiate outward in a slow, blooming rotation. The orange and yellow areas appear lit from behind, as if light were passing through colored glass, while the green shapes carry softer internal highlights. The surface looks smooth and glossy, with a faint canvas-like weave beneath.

The mood is warm, rich and celebratory, held steady by the ordered symmetry. Its appeal is visual: saturated color, luminous contrast and a composition that feels both lively and calm.

The horizontal format and strong central axis suit a main wall above a sofa, bed or console. The deep maroon frame anchors the piece in a room, and the orange and green give it energy.
