Ember Lace, Red Layers Beside Green

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Stacked horizontal bands of rust red, deep brown and cream rise on the left, covered in a fine, lace-like network of cracks. A narrow vertical seam of cobalt blue and glowing orange runs down the middle. To the right, a smooth green and golden-yellow field streaks sideways and is dotted with tiny bubble-like specks. The left edge fades into a soft, hazy glow.

Two textures share one frame. The cracked, cellular surface on the red-brown layers sits against the calm, glassy green field. The orange and yellow areas seem lit from behind, while the darker red bands read as denser and shadowed. Small bright highlights sit along the cream ridges.

Look closely and the image keeps shifting. The bands look dragged sideways, the layers climb in a slow upward rhythm, and the thin vertical seam interrupts the motion. The warmth feels molten and absorbing, and it works as a pure visual pleasure.

The horizontal format and warm, saturated palette suit a sofa wall, a console or a hallway. The green and blue notes pair well with natural materials and neutral rooms.
