Cobalt Eye in Amber, Abstract Glass Close-Up

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A tilted oval of deep cobalt blue fills the left of the frame, holding a hazy, pale blue-white center streaked with soft ripples. Around it runs a glowing amber-orange band edged by a dark brown-black rim. To the right, a teal and green field is dotted with tiny bubbles and fine streaks, and it softens into blur at the edge.

The concentric, eye-like oval is what sets this piece apart. The rings step outward in blue, amber, dark brown and teal, and the strong tonal contrast makes the colors look lit from behind, like light passing through glass.

Up close, the surface is full of small events: bright highlights on the bubbles, droplet-like specks, faint hairline cracks across the amber band, and streaks that sweep toward the right edge. Sharp focus sits on the left and center, and the right side dissolves into shallow depth of field.

The mood is vivid, luminous and absorbing, and the central form invites you to keep looking.

The horizontal format and saturated blue and amber suit a living room, studio or office wall as a single bold statement, and the color pairs well with neutral or dark surroundings.
