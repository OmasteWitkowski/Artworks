Eye of the Swirl, Pink Flower

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A single pink flower fills the center of the frame, seen from above. Its ruffled, papery petals are shaded with lilac between the folds, and a cluster of deep crimson stamens glows at its heart. Behind it, orange, gold and green grass is pulled into a spiral that circles the bloom.

The flower is sharp and brightly lit while everything around it is smeared into streaky, brush-like color. That contrast is what makes the piece distinctive: a still, crisp bloom at the eye of a whirlpool, with the cool pink set against warm orange and olive.

Look closely and the concentric bands of the swirl lead the eye steadily inward to the red center, while a thin blade of green grass curves along the upper left, following the spiral. The mood is energetic and playful, a bloom held still while the world spins around it.

Its horizontal format and saturated color suit a living room, bedroom, studio or creative space where you want one lively, colorful focal point.
