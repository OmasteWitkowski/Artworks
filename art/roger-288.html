Two shapes seem to look at each other across your wall

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Eye to Eye is a violet and ivory abstract in which a glowing red oval, like an eye, meets a large purple dome along one soft curve.

Maybe the sofa is new and the wall above it has looked bare ever since. Or the old picture now looks small in the room. You have probably looked at a lot of purple abstracts and found most of them are just fields of colour, with nowhere for the eye to land.

This one gives the eye a place to land. On the left, a cream-white form holds a deep red oval, ringed like an eye. On the right, a large violet dome fills the frame. The two press toward each other, and you stay a little longer than you meant to.

I'll tell you what the image shows, because that is what I know for certain. A cream-white form sits on the left. In it is the red oval, and just below it a soft fold that reads to me like a lip. On the right, the violet dome glows from deep plum to bright lavender.

What holds me is the meeting. The ivory form and the purple dome press toward each other along one soft, curving line. The red oval seems to look across at the dome, and for me it becomes a quiet face-to-face encounter. That is an impression, not a fact about the piece, and you may see something else entirely.

Look closer and the surfaces keep giving. They look smooth and wet, almost like glass, with bright glints on the ivory. Small bubbles, droplets and glittering specks sit on the dark left panel and the amber area above it. Flashes of cobalt blue, olive green and magenta show at the borders.

I believe art is felt before it is explained. There is no right reading here.

The format is horizontal, with a light left side against a saturated purple right side. That suits a wide wall over a sofa, a bed or a console, where one strong colour can lead the room.

The deep violet and near-black navy sit well in a neutral or moody space. The ivory and purple also suit a room that already has cream, plum, charcoal or deep blue in it.
