Hearth Light, Mirrored Abstract in Orange and Rust

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A bright cream-white form glows at the centre of this piece. Curved bands in orange, rust red and brown arch around it from both sides, and olive green shapes and a dark horizontal bar sit below. The left and right halves mirror each other exactly. Fine lines like wood grain run through the smooth, liquid-looking surfaces, and thin glowing outlines trace the curves.

The glow is what holds you. It is brightest at the middle and fades outward into deeper, warmer colour, so the whole image looks lit from behind. The symmetry pulls your eye inward and keeps it there.

Look a little longer and the arrangement starts to read as something. For some people it is a face, for others a totem figure, and for others a hearth or a doorway with light behind it. I would rather you decide than tell you.

The palette is warm and earthy, with amber and yellow near the light and olive green to steady it. It suits a wall in a room with wood, cream or natural tones, over a sofa or a bed, where a centred, horizontal piece can hold the middle of the room.
