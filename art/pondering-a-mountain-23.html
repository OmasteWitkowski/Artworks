Cobalt Mirror, Fallen Logs Below Snowy Peaks

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A still pond fills a mountain valley and mirrors a saturated blue sky in bright, broken patches. Big weathered logs and branches lie across the foreground. Dead stumps and golden reeds stand in the shallow water, and evergreen and bare trees line both banks. Snow-dusted peaks rise in the distance under streaky white clouds.

The colour is what stops you. Deep cobalt blue sits against rust-brown bark and warm ochre light, with deep shadows and glowing highlights. Short, curved, swirling strokes cover sky, water and wood in a thick, oil-paint-like texture, while the bark and grasses keep fine grain. The scene is representational but heightened and painterly.

The logs sweep diagonally from the lower left toward the upper right. The rippling reflections then carry the eye back across the water to the mountains. The mood is calm, quiet and open, with a wild stillness set against bold colour contrast.

The horizontal format and strong foreground suit a main wall over a sofa, bed or mantel. The cool blues and warm browns also work in cabin, lodge or modern-rustic rooms.
