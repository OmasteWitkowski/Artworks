Pondering a Mountain

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A still mountain pond is framed by bare trees, evergreens and golden grasses, with weathered fallen logs stretching across the foreground. Blue sky and clouds are reflected in the water, and snow-capped mountains rise in the distance. The whole scene has a swirling, painted look.

A vivid, painterly mountain wetland where saturated blue reflections meet warm, weathered driftwood and golden grasses, so the scene reads as both a real place and a rich textured surface.
