Cobalt Mirror, Fallen Logs Below Snowy Peaks

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A still pond in a mountain valley reflects bright blue sky, with large weathered logs and branches lying across the foreground. Dead stumps and golden reeds stand in the water, and evergreen and bare trees line both banks. Snow-capped peaks rise in the distance under a streaky blue and white sky. The whole scene has a swirling, painterly texture and strongly saturated colors.

The story of this pieceCobalt Blue Meets Rust and Gold in One Still Mountain PondA saturated blue sky drops into a still mountain pond, and big weathered logs lead your eye across it to the snowy peaks.
