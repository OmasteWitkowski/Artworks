Ember Petal, Melting Orange and Violet Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A glossy red petal-like shape glows just left of center, edged with thin pale aqua highlights. Around it, bands of burnt orange, mustard yellow, coral and periwinkle violet curve and melt into one another. Fine ripple lines run through each color field, and a large S-shaped orange ribbon sweeps through the right side.

The composition splits between warm and cool. Oranges and yellows fill the left and bottom, while a broad lavender-violet field opens across the center and right. The red form sits at the meeting point of the two. A pale violet oval floats in the right half, framed by curling orange lines, and spirals gather in the upper right corner.

Look closely and the red shape suggests a petal or flower bud without ever becoming one. The contour lines inside the color bands read like wood grain or topographic ripples, and the edge highlights make the surface look wet and reflective. The mood is lively, warm and a little dreamy.

The wide horizontal format suits a sofa wall, bedroom or entry. Its warm-to-cool color balance can hold the center of a room with neutral furnishings, or pick up orange and violet accents already there.
