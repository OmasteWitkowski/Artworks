Ember Chalice, Mirrored Marbled Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Marbled ribbons of deep teal, emerald, burnt orange and saturated red swirl outward from a central vertical axis. They are mirrored exactly left to right. At the center, a pale heart- or chalice-shaped opening sits above a slim hourglass form that ends in a rounded, red-centered bulb. A warm yellow glow rises from the bottom edge.

The composition is perfectly symmetrical, and the center seems to glow against darker, richer surroundings. Fine contour lines trace every fold like ripples or wood grain. Translucent layers give the surface a smooth, glassy depth. The jewel-tone palette and the strong focal point set it apart from busier, all-over abstracts.

The mirrored folds can be read in several ways. They might be wings, faces or masks, though nothing is clearly representational. The eye is pulled down the central stem, then carried outward along the curling ribbons toward the corners. The effect is ornate, hypnotic and warm at its core.

Its horizontal format and deep, saturated field suit a wide wall, such as above a sofa, bed, sideboard or console. The glowing center works as a focal point in a room that welcomes bold color.
