A Glowing Heart at the Center of a Perfectly Mirrored Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ember Chalice is a mirrored, marbled digital abstract in teal, emerald, amber and red, built around a pale heart-shaped glow.

You have a wide wall above the sofa, the bed or the sideboard. The furniture is settled and the paint is done, but the wall still looks unfinished. Whatever you hang there needs to hold its own across all that width.

This piece does that from the middle. Marbled ribbons of deep teal and green pour outward from a pale, glowing opening shaped like a heart or a chalice. The edges are dark and rich, so the center looks lit from within.

First it goes to the center. A pale heart- or chalice-shaped opening sits above a slim hourglass stem that ends in a rounded bulb with a red center. A warm yellow glow rises from the bottom edge, and the stem leads your eye down toward it.

Then the eye follows the ribbons outward. They curl and loop away from the center and sweep toward the corners. Everything is mirrored exactly left to right, so the movement has a steady pulse, like a slow beat.

Look longer and the folds on either side start to read as something else: wings, faces, masks. Nothing in the picture is clearly any of them. I'll leave that reading to you.

Come closer still and you will see fine contour lines tracing every fold, like ripples or wood grain. Translucent layers overlap beneath them, which gives the surface a smooth, glassy depth. It is listed as digital art.

The format is horizontal, so it suits a wide wall: above a sofa, a bed, a sideboard or a console. The glowing center works as an anchor, and the dark, saturated edges frame it.

The palette is deep and dramatic. It suits a room that welcomes strong color, such as a living room, a dining room or a bedroom, and a room that is quieter and neutral can take it as its one bold statement.
