A heart with two glowing red lenses at its center

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · horizontal

Ember Heart is a mirrored red-orange abstract inside a thin-lined heart, glowing like light through colored glass.

You know the wall. It's over the bed, or the sofa, or at the end of the hallway, and it has looked a little bare for a long time. Maybe the furniture is settled and the paint is right, and you still haven't found the one piece that belongs there.

Picture a heart drawn in a thin dark red line, centered on pale cream. It's a plain shape you can read from across the room. Then step closer and find that the heart is full of light.

Inside the outline, the pattern is mirrored down the middle. Two large rounded diamonds in deep red-orange sit side by side, their edges glowing yellow-orange. A green-gold X joins them at the center. Small teardrops and a pointed, leaf-like shape run along the center line.

What holds me here is the light. The red shapes look softly lit from within, the way colored glass does when the sun is behind it. Bands of amber, plum and olive curve in from the upper corners and carry your eye to the crossing point in the middle, then back out again.

The surfaces are glossy, with a fine speckle and tiny bubble-like droplets. They look liquid, though that is only how they look. Some people will see a pair of eyes or a mask in the two big shapes. You may see something else entirely.

What the heart means is yours to decide. I believe art is felt, not seen, and your own response is the only test that counts.

Ember Heart is a single, centered piece for a wall that wants warmth. The cream surround lets it stand alone over a bed, over a sofa, on a console wall or at the end of a hallway. The format is horizontal, so it suits a wide wall.

The mood is warm, rich and a little celebratory. It carries a romantic note without the look of a greeting card, because the heart is plain and the pattern inside it is where the interest lies.

Screens differ, so I can't promise an exact match. Expect warm burnt orange, deep red and amber on a cream ground. If it doesn't look right in your room, Fine Art America's 30-day money-back guarantee covers you.

Pay attention to whether it stops you. That is all the test there is. If the glow, the colors or the shape of the heart pull you back for another look, that is reason enough, and you don't need to know anything about art to trust it.
