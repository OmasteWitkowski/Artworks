Ember Glow, Translucent Red Glass Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Glossy, translucent shapes in saturated red, warm orange and amber gold overlap and melt into a pale cream-yellow ground. Teal and deep cobalt blue gather along the right edge, with burgundy, soft violet and touches of olive and dark green around them. Warm light seems to glow through the red and amber forms.

The focus falls on the red glass at upper center, with its crisp, wet-looking edge, speckled interior and tiny bubbles. A dark crimson slab sits below it, and a fine golden glitter shows at lower left. Everything else softens into blur, so the sharp jewel-like detail stands out against pastel surroundings.

Look closely and the shapes drift diagonally from upper left to lower right, in swirled bands at left and rounded blobs at right. The movement is slow and molten. The overall feeling is warm, vivid and playful, like colour that is celebrating something.

Its horizontal, edge-to-edge format and warm palette suit a living room, dining area, entry or office. It would work especially well on a neutral or cream wall, where the red and amber can act as the room's focal colour.
