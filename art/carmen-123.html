Ember Glow, Translucent Red Glass Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Glossy, translucent shapes in red, orange, amber, teal and deep blue overlap against a pale cream-yellow background. Warm light glows through the red and amber forms, and bright highlights catch on small bubbles and wet-looking edges. The lower left has a fine golden speckle, and the edges of the frame fall into soft blur. The overall look is liquid, colourful and abstract, with no identifiable scene.
