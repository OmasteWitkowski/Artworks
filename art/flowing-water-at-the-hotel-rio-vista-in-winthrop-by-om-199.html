Gilded Shallows, Clear Water Over Pebbles

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

Clear, shallow water moves over a bed of small rounded stones, seen from directly above. In the upper half, golden, tan and rust-colored pebbles glow in warm daylight beneath the rippled surface. Lower down, the water turns cooler and darker, a muted blue-green with soft swirling currents. Small white ripples and flecks of foam cut across the middle and right side.

The warm-to-cool shift is the heart of this piece. Sunlit stone gives way to deeper blue-green water, and bursts of white foam interrupt the change. The frame runs edge to edge with no horizon and no single focal point, so the eye keeps traveling across the surface. The result is a realistic photograph of water with the all-over rhythm of a pattern.

Look closely and the picture rewards it. Countless small stones sit beneath a glassy, crinkled surface, and the ripples bend and break the light. The overall feeling is calm and fresh, led by the clear water and the soft palette.

The horizontal format and balanced warm and cool tones suit a sofa, bed, console, entry or bathroom wall. It can sit quietly in a neutral room or add a natural, textured note to one with blue, green or earthy tones.
