Spider Sculpture

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

A large dark brown spider sculpture with thick, jointed legs and a rounded, textured body sits on a bright green lawn. Its two front pedipalps point toward the viewer and strong shadows fall across the leafy ground cover. Rocks and plants are visible along the top edge of the frame.

A head-on view of a giant patinated spider sculpture against vivid green grass makes a bold, slightly startling and memorable image, with detailed texture and sharp sunlit shadows.
