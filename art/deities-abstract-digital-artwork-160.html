Facing Embers - Mirrored Orange and Red Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Two sets of flowing, marbled shapes mirror each other across a narrow vertical strip, set inside a large red-orange circle. Each half holds a glowing orange oval that reads like an eye, with swirling forms around it like a muzzle or mask. Burnt orange, deep red and dark maroon dominate, with touches of teal and steel blue, golden yellow and pale cream. Wavy banded panels run to the outer edges inside a dark maroon border.

The pull of this piece is the unexpected sense of two creatures or masks facing each other. The symmetry is exact, and the curving bands bend inward toward the center line and loop around the glowing ovals. Bright orange and yellow ribbons seem to radiate against black-brown recesses, giving a backlit, stained-glass effect.

Look closely at the surface. It is smooth and glossy, and fine contour lines ripple through the shapes like wood grain or topographic lines. The faces are an impression rather than a stated subject, so what you find in the shapes is up to you. The mood is warm, intense and a little mysterious.

The high contrast and strong center seam make this a confident horizontal piece for a living room, den, office or lounge. It works best in a space where deep, warm colors are welcome.
