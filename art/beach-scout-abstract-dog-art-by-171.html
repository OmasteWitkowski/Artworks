Sandy Smile, Dog and Green Ball on the Beach

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

A reddish-brown dog lies on pale sand with its ears up and its mouth open, looking straight toward the viewer. A green-yellow ball rests on the sand in the lower foreground. Behind it lie a strip of calm water, dusty blue hills and a bright blue sky with soft white clouds.

What sets this piece apart is the dog's open-mouthed, expressive face and its coat, which heavy black outlines break into patches of chestnut and burnt orange. The single olive-green ball is the only sharp accent against the blue and sand.

Look closely and the wavy outlines on the clouds, the sand and the coat give the still scene a flickering rhythm. The dog sits right of centre and the ball sits lower right, so the eye drops from the face to the ball and back. Short shadows under both anchor them in clear daylight. The mood is playful and relaxed, a dog resting at the beach beside its ball.

The horizontal format and flat, graphic colour suit a lively spot above a sofa, in a hallway or mudroom, or in a casual coastal or family room. It reads clearly from across the space and makes an easy gift for a dog lover.
