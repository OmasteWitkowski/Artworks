One Open-Mouthed Dog, One Green Ball, One Bright Blue Sky

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

"Sandy Smile, Dog and Green Ball on the Beach" is a stylized digital illustration of a reddish-brown dog resting on pale sand, drawn in bold black outlines.

You've finished the room. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and there is still one wall that looks empty every time you walk past it.

Now picture a reddish-brown dog on that wall, lying on pale sand with its ears up and its mouth open, looking straight at you. A green ball sits in front of it, and a bright blue sky fills the space above.

Most people see the dog's face first. The mouth is open and the gaze is direct. I read it as a happy dog, though that is my reading and yours may differ.

Then the eye drops. The dog sits right of centre and the ball sits lower right, so your gaze travels from the face down to the ball and back up again. The ball is the only sharp green-yellow note in a picture of blue and sand, so it is easy to find.

Look closer and the coat is not smooth. Heavy black outlines break it into patches of chestnut and burnt orange. The same wavy lines run through the clouds and the sand, which gives a still scene a flickering rhythm.

This is a stylized digital illustration with flat colour and a graphic, poster-like finish. It is not a photograph. Behind the dog are a strip of calm water and dusty blue hills. Short shadows under the dog and the ball tell you it is bright, clear daylight.

The horizontal format suits a statement spot above a sofa, a console or a bed. It also works in a hallway, a mudroom or a casual beach-style room.

The flat colour and heavy outlines read clearly from across a room. The mood is playful and relaxed, a dog resting at the beach beside its ball.
