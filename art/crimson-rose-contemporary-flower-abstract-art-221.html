Ember Spiral Rose in Stained Glass Pattern

Botanical & Flowers · square

A single rose seen from above fills a nearly square white frame. Thick white lines trace its petals in a spiral from the small center out to a scalloped edge. Each petal is filled with a dense pattern of dark maroon outlines in burnt orange, deep red and golden yellow, with small touches of olive green and slate navy.

What sets it apart is the contrast between the open white spiral channels and the mosaic inside the petals: tiny cells, circles and miniature rose-like swirls, packed tightly. The surface glows as if light passes through colored glass. The light comes from no single direction, and a faint embossed texture sits beneath the pattern.

Look closely and the eye is drawn inward along the white lines, then pulled outward again into the small curling loops. It is a stylized rose, decorative and graphic rather than botanical. It is warm, vivid and energetic, and it is meant to be enjoyed for its color and detail.

The centered composition and clean white ground make it a confident focal point. It suits a wall where a bold, warm floral should stand on its own.
