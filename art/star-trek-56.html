Ember Mask: Glowing Amber Symmetrical Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ribbons of deep amber, golden yellow and peach curl across a near-black ground in perfect left-right mirror symmetry. At the center, swirling lines form a mask-like face with two spiral eyes, a pointed nose and a wide curved band below, all inside a rounded golden halo. Scrolling forms fold outward to both sides and into the lower corners.

The light is what sets this piece apart. The bright bands look backlit, and thin pale highlights trace the edges of each curve, so the whole composition seems to glow from within like molten glass. The surface is smooth and glossy, with overlapping translucent layers and fine concentric lines that reward a closer look.

The face is an optical suggestion rather than a depicted subject. It appears when the eye joins the spirals, the point and the band, then dissolves back into pure ribbon and curve. The S-shaped folds ripple and drift like smoke, and the mirror symmetry keeps all that motion in balance. The mood is warm, dramatic and a little otherworldly.

In a horizontal format, the dark ground and strong central focus suit a large wall. It works over a sofa, bed or console, or in any room that wants a bold, warm point of color.
