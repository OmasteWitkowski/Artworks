Cobalt Gust, Blue and Amber Streaks

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Streaks of cobalt, sky blue and teal sweep diagonally from the upper left, threaded with moss green and small amber and gold glints. On the right, the strands give way to a smooth, deep indigo field marked by a few faint curved lines. Below, the lower center darkens into a speckled shadow above a greenish edge and a pale blue strip.

The sweep looks like feathers or wind-swept strands, and it meets the calm cobalt mass with real force. The surface reads as glossy and glass-like, with fine scratch-like lines, small ridges and thin vertical streaks pulling downward. Warm amber against the cool blues keeps the color from going flat.

Look closely and the piece moves between two speeds. The left side is dense and fast, with sharply drawn detail. The right side is soft and pooled, with room for the eye to rest. The light seems to pass through the surface rather than sit on it, which gives the blues depth.

In a living room, bedroom, office or hallway, the horizontal format suits a sofa, bed or console wall. The cool palette sits easily with neutral, white or warm wood interiors, and the amber accents pick up brass, leather or timber.
