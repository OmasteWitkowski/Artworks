A Gust of Cobalt Blue Caught Mid-Sweep, With Sparks of Amber

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Cobalt Gust, Blue and Amber Streaks is a horizontal abstract where feather-like strands run into a calm, deep indigo field.

You know the wall. It's the one above the sofa, or the bed, or the console, and the room around it is finished. The furniture is where you want it and the paint is settled. That one stretch of wall still looks like it's waiting.

This is the piece I'd hold up to it. Streaks of cobalt, sky blue and teal sweep in diagonally from the upper left, threaded with moss green and small glints of amber and gold. On the right the whole thing goes quiet, into a smooth, deep indigo.

I can't give you a tidy label for this one at a glance, and I don't think you need one. What stops you is the movement. The strands curve and fan like feathers or wind-swept hair, and they look like a gust caught mid-motion.

Stay with it and the image moves between two speeds. The left and top are dense and fast, with sharply drawn lines. The right is soft and pooled, with room for your eye to rest. Thin vertical streaks pull downward at the lower right, and the lower center falls into a dark, speckled shadow above a greenish edge and a pale blue strip.

The surface looks glossy and glass-like, with fine scratch-like lines and small ridges. The light seems to pass through it rather than sit on top, which gives the blues their depth. The amber glints are small, but against all that cool blue they keep the colour from going flat.

What it means is yours to decide. I believe art is felt, not seen, and your own response is the only test that counts.

The horizontal format suits a sofa, bed, console or desk wall. The busy left side and quiet right side give it a natural weight, so it holds a wall without needing a crowd of other pieces around it.
