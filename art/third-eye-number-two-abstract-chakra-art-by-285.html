Exact geometry, with wings that never quite sit still

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · horizontal

Indigo Wings, Third Eye Symbol is a mirrored indigo emblem on white, where flat, crisp geometry meets soft marbled wings.

You know the wall. Maybe the sofa is new and the space above it suddenly looks bare. Maybe the bed is made, the paint is settled, and that one stretch of wall still has nothing steady to rest your eyes on.

This is the piece I would hold up to that wall. It is one deep indigo emblem in the middle of a white field, perfectly balanced left to right, with plenty of room around it. It is a single calm focal point, and it reads from across the room.

From a distance you see a circle, a triangle and two wings, all in one deep blue. The circle is large and crisp. Inside it a downward-pointing triangle holds a bold Om-style glyph, topped by a crescent and a dot.

Step closer and the wings change. The circle, triangle and glyph are flat and smooth. The wings are filled with a soft, swirled, marbled pattern in blues that shift slightly from violet-blue to dark navy. The shape stays still, but the pattern in the wings keeps moving.

That pairing of exact geometry and gentle marbling is what holds my attention here.

If you know the third-eye or brow chakra symbol, you will probably recognise the form. If you don't, it still works as a balanced shape in one deep colour. What it means is yours to decide.

The piece is horizontal, centered and symmetrical, so it suits a wall that needs one calm focal point: over a sofa, over a bed, above a console, or down a hallway. The white ground sits easily in light, neutral rooms, and the deep indigo adds weight.

It also suits a room set aside for yoga or quiet, since the form itself supports a spiritual or meditative reading. But it doesn't need that reading to earn its place. A strong blue-and-white graphic is reason enough.
