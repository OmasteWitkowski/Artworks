Veiled Diamond, Symmetrical Violet and Magenta Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A softly glowing pink-lilac diamond sits at the centre of a deep indigo field. Around it, smooth translucent ribbons in magenta, violet and royal blue fold outward like layered veils or petals. Thin outlines trace each fold, and pointed tendrils flick sideways while tapering forms rise and fall at the top and bottom. Small spiral curls sit near the left and right edges, inside a dark navy border.

The whole composition mirrors left to right and top to bottom, so every curve has a twin. That strict symmetry holds the rippling movement in a calm, balanced pulse. The colour is rich and saturated, with coral and pale aqua glints at the edges. The central glow gives the layered shapes the look of backlit glass.

Look longer and the side forms begin to suggest stylized eyes, faces or wings in profile. Nothing is literally depicted, so what you find in them is your own. The mood is dreamlike and mesmerizing, and the piece works equally well for anyone who simply loves deep purple and blue.

Its horizontal format and dark, saturated border suit a wide wall, such as above a sofa, a bed or a console. The luminous centre draws the eye, and the jewel tones sit well in moody rooms or as a bold contrast against pale walls.
