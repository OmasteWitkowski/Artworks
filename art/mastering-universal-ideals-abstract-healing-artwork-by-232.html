Veiled Diamond, Symmetrical Violet and Magenta Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal abstract in deep indigo, violet and magenta, built from smooth translucent ribbons that mirror each other left to right and top to bottom. A softly glowing pink-lilac diamond sits at the centre, with matching shapes on each side that can resemble stylized eyes or faces. Thin curling tendrils and tapering forms at the top and bottom extend toward a dark navy border. The overall effect is glossy, luminous and perfectly balanced.
