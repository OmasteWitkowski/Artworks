Mastering Universal Ideals Abstract Healing Artwork by Omaste Witkowski

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A wide, symmetrical abstract in saturated purples, magentas and blues on a dark navy ground. Smooth, translucent, ribbon-like shapes spread out from a glowing central diamond and repeat in mirror image. Eye-like and winged forms appear at the edges and the top and bottom.
