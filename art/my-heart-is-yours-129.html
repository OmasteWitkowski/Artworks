Hundreds of tiny spirals, one wall of glowing teal and orange

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ripple Carnival is a wide, edge-to-edge swirl abstract in teal, orange, magenta and deep blue, listed as digital art, with a surface that looks like rippled liquid.

You know the wall. It's the wide one over the sofa, or the bed, or the console. The room around it is finished, and the wall still looks like it's waiting for something.

This is a piece for that wall if you want colour, and plenty of it. Teal runs through the middle, orange and red glow in the corners, and magenta and violet flicker in between. It is a horizontal piece, so it already has the shape of that wall.

The colour comes first. Bright teal sits through the center and right, vivid orange and red glow in the upper corners and through the middle, and deep blue and violet gather along the bottom.

Then you step closer and the surface turns into hundreds of small spirals and curling tendrils. Fine pale highlights run along the edges of the curls, as if light were catching a rippled surface. It reads as marbled and liquid, with a faint metallic shimmer.

There is no single focal point, so your eye keeps travelling. Look longer and three faint upright, arch-like shapes appear, running top to bottom through the middle and sides. They slow the circular motion down and loosely divide the picture into sections.

I can't tell you what it means, and I'd rather not. The pleasure here is the colour and the pattern, and what you feel in front of it is up to you.

Ripple Carnival is wide and horizontal, with color all the way to the edges. It suits a broad wall above a sofa, bed, console or desk, anywhere you want one vivid statement. The warm corners and cool center hold attention from across the room, and the fine swirls reward a closer look.

It works best with simpler surroundings that let the palette stand out. The mood is energetic, playful and dreamlike.
