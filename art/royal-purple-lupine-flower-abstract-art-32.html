Spiral Lupine, Blue and Violet Flower Spike

Botanical & Flowers · vertical

A single tall lupine spike rises up the center of the frame, its rounded blossoms stacked in rows of cobalt blue and orchid violet and tapering to a tip of small yellow-green and violet buds. A few green leaves curl in at the lower left. Behind the flower, soft bands of green and sky blue are scattered with circular swirls, all inside a deep indigo-purple border.

What sets this piece apart is that every petal, leaf and background shape is curled into a small spiral. The flower looks like it is made of swirling ribbons of blue and purple, sitting between realism and abstraction. It is clearly a lupine, but it is drawn in curls and heightened color.

Look closer and the spirals repeat at every scale, from the tiniest bud to the circles in the background, giving the whole image a gentle rotating motion. The upward taper of the spike lifts the eye toward the top. The light is soft and diffused, with pale yellow-green and pink highlights on the petal edges, so the blossoms seem to glow from within. The surface has a fine canvas-like grain with smooth, brush-like transitions.

Its vertical shape suits an entryway, a narrow wall, or the space above a console. The saturated blue and violet pair well with white, soft gray or green rooms and bring a cheerful, spring-garden feel.
