A Flower Bed Bent Into a Bowl Under a Mountain Sky

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

"Bowl of Summer" is a wide-angle photograph in which a yellow and purple garden bed curves toward you, with forested ridges and big white clouds behind it.

You bought the new sofa, and now the wall above it looks bare. Or the art you hung years ago looks small in a room that has grown around it. You have been looking at that wall for months, and nothing you've found has felt right.

This is the piece I'd hold up to that wall. A round bed of yellow flowers sits in the middle of the picture, with purple spikes behind it. Behind them are dark evergreens, slate-blue mountain ridges, and a bright sky full of white clouds. The whole view curves gently, like the inside of a bowl.

The first thing your eye does is land on the yellow. The flat-topped blooms gather in the center of the bed and pull you in. From there the purple spikes lead you up and to the right, the evergreens on either side hold the frame, and the ridges send you on toward the sky.

The curve is what sets this picture apart from most flower photographs. The wide-angle view bows the horizon and the edge of the bed, so the garden seems to wrap around you instead of sitting flat in front of you. A straight garden view or a close-up of petals doesn't do that.

Then there is the contrast. The flowers are dense and still, and the clouds seem to push up and outward. The sun lights the cloud tops white and leaves gray-blue shadows beneath them. The grass and blossoms are evenly lit, and the finish is slightly softened, with a faint painterly feel.

I'm not going to tell you what to feel in front of it. I believe art is felt, not seen, and your own response is the only test that counts.

This is a horizontal piece, so it suits the wide spaces: over a sofa, over a bed, along a console, or at the end of an entry. The blue sky and green grass open up a wall, and the bright clouds bring a lot of daylight into the picture.

The palette of sky blue, grass green, violet and yellow suits a living room, sunroom, entry or bedroom. It is a sunny, open, cheerful picture, and that is reason enough to want it.
