Bowl of Summer, Flower Garden and Mountain Sky

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A rounded garden bed fills the foreground. Yellow flat-topped flowers gather in the center, tall purple-blue spikes rise behind and to the right, and small lavender-blue blossoms trim the front edge. Green grass sweeps in along the bottom corners, and tall evergreens stand at both sides. Beyond them, forested mountain ridges sit under a bright blue sky of large white clouds.

What sets this view apart is its curve. The wide-angle perspective bows the horizon and the edge of the bed into a gentle bowl shape, so the garden seems to wrap around the viewer. The yellow center draws the eye first. The violet spikes, the dark evergreens and the slate-blue ridges then lead it back toward the sky.

Look longer and the contrast becomes clear. The flowers are still and densely layered, while the billowing clouds seem to push upward and outward. Strong sunlight lights the cloud tops white and leaves gray-blue shadows beneath, and the grass and blossoms glow evenly. The finish is slightly softened, with a faint painterly quality.

The horizontal format and bright blue-and-green palette suit a living room, sunroom, entry or bedroom, where it brings in daylight and a sense of open summer air.
