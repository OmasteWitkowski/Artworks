Cobalt Mirror, Still Lake Landscape

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A still lake mirrors a deep cobalt sky streaked with thin white clouds. Across the water sit a line of dark evergreens, a few small buildings and soft blue mountain ridges low on the horizon. In the foreground, a bank of dry straw-colored brush and fresh green patches curves toward the shore. A single bare tree stands at the right edge.

The sky color is the first thing you notice, and the lake repeats it almost exactly. Against it, the warm, wiry tangle of dry grass adds small, scattered movement. A fine painterly texture over the whole image gives the bright, saturated scene a brushed, canvas-like surface.

Look slowly and the picture settles into horizontal layers: sky, treeline, reflective water, grassy bank. The clouds sweep diagonally toward the center while the water stays level and quiet. The light is open and even, with soft shadows, so the mood is spacious and still rather than dramatic.

The wide format and strong blue suit a living room, bedroom, office or hallway where a calm, open landscape is wanted. The warm grasses tie it easily to wood, neutral and earth-toned rooms.
