Cobalt Mirror, Still Lake Landscape

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A still lake reflects a vivid blue sky streaked with thin white clouds. Across the water, a line of evergreen trees, a few small buildings, and soft blue mountain ridges sit low on the horizon. In the foreground, a grassy bank of dry straw-colored brush and fresh green patches leads toward the shoreline, with a single bare tree at the right. A fine, painterly texture covers the whole scene.

The story of this pieceA Cobalt Sky, Doubled in Still WaterA saturated cobalt sky lands on a still lake and repeats itself almost exactly, with warm wiry grasses in front.
