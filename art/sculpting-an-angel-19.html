Angel in the Marble - Mirrored Amber Wings

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

Two mirrored, wing-like forms rise along a dark central axis, glowing in burnt orange, amber gold and copper. Violet-blue and deep brown shadows trace their edges, and dark hourglass-shaped gaps open between them. The whole image is covered in tiny rippled highlights that shimmer like light on water or hammered metal. It sits inside a wide, softly mottled chocolate-brown border.

The strict symmetry and the glow set this piece apart. The forms lean gently inward and draw the eye upward, while the fine, flickering texture keeps the surface alive up close. The palette is warm and jewel-toned without becoming loud.

Beneath the image, a line of white text reads: 'I saw the angel in the marble and carved until I set him free.' Michelangelo. The words suit the wing-like shapes and give the piece a quiet, reverent tone. You can read it as an uplifting reminder or simply enjoy the color and light.

In a living room, bedroom, entry or study, the vertical format and dark border make it a calm focal point. The warm oranges and browns pair well with wood, leather, cream walls and brass accents.
