Amber Currents - Glowing Abstract in Cobalt Blue

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A close-up abstract filled with glowing streaks of amber, gold and red that rise vertically through the center of the frame. Deep blue fields frame the left and upper right, and flashes of white, green and teal run along the edges. Small round bubbles are scattered through the warm center, and one larger oval bubble sits at the lower left. The surface looks glossy and layered, like light passing through colored glass.

The story of this pieceWhy Is This Amber Center Full of Tiny Suspended Bubbles?A warm amber column studded with tiny bubbles rises between fields of cobalt blue, and it looks lit from behind.
