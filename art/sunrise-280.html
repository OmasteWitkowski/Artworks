Amber Currents - Glowing Abstract in Cobalt Blue

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Streaks and flowing bands of amber, gold and crimson rise vertically through the center of this close-up abstract. Deep cobalt blue fills the far left and upper right, and flashes of white, green and teal run along the edges. Tiny round bubbles hang in the warm center, and one larger oval bubble sits at lower left.

The glow is what sets it apart. Light seems to pass through the material from behind, so the oranges and yellows burn while the edges flare with prismatic blue and green. Dark maroon and brown passages give the brightness strong contrast, and fine parallel striations, like combed lines, run through the middle.

Look longer and the movement appears. The streaks sweep upward and slightly outward like flames or rising currents, while a curved ridge across the lower middle pushes against them. A dark peak-like shape anchors the base, and the scattered bubbles give the eye places to rest. Warm heat against cool blue is the whole story here, and it needs no further explanation.

The horizontal, edge-to-edge format makes a vivid focal point over a sofa, bed or console, or in an entry. The warm center suits neutral walls, and the cobalt edges pair well with deep blue or charcoal rooms.
