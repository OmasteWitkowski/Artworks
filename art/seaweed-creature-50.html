Green Lens, Golden Arc

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A round, dark-rimmed opening sits left of center, its interior a mix of blue-violet and gold. Bands of vivid green, golden yellow and teal curve around it in concentric, wave-like arcs. A golden-brown band sweeps over the top toward the upper right. The surface looks glossy and translucent, with fine streaks, ripples and tiny bubble-like specks.

The circle reads like an eye or lens, and everything else in the frame bends toward it. Bright highlights along its edge and deeper blue-violet and brown pockets around it make the color feel lit from within. There is no horizon and no background, only the close, absorbing swirl of color.

Look longer and the details start to separate: a line of small sparkling particles along the left side, cyan glows against amber depths, and the way the golden arc pulls the eye across the circle and out to the right. The mood is energetic and vivid, and the piece works simply as a striking image.

Its horizontal, full-bleed format and saturated greens and yellows suit a wall that needs a confident color statement, such as above a sofa, console or desk.
