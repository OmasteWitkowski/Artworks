Green Lens, Golden Arc

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A close-up abstract image of swirling green, yellow, teal and blue bands that look like glossy, translucent glass. A round dark-rimmed opening sits left of center, with blue-violet and gold tones inside. A curved golden-brown band sweeps over it toward the upper right. Tiny bubble-like specks and fine streaks add texture across the surface.

The story of this pieceOne Green Lens Pulls Every Color Toward ItA round, eye-like opening anchors a field of glossy green, golden yellow and teal, and a single golden arc sweeps across it.
