Ember Drop, Glowing Orange-Red Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A large, smooth orange-red form hangs from the top of the frame and narrows to a soft point near the bottom center. A thin, slightly curved crease runs down its right side. Around it, fields of dusty mauve-pink, pale olive-cream and bright green blur gently into one another.

The form looks translucent, as if lit from within or from behind, with darker red-brown shadow near the top and along its right edge. Burnt orange, deep red and rust meet emerald green, a vivid contrast kept calm by silky gradients and softly blurred edges. It could read as a petal, a drop of molten glass or something else entirely, and the image never settles the question.

The eye travels down the glowing form to its tip, while the green sweeps diagonally upward on the right and the rounded pale shapes on the left add a slow counter-curve. The mood is warm, quiet and softly dramatic, a sensual play of color and light with no message to decode.

Its horizontal format and single dominant form suit a wall that needs one warm focal point, such as above a sofa, bed or console. The soft edges keep it restful in a room, and the orange-red and green give it presence.
