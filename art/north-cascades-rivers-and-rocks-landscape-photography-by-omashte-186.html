Upstream Quiet, Green River Through Evergreen Valley

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A clear green river winds through a narrow valley lined with tall evergreen trees and leafy shrubs. In the foreground, the bank is covered with rounded stones in gray, blue-gray, tan and rust. A steep, bare slope and a leaning tree trunk frame the middle distance, and a faint mountain rises into a pale sky behind. The scene is viewed from low to the ground, which makes the stones feel close and the river seem to stretch far away.
