Love and Light Sharing Space Abstract Shapes and Symbols Artwork

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A heart made of swirling blue, yellow, green and orange marbled color is divided by black symmetrical curving shapes, with a teardrop shape near the bottom. It sits in a black panel with a thin blue edge, surrounded by a border of swirled red, orange, yellow, green and blue.

A bold, mirror-symmetrical heart in glowing blue marbled color, cut by black ornamental shapes and framed by a rainbow swirl border, gives this piece striking contrast and graphic impact.
