A Glowing Amber Arch Under Falling Cobalt Ribbons

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Amber Arch and Cobalt Ribbons is a horizontal abstract of marbled blue, amber and gold, with a lit-from-behind glow at its center.

The sofa is in. The paint is settled. And the wall above it still looks like no one has decided anything. Maybe the old piece looks small now, or you have simply stopped seeing the empty space and started feeling it.

This is the image I would put there. A deep blue field, a warm amber arch in the middle, and long ribbons of cobalt hanging down around it. You can find the glow from across the room.

What holds me here is the glow. The amber looks lit from behind, the way light comes through colored glass. The blue around it is deep enough to make it burn warmer.

Gold, green and cream streaks fan out to the left and right of the arch. Below, everything softens into a blurred golden-olive ground, so the bright center has somewhere quiet to rest.

Three flat circles in teal, dark green and bright blue sit on top of the swirls. They are crisp where everything else is soft, and they lead your eye around the center and back again.

Look closer and the surface is full of detail: fine marbling, feathery wisps, small curls and spirals inside the larger shapes. Some people see a cavern, some a landscape, some a burst of light. None of those is the subject, so see what you see.

It is a horizontal piece, so it suits a wide wall: over a sofa, a bed, a console or a dining buffet. The amber center sits naturally in the middle of a piece of furniture and anchors the space around it.

The blue and amber hold the room when the furniture is plain, and they sit well with warm wood and neutral paint. The mood is vivid and dramatic, with warmth glowing against cool color.
