Cobalt Eclipse, Swirling Blue and Orange Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A large cobalt-blue disc fills the left of the frame, ringed by curling ribbons of orange and amber. Round, bubble-like orbs with spiral centers are scattered through it. To the right the colors cool into teal, aqua and emerald green, and the shapes loosen into softer, broken marbled patterns.

The pairing of saturated blue and burnt orange gives this piece its impact. Translucent overlaps make it look lit from within, like stained glass, and the bright color stands out against deep brown-black shadow. A dark crescent arcs over the disc and carries the eye from the heavy left side to the calmer right, so the energy has a clear direction.

Look closely and the spirals radiate outward from the blue orbs, while fine speckle along the top edge and in parts of the teal field adds a bubbly grain to the smooth, marbled surface. It is vivid and dreamlike, an immersive field of color that is enjoyable simply to look at.

On a wall, the weight of the saturated left side gives a room a strong focal point, and the cooler teal and green on the right keep it balanced. The horizontal format suits a space above a sofa, bed or console where one confident color statement is wanted.
