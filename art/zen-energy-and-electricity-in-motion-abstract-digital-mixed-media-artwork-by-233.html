Ember Window - White Line Geometric Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

A dense web of thin white lines divides this square into hundreds of triangular and fan-shaped cells. Inside each cell, soft marbled color swirls in orange, golden yellow and lime green, with teal, cobalt and navy gathering toward the edges and corners. Near the center, a warm orange and red star-like shape glows.

The crisp white linework against the blended color is what sets this piece apart. A faint warm outline runs along many of the lines, so the whole surface looks lit from behind, like stained glass or a kaleidoscope.

Look closer and small nested triangles spiral inward in several places, while wedges fan out from many points. The pinwheel-like center pulls the eye in, and the cooler blue-green areas around it let the eye rest before it travels back across the pattern.

Its bold warm center and square format make it a strong focal piece on a plain wall. It suits a modern living space, studio or office that wants energy and color from a precise geometric pattern.
