The Blue Well - Abstract in Crimson and Amber

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A deep blue pool sits at the centre of this image, speckled with tiny bubble-like dots. Around it, glossy curves of cream, amber and aqua fold inward like petals, and saturated red and magenta fill the corners. Bright highlights glint along the edges, and the surfaces look smooth and liquid.

What holds me is the light. The whole image seems to glow from behind or within, and the colours slide into one another with soft transitions rather than hard lines. The blue is dark at its middle and brightens toward the edges. It gives your eye somewhere to land, and the colour around it keeps pulling you back.

Stand close and you find fine speckles, faint streaks and thin veils of colour inside the blue. Step back and the curves sweep toward the centre like something slowly opening. I would rather you stand in front of it than read about it. What it becomes for you is yours to decide.

It is a horizontal image, so it suits a wide wall, such as over a sofa, a bed or a long sideboard. The colour is strong enough to hold a room whose furniture and paint are already settled.
