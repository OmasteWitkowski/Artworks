Ember Whirl: Red and Teal Liquid Spirals

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal abstract filled edge to edge with small glossy spirals in deep red, dark teal and black. Thin bright outlines make each swirl look like liquid or molten glass. Long, pale, translucent ribbons of light curve up through the pattern, and a small orange-yellow glow sits near the center-left. Cool turquoise gathers at the left and right edges while warm reds dominate the middle.

The story of this pieceHundreds of Tiny Spirals, One Orange Glow, and Ribbons of LightA dense field of glowing crimson and teal spirals, crossed by ribbons of light and warmed by a single orange spark.
