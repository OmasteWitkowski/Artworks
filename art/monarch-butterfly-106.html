Ember Totem, Mirrored Swirl Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Two mirrored masses of burnt orange, rust red and amber folds curl inward toward a narrow green channel running down the center. Small bulb-like shapes stack along that green spine. Bright yellow-orange highlights edge each fold, and dark maroon recesses sit behind them. Fine contour lines trace the swirls, and olive and yellow-green tones fill the lower corners under a warm orange border.

The left and right halves match exactly, which is what sets this piece apart. The symmetry turns flowing, marbled forms into something that reads as a glowing mask, a face or a standing figure, with the green center lit like a backlit core. The surface looks smooth, glossy and translucent, like satin or molten glass.

Nothing here is literally depicted. The face or totem is an impression made by the mirrored shapes, and you can look at it as pure color, line and motion. The ribbons pull the eye inward, and the green column carries it up and down, so the piece feels hypnotic and warm.

Its horizontal format and central axis suit a wall that needs a strong focal point, such as above a sofa, a bed or a console. The orange and red bring warmth, and the green gives a cool counterpoint that works with olive, brown and neutral rooms.
