Ember Lantern: Glowing Amber Oval in Burgundy and Blue

Abstract Glass Macro · square

A square close-up of glossy, rounded shapes that overlap like smooth petals or pebbles. Deep burgundy and rust-orange fill much of the lower half, while a large amber-gold oval with a bright edge sits right of center. Patches of blue, olive green and pale yellow glow around it. Tiny bubbles are scattered across the shiny surface, and the light seems to pass through the material.

The story of this pieceThe Amber Oval That Glows Like Light Through GlassOne glowing amber oval, edged in bright light and set in burgundy and blue, is the whole reason to look twice.
