Ember Lantern: Glowing Amber Oval in Burgundy and Blue

Abstract Glass Macro · square

A square close-up of smooth, glossy shapes that overlap like petals or pebbles. A large amber-gold oval with a bright, light-struck edge sits right of center. Deep burgundy and rust-orange fill the lower half, with patches of cobalt blue, olive green and pale yellow glowing around them.

The light seems to pass through the surface from behind, so the amber, blue and green areas glow against the dark red-brown. Thin raised ridges mark the borders between shapes, and tiny round bubbles are scattered across the glossy finish. That combination gives the image a stained-glass luminosity that is rare in warm abstract art.

The forms read as petals, lobes or eggs only by resemblance. There is no horizon and no fixed subject, so the image rests on color, curve and glow. The rounded shapes press against one another along slow diagonals, as if caught mid-flow. It rewards long looking, and the pleasure is in the color and form themselves.

The dark burgundy base and the single glowing focal point suit a sofa wall, a console, a dining room or a quiet study. The square format also works well as one of a pair.
