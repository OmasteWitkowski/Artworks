Jewel Totem, Mirrored Stained Glass Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A tall, perfectly mirrored abstract built from rounded pebble and oval cells outlined in thick black lines, much like a stained-glass window. Deep maroon and navy cells stack down the center and sides around a glowing column of green and orange, with a turquoise burst at the middle. Yellow and lime striped bands run along both edges, and purple and magenta shapes close off the corners.

The stained-glass look is what sets this piece apart. The black lines are flat, bold and smooth, and they interlock the surface like leading. Inside them, every cell is filled with soft, swirling, marbled color that looks like liquid or smoke. The color reads as backlit, so the yellow, orange and green areas glow against the darker cells.

Look longer and the symmetry turns the pattern into a totem- or mask-like figure, with the turquoise center as its focal point. The horizontal yellow bands at the sides give a ribbed, rhythmic pulse outward from the axis. The marbled swirls add a gentle fluid motion to an otherwise ordered, static structure.

Its vertical format and dark maroon and navy base suit a hallway, a stairwell or a narrow wall where a bold, colorful focal piece is wanted. The bright center keeps it lively in a room of quieter colors.
