A Purple Caterpillar Crawling Down a Glowing Green Leaf

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

Quiet Passenger is a photograph with a painterly finish: one small violet caterpillar with white dots on a leaf that looks lit from within, against deep teal-black.

You know the wall. It might be above the console in the hallway, beside the reading chair, or behind the desk. The room is finished and the paint is settled, but that wall is still bare, and you haven't found anything you want to put there.

This is the picture I would ask you to look at for that wall. It is a leaf, lime green and glowing, hanging in the dark. Halfway down it, a small purple caterpillar with a row of white dots is crawling toward you.

I can't give you a tale about where this was taken or how long I waited for it, and I won't make one up. What I can tell you is what happens when you look at it, because that is the part that stays true.

First you see the glow. The green and golden-yellow leaves look lit from within, and the background is so dark, a blackish teal with a touch of cobalt blue at the left edge, that the greens seem to float. Then you see the caterpillar. It is the only purple in the picture, so your eye lands on it before you decide to look.

Then the eye follows it. The thin reddish-brown twig arcs across the top, the leaves hang and angle downward, and the caterpillar leads you diagonally along the central leaf. The leaves at the left are soft and out of focus, and the wide dark space below gives the whole cluster room.

The surface is brushed and oil-paint-like, with a faint canvas-like grain over a photographic close-up. That finish turns a tiny creature into something closer to a portrait subject. You can enjoy it as an arrangement of green, gold and violet, or you can look closer at the dotted body and the pale veins in the leaf.

This is a horizontal piece with a dark ground and saturated colour. It suits a wall where you want a rich, quiet nature image rather than a pale or busy one: over a console, behind a desk, beside a reading chair, or along a hallway.

The wide dark area in the lower part of the picture gives it weight, and the glowing greens act as a calm point of colour. That works well in a study or reading corner, where you want something to rest your eye on.
