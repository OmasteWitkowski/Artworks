A Cobalt Window Opens Down the Middle of These Clouds

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

Cobalt Window in the Clouds is a tall, painterly sky study: one ragged channel of deep blue cutting through bright white cumulus.

You know the wall. It might be the tall one by the stairs, the stretch above the bed, or the narrow space in the entry. The room is finished, and that one wall still looks like nobody decided anything about it.

Now picture looking straight up and finding the clouds have parted. Bright white banks press in from both sides, and between them runs a ragged channel of deep cobalt blue, straight down the middle of a tall frame.

The first thing you see is the blue. It is the most saturated part of the picture, and its edge is jagged, not smooth. Depending on the moment, it reads as a window in the clouds or a river running through them.

Then you notice the lines. Thin, wavy strokes curl along the cloud edges and through the blue, like brushed or liquid paint. The surface is smooth and painterly, with a faint canvas-like grain. It is a sky, but not the ordinary blue-sky-and-clouds picture.

Stay a little longer and the clouds seem to press in from both sides while the blue channel pulls your eye up and down the frame. The sunlit cloud tops almost glow against the dark blue. Gray-blue shadows gather at the lower corners and give the heaviest banks their weight.

I made this as digital art, a painterly sky and cloud study. What it gives you is the lift of looking straight up on a bright, clear day, with no more explanation than that.

The vertical format suits a single wall where the sky can stand alone: over a bed, in an entry, along a stairwell, or in a living room with a tall stretch of wall. The tall blue channel does the work of drawing the eye up and down.

The palette is strictly cobalt and white, with soft gray-blue shadows. It brings light and air into a room without competing with the colors already there. The mood is open, fresh and calm, and that comes from what is in the image, not from any promise about how you will feel.
