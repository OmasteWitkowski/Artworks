Cobalt Window in the Clouds

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

Bright white cumulus clouds pile up on both sides of a ragged channel of deep cobalt blue. The channel runs down the middle of a tall frame, and a second patch of blue shows at the top right. Gray-blue shadows gather at the lower corners and give the cloud banks weight.

What sets this sky apart is the blue opening itself. It is the most saturated part of the image, and it reads like a window or a river cutting through the clouds. Thin, wavy lines curl along the cloud edges and through the blue like brushed or liquid strokes. The surface is smooth and painterly, with a faint canvas-like grain.

Look longer and the clouds seem to press in from both sides while the blue channel pulls the eye up and down the frame. Sunlit cloud tops glow against the dark blue, and the heaviest, brightest masses sit at the bottom. The overall feeling is open, fresh and calm, the lift of looking straight up on a clear, bright day.

The vertical format and strict blue-and-white palette suit a single wall where the sky can stand alone, such as a living room, bedroom, entry or stairwell. It brings in light and air without competing with other colors in the room.
