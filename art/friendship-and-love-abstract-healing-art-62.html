Ember Current, Orange and Red Swirls Over Blue

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ribbons of glowing orange, yellow and red twist across a deep blue ground filled with small swirls. On the left the orange forms curl into scrolls and loops. On the right the red forms rise into tall, branching, vine-like shapes. The scene sits inside a dark oval, set in a wide near-black mat with a brushed, canvas-like border of orange, red and blue.

The warm ribbons look backlit, like embers or molten glass, and the saturated blue behind them gives the strongest contrast in the piece. The smooth, glossy interior contrasts with the textured border, which carries the same colors out to the edges. A thin vertical red shape near the center divides the spirals from the tendrils.

Look closely and the motion works in layers. The spirals on the left and the rising tendrils on the right pull the eye across the oval, while the small whorls in the blue add a steady current underneath. Some viewers may see vines, antlers or embers in the red and orange forms. These are impressions only, and the image is purely abstract.

The dark mat and strong color make this a natural focal point for a wide wall, such as above a sofa, bed or console. It suits rooms that want warmth, energy and contrast, and the dark surround lets the colors hold their own against light or dark walls.
