Orange scrolls and red tendrils burning on deep blue

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ember Current is a horizontal abstract in which glowing orange spirals and climbing red tendrils swirl over a cobalt field, held inside a dark oval.

You bring in the new sofa, and the wall above it suddenly looks bare. The old picture looks small now. You have been looking at that wall for months.

Picture a wide piece up there that you can read from across the room. Orange and red burn against deep blue inside a dark oval, and the black mat around it makes the colour look lit from behind.

I won't tell you this image is something it isn't. It is an abstract. There is no scene in it and nothing to name, and it is listed as digital art.

What I can tell you is what you find when you stay with it. On the left, yellow-orange ribbons curl into scrolls and loops that look backlit, like embers or molten glass. On the right, red forms twist upward into tall, branching shapes. A thin red line near the centre sits between the two sides.

Under all of it, small whorls in the blue keep a steady current moving. Some people see vines in the red, or antlers, or fire. Those are impressions, and they are yours to keep or ignore.

Then the frame does something unexpected. The inside of the oval is smooth and glossy, while the border around it looks brushed, like canvas, and repeats the orange, red and blue out to the edges.

The shape is horizontal, so it suits a wide wall: above a sofa, a bed, a console or a fireplace. The near-black mat and strong contrast read from across the room, and they also hold up when you walk close.

It suits rooms that want warmth, energy and some drama. The mood is lively and intense, and the colours are bold enough to be the focal point of the room.
