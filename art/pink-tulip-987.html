Pink Tulip

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A single pink tulip with white-edged petals stands in bright sunlight, with its petals partly open to show a deeper rose interior. Behind it, blurred pale stones and green foliage form a soft background. A fine canvas-like texture covers the whole image.
