Bayside Beauty

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

A rocky shoreline of dark, rough stones curves around a quiet blue cove under a deep blue sky with a few soft clouds. Windswept trees stand on the headland, and bright green grass fills the foreground. The whole scene has a painterly, textured finish.

A vivid coastal scene in which dark volcanic rock, calm blue water and glowing green grass sit under a deepening blue sky, rendered with a painterly canvas texture.
