Electric Thicket, Glowing Blue Stalks

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A dense thicket of tall, blade-like stalks fills the frame in cobalt blue, royal blue and emerald green. Pale, silvery stems sweep diagonally through the left and center, and a bright arching stem crosses the middle. A darker, shadowy mass sits across the top.

What sets this piece apart is the glow. The color seems to come from within the stalks rather than from one clear light source, and the surface has a brushed, feathery, slightly metallic sheen with fine looping lines. Small sparks of orange-red, rust and violet flicker through the cool blues, adding a vibrating energy.

There is no single focal point, so the eye travels along the long diagonal and curving strokes, from lower left toward upper right, and then drops back into the tangle to find new crossings. The image sits between a recognizable natural subject and pure abstraction. It can be enjoyed simply for its vivid color and movement.

The horizontal, edge-to-edge composition suits a wall that needs energy and depth, such as above a sofa, a bed or a console. The deep blues give it presence in a room, and the warm accents pick up well against neutral or earthy surroundings.
