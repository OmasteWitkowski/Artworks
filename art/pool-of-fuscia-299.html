The Amber Loop at the Heart of a Purple and Gold Wall

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Gilded Ripple is a glossy, close-up abstract: a golden pool with an amber curl at its center, wrapped in vivid magenta-purple.

The sofa is in. The paint is settled. And one wall, the one you look at every evening, is still bare. Everything you've found for it is soft, safe and a little forgettable.

Now picture a horizontal piece of deep magenta-purple with a golden oval glowing at its center. Inside the gold, a small amber loop catches your eye and holds it. That is Gilded Ripple.

I'll start with what is plainly there. A golden-yellow pool sits slightly left of center, held inside a rim of vivid magenta-purple. Purple and gold sit close to opposite each other in colour, which is why the image stops you from across a room.

At the middle of the gold is a looping amber curl, like a droplet or a ripple caught mid-form. It is a second, smaller focal point inside the first, and your eye keeps going back to it.

Then the piece rewards a longer look. Thin, finely drawn ripple lines cross the yellow pool. Tiny bubbles and beads gather along the edges. Soft round highlights glow across the surface, and fine white specks scatter across the top. It looks wet and in motion, held in a single instant.

What you take it to be is yours to decide. I'd rather you stand in front of it and see.

Gilded Ripple is a horizontal piece with a strong center, so it suits the wall over a sofa, a bed or a sideboard. In a room of quiet neutrals, it gives the eye one place to land and one strong colour to remember.

It is not a quiet, tone-on-tone abstract. It is vibrant and luminous, and it works best where you want the wall to carry the room's colour.
