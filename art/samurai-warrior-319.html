Amber Mask, Symmetrical Swirl Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Orange, amber, yellow and deep red bands swirl across a horizontal canvas, mirrored exactly from left to right. At the centre the curves come together as a mask-like face with two eye-like shapes. Lime-green spirals sit on either side, and wavy blue-grey bands with pink and orange stripes run across the top corners.

What holds me is the symmetry. It turns pure colour into a figure, almost a totem, and the glowing green spirals frame it. Bright white highlight lines run through the dark reds and give the surface a backlit, glass-like sheen. Fine thin lines are layered over the broad bands, so the surface looks glossy and liquid.

Look at it from across the room and you get the warmth and the face. Come closer and the loops, ripples and thin lines keep opening up. Every curve leans in toward the centre line, so your eye keeps coming back to those two eye-like shapes. What the figure means is yours to decide.

The horizontal shape suits a wall that needs one strong focal point, like the space over a sofa or a bed. The warm palette stands out against calm, neutral walls.
