Swirling Summit, Mountain Meadow in Spirals

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A snow-dusted rocky peak rises over a reddish-brown wooded slope, a row of evergreens and a sunlit green meadow, all under a deep cobalt sky. Dark bands of shadow cross the grass, and a dark navy border frames the scene.

Every part of this realistic mountain view is built from spirals. They are tight curls in the rocks and trees, soft pale rings in the sky and large concentric circles in the shadowed foreground. The effect looks like thick, rhythmic impasto, so the scene reads as landscape and pattern at once.

Look closely and the diagonal slopes carry your eye down toward the center, while the shadow bands move it side to side. The border repeats the blurred swirl texture and ties the frame to the picture. The mood is fresh, bright and playful, with the calm of a clear day in the mountains.

Its vertical shape and saturated blue and green suit a hallway, stairwell, or the wall above a desk or console. It brings color and texture to a room without needing other art around it.
