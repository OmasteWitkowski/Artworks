Ember Totem: Mirrored Abstract in Cobalt and Amber

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · horizontal

A bright abstract with a glowing orange and green ornamental shape at the center, mirrored top to bottom and left to right. Deep blue fields fill the four corners, and teal and yellow-green swirls curl between them and along the edges. The surfaces look smooth and liquid, like marbled paint, and a thin dark border and yellow band frame the whole image. The strict symmetry gives it the look of a kaleidoscope or a decorative mandala.
