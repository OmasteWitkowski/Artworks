Ember Totem: Mirrored Abstract in Cobalt and Amber

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · horizontal

A glowing orange-and-amber diamond sits at the center, ringed by green coils and small circular spirals. Deep cobalt blue fills the four corners, and teal, aqua and yellow-green scrolls curl between them and along the edges. The surfaces are smooth and glossy, like marbled or melted paint, and a thin dark border and a yellow band frame the image.

The whole design is mirrored left to right and top to bottom, so the central form reads like a symmetrical, totem-like figure made of liquid swirls. The glow comes from color contrast alone. There is no cast shadow, just saturated orange and gold set against dark blue. A vertical column of orange shapes runs through the middle, and teal arrows point inward from both sides.

The curling lines spiral and fold inward and outward from the center, and the symmetry holds that motion in balance. The eye keeps traveling around the central form and finds new small spirals each time. The mood is lively and ornamental, with an underlying sense of order.

On a wall, the cobalt corners and bright center work as a strong color anchor in a horizontal format. It suits a living room, studio, entry or office that wants a bold, pattern-rich focal piece.
