Ember Wings, Symmetrical Orange Butterfly in Lace Filigree

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A symmetrical butterfly spreads its wings across the center of a horizontal frame, seen from above. Each wing is made of glossy, flowing bands of burnt orange, vivid red-orange and olive green, edged in bright yellow with touches of deep indigo and rust brown. A small brown body marked with a green diamond sits on the central axis.

The wings glow from within, like light passing through colored glass, and the bands carry fine wavy grain lines that look like marbled or silky liquid color. Behind the butterfly, a pale, even background is covered in lace-like red and olive scrolls and linked circles. The bold color and the delicate openwork set each other off.

Look closely and the curves sweep inward toward the body while the background scrolls swirl gently outward. The perfect left-right mirroring gives the piece a composed, ornamental feel. It is lively and celebratory, and it is meant to be enjoyed for its color and pattern.

The warm palette and strong center axis suit a living room, entryway, dining area or bedroom. It works well above a sofa, console or sideboard, where the horizontal format has room to spread.
