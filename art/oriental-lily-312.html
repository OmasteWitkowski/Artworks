The Rust-Red Detail That Makes This Yellow Lily Stand Out

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

Butter Light is a close-up photograph of one pale yellow lily, with six rust-red anthers hanging at its center.

You have a wide wall over the sofa, the bed or the console, and nothing you've tried on it has felt right. The room is finished. The wall is the one part that still looks unanswered.

Picture one pale yellow lily filling that wall. It is lit warmly from the side, with six rust-red anthers hanging at the center. The rest of the flower is soft and bright, and the anthers are what you notice first.

I'm not going to give you a made-up backstory for this flower. What I can tell you is what is in the frame, because it is what held me.

Six wavy-edged petals open around a cluster of long green filaments. Six rust-red anthers tipped with cream hang from them, and they are the sharpest thing in the picture. Fine rust speckles are scattered across the butter-yellow petals, and the yellow deepens to golden amber toward the throat. The flower seems lit from within.

The light comes from low and to the side, so there are soft shadows between the petals. Only the anthers and the nearest petals are in focus. The edges and the dim green background melt away, so your eye moves in from the soft outside toward the center.
