Molten Sun Mask, Mirrored Yellow Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

Golden yellow, amber orange and cream swirls curl and ripple across a tall canvas, framed by cobalt blue, red and emerald green. The shapes are pointed and leaf- or flame-like, with crisp curled edges and a smooth, glossy surface.

The composition is mirrored left to right and nearly top to bottom around a central seam and midline. At the center, dark purple almond shapes on either side of a pale green-gold area read as an abstract face or mask. That impression is ambiguous, and the eye can resolve it differently each time.

Look closer and the color glows as if lit from within, with yellows and whites bright at the center and edges. Small dark purple and maroon areas supply depth, and fine mottled grain breaks up the smooth surfaces. The balanced symmetry makes the movement feel like it is pulsing, in an ornamental, kaleidoscopic and slightly psychedelic way.

The tall vertical format suits an entryway, a stairwell, or a wall beside a door. In a neutral or dark room, its warm, lively color becomes the focal point.
