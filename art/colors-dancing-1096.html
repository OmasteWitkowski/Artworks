Colors Dancing

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Glossy, translucent shapes in blue, red, green, amber and purple drift across a soft cream background. A large amber form with a bright red centre sits at lower right, and small droplets and bubbles catch highlights around the frame. The colours overlap like light through coloured glass.

Jewel-bright blobs of translucent colour drift across a creamy ground, with a glowing amber and red glass form anchoring the lower right like light through stained glass.
