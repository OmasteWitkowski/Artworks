Ember Rosette: Golden Line Flower in a Glowing Oval

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A large rosette-shaped flower, drawn as a thin golden outline, fills a glowing oval. Its layered, pointed petals spiral outward from a tight central bud. Behind the line, glossy ribbons of burnt orange, amber, rust and chocolate brown swirl like molten color. A dark brown rectangular frame surrounds the oval, and more orange and yellow swirls flow outside it to the edges of the horizontal image.

The piece stands out for its light. A soft golden halo rims the oval, and the brightest yellow-orange sits at the lower right, fading to deeper brown toward the upper left and outer edges. The crisp, flat gold line against the smooth, marbled color gives the image a jewel-like, stained-glass quality, like a medallion or cameo.

Look closely and the eye follows the petals in a spiral to the small central bud, set slightly above the middle of the oval. The flower could be a rose or a succulent rosette. The curling background swirls echo the spiral of the petals, so the flower and the flame-like color seem to move together. It is a warm, radiant image that works simply as a rich, decorative piece of color.

The symmetrical, framed layout and the deep brown, amber and burnt orange palette suit rooms with warm woods, leather, brass or earthy textiles. Over a sofa, console, bed or desk it gives a glowing focal point.
