Ember Bloom, Orange Flower on Black

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A single orange-yellow, daisy-like flower sits at the center of a black panel, seen straight on. About nine smooth petals fan out from a dark violet-purple, grainy, domed center. Their tips curl slightly and fall at uneven lengths. Fine veins run the length of each petal, and soft highlights catch their ridges and edges.

What makes it distinctive is the black field and the border. With no visible light falloff in the background, the flower seems to glow. Around the panel runs a wide border made from an enlarged, softened, amber-orange version of the same flower. Its petals fill the corners and a few dark stems show at the sides, so the image looks like a picture set in its own warm mat.

Look longer and the warm petals begin to move like a pinwheel or sunburst, relaxed rather than rigid. The sharp, satin-smooth bloom against its blurred twin makes a quiet play between near and magnified, crisp and soft. It is mainly a piece to enjoy for its color and contrast, bold and warm with a dramatic edge.

On a wall, the saturated orange and deep black give a room one clear focal point. It suits a dark or neutral setting, such as a living room, hallway or study, where a single strong accent color is wanted.
