Ember Medallion, Symmetrical Orange Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A symmetrical abstract in blazing oranges and yellows with a dark brown, mask-like shape at the center. Red petal-like forms sit at the top and bottom edges. Smooth, translucent swirls curl outward in mirrored patterns that suggest flames or flowing smoke. The overall effect is warm, glowing and ornate.
