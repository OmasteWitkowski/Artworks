Ember Medallion, Symmetrical Orange Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A dark brown and olive medallion sits at the center of this horizontal abstract, ringed by smooth, translucent swirls of saturated orange and golden yellow. Red petal-like forms anchor the top and bottom edges. Fine wispy lines and small speckled red details appear near the center.

The pattern is mirrored left to right and top to bottom, so the whole image balances around a single point. The medallion can read as a mask or a face, though it is only an impression. The bright edges look luminous while the center stays darker and more muted, and that pulls the eye inward.

Look longer and the curves repeat outward and curl into spirals at the corners and sides. They give the piece a flame-like, rhythmic motion, as if the layered ribbons were smoke or molten glass. The formal symmetry keeps all that heat ordered and ornamental.

The warm color and strong central form suit a focal wall in a living room, den, bedroom or office, where a bold, glowing piece is wanted.
