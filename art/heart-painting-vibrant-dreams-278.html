A Heart You Know. An Inside You Don't.

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · horizontal

Ember Heart is a large red heart filled with a mirrored world of glossy swirls, glowing green and gold, and a dark red spine down the middle.

You know the wall. It's over the bed, the sofa, or the console in the entry. The room is finished and the wall is still bare, and nothing you've tried feels like it belongs there.

Picture one large heart on that wall, centered on soft cream. At first glance it's a shape you know. Then you step closer and the inside isn't what you expected.

The outline is simple, but the inside is a mirror image. Glossy red-orange and deep crimson swirls curl up and fold outward from a dark red band that runs straight down the center. The two halves meet there, and the curls seem to open and close like wings or petals.

On each side, olive and yellow-green shapes glow with gold outlines, with a few small touches of cobalt blue. Near the bottom point there's a hint of violet. The greens look backlit, as if light were coming through from behind.

Look closer at the red and you'll see a fine speckled grain. The surface is smooth and polished, and the swirls look like ink moving in liquid. A thin dark outline and soft highlights along the edge make the heart look like glass or enamel.

A heart means something a little different to everyone who looks at it, and I'd rather leave that to you. Some people will see warmth and affection. Others will simply enjoy the color and the way the two halves meet.

This is a single focal piece for a plain wall: over a bed, a sofa, a console, or in an entry. The strict symmetry gives it a formal, composed presence. The cream background keeps the wall quiet around it.

The red is strong enough to be the one bold note in a quiet room. The olive and emerald greens help it sit comfortably with natural and neutral colors.
