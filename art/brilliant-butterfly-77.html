Mirror Wings, Two Orange Butterflies in a Lilac Border

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

Two large butterflies face each other across a ribbed, reddish-brown body that runs on a diagonal. Their orange wings are striped in black and edged in yellow with dark dots, with patches of cobalt blue swirling near the body. They sit on a muted grey-brown field, inside a lilac border dotted with small yellow and orange flower-like marks.

What sets this piece apart is that every area is made of curling spirals. The wings, body and background all look like thick, glossy, wet paint or glazed relief. The bold orange and yellow stand out against the quiet grey-brown, and the border makes the scene feel like a decorative tile or plaque.

Look closely and the spirals never stop. They ripple through the background and curl inside the wings, giving the image a gentle fluttering rhythm. The mirrored wings, cropped at the edges, fill the frame and make the pair feel lively and close.

Warm, playful and decorative, this horizontal piece suits a sunroom, kitchen, bedroom nook or any wall that wants a bright orange-and-lilac accent.
