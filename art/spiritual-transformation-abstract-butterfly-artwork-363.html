Ember Wings, Fiery Kaleidoscope Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A large butterfly silhouette with spread wings and long antennae sits at the center of a dark maroon background. Its wings are filled with swirling, mirror-symmetrical patterns in glowing orange, gold and red, with olive green and purple near the body. The lines look like flames or molten glass and flow outward from the center. A thin darker border frames the whole image.
