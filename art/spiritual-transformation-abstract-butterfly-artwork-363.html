Ember Wings, Fiery Kaleidoscope Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A large butterfly with outspread wings and two long antennae sits at the center of a deep maroon-brown field. Its wings are filled with flowing, mirrored swirls in fiery orange, golden amber and brick red. Olive green and violet gather near the slim body. A thin darker border frames the whole image.

The butterfly acts as a window onto the pattern. Ribbon-like curls and fine glowing threads look like flame or molten glass, and they seem lit from within against the dark ground. The strong contrast makes the silhouette read clearly from across a room.

Look closer and the lines ripple outward from the body toward the wing edges, like flickering heat. The mirror symmetry keeps that movement balanced and calm. The cooler green and violet at the center give the warm oranges somewhere to rest. The surface is smooth and polished, with no visible brush texture.

Its horizontal, centered design suits a wall that needs a warm, dramatic focal point, such as above a sofa, bed or console. The dark maroon surround pairs well with deep wall colors, wood tones and warm lighting.
