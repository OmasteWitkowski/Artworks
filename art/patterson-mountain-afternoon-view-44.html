Herd Below the Golden Hill, Painterly Horse Landscape

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A herd of horses grazes in a fenced paddock beside a small pond. They are mostly brown, bay and black, with a few white or grey. Behind them a large hillside of dry golden grass rises, dotted with dark evergreens, under a blue sky with thin clouds and hazy mountains. Sagebrush, a wire fence and bright green grass fill the foreground.

What sets this piece apart is the way the hillside glows against the cool sky. Warm, low-angled light rakes across the slope and picks out the edges of the trees. A swirling, brush-like texture runs through the whole scene, most visibly in the sky and hill, so it feels like thick paint rather than a plain view.

The horses sit in a loose row across the middle of the picture, small against the big hill. The diagonal of the slope leads the eye up from left to right, and the fence post and shrubs frame the foreground. The mood is quiet and pastoral, with the open feeling of the end of a day.

The wide horizontal format and warm ochre palette suit a long wall above a sofa, bed or mantel. The blue sky and sage tones keep the gold from feeling heavy, so it pairs easily with rustic, neutral or earthy rooms.
