The Hillside That Glows Over a Quiet Herd of Horses

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

Herd Below the Golden Hill is a painterly horse landscape: a row of grazing horses, a glowing ochre slope and a cool blue sky, all in a swirling, thick-paint texture.

You know the wall. It's the long one above the sofa, or the bed, or the mantel. The furniture is settled and the paint is chosen, but the wall still looks unfinished every time you walk past it.

Now picture a big golden hillside there, with a small herd of horses grazing at its foot. That is what I want you to look at on this page.

I'll tell you what I see, because that's the honest place to start. At the foot of a large hill of dry, golden grass, a herd of horses grazes in a fenced paddock beside a small pond. Most are brown, bay and black, with a few white or grey ones among them.

Warm, low-angled light rakes across the slope. It turns the grass gold and picks out the edges of the dark evergreens. Above it the sky stays a cool blue, with thin clouds and hazy mountains in the distance.

Then there is the surface. A swirling, brush-like texture runs through the whole scene, most visibly in the sky and the hill. It looks like thick oil paint, so a quiet pasture reads as something warmer and a little storybook. The piece is listed as digital art.

The horses are small against the big hill, and they are in no hurry. The slope leads your eye up from left to right. The mood is calm and open-country, with the feeling of the end of a day.

This is a wide horizontal landscape, so it suits a long wall above a sofa, bed, mantel or console. The golden hill does the work of a warm focal field, and the blue sky and sage tones keep it from overpowering the room.

The palette is warm and earthy, so it pairs easily with rustic, neutral or earthy rooms. The mood is quiet and pastoral, which makes it a good fit for a living room, a bedroom, or a ranch-style home.
