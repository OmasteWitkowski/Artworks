Silver Thread - Black and White Water Splash

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A thin column of bright white water drops into a dark pool, ending in a burst of foam at about the midpoint of the frame. A pale reflection continues down through the lower half. Fine, wiry streaks of spray fan out around the stream, and short horizontal ripples and white flecks texture the pool.

What sets it apart is the way the spray looks drawn rather than photographed. The strands are thin and hair-like, and they radiate from the central stream like brushed lines. The surface is smooth, painterly and slightly oily, so a real splash becomes a semi-abstract graphic image in charcoal, near-black and glowing white.

Look at the contrast between the two halves. Above, the spray shoots upward and outward in a loose fan. Below, the pool is calmer, with a trailing reflection that echoes the column. The light gathers along the central axis and the edges fall into shadow, so the eye is drawn straight down the line of water. A thin black border frames the image.

The tall vertical format and strong central line suit narrow walls, hallways, stairwells or the space above a console. It works as a single bold statement in a room with a dark or neutral palette, or as the anchor of a black-and-white grouping.
