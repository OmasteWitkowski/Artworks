Midnight Star Tunnel, Symmetrical Blue Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Nested ellipses and rectangles in near-black navy frame a small four-pointed star at the center, with a tiny cluster of star forms inside it. Behind the geometry, electric cobalt swirls ripple like liquid or flame and mirror each other left and right.

What sets this piece apart is its tunnel-like layering. Dark, flat navy bands separate each ring sharply, so the bright blue areas look lit from within. The result is a glossy, kaleidoscopic abstract where crisp geometry meets fluid pattern, in strict symmetry.

Look longer and the two movements start to work against each other. The swirls flow outward and upward, while the concentric rings steadily draw the eye back to the center. The cool blues and the central star can suggest night sky, water or energy, but the image leaves that reading to the viewer.

In a horizontal format, the deep navy and strong contrast give it presence above a sofa, bed, console or desk. It suits rooms that want a bold, calm focal point in blue.
