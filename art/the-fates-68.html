Ember Mask: Mirrored Red Floral Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A glowing red-orange flower form sits at the center of this horizontal, perfectly mirrored composition. Paired round, eye-like circles are stacked above it, green leafy shapes arch across the top and sprout along the bottom, and golden-yellow florets and dark brown shadows fill the sides. Rippling lines swirl through every layer and curl into spirals at the corners.

What sets it apart is the double reading. The mirrored center is a flower and, at the same moment, a symmetrical mask or creature with round eyes. The reds look lit from within, and the dark edges push that warm center forward. The surface feels like layered, rippling glass or liquid, with fine petal detail and small spiky yellow florets adding a grainy, organic texture.

Look longer and the eye travels outward along the echoing contour lines, then back to the bright center. The image seems to pulse. It can read as playful or as mysterious, and it is simply intense and vivid.

The saturated warm palette suits a wall that needs a strong focal point, such as above a sofa, bed or console, or in a room that welcomes bold color and pattern.
