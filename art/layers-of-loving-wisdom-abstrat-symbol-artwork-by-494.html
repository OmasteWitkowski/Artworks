Ember Heart, Mirrored Swirl Heart in Amber and Olive

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A large heart fills the center of a horizontal canvas, built from mirrored swirls and curls. Deep crimson sits in the two upper lobes, a chartreuse band crosses the middle, and a glowing orange form anchors the center, ending in peach at the lower point. A soft, pale edge outlines the heart.

What sets this heart apart is its light and its pattern. It seems to glow from within against a muted olive-brown field, and that field carries fainter echoes of the same paisley and flame-like curls. The color bands flow side to side while the whole design holds a strict left-right symmetry.

Look closer and the swirls curl outward from the central vertical axis, pulling the eye toward the orange center. The smooth, marbled-looking surface keeps the ornament soft rather than sharp. The heart gives it a feeling of warmth and affection, and the pattern keeps it decorative rather than sentimental.

The horizontal format and strong central focal point suit a wall above a sofa, bed or console. The olive, amber and orange palette sits easily with warm neutrals, wood tones and earthy interiors.
