Ember Heart, Mirrored Swirl Heart in Amber and Olive

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A large heart fills the center of a horizontal canvas, built from mirrored swirls and curls in red, orange, yellow-green and peach. Deep red sits in the two upper lobes, a chartreuse band crosses the middle, and a glowing orange form sits at the center. The heart's edge is a soft pale glow. Behind it, a muted olive-brown background carries fainter echoes of the same swirling pattern.
