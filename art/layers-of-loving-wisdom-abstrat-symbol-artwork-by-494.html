Layers of Loving Wisdom Abstrat Symbol Artwork by Omaste Witkowski

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A large heart shape sits in the center of a horizontal frame, filled with symmetrical, swirling patterns in red, orange, gold and lime green. The background is a muted olive and brown version of the same curling forms. A soft pale glow outlines the heart.

A mirror-symmetric heart of glowing red, orange and lime swirls stands out against a quiet olive-brown field of the same pattern, giving a warm, jewel-like focal point.
