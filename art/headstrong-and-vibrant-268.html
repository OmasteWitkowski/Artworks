Where Amber Meets Violet, Edged in Thin Blue Lines

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ember Tide is a wide, glossy digital abstract in which warm amber pours into cool violet around a dark swirl.

You bring in the new sofa and the room looks right, until you look up. The wall above it is still bare, or still holds the small piece that suited the old room. You have thought about it for months, and nothing you scroll past has looked like it belonged to you.

This is Ember Tide. Amber, gold and burnt orange ribbons pour in from the left. On the right, deep violet curves around a large dark swirl. The surface looks wet, like melted paint under a bright light. There is no object in it to name, only flow.

I will tell you what I see, because that is the honest way into this piece. Your eye starts on the left, where the warm ribbons are. They drift toward the dark mass right of centre, and the purple on the right bends around it like a tide coming in.

Then come the thin electric-blue lines tracing some of the edges, bright against the dark brown shadows. Small touches of dusty rose and copper sit in the folds. Look closer and fine ripples and little whorls show up inside the big curls.

What I keep coming back to is where the warm and the cool meet. The left feels like late sun and the right like dusk coming in. The dark centre holds the two together.

You may see a tide, a vortex, a pour of paint, or something else. It is felt first, and the meaning is yours.

Ember Tide is a horizontal piece, so it suits a wide wall: over a sofa, a bed or a long console. The warm half will pick up wood, leather or brass. The violet side is a cooler counterpoint.

It also works in a neutral room that wants one strong colour. The look is rich and dramatic, with warm set against cool.

Measure the wall, or the width of the sofa or bed below it, and mark the size on the wall with paper or painter's tape before you choose. The listing lets you pick from the available sizes. I can't state a maximum size here.

Screens vary, so I can't promise an exact match. The main colours are burnt orange, amber gold, deep violet and a dark brown-black, with small touches of electric blue. If the print isn't right in your room, the 30-day money-back guarantee applies.

I believe there is no good or bad art, and your own response is the only test that counts. If this image stopped you, that is a good enough reason to want it on your wall.

Picture the wall again, this time with amber on one side, violet on the other and the dark swirl sitting just above the middle of your sofa. Imagine the blue lines catching the light when you walk in.

If that is the wall you have been waiting to fill, go to the listing, choose your size and surface, and see it at the scale of your room.
