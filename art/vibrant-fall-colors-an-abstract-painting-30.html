A Lit Window of Green and Gold in a Dark Marbled Frame

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

Lantern Window is a square digital abstract whose swirling center glows like light through colored glass.

You know the wall. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and that one stretch of wall still looks unfinished. You have been looking past it for months.

Picture a dark, softly marbled brown square hanging there. In the middle, a window of color seems lit from behind. That is Lantern Window.

I'll tell you what I see, because that is the honest story of this piece. The center looks backlit. Yellows, teals and creams glow the way sunlight does through colored glass. A soft orange-brown halo rings the panel, which makes the light feel warmer.

Around it, the border is wide, dark and mottled brown. Look closely and you'll find faint, ghostly swirls in it that echo the movement inside. The center is quick and tangled, with yellow and teal lines running through it. The border moves slowly. The two paces together are why the piece keeps your attention.

There is no recognizable subject here, only ribbons of forest and olive green, teal, golden orange and lemon yellow curling and folding. A pale cream and silver band sweeps down the right side. A deep crimson round form sits at the lower left. Bright mint and yellow gather at the lower right. What it means is yours to decide.

The dark border and warm glow suit rooms with wood, leather, deep greens or earthy tones. The color is densest in the middle, so the piece works as a single statement rather than part of a gallery grouping.

As a square, it suits a sofa wall, the space above a console, an entry, a desk, or a reading corner that needs a focal point.
