Lantern Window, Glowing Abstract Swirl in Green and Gold

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

A square window of glossy, swirling color glows at the center of this abstract. Ribbons of forest and olive green, teal, golden orange and lemon yellow curl and fold together, with a pale cream and silver band sweeping down the right side. A deep crimson round form sits at the lower left, and bright mint and yellow gather at the lower right.

What sets it apart is the light. The center looks backlit, like sun through colored glass, and a soft orange-brown halo rings the panel. Around it, a wide, dark, mottled brown border carries faint ghostly swirls that echo the movement inside, so the whole piece reads as a lit window set into a dark frame.

Look longer and the pace changes. The center is dense, quick and tangled with yellow and teal lines. The border moves slowly, in quieter marbled passages. The contrast between glassy smoothness and fine painterly grain rewards a closer look.

The dark surround and warm glow suit rooms with wood, leather, deep greens or earthy tones. As a square, it works well over a sofa, console or desk, or as a focal point in a quiet corner.
