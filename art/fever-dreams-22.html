Ember Tide, Swirling Orange and Blue Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Glossy ribbons of orange, amber and deep red curl across the top of the frame, folding into electric blue currents below. Dark navy and near-black shapes sit between the folds and along the edges, so the bright colors seem to glow from within. Touches of magenta, lavender and teal appear in the blue passages.

What sets this piece apart is the strong warm-versus-cool contrast. A large red-orange loop sits in the upper center, and the cool blue rises through the lower half. Orange and amber bands cross into the blue on the right. The surface looks smooth and glassy, with fine feathery ripples, tiny speckles and hairline streaks in the red area and the blue below.

The eye travels along the curving bands, from the bright edges into the dark recesses and back out again. The folds pull in different directions, which gives the image a sense of liquid in motion. The mood is energetic, fiery and dramatic.

In a room, the horizontal format suits a wide wall above a sofa, bed, console or fireplace. The dark edges ground the image, and the orange and blue give a space a strong, confident focal point.
