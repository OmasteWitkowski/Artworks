Innocence Reborn As Abstract Healing Art

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal abstract of swirling orange, amber and red ribbons with soft cream highlights and faint circular ripples. The swirl sits inside a black mat that is surrounded by a textured, mottled orange border. The colors suggest flame or molten glass, but no specific subject is shown.

A glowing, flame-colored abstract swirl in orange, gold and deep red, held inside a built-in black mat and textured orange border that makes the center look lit from within.
