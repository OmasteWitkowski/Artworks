Ember Ripple, Red and Orange Flowers

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

Two large red flowers with dark centers and fiery orange edges sit on a diagonal, one at the lower left and one at the upper right. Their petals stretch into flowing, wave-like ribbons. Translucent green and white swirls overlap them like rippling glass, and yellow-orange blooms and stems glow faintly behind.

What sets this piece apart is that the petals look liquid. Fine streaks run through the glossy red, and the edges glow orange and yellow against deep maroon shadows. The flower centers and stems stay recognizable, so the image is vivid and kaleidoscopic without losing its subject.

The composition fills the frame edge to edge with overlapping layers and no single resting point, so the eye keeps moving. The green waves in the opposite corners echo the two blooms and balance the heat of the red with something cooler and watery.

The colors are bold and the mood is playful and energetic. It suits a living room, an entry or an office wall that needs a warm, lively focal point.
