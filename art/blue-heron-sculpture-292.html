One cool blue bird in a garden that never stops moving

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

Teal Sentinel is a digital artwork of a tall teal bird standing in a swirling green and pink garden, and it is made for a wide wall that needs colour.

You know the wall. It's over the sofa, or the bed, or the long console, and the paint and furniture are settled. Everything else in the room is finished, and that one stretch of wall still looks bare. You have probably been looking at it for months.

Now picture a tall, slender bird in luminous aqua-teal standing on a garden path. Its neck curves in an S and its beak points up and to the left. Behind it the garden is deep green, with soft pink blossoms to the right. That is Teal Sentinel.

The bird is the one smooth, cool shape in a garden that is all motion. Pale blue-white highlights run along its neck and chest. Everything around it is deep forest and olive green, pooling into shadow behind it.

Look closer and the garden changes. Every leaf, branch and feather is made of flowing, wavy lines, like thick ripples of paint. The whole scene seems to hum gently. The curve of the neck gives your eye somewhere to rest before it goes wandering through the leaves and the pink flowers again.

Small touches of rust, red and golden orange sit in the path and the foliage. They keep all that green and blue warm.

The bird holds a quiet, upright poise in the middle of all that abundance. What that means is yours to decide. You might feel calm, or you might feel colour, or you might just like the blue. Any of those is a good reason to want it.

This is a wide horizontal piece with a strong vertical figure in it. That suits a wall over a sofa, a bed, a console or a sideboard, or a long stairwell wall. The foliage runs edge to edge, so the image fills a wide space instead of floating in the middle of it.

The deep greens give a big wall some weight, and the aqua and pink add a lift. It works well against walls and furniture in quieter tones, in a living room, dining room or entry.
