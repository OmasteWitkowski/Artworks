Indigo Veil Yin Yang

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A tall cream-white oval holds a yin-yang symbol on a plain cream ground. One half is clean white with a small deep indigo dot. The other half is filled with swirling, mirrored patterns in indigo, red-violet, sand and gold, and holds a small white dot. The ring of the oval picks up dusty mauve, muted teal and amber.

The patterned half has a glossy, translucent sheen, like folded silk or liquid glass. Fine ripples and lace-like folds repeat outward from the center in kaleidoscope-like waves. The white half is flat and empty, so the two sides contrast sharply: dense jewel color against quiet space. The dark indigo-to-red top softens into gold and cream toward the bottom, and the oval edge keeps the whole pattern sharply contained.

The yin-yang symbol stands for balance and duality, and here the idea is carried by the picture itself. Each half holds a dot of the opposite tone, the S-curve gives a gentle rotating motion, and the near-mirrored edges keep everything even. It can be read as a calm, contemplative image, or enjoyed purely for its color and surface.

The vertical oval and light cream ground suit a bedroom, entryway, study or meditation corner, where a single tall piece can serve as a calm focal point. The deep indigo and warm gold sit well with neutral walls, natural wood and soft textiles.
