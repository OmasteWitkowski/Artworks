Magenta Lantern, Glowing Oval Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

A large magenta-pink oval sits at the center of a square, full-bleed image. A purple oval presses in from the upper left, tall red forms rise along the right side, and a green field fades into yellow at lower left. Thin bright and dark outlines separate the smooth, rounded shapes.

The color seems to glow from within, as if light were passing through it. The magenta oval has a fine bright rim and soft gradients inside, like glass or a glossy droplet. Tiny pale and golden specks dust the surface, and a few feathery veins show in the orange-red area at lower center.

The shapes bulge and flow with an upward and sideways pull. The eye starts at the magenta oval, moves to the red forms on the right, then drifts across to the green-yellow mass at the left. Nothing here is a literal subject. The pleasure is in the saturated color, the smooth curves and the luminous depth.

The square format and strong color let it hold a wall alone, as a lively focal point above a sofa, console or bed. It also suits a neutral room that needs one rich, saturated accent.
