A magenta oval that glows like light through glass

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

Magenta Lantern is a square abstract of smooth, glowing shapes in red, purple and emerald green, made for one wall that needs to be looked at.

You know the wall. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and that one stretch of wall still looks bare. You've probably walked past it for months without finding anything that fit.

Picture a large magenta oval in the middle of that wall, glowing as if light were passing through it. Around it, red, purple and green press in close. That is Magenta Lantern.

I'll tell you what is in the image, because nothing here is a literal subject. It is shapes and color. A big magenta-pink oval sits at the center with a fine bright rim, like the edge of a glass bead or a glossy droplet. A purple oval presses in from the upper left. Tall red forms rise along the right side. At lower left, a green field fades into yellow.

The color seems to glow from within. Each shape has soft gradients inside it, and thin bright and dark outlines keep the curves apart. My eye starts at the magenta oval, moves to the red forms on the right, then drifts across to the green and yellow on the left.

Look closer and the surface changes. Tiny pale and golden specks are scattered across it. In the orange-red area at lower center there are a few fine, feathery veins. The curves are calm and the color is bold.

I'm not going to tell you what it means. What it says to you is for you to decide.

This piece works best as the single focal point on a wall: over a sofa, a console or a bed. The square format and strong color let it hold the wall alone.

It also suits a neutral room that wants one rich, saturated accent. The color is bold, so it does best with calm space around it. Leave the wall plain and let the piece do the talking.
