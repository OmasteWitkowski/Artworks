Indigo Eye, Iridescent Glass Orb

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A large glass orb fills the frame, its curved surface glowing in deep indigo and cobalt blue with copper, amber, violet and silver. Tiny bubbles and circular highlights are scattered across the glass. Near the center sits a dark ring, like a lens or an eye, and blurred curved glass sweeps up from the lower corners. Behind it is a soft taupe-grey glow.

What sets this orb apart is the contrast between the deep, dark center and the shimmering oil-slick sheen at its rim, which shifts from blue to purple to gold. The surface is dense with crisp detail, while the soft foreground and background glass keep the sphere floating and slick.

The eye is drawn first to the dark ring, then travels around the bright edge and into the clusters of tiny bubbles. Depending on the mood of the viewer, the orb can read as a watching eye, a camera lens or a distant planet. It is a quiet, mysterious image that rewards a slow look.

The horizontal format and dark, jewel-toned palette suit a wall that needs a focal point, such as above a sofa, in a study or in an entry. The deep blues sit well against warm neutrals and grey walls.
