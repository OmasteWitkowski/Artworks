Indigo Eye, Iridescent Glass Orb

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A large glass sphere fills the frame, its surface shimmering with blue, purple, copper and silver iridescence. Tiny bubbles and glowing circular highlights speckle the curved surface, and a dark ring sits near the center like a lens or an eye. Curved, blurred glass shapes crowd the lower corners and a soft taupe-grey background surrounds the orb. The overall look is glossy, dense with detail and a little otherworldly.

The story of this pieceThe Dark Ring at the Center of This Glass Orb Looks BackA dark, jewel-toned glass orb with a ring at its center that reads as an eye, a lens or a distant planet.
