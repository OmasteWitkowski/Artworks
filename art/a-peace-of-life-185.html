A Peace Symbol With Smoke Swirling Inside It

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · horizontal

Peace in Smoke is a large black peace symbol on deep red, filled with mirrored gray and white swirls, offered as a print on Fine Art America.

You may have a wall that has bothered you for months. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and the space above it still looks unfinished. Plain, safe art hasn't helped. You want something that reads from across the room.

This is a black peace symbol inside a black-outlined oval, on deep red. You know what it is at a glance. Then you step closer, and the three sections turn out to be full of swirling gray, black and white that looks like smoke or marbled ink.

From the doorway, the picture is bold and simple. Heavy black lines, a saturated red field, and a symbol everyone knows. It has a poster feel, and it doesn't need explaining.

Then the interior opens up. The swirls curl and billow outward from the center in mirrored pairs. The vertical bar splits the image into left and right halves, and each curl on one side is answered on the other. The lower wedge holds a similar pattern. It has the pull of a kaleidoscope.

Look at the textures. The black bars are solid and smooth and they stay perfectly still. Inside them, the smoke moves in soft tonal gradients, with a few fine dark filaments running through. The red behind it is streaky and mottled, like vertical brushed fibers, and it darkens slightly toward the edges of the oval.

Most peace-sign art is flat and simple. I wanted this one to have both: a hard, static symbol, and a soft, intricate fill that rewards a longer look. The peace comes from the symbol itself. What you feel in front of it is yours to decide.

This is a horizontal statement piece. The oval fills most of the frame and leaves a red border on all sides, so it sits on a wall like a confident focal point. The red gives presence next to white, black or neutral surroundings.

It suits a den, studio, music or media room, an entry, or a living room wall where you want one strong piece rather than a gallery of small ones.
