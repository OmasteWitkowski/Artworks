Peace in Smoke - Black Peace Symbol on Red

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · horizontal

A large black peace symbol sits inside a black-outlined oval on a deep red field. The red is streaky and mottled, like vertical brushed fibers, and it darkens slightly toward the edges of the oval. Each of the symbol's three segments is filled with gray, black and white swirls that curl outward from the center.

The swirls mirror each other across the vertical bar, and a similar pattern fills the lower wedge. They look like smoke or marbled ink, with soft tonal gradients and a few fine dark filaments. The heavy, solid black lines stay still while the interior billows around them. This contrast between a stark symbol and a delicate, intricate fill is what sets the piece apart.

Peace is carried by the symbol itself. The strong red, black and white palette gives it a bold, graphic, retro-poster feel. Up close, the symmetry rewards a slower look, with each swirl echoed on the opposite side like a kaleidoscope.

The horizontal format and red border make it a confident focal point on a wall. It suits a living room, den, studio or music space, and it pairs well with black, white or neutral surroundings.
