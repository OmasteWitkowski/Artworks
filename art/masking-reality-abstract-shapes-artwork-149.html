Ember Crest, Winged Mask on Black

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A mirrored, mask-like figure fills a solid black panel. Horns or wings curl up and out from a central vertical axis. A face-like core glows orange and red, with a red mouth-like shape, and cross-shaped blue forms extend below it and out to the sides. Magenta pink, turquoise, yellow and lavender swirl through the fill.

What sets it apart is the contrast. The marbled color looks backlit, like stained glass, and the black ground is flat and smooth. Because the figure is perfectly symmetrical, it reads as part mask, part emblem and part winged crest.

The curls spiral inward and pull the eye toward the glowing middle, while the outward-sweeping tips give the form lift and flare. The look is bold and ceremonial, and the mirroring keeps it balanced. Whether you see a face, a mask or a pure pattern is left to the viewer.

A wide, brushy, multicolored border and a thin red inner line frame the panel. The horizontal format suits a sofa, console, bed or desk wall, and the strong contrast stands out best in a neutral or dark room.
