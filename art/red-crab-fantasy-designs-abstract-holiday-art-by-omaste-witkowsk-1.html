Ember Claw - Crimson Crab Silhouette

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · square

A single red crab is seen from above against a plain white ground. Both claws sweep up and outward, and the antennae and spiky shell edges are visible. Four walking legs fan out on each side, and two folded front legs point down at the bottom. Thin white lines divide the shell and claws into plates and joints, and a small white crescent sits at the center of the lower shell.

What sets this crab apart is its fill. Instead of a flat red, the body is a mottled, kaleidoscopic pattern of swirls, teardrops, diamonds and small mirrored motifs in deep crimson, burnt orange, amber and dark maroon. Glowing spots inside the red give the flat silhouette a backlit, jewel-like depth, like stained glass. The edges are crisp and the white linework is clean.

Look closely and the piece plays two ideas against each other. The outline is a clear, tribal-style crab, and the pattern inside is a separate abstract design. The near-perfect symmetry keeps it steady, while the raised claws and spread legs make the crab look poised and active. There is no cast light or shadow, so the effect stays flat, graphic and bold.

The square format and generous white space around the crab let it stand alone on a wall. Its red and white palette and its coastal subject suit a kitchen, dining room, beach house or seafood-themed space.
