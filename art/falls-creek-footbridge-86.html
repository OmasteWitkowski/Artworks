Red Hat at the Far End of the Footbridge

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · vertical

A weathered wooden footbridge made of two planks crosses a rocky mountain stream and leads toward a small figure in a red top and hat, who is raising a camera or binoculars. White water rushes around boulders on either side. Tall evergreen and bare-trunked trees fill the background, lit by soft daylight with a little blue sky at the top right. The image has a smooth, slightly painterly finish.
