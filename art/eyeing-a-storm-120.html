Jewel Eye Vortex, Cobalt Blue Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A deep cobalt oval sits at the center, marked with rippled, layered contour lines like folds or shell ridges. A bright green ring circles it, and wavy golden-yellow bands sweep around the outside. Olive and mustard browns, pale aqua highlights and touches of amber appear at the edges.

The nested rings of yellow, green and blue form a target that fills the frame to every edge, with no horizon and no empty space. The surfaces look glassy and wet, with bright highlights along the ridges and near-black navy in the creases. Small pinpoint glints catch the light at the right. The overall effect is a jewel-like focal point.

The concentric rings pull the eye inward toward the blue center, while the outer golden bands flow around it like ripples on water. The form can be read as an iris or a vortex, and either reading feels intense, immersive and a little otherworldly. A rougher, flaky brown patch at the upper left gives the smooth surfaces something to push against.

The horizontal format and saturated blue, green and gold make this a confident statement piece. It suits a living room, studio, office or entry, in a space that can carry strong color and a clear central focus.
