Staring Into The Abyss Abstract Art by Omashte

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal abstract design of four mirrored cream scroll shapes outlined in black, each holding a swirling orange form. Between them is an olive-green cross-shaped center, and the edges are framed in deep red, brown and dark green. The warm palette and symmetry give it the look of a stained-glass or kaleidoscope pattern.

A warm, stained-glass-like abstract in which glowing marbled orange forms sit inside bold black-outlined cream scrolls, mirrored four ways around an olive-green center.
