Gilded Totem - Mirrored Gold and Violet Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A mask-like face sits at the center of this horizontal abstract. It has a rounded, shield-shaped outline, dark violet curves that read as eyes and a mouth, and a pointed yellow shape above. Rippled bands in golden yellow, deep violet and indigo flow outward to both sides, with notes of burnt orange, magenta and olive between them.

The composition is mirrored almost exactly left to right. The central figure is built entirely from liquid purple contour lines on glowing yellow, so it reads as a creature or totem inside a field of patterns. A second mirrored shape, like a vase or torso, sits below it. Fine dark lines, thin and often doubled, trace every fold, and small white-blue glints give the surface a glassy sheen.

Look longer and the translucent layers begin to overlap and the ripples echo across both halves. The face can be read as a mask, a guardian or a totem, depending on the viewer. It is a rich, hypnotic, energetic piece, enjoyed for its color, flow and detail.

Its wide format suits a long wall above a sofa, bed or console. The gold and violet contrast adds a strong color anchor to a room, and it works best in a space that can carry bold pattern.
