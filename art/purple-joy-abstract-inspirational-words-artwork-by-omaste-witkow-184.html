Look closer: every letter of JOY holds its own swirl

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Joy in Jewel Tones is digital word art: tall, black-outlined letters and two hearts, each filled with its own marbled color on clean white.

You know the wall. It might be the hallway, the spot over the console, or the stretch above a desk. The furniture is settled and the paint is done, but that one space still looks like nobody decided anything.

Now picture the word JOY there, in tall, narrow letters outlined in thick black, so the whole word reads from across the room. Every letter is filled with glowing color: golden yellow, royal purple, cobalt blue and orange, all set on clean white.

From across the room, you read a word. Step closer and the letters stop being plain shapes. Each one holds its own swirl: fluid, marbled color, with paisley-like curls that turn back on themselves.

In the J and the O, the patterns mirror each other, almost like a kaleidoscope. Inside a single shape the color runs from warm to cool, gold into teal, purple into magenta. The thick black outlines keep every shape crisp, which gives the lettering a stained-glass, cartoon-like punch.

Beside the J sits a large heart with a smaller purple heart nested inside it. The letters lean slightly and stretch from the top edge to the bottom, so the whole word has an upward lift. White space in the O and between the J and the heart gives the color room to breathe.

I made this as a digital illustration. The word says what it says, and the color gives you plenty to look at. What it means in your room is yours to decide.

The horizontal format suits a spot over a desk, a shelf or a console. It also works in an entryway, kitchen, studio, hallway or a child's or teen's room. The heavy outlines and saturated fills carry across a room, so it works as a bold statement on a modest to medium wall.
