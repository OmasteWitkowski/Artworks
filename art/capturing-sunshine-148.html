Ember Iris, Golden Bloom Aglow in Darkness

Botanical & Flowers · vertical

A single yellow-orange iris sits at the center of a near-black background. Its upper petals are ruffled, and a frilled lower petal hangs beneath them. A thin green stem is faintly visible above and below the bloom. The edges are soft and feathered, and the whole flower floats in a large field of dark space.

The light is what sets this piece apart. Bright cream-yellow gathers on the frilled petal at lower left and deepens to amber toward the right and below. Against the dark ground the petals look lit from within. Their curling, rippling edges give the bloom a gentle, flame-like motion.

Look closer and you find quieter colors: muted olive green, faint dusty rose and hints of dark violet in the shadows. A fine grain across the surface gives the image a soft, painterly, almost dreamlike quality. The eye travels from the bright left petal across to the right and down to the lower petal. The mood is calm and intimate, a single bloom glowing in darkness.

The vertical format and generous dark space suit a focal wall where a warm point of color is wanted. It works well in a room with deep or neutral tones, alone or as part of a group of florals.
