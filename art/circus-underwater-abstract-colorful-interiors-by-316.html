Thread of Light, Green and Yellow Abstract Macro

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Soft, overlapping, rounded shapes of blue, green and yellow melt into one another across a horizontal frame. On the left the colours run cool, in teal and blue-green. On the right a large yellow-green field glows, and a patch of red-orange warms the bottom edge.

One thin, shimmering line of pink and blue curves from the top left down to the bottom centre, like a ribbon of light. It divides the cool shapes from the warm ones and gives your eye a path through all that colour.

The surfaces look smooth, glassy and liquid, and the light is diffused rather than harsh, with brighter highlights tracing the curved edges. Up close there is more to find: fine speckles in the upper left and a few tiny glossy bubbles resting on the surface.

I believe art is felt before it is understood. I'd rather you stand in front of this one and notice where your eye goes first, and what the colours remind you of. That response is yours, and it is the only test that counts.

The horizontal shape suits a wide wall, such as over a sofa, a bed or a sideboard. The cool and warm halves sit comfortably in a room with neutral or earthy tones and add a clear note of colour.
