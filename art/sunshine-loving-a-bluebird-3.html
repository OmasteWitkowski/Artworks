Wings Into the Golden Sun - Blue Bird Relief

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A deep blue bird with outspread wings sits at the left of a horizontal panel, beside a golden sun disc built from concentric rings and dotted bands. Wavy yellow rays stretch out from the sun in nearly every direction. A stone-colored border with small symbols and decorative marks frames the scene.

The cool cobalt of the bird and the saturated gold of the sun glow against a muted slate gray-violet ground. The bird and sun overlap and balance each other, and the raised, molded-looking figures sit inside a surface of thick, swirling, brush-like curls. The result mixes folk-art or mosaic-like relief with an oil-paint-like texture.

Look closely at the dotted and grooved detail on the sun and the small marks along the border. The rays lead the eye around the disc, and the spread wings suggest flight or a landing. The background swirls rotate quietly while the bird and sun hold still. The overall feeling is vivid and emblem-like, with warmth from the sun against the cooler bird and ground.

The horizontal format and strong color contrast give a wall one clear focal image. The muted gray-violet ground keeps the blue and gold from overwhelming a room, so it works above a sofa, console or bed.
