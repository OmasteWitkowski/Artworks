Catch Another One - Sunset Surfer Medallion

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

A black surfer stands on an angled board with arms outstretched, set among black and white palm trees. Behind the figure, a warm orange-to-yellow sunset block glows, with tropical leaves, curling flourishes and a splash of blue water. All of it sits inside a deep navy circle ringed in glowing yellow, with small white sparkle stars for highlights.

The circle is the heart of the piece. A symmetrical border of glassy, bubble-like lobes in red, green, blue and violet fills the corners and radiates outward, so the scene feels like a view through a porthole or a lens. Retro surf-poster style meets kaleidoscope pattern in one clean, crisp-edged design.

Gold script at the lower right reads "Waves Come. Let the other guys go. Catch another one." and is attributed in the image to Duke Kahanamoku. The surfer's open arms and gliding stance suit the words: a sunny, carefree picture of patience and of taking the next opportunity.

The saturated navy, gold and orange make this a lively focal point for a beach-minded living room, den, game room or surf-themed space. The horizontal format suits a spot above a sofa, bar or bed.
