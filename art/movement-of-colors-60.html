Violet Crease, Lilac and Navy Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Smooth lilac and mauve forms curve across the frame and meet at a small crease just left of center. Deep navy fills the upper left and right. Thin pale rims trace the edges, a coral-red line follows the right curve, and a small bright white spark sits above a pale mint and cream wedge. The lower edge softens into peach and tan.

What sets this piece apart is how much glow it gets from so little. The near-black navy makes the purple forms look lit from behind, and the long curves converge on that one white point. The surfaces have a satin, glass-like sheen, with fine speckles across the ribbon and lobe and hairline creases in the lower band.

The longer you look, the more the soft gradients and rounded shapes slow the eye. The mood is calm and dreamlike, with nothing to decode. It can simply be enjoyed as a rich study of color and form.

The horizontal format and generous dark areas suit a wide wall above a sofa, bed or console. They give a cool purple and navy palette room to anchor a quiet, modern space, and the peach-tan base adds a note of warmth.
