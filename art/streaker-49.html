Ember Eye in Electric Blue

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A glowing oval ring of yellow-green encircles a crumpled, reddish-brown and gold center that resembles an eye. Around it, translucent layers of electric blue and turquoise ripple in fine curved lines. A broad band of golden orange sweeps in from the left, and small bubble-like dots dot the upper right. The overall effect is glassy, luminous and swirling, like a close-up of a transparent natural structure.
