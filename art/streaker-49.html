Ember Eye in Electric Blue

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A glowing yellow-green oval holds a ruffled, crumpled center of reddish-brown and gold that reads like an eye. Around it, translucent layers of electric blue and turquoise ripple in fine curved lines. A broad golden-orange band sweeps in from the left, and small clusters of bubble-like dots sit at the upper right and lower left.

The jewel-like center is what sets this piece apart. Its rough, coral-like texture is ringed by a bright lime rim, and magenta, rose and plum tones surface inside the core. Warm gold on the left balances the cool blue field on the right, so the image feels vivid and still holds together.

Look closer and the concentric ridges pull the eye steadily inward, while a few bright looping strokes curl at the right edge. The scale and identity of the form are ambiguous. It could be something tiny or something vast, a hidden miniature world, and part of the appeal is that you decide what you are looking at.

The horizontal format and saturated blues give it presence over a sofa, console, bed or mantel. It suits a room that wants one confident, colorful focal point, and the gold and orange tones warm a cool palette.
