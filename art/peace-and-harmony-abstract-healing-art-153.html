Ember Oval, Mirrored Ribbon Abstract

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A tall oval filled with looping ribbons of red, orange and golden yellow glows against a solid black field. The pattern mirrors itself top to bottom and left to right. Red V-shaped forms sit at both ends and point toward a bright yellow knot of loops at the center.

The oval seems to light itself. Yellow and orange burn brightest in the middle, and a soft red-pink halo rims the edge. The glow comes from within the colors, not from light cast from outside. Its distinctiveness lies in that contrast of a formal, exact symmetry with fluid, slightly translucent bands that overlap one another.

Look closely and the curls and spirals turn inward, then reflect across the center axis. The eye is pulled to the middle and then back out along the swirls. Touches of magenta-pink, olive green and teal sit among the warm tones. A thin, muted multicolored border with a faint woven grain frames the black panel. The balanced, ornamental layout has a calm, ceremonial feeling.

The vertical format and strong central axis suit a narrow wall, an entry, or the space above a console. The deep black field lets the warm color stand out in a quiet room.
