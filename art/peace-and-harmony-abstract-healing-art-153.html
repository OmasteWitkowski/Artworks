Red at both ends, a gold knot at the center, glowing on black

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

Ember Oval is a tall, exactly mirrored abstract of looping ribbons that seems to light itself against a deep black field.

You know the wall. The sofa is new, or the paint is finally right, and one narrow stretch still looks bare. Maybe it's the entry, or the space above a console. You have held up a dozen things in your mind and none of them felt like yours.

Picture this there instead: a tall oval of red, orange and golden yellow ribbons, floating in a wide field of black. The color looks as if it lights itself from the inside.

I can't give you a made-up memory for this piece, so I'll tell you what the eye finds. First it takes in the glow. Yellow and orange burn brightest in the middle, and a soft red-pink halo rims the edge of the oval. The light doesn't fall on the picture from outside. It sits inside the colors.

Then you notice the symmetry. The pattern mirrors itself top to bottom and left to right, exactly. Red V-shaped forms at the top and bottom point toward a bright yellow knot of loops at the center.

Then the movement starts. Curls and spirals turn inward and reflect across the center line. Your eye goes to the middle, then travels back out along the swirls, and you can do it again.

Small touches of magenta-pink, olive green and teal sit among the warm tones. They are easy to miss at first. A thin, muted border with a faint woven grain frames the black. The formal, balanced layout feels ceremonial to me. That is my reading, and yours may differ.

The vertical shape and central axis suit a narrow wall, an entry, or the space above a console or sofa. It works as a single statement piece and doesn't need a gallery wall around it.

The wide black field gives the warm color room, so it suits a quiet or dark-toned room where you want one point of heat. The composition works at medium to large sizes, where the black and the glow can carry.
