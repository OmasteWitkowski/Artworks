Cobalt Dome and Ember Seam, Glossy Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A large, deep cobalt blue dome fills the top of the frame, edged by a thin dark line. Below and beside it, smooth glossy pools of burnt orange, amber, magenta, pale yellow-green and bright green meet along thin glowing borders. Tiny bubbles and fine specks dot the warm and magenta areas.

What sets this piece apart is the contrast of cool and warm. The huge blue mass presses against the glowing orange and magenta shapes, and the thin luminous lines between them make the colors look lit from within. The tight crop leaves no horizon and no subject, only color and curve.

Look longer and the curves bend and pinch toward a narrow seam near the center, drawing the eye down from the blue into the warmer forms below. The mood is vivid, energetic and playful, and the smooth, rounded shapes can also feel calm.

The saturated blue and orange make a strong focal point in a living room, studio, office or entry. The color is bold enough to anchor a room, and the small bubbles add detail up close.
