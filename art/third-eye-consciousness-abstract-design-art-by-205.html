Cobalt Hush, Textured Blue Color Field

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal field of deep cobalt and royal blue fills the whole frame. Fine vertical streaks and flecks in slightly lighter, darker and greener blues create a soft, grainy surface, with a few touches of brighter violet-leaning blue. There are no objects, no horizon and no visible light source.

The color is saturated and unbroken, and the grain keeps it from going flat. The texture looks soft and fibrous, a little like felt, suede or fine static. The short vertical strokes give a faint downward, rain-like shimmer, but nothing pulls the eye to one spot.

Looking longer, the piece is mostly about the small shifts in tone. Slightly lighter and darker blues flicker across the surface, so the field changes as you move from close to far. The effect is calm, steady and immersive, and the color is reason enough to live with it.

Its even, all-over composition suits a wide wall where you want a strong blue presence without a focal point. It sits comfortably above a sofa, bed or console, and it pairs easily with neutral rooms or with other textured, quiet work.
