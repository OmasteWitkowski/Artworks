Amber Tide in Cobalt, Translucent Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Deep cobalt blue fills the upper left and sweeps along the bottom, while glowing amber, orange and gold gather through the center. Green, teal and yellow translucent areas open up on the right. Curved bands arc across the frame, crossed by a dark diagonal line and a horizontal band of purple and orange.

What sets this piece apart is the way it seems lit from behind. Light appears to pass through thick, glossy layers, leaving warm glowing patches against deep blue shadows and bright white glints along the ridges. Fine ripples run down the left edge, a cracked rust-brown strip marks the left-center, and a few tiny bubbles in the lower right suggest a glass-like material.

Look longer and the image shifts between soft, blurred swirls and sharp ridges. The eye moves from the blue field toward the amber center and back along the curves. It has no recognizable subject, so the color, light and flow are the whole experience.

Its wide horizontal format and strong warm-cool contrast suit a living room, office, entry or bedroom wall where one vivid, jewel-toned piece can set the color of the space.
