Amber Tide in Cobalt, Translucent Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A close-up abstract of translucent, glass-like color. Deep cobalt blue fills the upper left and bottom, while glowing amber, orange and gold sweep through the center. Green, teal and yellow areas fill the right side, crossed by a dark diagonal line and a purple-orange band. Fine ripples, a rust-colored crackle strip and a few tiny bubbles add detail to the glossy surface.

The story of this pieceCobalt Blue and Amber Gold, Glowing Like Light Through GlassDeep cobalt and glowing amber seem lit from behind in this glossy, glass-like abstract, and a few tiny bubbles give the translucence away.
