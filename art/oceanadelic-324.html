Star in the Blue, Abstract Glass Close-Up

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A deep blue oval sits off-centre, glowing as if lit from behind. In its middle floats a small pale starburst, and a ring of tiny bubbles traces the edge of the oval. Around it, bands of yellow, teal and green rise vertically on the left, while broad curves of orange, blue and green sweep toward the lower right.

The blue is the most saturated colour in the picture, and the yellow edges carry bright highlights that make the whole piece look lit from inside. Up close, the glass is rippled and layered, with fine veining and smooth, glossy changes from one colour to the next.

What holds me is the starburst. It is very small against so much colour, and it reads to me like an eye, or a single snowflake in deep water. You may see something else, and that is fine. Look at it for a minute and the bubbles, the veins and the quieter vertical lines on the left start to show themselves.

This is a horizontal image with a clear focal point and strong colour. It suits a wall that needs one confident piece, such as over a sofa, a bed or a console. The blue and yellow work with warm neutrals as well as cooler rooms.
