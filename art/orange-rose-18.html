Ember Rose in Molten Gold

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A single rose fills the frame, its petals spiraling inward from the outer edge to a dark center. The petals look raised and sculpted, with bright golden ridges and deep orange-red hollows. Behind it, the background breaks into rough block-like sections speckled with tiny bubble-like rings and fine cracks. The whole image glows in gold, amber and rust, so the flower looks like it was formed from molten metal.

The story of this pieceA Rose That Looks Cast in Molten GoldOne rose, seen from above, looks cast in glowing gold, and the warmth carries across a room.
