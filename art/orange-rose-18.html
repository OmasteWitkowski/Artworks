A Rose Pressed in Gold: Burnished Rose on Amber Tiles

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal · prints up to about 56 inches wide

The first thing you see is one rose, head-on, sitting just left of center in a wide horizontal frame. Its petals spiral inward, and every fold looks raised and creased, as if the flower had been pressed into gold or copper foil. It reads as sculpted metal before it reads as a soft petal.

The colors are burnt orange, deep amber, golden yellow and rust red-brown. Warm light runs along the raised edges of the petals and turns them into bright gold ridges, while the recesses settle into dark brick red. Pale cream highlights, a muted peach and a few touches of olive-gold keep the glow from going flat. The surface looks like light on polished brass.

Look closer and you find that the rose is not floating in empty space. Behind it, square panels are divided by visible horizontal and vertical seams, like a wall of hammered tiles. Tiny ring-like speckles cover the background and give it a pitted, weathered grain. The rose overlaps those seams, and a few loose shapes at the upper right and right edge break the grid, so the pattern never feels too tidy.

Follow the ridges and your eye travels inward around the spiral to the center of the bloom, then back out across the tiles. The creases and speckles keep offering new detail the longer you stand in front of it. The overall feeling is warm and opulent, like antique metalwork. That richness is the whole appeal for me.

I think this piece suits a large wall best. The horizontal 3:2 shape fits the space above a sofa, bed, fireplace, sideboard or dining table, where the orange-gold glow can anchor a room. The tones sit comfortably with dark wood, leather, neutral walls and deep greens. The file is 5610 x 3739 pixels, which supports about 37 inches on the long edge at 150 ppi, or about 19 inches at a full 300 ppi. Larger sizes are possible at lower resolution. A smaller print would still work as a focal point in a study or entry.

If you are looking for a warm, tactile floral that does not look like a typical flower picture, I invite you to see the full image and zoom in on the folds and the tiles.
