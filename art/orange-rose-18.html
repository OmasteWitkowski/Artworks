Orange Rose Abstract Macro Photography

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A rose viewed from above fills the frame, its petals curling inward in a tight spiral. The whole image is in glowing gold, orange and deep reddish-brown, and the surface looks like embossed metal. Small bubbles and speckles texture the rectangular panels behind the flower.

A rose that appears sculpted from molten gold, with glowing relief-like petals set against a bubbled amber grid of panels, gives the flower a rich, metallic presence.
