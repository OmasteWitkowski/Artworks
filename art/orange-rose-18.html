Ember Rose, Gold Embossed Rose in Tiled Panels

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A single rose is seen from above, its petals curling in toward a dark center. It looks embossed in gold and copper metal. Bright highlights trace the raised petal edges against deep rust and amber shadows. The rose sits among square, tile-like panels, some covered in tiny bubble-like speckles and fine cracks, which give the surface a hammered, aged feel.
