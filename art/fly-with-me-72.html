Cool Indigo Over Hot Red: Swirls Like Glowing Ink

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Indigo Ember Current is a wide horizontal abstract in which cool violet above meets flame-like red below, joined by glowing cream scrolls.

You bought the sofa. The paint is done. And the wall above it still looks bare, or the old picture there looks small now that the room has grown around it.

This is the piece I would hold up against that wall. It is wide, dark and bright at once, and it gives a quiet room one thing to look at first.

I will not tell you a made-up story about this piece. I would rather tell you what I see when I stand in front of it, and let you find your own.

From across the room it reads as two temperatures. Deep indigo and violet fill the top. Vivid red, orange and golden yellow rise through the bottom and right, like flames billowing upward. Between them, creamy white passages glow as if lit from behind.

Come closer and the ribbons separate into thin curling outlines and soft, translucent overlaps. Spirals turn in the upper right and center. A dark, tangled knot with turquoise and gold sits left of center, and your eye keeps returning to it before it goes looping off again.

Nothing in it is a recognizable thing. It is colour and light moving, and what you make of it is yours. Look closer and you will keep finding something new.

The wide horizontal format suits the long spaces in a room: over a sofa, a bed, a mantel or a long console. The swirls carry across the width, so the piece rewards a generous size.

Its saturated palette is at its best against neutral or dark walls, where the indigo deepens and the reds and creams stand out. The mood is energetic and dramatic, and the colour alone is reason enough to want it.
