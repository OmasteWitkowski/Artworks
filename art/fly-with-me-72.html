Indigo Ember Current, Fluid Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ribbons and scrolls of color curl and fold into one another across a wide horizontal frame. Deep indigo and violet fill the top. Vivid red, orange and golden yellow billow upward through the bottom and right. Creamy white passages glow between them, with small touches of turquoise, green and magenta.

The piece is distinctive for the contrast between its cool upper field and its hot lower field. Translucent, scroll-like swirls join the two and look like glowing ink suspended in liquid. Thin curling outlines and soft overlaps give the surface a smooth, silky, marbled quality, with no visible brush marks.

Look closer and the eye is carried in loops: spirals in the upper right and center, flame-like forms rising from below. A dark, tangled knot with turquoise and gold accents sits left of center and anchors the movement. The glowing creams read as backlit against the dark indigo, like light behind stained glass.

The wide format and saturated palette suit a large wall above a sofa, bed or console. It gives a neutral or dark room one bold, energetic focal point.
