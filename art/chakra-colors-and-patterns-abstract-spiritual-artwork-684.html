Carnival Mirror - Magenta Kaleidoscope Oval

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A tall oval filled with a mirrored pattern of swirling magenta, crimson and purple shapes on a near-black maroon background. Small cobalt-blue notches run down the center line, and acid-yellow and golden curves frame the left and right edges. The forms loop and flow in smooth glossy gradients, repeating in two stacked bands. The effect is bright, glowing and kaleidoscopic.
