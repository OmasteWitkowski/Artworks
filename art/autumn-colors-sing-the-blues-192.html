Electric Delta, Blue Channel Through Brown-Rimmed Ovals

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A vivid electric-blue channel runs diagonally from the lower left and splits into branches across a field of rounded, oval forms. Each form has a raised, ridged brown rim, and the surface is finely pitted, scratched and speckled. Pale yellow-green glows sit inside several shapes, and bright specks catch the light along the blue.

The distinctive thing here is the contrast. Saturated blue, cobalt and slate set against deep brown, rust and amber give a cool palette real warmth at its edges. The forms crowd together, densest in the upper right, while the blue channel keeps the eye moving through the middle. The metallic, oil-slick edges make the surface feel crusted and crystalline.

Look closely and it reads two ways. It can be a hidden miniature world, like a slide under a microscope, or a frozen landscape seen from far above, like a river delta. Neither is a stated subject, and the picture does not settle the question. That ambiguity keeps drawing the eye back in.

In a horizontal format, it suits a sofa wall, console, desk or entry, anywhere a saturated blue statement with plenty of fine detail is wanted. The color reads from across the room, and the texture rewards a closer look.
