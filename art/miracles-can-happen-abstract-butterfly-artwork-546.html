Ember Wings - Mirrored Red and Gold Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A large butterfly spreads its wings across the center of the frame, its red body and cream head set against a deep red and maroon ground. The wings are filled with glossy, marbled swirls of gold, orange, blush pink and lavender, with small touches of lime green and pale aqua. Two thin antennae curve above the head.

The image is mirrored exactly across the center, so the swirls ripple outward from the body in a pulsing, kaleidoscope-like rhythm. The wings look translucent and lit from within, glowing against the darker surround. Faint ornamental swirls repeat in the background around them.

Look closely at the wing surfaces. The overlapping layers look like liquid marbling, smooth and fluid with no visible brushwork. A dark maroon border and a thin glowing orange inner edge frame the image like a mat. The result feels warm, ornate and celebratory, a clear butterfly silhouette rich with pattern.

The horizontal format and saturated reds and golds suit a sofa wall, bedroom, dining room or entry. It gives a room a warm, jewel-toned focal point.
