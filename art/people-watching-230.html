Ember Ring, Glowing Green and Lavender Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Rounded, translucent shapes press together across the whole frame. A pale yellow-green form sits near the center, with lavender-purple above it and blue-teal at the left. Hot red-orange forms curve around the edges and corners, and deeper burgundy settles into the shadows.

The colors look lit from behind or within. Bright translucent centers, pale luminous edges and glossy highlights give the surface a stained-glass glow. Fine bubbles and specks are scattered across the green and purple areas. The cool core and the fiery border pull against each other, so the eye is drawn inward.

The shapes move softly, like cells or smooth pebbles pressed together. There is no horizon and no single object, so the eye can travel from the red edges to the calm, glowing middle and back. The piece is about color, light and surface, and that is reason enough to want it.

In a horizontal format, it can anchor a wall above a sofa, bed or console. The warm border adds energy to a room, and the glowing center keeps it from feeling heavy.
