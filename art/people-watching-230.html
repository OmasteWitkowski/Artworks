A green and lavender glow inside a ring of red-orange

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ember Ring is a horizontal abstract of translucent, glass-like shapes that look lit from within and edged in hot red-orange.

You have the sofa, the paint and the lamp. Then there is the wide wall above it, which has looked a little empty since the day the room was finished.

Picture something there that gives the room a glow. This is Ember Ring: a pale yellow-green and lavender center, lit as if from behind, held inside a curve of hot red-orange.

At first glance it is color. Red-orange wraps the edges and corners, and your eye follows it inward to a cooler middle of yellow-green, with lavender above and blue-teal at the left.

Then the surface starts to show. Tiny bubbles and specks are scattered across the green and purple areas. Thin white veils and glossy highlights sit along the edges, and pale, luminous lines separate one rounded shape from the next. The shapes press together softly, like cells or smooth pebbles.

There is no horizon and no single object to name. Your eye can travel from the fiery border to the glowing center and back again. That is the whole invitation. What it means to you is yours to decide.

Ember Ring is a horizontal piece, so it suits a wide wall: above a sofa, a bed, a console or a sideboard. The warm red-orange border can anchor the space, and the glowing center keeps it from feeling heavy.

If your room is quiet in tone, this gives it one confident point of color. If your room already has color, look at the teal, amber and burgundy in the image and see whether any of them are already in your room.
