Heart on a Red Thread, Jewel-Toned Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A thin red line rises through the middle of this close-up abstract and ends in a small red heart-like shape at the top. Lower down, a second small red shape and a clear round droplet sit on it. Around the line, smooth, translucent shapes in olive and lime green, violet, teal, golden yellow and cobalt blue flow in slow waves from left to right.

The red line is what sets this piece apart. Against the broad, soft color fields it reads like a tiny plant or a hanging pendant, and it gives the eye a place to land and travel up and down through the frame. Light seems to pass through the shapes so they glow like stained glass, with the deepest color where they overlap. Thin pale rims, a few bubble-like specks and the wet-looking droplet add a glossy, liquid feel.

The image is cropped tightly so the shapes continue past the edges, and the left and right sides balance each other around the red axis. It is playful and vivid, with a calm flow, and the small heart-like form adds warmth and a little whimsy. You can read it as a plant, a pendant or pure color and shape.

In a horizontal format, it suits a wall above a sofa, bed, console or desk where a saturated, jewel-toned accent is wanted. The dark olive and violet masses ground the bright red, so it holds its own in both quiet and colorful rooms.
