Heart on a Red Thread, Jewel-Toned Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A close-up abstract made of smooth, translucent shapes in green, yellow, purple, teal and blue that seem to glow from within. A thin red line rises through the middle and ends in a small red heart-like form at the top. A clear droplet and a second small red shape sit lower along the line. Soft waves of color flow across the frame, outlined by thin pale edges.

The story of this pieceOne Red Thread Through a Sea of Glowing ColorA thin red line, a small heart-like shape and a wet-looking droplet hang in a stained-glass sea of color.
