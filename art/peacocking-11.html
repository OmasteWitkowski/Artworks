Emerald Pageant, Three Peacocks

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A male peacock stands at the center with his tail opened into a huge semicircle of blue-and-green eyespot feathers. In the foreground a second peacock walks left across straw-covered ground, its long green train trailing behind. A third bird perches high at the upper right among branches and chain-link fencing.

The fan is the star. Its eyespots glow against dark forest-green foliage, and the saturated emerald, peacock blue and straw gold give the whole scene a luminous, showy energy. Fine feather barbs radiate like threads, and the feathers and straw share a swirling, brushed texture.

Look closely at how the scene is built. The fan sweeps outward in a wide arc from the central bird's blue neck, the walking peacock pulls the eye diagonally to the left, and the perched bird adds a calm vertical note at upper right. The mood is bold and lively, a celebration of natural display.

Its wide horizontal format suits a wall above a sofa, bed or console, or a dining room that can carry strong color. The deep greens and blues hold their own against dark woods and neutral walls.
