Emerald Pageant, Three Peacocks

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A male peacock stands at the center with his tail spread into a huge fan covered in blue-and-green eyespots. In the foreground, a second peacock walks left across a straw-covered ground, its long green train trailing behind. A third peacock perches high at the upper right among branches and a chain-link fence. The colors are intensely saturated, and the feathers and straw have a swirling, finely brushed texture.

The story of this pieceThe Peacock Fan That Glows Against Dark FoliageA fully opened peacock fan glows in emerald and peacock blue while two more birds complete the scene.
