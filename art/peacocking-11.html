Peacocking - Colorful Peacock Photography

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A male peacock stands with its back to the viewer, its tail fully fanned into a large green semicircle dotted with blue eyespots. Another peacock with a long iridescent tail walks across straw-covered ground in the foreground, and a third perches on a branch at upper right. A wire fence and leafy trees fill the background, and the whole scene has a swirling, painterly texture.

A fully fanned peacock tail glows in emerald and blue eyespots, set within a swirling, painterly treatment that turns a trio of birds into a vivid, texture-rich wildlife scene.
