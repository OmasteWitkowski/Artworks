Ember Heart, Swirled Amber Flame

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A tall flame rises through the center of a dark brown-black field. Its core is nearly white, and golden-orange swirls surround it and fade into rust and burnt umber at the edges. The tip curls toward the upper right. Soft round shapes and a few small spirals are scattered through the darkness around it.

The flame looks as if it were built from thick, swirled paint. Fine curling grooves run across the bright areas, and small spirals and bubble-like curls roll through the flame and background. The result is a stylized, painterly fire with strong glow rather than literal photographic detail.

Look closely at where the light changes. White-hot at the center, it moves through creamy yellow and amber into shadow, and the brightest point sits just right of center. Small eddies inside the form give it an upward flicker, while the dark edges hold it in place. The mood is warm, intense and energetic.

The vertical format and high contrast give the piece presence on a single wall. It suits a living room, den, study or reading corner where deep browns and warm light are already part of the room, and it works well near a fireplace.
