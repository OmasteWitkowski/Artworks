Ember Constellation, Glowing Stars in Red and Green Liquid Color

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Glossy fields of crimson red, deep forest green and creamy off-white melt into one another like liquid currents. Thin white branching lines that resemble antlers curl across the surface, and small glowing star shapes are scattered through the upper and lower areas. Touches of dusty blue-grey, violet and peach soften the transitions.

What sets this piece apart is the way the crisp, luminous white forms sit against the smooth, melting colour. Bright highlights on the stars and branches look backlit and stand out against the darker red and green, so the eye keeps moving from one star to the next around the frame.

Look closely and you can follow the curling tendrils as they sweep diagonally from lower left to upper right. The warm red band across the upper left holds the image steady while cool green gathers at the right and soft cream opens up in the lower center. The mood is lively and dreamlike, and you can read the red-and-green palette as festive or simply as rich colour.

The horizontal, full-bleed format suits a sofa wall, a bedroom headboard or a console, wherever a bold, warm focal point is wanted. The red and green can anchor a room's palette, and the cream areas give the eye a place to rest.
