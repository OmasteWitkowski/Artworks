Purple Lilac Love

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

Two thick clusters of purple lilac flowers hang among large green leaves, with each small star-shaped floret visible. Behind them, thin branches and a bright, blurred patchwork of green light fill the background. The whole image has a textured, painted look.

Richly saturated purple lilac blooms stand out against deep green foliage, and a painterly oil-like texture gives the garden scene a tactile, vivid presence.
