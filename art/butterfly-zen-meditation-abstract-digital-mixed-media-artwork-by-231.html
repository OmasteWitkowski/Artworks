Filigree Wings, Ornate Golden Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A single butterfly spreads its wings across the center of a horizontal canvas, seen from above. Golden orange, olive and lime green and deep navy glow through the wings, with touches of teal, sky blue and violet. The body is orange with a patterned, heart-like abdomen, and the antennae and tail tips curl into tiny spirals.

What sets it apart is the linework. Fine navy swirls, scrolls and leaf-like shapes fill every wing over mottled, stained-glass-like color. Behind the butterfly, a pale lace-like web of cell-shaped outlines in soft white, pink and lavender repeats the same line language, so the insect and its ground feel like one drawing.

Look closely and the curls repeat everywhere: in the wing scrolls, the antennae, the tail tips and the web that radiates softly outward. The mirrored left and right wings give the piece a calm, balanced order, while the density of pattern keeps rewarding a second and third look. It is a cheerful, ornate piece, and its appeal is rich color and intricate detail.

The pale, evenly lit ground keeps the colors bright, so it works as a focal point above a console, sofa or bed, or in a desk or reading nook that needs a lively, decorative accent.
