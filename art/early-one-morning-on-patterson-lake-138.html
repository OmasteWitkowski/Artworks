Off Duty Quiet - Orange Figure on a Green Lake Dock

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

A figure in a bright orange top sits alone at the far end of a floating dock, seen from behind and looking out over rippled green lake water. A small pale boat is tied along the dock's side, and the dock's speckled surface catches soft highlights.

In the lower foreground, a brown sign reads 'NO LIFEGUARD ON DUTY'. Set against the lone orange figure, it adds a dry, wry note to a very still scene. The orange is the brightest color in the picture and stands out against the moss, olive and dark greens of the water.

The dock runs diagonally from the lower left toward the seated figure, and open water fills the right side and background, which leaves the figure with plenty of room and quiet. The light is soft and diffused, with no hard shadows. The finish is smooth, painterly and slightly glossy, like oil paint, and a dark border frames the whole scene.

In a living room, bedroom, study or lake-style space, this horizontal piece gives a calm green field with one warm point of focus, and the sign gives guests something to smile at.
