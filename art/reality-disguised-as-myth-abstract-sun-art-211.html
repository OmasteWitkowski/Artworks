Solar Ember: Twelve-Rayed Sun Emblem

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A sun-like shape sits at the center of a deep black background, built around a black-outlined oval with twelve curling, pointed rays. The rays and the center are filled with swirling orange, yellow, blue and pink color that looks like marbled liquid. The design is symmetrical, and a blue horizontal band runs through the middle of the oval. A thin blue border frames the whole image.

The story of this pieceCan You See the Eye in Twelve Glowing Rays?Twelve whipping, ember-bright rays spin around a black-ringed oval, and the colour looks lit from within.
