Reality Disguised As Myth Abstract Sun Art

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A stylized sun made of curving, pointed rays spreads out from a black-outlined oval at the center. The rays and the center are filled with swirling orange, blue, pink and red, and the figure sits on a near-black background inside a thin blue border. The design mirrors top to bottom.

A glowing, radially symmetrical sun symbol whose marbled orange, blue and pink rays curl against deep black, so it reads as both a bold graphic emblem and a liquid-color abstract.
