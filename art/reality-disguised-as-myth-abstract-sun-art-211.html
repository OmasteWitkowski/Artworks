Solar Ember: Twelve-Rayed Sun Emblem

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A sun-like emblem glows at the center of a near-black field. Twelve curling, pointed rays spin outward around a black-outlined oval, and a horizontal blue band crosses its middle. The rays and center are filled with marbled orange, golden yellow, cobalt blue and coral, with touches of rose pink and magenta. A thin royal-blue border frames the whole design.

The design is mirrored top to bottom, so the rays whip outward in a steady S-shaped rhythm, as if the whole wheel were spinning. Soft luminous edges make the warm color look lit from within, and the surfaces have a smooth, liquid marbling instead of visible brushwork.

Look closely at the central oval. Its mirrored pattern can read as an eye or a face, though that is a matter of viewer interpretation. The sun and mandala readings of the radial form are equally open. The piece works first as pure color, contrast and symmetry.

In a vertical format with a strong central focal point, it can anchor a wall as a single warm statement, especially against dark or neutral surroundings.
