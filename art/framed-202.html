Orange Ribbon Frame, Blue Teal Marbled Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A wavy ribbon of saturated orange-gold runs along the top, down the left side and across the bottom, framing a dark blue square on the left and a larger panel of teal and blue ripples on the right. Glossy bands of cobalt, teal, emerald and navy curl into small spirals, with a bright green curl near the top.

What sets this piece apart is the simple, graphic frame. The orange ribbon is heaviest at the top right and along the lower edge, and it wobbles like a loose hand-drawn rectangle around a cool ground. The color edges are crisp, and thin pale highlights give the swirls a wet, reflective sheen.

Look closer and the motion is everywhere: ribbons fold and whirl into little eddies in the corners, at the top center and in the right panel. The shapes there may look like a face with two spiral eyes. It is a chance resemblance, but it adds a touch of play to an otherwise non-representational design of color and flow.

The horizontal format and strong warm and cool contrast suit a wall that needs energy, such as above a sofa, a console or a desk. The blues and teals settle into modern rooms, and the orange adds a lively accent.
