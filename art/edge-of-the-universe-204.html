Satin Tide, Iridescent Blue and Teal Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Glossy, curving shapes fill the frame in close view. A large lobe of cobalt blue turns to turquoise teal and curls in from the upper right, while a thin, lens-shaped sliver of yellow, orange and blue lies along the bottom. Thin rainbow-like bands run through the color, and hot red gathers in the corners.

The color looks wet and glassy, but the center is a pale, finely brushed surface of fine diagonal lines that reads like satin metal. Tiny droplets and specks gather along the left curve and the edges of the blue lobe. That meeting of liquid shine and metallic texture is what sets this piece apart from other colorful abstracts.

Look longer and the light becomes the subject. It seems to pass through the translucent layers, so the blues and teals glow against the muted copper-brown. A strong diagonal carries the eye from the red corners to the bright upper right, then down the pale central curve to the yellow sliver. On the right, the forms melt into soft, blurred bronze, and that selective focus gives the image depth. It can be enjoyed simply as rich, fluid color.

The horizontal format suits a wall above a sofa, bed or console, or a room that needs one saturated focal point. The warm copper-brown base keeps the vivid blue and red from feeling harsh.
