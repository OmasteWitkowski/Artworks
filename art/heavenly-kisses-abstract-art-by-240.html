Eye or lips? Look closer at this mirrored orange bloom

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · horizontal

Ember Bloom Kaleidoscope in Orange and Pink is a digital abstract with perfect four-way symmetry and a glowing almond shape at its center.

You know the wall. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and that one stretch of wall above it still looks unfinished. You have probably stood there more than once, tape measure in hand, not quite sure what belongs.

Picture something warm there. Orange, red and golden petals glow around a soft rose-pink center, and a bright almond shape sits in the middle with light streaking out sideways from it. It reads clearly from across the room, and it still gives you something to find when you walk up close.

I'm not going to hand you a made-up backstory for this one. What it has is what the eye discovers, and it happens in a particular order.

First you see the frame: rounded petal shapes outlined in orange, overlapping all the way around the edge. They look backlit, a bit like stained glass, and their color is mottled and slightly grainy, as if sponged on.

Then your eye drops inward to the calm middle. The texture changes completely. It is smooth, glossy and liquid-looking, with white and cream swirls curling toward a glowing almond shape. Some people see an eye. Others see lips. I leave that to you.

Then you notice the tiny speckles scattered across the pink like stars. The whole thing is mirrored exactly, left to right and top to bottom, so the pattern rewards a longer look. This is what I mean by look closer.

The format is horizontal, which suits the wall above a sofa, a console, a bed or a desk. The color is saturated and the symmetry is strong, so it can hold a wall as a single focal piece. It can also act as a warm accent next to quieter neutrals.

It pairs well with neutral or deep-toned rooms, where the oranges and golds can glow against a calmer background.
