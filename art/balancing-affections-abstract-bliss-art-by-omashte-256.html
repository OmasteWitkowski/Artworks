Everything in this red pattern pulls back to one small diamond

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

Garnet Jewel is a mirror-symmetrical abstract in crimson, golden yellow and deep purple, with a glowing diamond of violet and blue swirls at its center.

You have a wall that has bothered you for a while. Maybe the sofa is new and the old picture now looks small above it. Maybe the room is calm and tidy and missing one thing that makes you glad to walk in.

This is the piece I would hold up to that wall. It is deep crimson, with big curls of golden yellow and indigo purple, and in the very middle sits a small diamond that seems to glow. Stand back and you see a bold red square. Come closer and the middle starts to move.

The whole piece is mirrored, left to right and top to bottom. Everything fans out evenly from the center and sweeps into the four corners. There are open triangles of red at the top and bottom, so the pattern has room to breathe.

Each curling arm of purple or yellow has a thin line winding into its middle. Your eye follows the line in, and then the whole design pulls it back to the center. That is the part people remember. They call it the red one with the glowing center.

The surfaces are different from each other. The red and yellow shapes are flat and graphic, with a mottled, painterly grain that looks like thick brushed paint. The diamond is smoother, with liquid ripples of violet, blue and pink. It holds the only real glow in the piece. There are no shadows and no direction of light. The warmth comes from the colors themselves.

Some people see wings in the shapes. Some see horns. Some see neither. I leave that to you.

The square format and even symmetry make it easy to center over a sofa, a bed, a console or a stair landing. You don't have to work out which side is heavier, because both sides match.

In a neutral room it gives you one confident burst of red. In a room that already has color, it is bold enough to hold its own. The mood is energetic, warm and a little festive.

Picture the wall that has been bothering you with this on it, centered and square, with that small glowing diamond drawing the eye in. The red carries across the room, and the spirals give you something to look at up close.
