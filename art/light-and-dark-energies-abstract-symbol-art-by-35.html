Indigo Portal: A Jewel-Toned Yin Yang

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A tall oval holds a large yin-yang symbol. One half is a pale, milky cream with a small navy dot. The other half is filled with swirling plum purple, deep blue, crimson and cream, and it holds a small white dot. A ring of marbled color in violet, peach, teal and gold surrounds the symbol, and the whole oval floats on a dark, finely flecked navy ground.

The contrast is the point. The white half is flat, graphic and softly glowing, while the colored half looks glassy and backlit, with deep saturated purples and blues opening into warm peach highlights. The stretched oval and the mirrored border give the image the look of a glowing cabochon or a portal.

Look closely and the S-curve of the symbol sets the whole image turning, and the border repeats from the center outward like a slow kaleidoscopic pulse. The yin-yang carries its traditional themes of balance and of light and dark held together, and the image also works as pure color and contrast.

The vertical format and dark vignette give it a strong focal presence. It suits a quiet corner, a meditation or reading space, or a bedroom or living room that wants one deep, jewel-toned accent.
