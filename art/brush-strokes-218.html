Luminous Curl, Cream Swirls on Blue and Red

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Glossy, glass-like shapes fill the frame in periwinkle blue, deep brick red and dark plum. Bright cream-white highlights cross them, glowing pale yellow at the edges. A large pale form sits left of center, a hooked white shape rises at upper right, and a curled spiral winds inward at lower right.

What sets this piece apart is the light. The highlights blow out to near white against the saturated color fields, and fine, feathery streaks run through the pale forms like strands of hair. Tiny bubble-like dots are scattered across the red, and thin curved lines divide the image into cell-like segments.

There is no horizon and no single focal point, so the eye keeps moving. It follows the sweeping streaks, circles the spiral and drifts between the cool blue on the left and the warm red and plum at upper right. The effect is energetic, vivid and fluid.

Its horizontal, full-bleed format suits a living room, office or studio wall where a bold, color-rich focal point is wanted. The dark plum and red anchor the space, and the cream highlights bring light into it.
