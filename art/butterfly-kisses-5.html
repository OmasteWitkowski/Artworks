Summer Stillness, Swallowtail on a White Daisy

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A pale yellow swallowtail with bold black stripes rests with its wings fully open on a white daisy with a bright yellow center. The wing edges are lined in black with small yellow spots, with hints of blue and orange near the tail. Behind it, a soft blur of purple, pink and green fills the frame.

The butterfly and daisy are the brightest, clearest areas, set against a dark, muted background. The wing veins and petals stay crisp, while the background carries faint swirling, painterly marks that look like ripples.

Look closely at the background. The circular strokes give the still butterfly a gentle sense of drift, like a quiet moment in a summer garden. It is a calm, delicate scene, and the contrast of black stripes on pale wings against the white flower is reason enough to enjoy it.

The horizontal format and the wings spanning nearly the full width suit a sofa, console, bed or desk wall. The dark, glowing background adds depth to light walls, and the yellow and white pick up warm or neutral rooms.
