Black Stripes, Pale Wings, and a Background That Drifts

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

Summer Stillness is a painterly close-up of a yellow-and-black swallowtail resting on a white daisy, wings fully open.

You bring home a new sofa, and the wall above it suddenly looks bare. Or the old picture that used to fit now looks small in the room. You know you want something, but you can't quite say what.

Picture a long, horizontal piece there. A pale yellow butterfly with black stripes spreads its wings nearly edge to edge. Under it is a white daisy with a bright yellow center, and behind both is a dark, soft glow of purple, pink and green.

The first thing you see is the contrast: black stripes on pale yellow wings, set against a clean white daisy. They are the brightest, clearest things in the frame, so your eye lands there before it goes anywhere else.

Then it moves to the edges. The wings are lined in black with small yellow spots, and near the tail there are hints of blue and orange. The wing veins and the daisy petals stay crisp.

Now look at the background. It is a soft blur, and it is not empty. Faint swirling strokes make ripple-like circles in the purple, pink and green. The butterfly itself is perfectly still, but those marks give the scene a gentle sense of drift, like a quiet moment in a summer garden.

I'd rather you stand in front of it than read about it. If the contrast makes you stop, that is reason enough.

The format is horizontal and the wings span nearly the full width, so it suits the wide wall over a sofa, a bed, a console or a desk. It could also be the calm focal piece in a reading nook or sunroom.

The dark background gives it depth on a light wall. The yellow and white pick up the warmth in neutral or warm rooms. The scene itself is delicate and quiet, with a feeling of summer.
