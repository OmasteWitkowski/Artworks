A Daisy Covered in Swirls Like Piped Icing

Botanical & Flowers · vertical

Swirl Daisy, White Flower on Green Spirals is a digitally manipulated photograph of one white daisy, textured with fine curls and set against glossy green spirals.

You know the wall. It might be the narrow one in the hallway, the stretch by the front door, or the space beside the kitchen window. It's been bare for a while, and every time you walk past it you think it needs something.

Maybe you've nearly bought a daisy print before and put it back, because it looked like every other white flower on a white background. This one is different. The daisy is easy to recognize, but its surface looks sculpted, as if someone piped it in icing.

From across the room you see a white daisy facing you straight on, with long, rounded petals fanning out evenly around an orange-gold center. The petals glow against the dark greens, and the center is the warmest point in the picture.

Step closer and the surface changes. Tiny curling swirls cover every petal and the dense, scrolled center. They give the flower a smooth, thick look, like piped icing or impasto. There is faint pink-gray shading in the folds of the petals.

Behind the flower, big spirals of dark and bright green curl and drift like glossy bubbles. They echo the small swirls on the daisy, so the whole image reads as one pattern. Low in the frame, a few bright green patches look like light coming through leaves.

It is a digitally manipulated photograph, and the daisy stays a daisy. I'd like you to stand in front of it and see what you feel. Your own response is the only test that counts.

This is a vertical piece with one strong focal point, so it suits a hallway, an entry, a kitchen, a sunroom or a powder room. The greens and white sit comfortably with natural wood, cream and warm neutral rooms.

It also works as part of a small group of florals, if one wall needs more than one piece. The mood is fresh, playful and cheerful.
