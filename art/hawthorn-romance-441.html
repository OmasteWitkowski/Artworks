Hawthorn Romance

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A cluster of ruffled pink and magenta flowers with deeply lobed green leaves grows next to a weathered tree trunk with a dark knot. The background is a soft blur of green, gray and mauve. The whole image has a swirled, painterly texture.

Saturated pink blossoms stand out against a knotted, textured tree trunk, and the swirling painterly finish gives the scene a tactile, brushed-paint quality.
