Amber Teardrop Glow, Violet and Magenta Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Soft pink, magenta and deep purple shapes flow across the frame, with a large amber teardrop form in the center right. On the left, a pale olive oval and a patch of green sit against streaks of bright red at the bottom. Small round bubbles of various sizes cluster on the right, and a patch of dark specks sits inside an orange shape near the top. The whole piece glows as if lit from behind, with smooth, glossy curves.

The story of this pieceThe Amber Teardrop That Seems Lit From BehindMagenta, deep violet and burnt amber glow in long curves, with a chain of crisp bubbles, as if light were passing through layers of glass.
