Molten Mirror, Symmetrical Orange and Yellow Swirl Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Three lens-shaped panels of swirling orange and golden yellow sit side by side, each filled with smooth, liquid-looking curls and fine marbled veins. Narrow columns of darker red-brown filigree run between them. At the center, a small olive-green triangle and a red diamond-shaped eye sit among curled flourishes.

The image is mirrored left to right and top to bottom, so the pattern repeats with a steady, kaleidoscopic rhythm. Bright yellow passages glow like hot highlights, while the red and brown areas read as shadow. The small green triangle at the center is the one cool accent in an otherwise fiery palette.

Look closely and you will find small bead-like highlights, curled horn-like flourishes near the top and bottom edges, and swirling lines that pull the eye gently upward along each vertical axis. The fire-like glow is an impression the color invites. What stays constant is the order of the symmetry set against the fluid motion inside it.

Its warm, horizontal format suits a wall that needs a confident focal point, such as above a sofa, bed or console. The saturated orange and amber pair naturally with neutral walls and dark wood.
