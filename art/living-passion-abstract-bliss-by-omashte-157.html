Find the One Green Triangle in a Field of Mirrored Glow

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Molten Mirror is a symmetrical digital abstract in orange, yellow and amber: three glowing panels, dark filigree columns, and one small cool accent at the center.

Maybe the sofa is new and the wall above it suddenly looks bare. Or the old picture has started to look small in a bigger room. You know what you want there: something warm, wide and confident, and not the same orange swirl you keep scrolling past.

This is the piece I'd put in front of you. Three tall, lens-shaped panels of orange and golden yellow sit side by side, and every curl in them is mirrored left to right and top to bottom. Most orange abstracts are loose and chaotic. This one is ordered, and you can see that from across the room.

From a distance you see heat and glow. The yellows look like hot highlights, and the reds and browns sit back like shadow, so the whole image looks lit from within. That fire-like feeling is an impression the color invites. It isn't a subject, and what you make of it is yours.

Step closer and the panels turn into smooth, liquid-looking curls with fine marbled veins. Small bead-like highlights catch the eye. Narrow columns of darker red-brown filigree run between the panels, and curled, horn-like flourishes sit near the top and bottom edges.

Then you reach the center. A small olive-green triangle and a red diamond-shaped eye sit among the flourishes. It's the one cool, sharp note in a fiery palette, and the eye tends to rest there before it moves on. The steady symmetry holds the pattern in place while the swirling lines pull your gaze gently upward.

The horizontal shape suits the wall above a sofa, bed, console or fireplace, anywhere you want a bold but orderly burst of color. The orange and amber sit naturally against neutral walls and dark wood, and the dark filigree columns keep the brightness from feeling flat.

It's a focal piece. If your room is settled and the wall is the one thing missing, this gives it a center of gravity without asking the furniture to change.
