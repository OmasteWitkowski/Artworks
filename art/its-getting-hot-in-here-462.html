Its Getting Hot in Here

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A warm abstract image in which a large pale-yellow shape glows against deep orange and red. Thin curving bands of gold and amber flow around it and sweep toward the right side. Small bubbles and teardrop-shaped droplets are scattered across the lower right.

A glowing, molten-looking abstract in fiery oranges and yellows, with layered flowing ribbons and tiny suspended bubbles that give it depth and warmth.
