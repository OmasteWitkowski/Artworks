Red Among the Green: Half-Timbered Pond Reflection

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

A still pond fills the foreground, holding the reflection of a red half-timbered building, a split-rail wooden fence and a slender leafy tree. Behind the water, a grassy bank and layers of dense green trees rise under a pale gray sky. Bright green grass edges the bottom, and leafy branches reach in from the upper right.

The red building glows through the foliage and returns as broken red reflections in the water. It is the one warm note among olive, moss and deep forest greens. A swirling, painterly texture runs across the whole image, giving the leaves, grass, sky and rippled water a stylized, oil-like grain.

The eye travels from the grass across the pond, along the diagonal fence, and up through the tall tree to the red building. The soft overcast light casts no hard shadows, so the greens stay rich and the scene stays quiet. The curling texture gives the foliage a gentle sense of motion against the still water.

The piece suits a living room, bedroom, study or office where a calm green landscape with one confident accent of red is wanted. Its horizontal format works well over a sofa, console or bed.
