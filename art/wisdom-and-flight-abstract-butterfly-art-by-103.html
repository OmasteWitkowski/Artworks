The Orange Butterfly Whose Wings Swirl Like Marble

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

Ember Wings is a single, perfectly mirrored butterfly in orange, gold and red, set on clean white.

You have a room that is finished. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and one wall still looks like it is waiting. Everything you have tried on it looks like something you have already seen.

Now picture a single butterfly there, wings wide open, in orange so saturated it seems to glow against the white around it. It is bold enough to read from across the room, and it keeps giving up more detail the closer you walk.

From a distance, Ember Wings is a clean, graphic shape: one butterfly seen from above, mirrored exactly left to right, with a generous margin of white around it. The wing tips sweep up and out toward the top corners. The smaller lower wings balance the large upper ones.

Step closer and the wings stop being flat color. Inside each wing, orange, red and gold swirl together like marbled paper, soft and a little watery. Fine dark brown lines trace the veins and divide the wings into panels, so the soft color sits inside a crisp frame.

Rows of bright yellow oval spots follow the wing edges. Near the edges there are also a few muted slate blue-gray patches, a cool note that makes the oranges look warmer. Down the center runs a pale pink-lavender body, and thin antennae with oval tips rise from a round yellow head.

Where the wings meet the body, a pale, glowing yellow-white area sits at the center. The curved vein lines and the swirling color seem to flow outward from it. The wings are held still, but the color inside them is not. That is the part I'd ask you to look closer at.

Ember Wings is a horizontal piece with a wide white margin, so it has room to breathe. That makes it an easy accent over a console, a desk, a bed or a sofa, particularly in a room with white or neutral walls where you want one bright, warm note.

It also sits well in a gallery wall of other warm pieces. The mood is sunny, cheerful and lively, and it suits a sunroom, a living room or a bedroom.
