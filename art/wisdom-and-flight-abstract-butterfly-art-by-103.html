Ember Wings, Symmetrical Orange Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A single butterfly with fully open wings sits in the center of a plain white field, seen from above. The wings glow in vivid orange, golden yellow and red-orange. A pale pink-lavender body runs down the middle, and thin antennae with oval tips rise from a round yellow head.

What sets it apart is the color inside the wings. Marbled, watery swirls of orange, red and gold are held within crisp, dark brown outlined vein panels, and rows of bright yellow oval spots follow the wing edges. The whole butterfly is mirrored exactly across the pale pink-lilac center, with soft slate blue-gray patches near the wing edges as a cool counterpoint.

Look closer and the curved vein lines and swirling color seem to flow outward from a pale, glowing yellow-white area where the wings meet the body. The wings are still, but the color inside them is not. The mood is warm, sunny and lively.

The white margin and the saturated color make it an easy accent for a neutral room. It suits a wall over a console, desk or bed, or a gallery wall of other warm pieces.
