Blue Diamond Seam - Mirrored Bare Trees

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A tall, mirror-symmetrical image of bare tree trunks and branches rises on either side of a thin dark vertical line. The limbs fork and cross into X-shapes and frame a bright blue diamond in the middle. Smaller blue diamonds repeat above and below. Fine dark brown and purple twigs spread over pale lavender and muted olive.

What sets this piece apart is the glowing blue center. It stands out against the darker, muted branches, and the central seam divides the picture like a kaleidoscope or Rorschach image. The pattern is exact, but the trees stay recognisable. A swirled, liquid-looking texture runs through the blue areas and gives the symmetry a gentle rotating motion.

Look closely and the branches radiate outward from the central junctions. The mirrored forms pull the eye along the vertical axis and into the blue diamond. Small partial forms at the left and right edges continue the mirroring. The effect is striking, slightly otherworldly, and driven by pattern and color contrast.

The portrait format suits a hallway, entry, stairwell or a narrow stretch of wall. The deep blue and aubergine tones work with modern rooms that want one bold, graphic focal point.
