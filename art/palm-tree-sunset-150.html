Ember Horizon, Palm Against a Storm-Blue Sunset

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

A palm crown stands in the center foreground, its green fronds fanning outward and arcing down against the sky. Behind it, a band of burnt orange glows along the horizon above a calm blue-gray sea. Peach and amber fade upward into dark blue and violet clouds that stretch in long horizontal bands.

The light is what sets this piece apart. The fronds are backlit, so their tips pick up warm orange while their centers stay in cool green shadow. The glowing horizon and the heavy storm-blue clouds pull in opposite directions, and the still water keeps the scene quiet. A fine, swirled, brush-like texture covers sky, sea and leaves alike, giving the whole scene a painted feel.

Look past the first glance and the composition rewards time. Cloud, glow, haze and water stack in layers behind the tree, and the dark clouds at upper right balance the palm's weight on the left. The mood is warm and calm, a tropical evening held at its most colorful moment.

The horizontal format and strong orange-and-blue contrast suit a wall above a sofa, bed or console. It brings warmth to a neutral room, and its green, peach and navy tones sit comfortably beside coastal or relaxed modern interiors.
