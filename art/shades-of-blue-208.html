Cobalt Seam - Mirrored Blue Glass Tile Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · vertical

Two mirror-image vertical panels show a grid of square glass tiles in saturated cobalt blue, pale aqua and icy clear glass. Each tile has a surface of small raised bubble-like circles, and dark brown-black lines separate them. The grid curves and warps slightly, so the rows seem to bulge outward. A narrow dark blue strip divides the two halves, and the outer edges fade into deep blue shadow.

The story of this pieceTwo Halves of Glowing Blue Glass, Split by One Dark SeamTwo mirror-image panels of glowing cobalt glass tiles meet at a narrow dark seam, and the grid bows outward like a pair of softly bulging doors.
