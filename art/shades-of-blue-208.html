Cobalt Seam - Mirrored Blue Glass Tile Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · vertical

Two mirror-image vertical panels hold a grid of square glass tiles in saturated cobalt and royal blue, pale aqua and icy clear glass. Each tile is covered in small bubble-like dimples, and dark brown-black lines separate them. A narrow dark blue strip runs down the center, and the outer edges fade into deep blue shadow.

The tiles seem to glow from within. Blue tiles are rich and luminous, clear tiles catch bright white highlights, and the dark grid keeps every color strong. The grid bends and tilts slightly, so the rows seem to bulge outward, and the mirrored halves open and close like a pair of doors. Darker tiles scattered among lighter ones give a checker-like balance.

Look closely and the pebbled, frosted surface of each tile contrasts with the rough matte lines around it. A few tiles are cropped at the borders, and faint touches of soft violet-pink show along some lower edges. The mood is cool, ordered and quietly mesmerizing, and the jewel-like color is reason enough to live with it.

Its vertical format suits a narrow wall, entry, hallway or bedroom, or the space above a console. The deep blues and dark edges anchor a cool-toned room without needing other colors to support them.
