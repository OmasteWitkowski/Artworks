Sharp white daisies, and a stone that looks stirred like paint

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

Still Daisies and Stirred Stone is a digital artwork of crisp white daisies in front of a weathered stone block, with the stone and leaves turned into oil-paint swirls.

The sofa is in. The paint is settled. And the wall above it has stayed bare for months, because nothing you found looked like it belonged to you.

Now picture white daisies with golden yellow-orange centers, sharp and calm, set in front of a big weathered stone block. Behind and around them, the stone and the leaves have turned into looping curls and ripples, as if thick paint were being stirred. The flowers hold still while everything else moves.

The contrast. The daisies are the brightest thing in the picture, so your eye lands on them straight away. Then it starts to wander, because the surface around them won't stay still.

Look closer and the swirls turn into small whorls spread through the leaves and the rock. The stone has mottled, cracked patches of color. A cool slate-teal patch on the left side of the stone gives the warm cream and gold something to push against.

The daisies step down in a loose diagonal from the upper center to the lower center, so your eye travels through the picture instead of resting in one spot. Small round buds gather toward the right edge, still closed.

The piece is listed as digital art with a painterly swirl treatment. What it means is yours to decide. I'd rather you stand in front of it than read about it.

It's a horizontal piece, so it suits the wall above a sofa, a bed, a sideboard or a console, or a long hallway wall. The diagonal of daisies leads the eye across the frame, so it works as a focal point and doesn't just fill space.

The cream, green and mauve-brown tones sit easily in a neutral room. The white flowers pick up light, and the mood is quiet, fresh and a little whimsical.
