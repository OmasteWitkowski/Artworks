Crimson Axis - Symmetrical Blue and Gold Swirl Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ribbons of cobalt blue, deep crimson and golden yellow swirl across a horizontal field, mirrored exactly along a narrow red line down the center. Pale yellow bands curve beside the spine and branch into curling shapes. Blue and red lobes sit at mid-height on either side, and fine, tightly packed contour lines fill the edges and corners like marbled paper.

The perfect symmetry is what sets this piece apart. Down the glowing red axis, the fluid shapes resolve into a figure that can read as a butterfly, a mask or an inkblot, yet nothing is literally depicted. The color is highly saturated, and bright yellows and blues sit against dark reds and purples for strong contrast.

The light is even and luminous, with no single source and no cast shadows. Glossy highlights and translucent overlaps in the pale blues and lavenders give the surface a stained-glass glow. The calm, broad center is balanced by turbulent, finely patterned edges, and the eye keeps returning to the vertical axis.

This is an energetic, hypnotic image that rewards a long look. It suits a wall that needs a strong focal point, such as above a sofa or bed, over a console, or in an office or den where bold color and symmetry can hold the room.
