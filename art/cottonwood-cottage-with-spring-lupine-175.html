A Glowing Door at the End of a Curving Purple Meadow

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

This is "Glowing Door Cabin in a Wildflower Meadow," my landscape photograph of a small wooden cabin under tall sunlit trees.

You know the wall. It might be above the sofa, the bed or the desk. The furniture is where you want it and the paint is settled, but that wall has looked unfinished for months.

This photograph is for that wall. A small cabin sits under tall trees, and its wooden front door is lit by low, warm light. A band of purple wildflowers curves across the meadow toward it, so your eye has somewhere to go.

I can't tell you a made-up story about this cabin, so I'll tell you what the picture does. First you see the green: leafy and olive, layered in trees, shrubs and feathery grass. Then the purple-blue flower spikes catch you, curving through the middle of the scene.

Follow that curve from the lower left and it ends at the cabin. It's small, with a gray metal roof, a stone chimney and smooth wooden walls in warm reddish-brown. Trees stand on both sides of it, so it feels tucked away.

Then the light shows itself. It comes in low and gilds the tops of the trees and the tall trunks, and it makes the front door glow. The trunks pull your eye up into that lit canopy. A faint mountain shows between the trees at the left, and a pale blue sky sits behind it all.

The tones are slightly heightened, which deepens the greens and sets the purple against them. The scene itself is still. Nothing in it is hurried.

This is a horizontal landscape, so it suits the wide spaces: above a sofa, a bed, a console or a desk. The greens are the main color and the purple is an accent. That makes it easy to pair with wood, linen, stone and neutral paint.

It has the feeling of a quiet summer retreat. It suits a living room, bedroom, den or lodge-style space where you want a storybook scene rather than a grand vista. The layered trees and the upward pull of the trunks let it hold a medium-to-large wall comfortably.
