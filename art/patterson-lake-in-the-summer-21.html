Cobalt Hush, Blue Lake in a Green Valley

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A deep blue lake fills a curving valley between olive-green and golden grassy hills. On the right, dark evergreen-looking trees cover a steep slope. A narrow road winds along the near shore, and layers of forested ridges and lavender-blue mountains recede under a bright sky streaked with white cloud.

The water is a saturated cobalt against the olive and deep teal-green hills, and that is what holds the eye. A fine, swirled, brush-like texture covers the whole scene, so sky, slopes and water share one hand-painted, canvas-like grain. The scene is clearly recognizable, but its surface is stylized.

The eye enters at the road at lower left, crosses the lake and climbs the ridgelines to the horizon. Foreground hills frame a V-shaped valley, long horizontal clouds stretch across the sky, and short ripples give the water a gentle motion. The light is clear and sunny, with soft side light and few hard shadows. The mood is open, calm and fresh.

The wide horizontal format suits a wall that needs a sense of distance, such as above a sofa, a bed or a console, or in an office. The blue and green read cleanly against both light and dark walls.
