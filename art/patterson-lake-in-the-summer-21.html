Cobalt Hush, Blue Lake in a Green Valley

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A blue lake fills a curving valley between green and golden grassy hills. On the right, dark evergreen trees cover a steep slope, and a narrow road winds along the near shore. Layers of forested ridges and blue mountains recede behind the lake under a bright blue sky with streaks of white cloud. A swirling, brush-like texture covers the whole scene.
