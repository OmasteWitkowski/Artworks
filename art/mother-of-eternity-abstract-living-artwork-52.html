Ember Emblem - Symmetrical Orange and Navy Abstract

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A glowing center of orange, red and gold teardrop and leaf shapes sits inside a set of concentric blue-violet ovals. The design is mirrored left to right and top to bottom, so every angle repeats around a small diamond-like junction at the center. Pointed yellow forms anchor the top and bottom, and the red arms stretch out toward the side edges. A thin copper-orange border frames the whole piece.

The pull of this piece is the contrast between fire and cool. The warm core looks lit from within against a deep navy and violet field, and small turquoise accents flicker along the vertical axis. Crisp, notched, angular edges meet smoky, marbled swirls, so the strict symmetry never feels cold.

Look closely and the dark background is full of soft, drifting wisps that move slowly around the ovals. Meanwhile the central shapes radiate outward along the axes. The image has the ceremonial presence of an emblem or mandala, though it is simply a bold piece of color and pattern to enjoy.

Its vertical format and strong central focal point suit an entry, stairwell or narrow wall, or the space above a console or sofa. It holds its own against pale or neutral walls, and the dark blue ground also settles well into moody rooms.
