Ember Emblem - Symmetrical Orange and Navy Abstract

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A vertical abstract design in perfect mirror symmetry, with a glowing center of orange, red and gold shapes set against deep blue and violet. Oval rings surround the center, and smoky, marbled swirls fill the dark background. Angular notched forms and small flashes of turquoise add detail along the central axis. A thin orange-brown border frames the whole piece.

The story of this pieceA Fire Lit From Within, Held by Cool Blue OvalsA small fiery core, mirrored in every direction and wrapped in cool blue-violet ovals, makes Ember Emblem a bold piece you can read from across the room.
