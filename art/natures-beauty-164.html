Ember Meets Glow, Amber and Green Macro Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A close-up abstract of glowing green, yellow and amber surfaces that look translucent and glass-like. On the left, a rough, crackled orange-brown form with a fine white speckle contrasts with the smooth, glossy curves on the right. Thin pale lines sweep across the frame, and streaks of orange light run through the center. Dark brown patches at the lower right add depth to the bright color.

The story of this pieceRough as bark on one side, lit like glass on the otherRough, crusty orange-brown and liquid-looking green-gold meet in one frame, joined by fine curving lines of light.
