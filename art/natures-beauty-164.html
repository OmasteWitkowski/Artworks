Ember Meets Glow, Amber and Green Macro Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A close-up abstract of glowing green, yellow and amber surfaces that look translucent and glass-like. On the left, a rough, crackled orange-brown form carries a fine white speckle along its edge. On the right, smooth, glossy planes curve through lime and gold, crossed by thin raised lines. Dark brown patches at the lower right deepen the bright color.

The piece is distinctive for its contrast. Rough, bark-like texture sits directly beside liquid-looking, light-filled surfaces, and fine pale lines sweep across the frame to join them. The tight crop leaves no horizon and no single object, so the eye moves between texture, color and light.

Look closely and the surfaces seem lit from within. Warm orange streaks run diagonally through the center, and bright yellow highlights make the green-gold areas glow like jewels. The mood is warm and tactile, and it rewards slow looking. The color is attractive in its own right.

The horizontal format suits a wide wall above a sofa, console, bed or desk. Its amber, olive and burnt orange tones sit well in warm or earthy interiors, and the lime and teal give a fresh accent.
