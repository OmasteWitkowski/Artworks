Cobalt Summit, Posterized Snowy Mountain Peaks

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Snow-covered peaks rise in the center of this horizontal scene under a sky made of curved bands of cobalt and azure that lighten toward the horizon. Layered blue ridges and shadowed valleys step down to forested slopes and a pale, tan hillside in the foreground. Small flecks of white and pink are scattered across the slopes.

Heavy, slightly wobbly black outlines trace the rocks, snow patches and ridgelines, and the color is reduced to flat tonal steps. The result is a believable mountain view with a graphic, poster-like look. The sky bands repeat in a wide border of stepped blue and black, so the picture sits inside a layered frame of its own.

Look at how the diagonal ridges from lower left and lower right form a V-shaped valley that leads straight to the summit. The curved sky arcs pull the eye upward again. The bright, clear light on the peaks against the darker, muted foreground gives a crisp, cool feeling of open air and distance.

Its strong blues and clear central focus suit a living room, office, entry or den where a graphic landscape is wanted in place of a soft, realistic one. It pairs well with neutral walls, dark wood or other cool-toned decor.
