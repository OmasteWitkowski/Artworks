Hibiscus Abstract

Botanical & Flowers · square

A red hibiscus-like flower hangs upside down from a slender green stem, its crinkled petals glowing as if lit from within. A long thread-like stamen with tiny red tips trails below. The background is a dark, blurred mix of green leaves and golden-orange light spots.

A single glowing red blossom hangs like a small lantern against a dark, jewel-toned bokeh of green and amber, so the flower stands out vividly from its background.
