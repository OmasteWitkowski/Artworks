Cobalt Ember: Mirrored Abstract in Blue, Gold and Red

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A deep cobalt-blue oval sits at the center of this tall abstract, edged with dark red scrolls that curl like flames. Golden yellow and orange swirls fill the field around it. Teal and olive-green leaf-like shapes point toward the top and bottom edges, and small blue-and-white curls gather in the corners.

The blue center looks lit from within against the warm surround. There are no cast shadows, so the contrast comes entirely from saturated hues set against each other. The design mirrors itself exactly, left to right and top to bottom, which gives it a kaleidoscopic, ornamental order.

Look closer and the surface turns smooth and liquid. Marbled swirls and fine curling lines run through the blue and red areas, and small whorls sit inside the red ring. The curls spiral outward from the center and give the image a steady, pulsing rhythm.

In a portrait format, the centered symmetry makes this a natural single-piece focal point. It suits a living room, entry or bedroom wall that can carry bold color, and its blues and golds work with both neutral and richly colored rooms.
