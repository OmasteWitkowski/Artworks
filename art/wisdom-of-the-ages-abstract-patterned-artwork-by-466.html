Cobalt Ember: Mirrored Abstract in Blue, Gold and Red

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A tall abstract image with a glowing blue oval at its center, framed by dark red scrolling shapes that resemble flames or ornamental curls. Golden yellow and orange swirls fill the background, and green leaf-like forms point toward the top and bottom. The design mirrors itself left to right and top to bottom, like a kaleidoscope or mandala. The colors are saturated and the surface looks smooth and liquid-like.
