Ember Blooms, Yellow Wildflowers Against Dark Bark

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

Bright yellow-orange wildflowers with orange-red centers open toward the viewer in front of a dark, rough tree trunk. Green stems and broad, smooth leaves fill the lower edge, and more blooms fade softly into a grassy background. The petals radiate and curl in different directions, and the long stems sway between them.

The glow of the flowers is what stands out. The saturated petals look almost lit from within against the dark bark, which is painted in swirling, marbled patterns that look nearly liquid. Fine streaks mark the petals, and the leaves have a crisp, slightly glossy finish. The look is a heightened, painterly version of a wildflower scene rather than a literal record of it.

Look closely and the bark's swirls seem to echo the outward rhythm of the petals. The dark vertical trunk steadies the scene while the blooms rise and lean around it. The mood is cheerful, warm and energetic, the feeling of wildflowers in full bloom.

The horizontal format and strong warm color suit a living room, entryway, kitchen or sunroom. It works well on a neutral or dark wall, where the yellows and oranges can carry the room.
