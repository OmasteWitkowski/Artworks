Cobalt Tide, Glassy Blue Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A large, rounded mass of cobalt and royal blue sits at the center of this horizontal abstract. Glassy white and pale lavender highlights ripple through it, and small spiral whorls gather around its edge. Ribbons of red-orange, golden orange, emerald green and pale rose-beige curve around it against a deep navy ground.

The blue center looks like light on wet glass, and fine contour lines mark where each band of color meets the next. Warm reds and oranges fill the left and cooler greens and blues move across the right. An orange swirl at upper left and a beige lobe at lower right balance each other diagonally. The surface is smooth and fluid, with strong curving movement and high contrast.

The longer you look, the more small eddies and reflections appear along the ribbons. Some viewers may see a face or head in the blue center, while others see only liquid and light. Either reading works, and the colors carry the piece.

The horizontal format and dark navy edges suit a sofa, bed, console or desk wall. The saturated color makes it a clear focal point in a room.
