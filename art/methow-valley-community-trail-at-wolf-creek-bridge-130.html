Parked at the Pines, Footbridge Into the Forest

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A weathered wooden footbridge with plank decking and side railings runs straight ahead into a forest of tall pine trees. Patches of sunlight and shadow fall across the planks, and a bicycle leans against the left railing near the far end. Green needles, brown trunks and a small patch of blue sky fill the scene. The whole image has a smooth, swirling, painterly texture.

The story of this pieceA Bicycle Waits at the End of a Sunlit Bridge Into the PinesA sunlit plank bridge runs straight into the pines, and a lone bicycle leaning on the railing gives your eye somewhere to rest.
