Sunlit Emblem, Mirrored Abstract in Yellow and Green

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A symmetrical abstract in which marbled swirls and curling tendrils are mirrored left to right around a glowing yellow and orange center. Dark olive green and brown shapes spread in from the left and right edges. Teal, coral and cream accents appear throughout, and small dark curls sit in the corners. The surface looks smooth and fluid, with fine veined lines running through the colors.

The story of this pieceOne Glowing Center. Everything Else Mirrors It.A mirrored abstract where marbled swirls spiral out from a glowing yellow and orange heart, framed by dark olive and chocolate brown.
