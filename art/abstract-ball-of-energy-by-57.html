Abstract Ball of Energy Colorful Symmetrical Abstract Art

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A mirror-symmetrical abstract made of curling, marbled swirls in yellow, cream, brown, green and teal. A bright yellow and orange glow sits at the center, with dark green and brown forms spreading in from both sides. Small teal and dark maroon curls decorate the corners and edges.

A warm, glowing mirror-image abstract in which ornate marbled swirls of gold, teal and deep green unfurl symmetrically from a luminous yellow center.
