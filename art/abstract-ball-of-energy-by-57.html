Sunlit Emblem, Mirrored Abstract in Yellow and Green

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A glowing yellow and orange center sits at the heart of this mirrored abstract, with marbled swirls and curling tendrils spiraling outward to both sides. Dark olive green and chocolate brown shapes spread in from the left and right edges. Teal, coral and cream accents run through the curls, and small dark maroon and teal curls sit in the four corners.

The symmetry is exact. Everything balances around a central vertical axis, and a thin orange stripe at the center draws the eye straight to the light. The bright core and the dark side masses give strong contrast, so the glow reads like a light source fading into cream and pale gold.

Look longer and the repeated curls build a steady rhythm. Some viewers may see an ornate emblem or a creature-like form in the pattern, while others will simply enjoy the color and flow. The surfaces look smooth and liquid, with fine veining like poured paint.

The wide horizontal format and warm, earthy palette suit a living room, bedroom or entry. It works well centered above a sofa, bed or console, where the symmetry can anchor the wall.
