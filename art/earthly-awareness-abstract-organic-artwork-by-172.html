Amber Lantern, Mirrored Gold and Brown Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A bright cream vertical channel glows at the center of this horizontal abstract. Stacked pale ovals flank it, and large golden discs sit at the top and bottom of the axis. Rounded ovals, circles and pebble-like shapes outlined in thin black lines fill the amber, chocolate and burnt sienna field around it.

The design is mirrored left to right and top to bottom. A thin horizontal slit crosses the central channel, forming a cross or butterfly-like focal point. The glow fades from cream into darker brown edges, and the gold shapes look backlit, like stained glass. The shapes are smooth and glossy with soft gradients, set against a faintly embossed, crackled background.

The eye is pulled into the bright center, then travels outward along wavy, vine-like black lines toward the crowded edges. The strict symmetry keeps that movement steady and balanced, so the piece feels calm and ordered. Its main appeal is its warm, jewel-like color and clear focal point.

The warm palette and open, bright center suit a living room, bedroom, entry or office. It works well centered above a sofa, bed or console, and the dense edges give it presence from across the room.
