Berry Lanterns, Orange Berries Against Dark

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A drooping cluster of small round orange and yellow berries hangs from thin pinkish stems, with a few red-tinged berries mixed in. Green feathery leaves arch across the top of the frame. The background is dark, soft and blurred, with a fine mottled, painterly texture. The berries stand out as the brightest, warmest part of the image.
