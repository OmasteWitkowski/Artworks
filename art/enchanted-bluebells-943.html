Enchanted Bluebells

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A dark navy blue shape with pointed lobes fans out from a green-gray center, resembling a leaf or flower. It sits against a taupe and blue-gray background filled with swirling, marbled brushstroke patterns. A curved turquoise band crosses the lower right corner.

A deep navy fan-shaped form that seems to melt into a field of swirling, marbled, oil-like texture, brightened by a single turquoise sweep at the bottom.
