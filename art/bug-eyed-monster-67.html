Is it a mask? Look closer at this glowing swirl

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Glow Mask is a symmetrical liquid swirl abstract in orange, yellow, cobalt and emerald, mirrored exactly along its center line.

You have a wall that still looks bare. Maybe the sofa is new, or the old art looks small in the room. Whatever you hang there needs to hold its own, and most colorful abstracts don't.

This one holds its own. From across the room you see glowing orange and yellow against deep blue and green, and a yellow teardrop shape in the center. Then your eye settles, and the whole image seems to be looking at you.

I'm not going to tell you a story about where this came from. What I can tell you is what your eyes do with it, because that is the point of the piece.

The left and right sides are mirror images of each other, exactly. That is why it reads like a mask or a face: paired shapes sit in the upper corners, and a teardrop form sits in the middle above a green and turquoise band. It is a resemblance only. Nothing is depicted, and you are free to see something else.

Then look closer. Fine, thin lines run through every area like marbled liquid or oil on water. Pale highlights make the translucent bands look glossy and backlit, and the deep red and dark navy underneath give the glow something to push against.

The ribbons fold inward toward the center and sweep up and outward at the corners. The mirrored sides keep all that motion balanced, so it feels lively without feeling restless.

This is a bold, landscape-format piece with a strong center line, so it suits a focal wall: over a sofa, a long console or a desk. The color is saturated, so it works best in a room that can carry it, such as a living room, game room, studio or creative workspace.

It is a lively, playful and slightly hypnotic image. If your room is calm and neutral, this piece will be the energy in it.
