Jeweled Filigree Wings, Gold and Blue Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A single large butterfly sits at the center of a pale cream background, wings spread wide and seen from above. Its wings are packed with golden spirals, rings and white oval openings laid over rich cobalt blue, with touches of leaf green, burnt orange and blush pink. A thin dark red-brown outline defines the wing shapes, and the slender body and antennae run straight down the center.

What sets it apart is the surface. Each wing reads like lace, filigree or stained glass, with fluid blue and green swirls showing through the openings and a soft glassy gradient in the blue areas. Behind the butterfly, a faded mirrored scroll pattern frames the figure without competing with it, so the gold and blue stand out clearly.

Look closer and the spirals give each wing a gentle rotating rhythm, while the whole pose stays still and balanced, as if the butterfly is resting with its wings open. The exact mirror symmetry gives it a formal, ornamental, almost kaleidoscopic feel. It is a stylized butterfly, cheerful and celebratory, made to be enjoyed for its color and detail.

Its horizontal format and strong central focal point suit a wall above a sofa, bed or console, where the warm gold and deep blue can add a bright accent to a calm room.
