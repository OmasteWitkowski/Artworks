A Blue Void Ringed by Glowing Red and Orange Swirls

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Cobalt Core is a horizontal abstract in which a deep blue center is circled by luminous ribbons of red, orange, magenta and cyan.

The new sofa is in. The paint is settled. And the wall above it still looks like nobody has made up their mind about it.

Picture a large, deep blue shape on that wall, and around it ribbons of red and orange that look lit from behind. That is Cobalt Core. You may not need to read anything more to know whether it is yours.

I'll tell you what I see, because the image rewards a slow look. Start in the middle. The blue there is dense, dark and irregular in shape, and it sits very still.

Now move out toward the left and right. Orange and red swirls curl in toward that blue. Their edges are bright and translucent, and where two ribbons cross you get a glassy overlap in another color. Thin looping tendrils trail off the ends of the swirls, as if the motion hasn't quite stopped. Magenta, cyan, yellow, violet and teal appear in small accents. In the orange area at upper left you can make out a faint, fine grain.

Then there is the frame. A thin blue rectangle sits inside a softer, darker margin, so the picture seems to carry its own frame. It looks finished before it ever reaches a wall.

The colors look like dyed smoke or ink in water. That is only how it looks to me. What it means to you is yours to decide.

Cobalt Core is a horizontal piece with a strong center, so it works best where a wall is wide: over a sofa, a bed, a mantel, a console or a desk. In a room of neutral walls, the blue and orange become the dominant color, and the wall stops looking like an afterthought.

The mood is energetic and bold. The calm blue and the lively warm swirls pull against each other, and that contrast is what holds the image together.

Imagine coming home and seeing that deep blue center glowing above the sofa, with the red and orange curling in toward it. The wall would finally feel decided.
