Cobalt Core: Glowing Abstract Swirls

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A deep cobalt-blue form fills the center of this horizontal abstract. Ribbons of red, orange, yellow, magenta and cyan curl around it, and thin looping tendrils trail off the edges of the swirls.

The colors look translucent and backlit, like dyed smoke or ink in water. Bright edges glow against the dense blue, and glassy overlaps appear where the ribbons cross. A thin blue rectangular line sits inside a softer, darker margin, so the image reads as a frame within a frame.

Look at the center and the edges in turn. The blue is dense and still, while the warm swirls on the left and right move inward toward it. The contrast between calm and energy, and between cool and warm, is what holds the composition together.

The saturated palette and horizontal format suit a focal wall above a sofa, bed, console or desk. Against neutral walls the blue and orange become the dominant color in the room.
