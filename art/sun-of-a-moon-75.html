Cobalt Ring Lantern, Yellow Teardrop Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A thick, curling ring of deep cobalt blue sits at the center of this abstract, holding a glowing yellow teardrop like a flame. The ring rests on a pale yellow, cushion-like square with rounded edges. Around it, swirling bands of olive green, orange and rust brown bend toward the middle, and fine feathery tendrils curl outward.

What sets it apart is the contrast of light and texture. The yellow center and field look lit from within against the deep blue and fiery brown edges. Glossy white highlights give some curls a wet sheen. At the right, the rust-brown areas show a cracked, lace-like pattern, while ribbed bands run along the upper and left edges.

Look closer and small shapes float around the ring: a pale blue stick-like figure at the top right, star and crescent glints, and hook-shaped curls. They may read as creatures or symbols, but none is defined, so each viewer can find their own. The overall movement is a slow whirl pulling the eye to the yellow core.

The centered, horizontal composition makes this a ready focal point over a sofa, console, mantel or bed. The saturated cobalt and yellow suit a room that needs a bold, energetic color statement, and the rust and olive tones tie it comfortably to warmer palettes.
