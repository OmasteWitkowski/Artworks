A Yellow Flame Held Inside a Cobalt Ring

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

This is "Cobalt Ring Lantern, Yellow Teardrop Abstract", a horizontal abstract by me, Omaste Witkowski, with a glowing yellow teardrop at its center.

You know the wall. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and that one stretch of plaster still looks unfinished. You have walked past it for months.

Now picture a thick ring of deep cobalt blue on that wall, and inside it a yellow teardrop that looks lit from within. It sits on a soft yellow square, and a whirl of rust, orange and olive bends toward it. That is this piece.

The ring comes first. It is heavy and curling, cobalt and ultramarine with a little violet, and its edges are feathered like fine tendrils. Inside it the yellow teardrop reads like a flame or a lantern, which is why the title says lantern.

Then you see the pale yellow square under the ring. Its corners are rounded and it looks soft, almost like a cushion. The yellow here is smooth and glossy, like pools. Around it, bands of olive, orange and rust bend toward the middle, so the whole image feels like a slow whirl with the glow at its center.

Stay a little longer and the small things appear. There is a pale blue stick-like figure at the top right, a few star and crescent glints, and some hook-shaped curls. They might be creatures or symbols, but none of them is defined. I would rather leave that to you.

At the right, the rust-brown areas break into a cracked, lace-like pattern. Ribbed bands run along the top and left edges. Some curls carry glossy white highlights that give them a wet sheen.

The layout is horizontal and centered, with the rings arranged concentrically, so it sits in a balanced way over a sofa, console, mantel or bed. It could also hold an entry wall. The saturated cobalt and yellow carry across a room, so it works where you want one bold statement and not a quiet accent.

The rust, orange and olive in the border tie it to warmer palettes: wood, leather, terracotta, or walls in cream and neutral tones. The mood is energetic, vivid and a little dreamlike.
