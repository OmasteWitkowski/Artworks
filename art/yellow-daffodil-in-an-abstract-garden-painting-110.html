Ribbon Petals - Swirled Yellow Orange Double Flower

Botanical & Flowers · square

A single ruffled, double-petaled flower fills the square frame, seen close up and nearly front-on. Its golden-yellow and orange petals fan outward from a deep orange heart streaked with red. Cream and pale peach highlights catch the upper and left petals, and the bloom glows against a dark background of muted brown, olive and mauve.

What sets this piece apart is its surface. Every petal is covered in small spiral curls, so the flower looks made of curled ribbon or thick rolled paint. Behind it, larger overlapping swirl discs drift in loose, bubble-like clusters, like soft out-of-focus lights.

Look longer and the swirls give the petals a gentle, rolling motion that leads the eye toward the orange center. A translucent lighter rectangle sits inside a darker outer border, so the image reads as a framed picture within a picture. The bright flower pushes past those inner frame lines into the dark surround. The mood is warm, cheerful and jewel-like, from the contrast of bright bloom and dark ground.

On the wall, the square format and dark, low-key ground let the golden-orange flower act as a warm focal point. It suits a neutral or deep-toned room, alone or beside other dark-grounded florals.
