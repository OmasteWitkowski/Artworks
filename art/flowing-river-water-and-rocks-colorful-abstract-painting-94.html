Every Curl Is a Tiny Whirlpool, and the Yellow Ones Glow

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Whirlpool Lights is a horizontal abstract of small glossy swirls, with yellow whorls glowing in a field of cobalt, violet and olive.

The sofa is in. The paint is done. And the wall above it still looks like it's waiting for something. Maybe the old picture suddenly looks small in the room, or maybe you've simply been looking at that empty space for months.

Picture a wide piece up there, deep cobalt blue with clusters of mustard and lemon yellow that seem to glow from inside. Look closer and the whole surface is made of tiny spiraling curls, each one like a small whirlpool.

From across the room you see color first. Blue sweeps in diagonal bands from the upper left, and the yellow clusters gather across the middle and lower right, pulling your eye down and across. Near the top center, a few bright cyan flashes break up the cooler blues.

Step closer and the picture changes. Every curl turns out to spin on its own, smooth and glossy, like thick wet paint being twirled, with a soft highlight inside each one. The yellow whorls could be lamps, or blossoms, or lights seen through water. I'd rather you decide than tell you.

Then there's the frame. A thin pale gold-green line encloses the main image, and a wide, darker border of the same swirls surrounds it. The border is so much darker that the center looks lit from within.

The piece is listed as digital art. I'd rather you stand in front of it than read about it, so what I can promise is what's actually in the image: a lot to look at, and more of it the longer you stay.

The wide horizontal shape suits the places where a room asks for width: above a sofa or bed, over a console, or at the end of a hallway. The dense detail and strong color contrast work well at a larger size.

The mood is lively, dreamy and shimmering, a little like a night garden or a watery scene of lights. The darker border gives the piece a built-in frame, so it holds its own on a plain wall.
