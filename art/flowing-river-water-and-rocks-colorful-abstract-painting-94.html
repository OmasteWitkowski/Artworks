Whirlpool Lights - Swirling Yellow and Blue Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Thousands of small spiraling curls fill this horizontal abstract, each one like a tiny whirlpool or thumbprint. Larger yellow-green swirls gather in clusters against a ground of cobalt blue, violet and olive. Bands of lighter blue sweep diagonally across the surface, and bright cyan accents flash near the top center.

The curls are smooth and glossy, like thick wet paint being twirled, with soft highlights inside each one. The yellow whorls glow like lamps or blossoms in cool water. A thin pale gold-green line encloses the main image, and a wide, darker border built from the same swirls surrounds it, so the inner panel seems lit from within.

Look closely and every curl spins on its own. Step back and the larger movement appears: blue sweeping from upper left, yellow clusters drawing the eye to the lower right. The mood is lively, dreamy and shimmering, a little like a night garden or a watery scene of lights. The rhythm of repeated marks recalls the turbulent swirls of Van Gogh-style painting.

The wide horizontal shape and built-in dark border suit a sofa wall, a bedroom or an open entry, where the blue and yellow can carry a room's color scheme.
