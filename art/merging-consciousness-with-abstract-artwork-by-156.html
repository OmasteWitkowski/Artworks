Cobalt Totem, Mirrored Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

Glossy cobalt-blue lobes sit at the top and bottom of this vertical abstract, flanked by curving emerald-green shapes. A pale pink, cream and white diamond glows at the center. Swirling peach, orange and red scrollwork fills the borders and corners, with touches of golden yellow and lavender at the edges.

The composition is mirrored left to right and top to bottom around the central medallion. The blue forms stack along a calm vertical axis, and the ornament repeats outward in every direction. The surfaces look smooth and liquid, with bright highlights that make each shape seem rounded and polished, like enamel or glass.

Look closely at the marbled swirls. They curl outward from the center toward the edges, and the steady mirroring gives the whole image a rhythmic pulse. The blue areas carry a faint fine grain, and no brushstrokes are visible. Read as a whole, the image suggests an ornate mask or totem, though that is simply what the shapes can look like.

This piece suits a wall that needs one rich, vertical focal point, such as an entryway, a hallway, or the space above a console or bed. The saturated blue and orange work well in a bold or maximalist room, and the symmetry keeps the effect balanced rather than busy.
