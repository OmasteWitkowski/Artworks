Look closer: a cobalt totem in perfect mirror symmetry

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

Cobalt Totem is a vertical digital abstract, mirrored in every direction, with a glossy finish that looks like polished enamel or glass.

You know the wall. It might be the narrow one in the hallway, the stretch above a console, or the space over the bed. The furniture is settled and the paint is right, but that one place still looks unfinished, and you have been walking past it for months.

Now picture a tall piece there that you can't take in with one glance. Deep cobalt blue shapes stack down the center. Emerald green curves sit on either side of them. In the middle, a pale cream-and-pink diamond glows. This is Cobalt Totem.

The first thing you see is the order. The picture is mirrored left to right and top to bottom, so everything sits in balance around that central diamond. The cobalt lobes at the top and bottom line up along a calm vertical axis, and the eye settles there before it starts to roam.

Then the colour takes over. The blue is deep and saturated, the green is a rich emerald, and the burnt orange and peach scrollwork fills every border and corner. Blue against orange, with green between, is a strong pairing. Touches of red, golden yellow and lavender keep the edges from going flat.

Look closer and you'll see the surfaces. They are smooth and liquid, with marbled swirls curling outward from the center. Bright highlights make each shape look rounded and polished, like enamel or glass. The blue areas carry a faint fine grain, and there are no brushstrokes anywhere.

Step back and the shapes may start to read as a mask, or a carved totem. That's simply what the forms can look like to me, and you may see something else. I would rather you stand in front of it and decide.

This is a vertical piece with a strong central axis, so it works best as the single focal point on one wall. An entryway, a hallway, a narrow stretch of wall, or the space above a console or bed would all give it room to be seen.

The saturated blue, green and orange suit a bold or maximalist room, or a calmer room that wants one rich accent. The symmetry keeps the colour orderly, so it feels balanced rather than busy.
