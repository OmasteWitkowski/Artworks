Ember Ribbons, Coral Swirl Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ribbon-like lines curl and loop across a horizontal field of coral orange, dusty rose and deep magenta. Near the center, a pale cream-yellow glow brightens the surrounding warm tones. Outlined scrolls and spirals gather in the lower half, and muted mauve and sage-grey passages along the left edge cool the fiery palette.

The contrast makes this piece distinctive. The curling lines are smooth and fluid, while the upper right is built from short, angular dashes of orange brushwork. Deep magenta shapes drift through the glow like shadows around a light source.

The eye travels in curves around the bright center, following lines that enter from the left edge and spiral outward. The warm glow can recall a sunset or drifting smoke, though the piece is non-representational and stays open to your own reading.

The horizontal format and warm palette suit a wall above a sofa, bed or console, or a room that needs energy and color. The glowing center gives the eye a place to rest.
