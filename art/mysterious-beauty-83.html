A glow at the center, and one corner that won't sit still

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ember Ribbons is a horizontal abstract of magenta scrolls drifting through a cream-to-coral glow, offered as a print in the size you choose.

The sofa is in. The paint is settled. And there's still one wall that looks unfinished, the one your eye keeps landing on.

Picture a horizontal piece there, warm from edge to edge, with a pale glow near the middle and ribbon-like lines looping around it. That's Ember Ribbons.

At first you see color: coral orange, dusty rose and deep magenta, with a patch of warm cream-yellow near the center. It reads like light spreading outward into the surrounding colors. The darker magenta and burgundy passages around it read as shadow.

Then the lines pull you in. Outlined ribbons and scrolls curl in from the left edge and gather into spirals in the lower half. Your eye travels in curves around the bright center instead of stopping on one thing.

Then you notice the upper right. The smooth, looping lines give way to short, angular dashes of orange, so the surface looks brushed and layered. That contrast between fluid curves and choppy strokes is what I'd point to if you asked what makes this piece itself.

Some people see a sunset, others see drifting smoke. It's non-representational, so what you see is up to you. This piece is listed as digital art, a painterly abstract.

Ember Ribbons is a horizontal piece in warm coral, rose and magenta. That shape suits the long wall above a sofa, a bed, a console or a sideboard, where the glowing center can act as the focal point of the room.

The mood is warm, energetic and fluid. It suits a living room, bedroom or entryway that wants color and rhythm. It needs no story to justify it: color, rhythm and warmth are reason enough to want it.
