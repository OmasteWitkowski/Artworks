Taurus Ember Window, Orange Zodiac Glyph

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A tall white oval sits at the center of a rectangular frame of swirling orange, red and amber pattern. Inside the oval is the Taurus zodiac glyph, a circle crowned by a curved crescent like a pair of horns. A soft yellow-orange glow runs along the inside edge of the border, so the oval looks backlit.

The glyph is filled with the same flame-like swirls as the border, so it looks cut from the pattern and set against the clean white. Small rose-like spirals and curling forms repeat through both, on a smooth surface with no visible brushwork.

The pattern circles the edges while the oval and glyph stay still. That contrast of motion and calm is what you notice on a longer look. The mirrored layout keeps everything balanced, and the white space around the symbol gives it room to stand out.

The warm orange and bright white make a vertical piece with strong presence. It suits a wall where one bold image should anchor the space, and it is a natural fit for astrology fans and Taurus birthdays (April 20 to May 20).
