Budding Bend, Painterly River Landscape

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · vertical

A river bends gently away from the viewer, its pale blue-green water bordered by a bank of rounded grey and cream stones and dry brown grasses. Tall, thin trees lean in the foreground, some bare and some just showing small new leaves, while a dense row of yellow-green trees lines the far side. A blue mountain rises at the horizon under a sky of soft blue. Every surface has a swirling, painted look, giving the scene a rhythmic, textured feel.
