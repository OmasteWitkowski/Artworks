One White Oval Glowing in a Midnight Blue Field

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

Midnight Om Lotus Mandala is a digital artwork: a luminous white oval holding layered lotus petals and a single Om, on deep navy and indigo.

Picture the wall you keep walking past. Maybe it's above the bed, at the end of the entry, or over a console where nothing has felt right. The room is finished and the wall is still waiting.

Now picture one bright white oval on a deep navy field, glowing the way a lit window glows at dusk. That's Midnight Om Lotus Mandala. It is tall and symmetrical, and the dark ground makes the center stand out from across the room.

The white oval comes first. It sits dead center, with a soft magenta-violet halo along its edge, and nothing else in the picture competes with it.

Then the petals. Rows of pointed, interlocking lotus petals, outlined in white, fan outward from the center. They shade from navy into purple-red, so the whole image looks lit from within. The flat, crisp linework sits against a smoky, mottled background, and that contrast gives the piece its character.

Last, the small oval at the very center. It holds an Om with a crescent and dot above it. The curled tail of the Om is the only spiral in the image, one small curve in an otherwise still, balanced design.

The Om and the lotus are recognized symbols in Hindu and yogic tradition, and the composition is quiet enough to be read that way. But you don't need to know any of that to want it. The color and the glow are reason enough.

This is a vertical piece, and it works best alone. Give it a wall of its own, such as above a bed, in an entry, over a console, or in a meditation or yoga corner. The dark field and bright center give it presence without needing anything beside it.

The palette is deep blue, purple and white, so it sits easily with a restful, dim or moody room. It also gives a pale wall a strong dark anchor.
