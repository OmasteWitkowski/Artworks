Midnight Om Lotus Mandala

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A large white egg-shaped oval glows at the center of a deep navy and indigo field. Inside it, rows of pointed, interlocking lotus petals, outlined in white and shaded from navy to magenta-purple, ring a smaller white oval. That oval holds an Om symbol with a crescent and dot above it.

What sets this mandala apart is how it uses light. The white oval carries a soft magenta-violet halo, and the petals darken outward, so the image seems lit from within. The crisp, flat linework sits against a smoky, mottled background, and a mirrored magenta pattern runs along the top and bottom borders.

The composition is strictly symmetrical, and the petals fan outward from the center in a radial rhythm. The curled tail of the Om is the only spiral in an otherwise still, balanced image. The Om and the lotus are recognized symbols in Hindu and yogic tradition, so the piece invites a quiet, contemplative reading, though its color and glow are reason enough to enjoy it.

The vertical format and dark ground give it presence as a single focal piece. It suits a meditation or yoga corner, a bedroom, or an entry wall where a calm, high-contrast centerpiece is wanted.
