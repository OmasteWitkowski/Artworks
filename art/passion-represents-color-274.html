Passion Represents Color - Fiery Abstract Glass Art

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal abstract of swirling, marbled shapes in orange, yellow, burgundy and plum. Semi-transparent layers overlap, and dark shapes in the center and left could be read as eyes or a profile. The surface has a fine painterly texture, and thin white highlights trace the curves.

A glowing, fluid abstract in which a plum-to-amber color shift and layered, translucent swirls hint at faces that appear and dissolve as you look.
