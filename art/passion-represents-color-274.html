Ember Tide, Marbled Orange and Plum Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal abstract in swirling marbled shapes, with burnt orange and golden yellow on the right and deep plum and maroon on the left. Translucent, overlapping dark forms on the left and center could be read as faint faces or profiles. A bright cream and yellow glow runs through the middle, with thin white highlights along a few edges. A fine pebbled texture covers the whole surface and gives it a painted look.

The story of this pieceFaces in the Embers? Look Again at This Orange and Plum GlowA backlit-looking swirl of burnt orange, gold and deep plum, with shapes that might be faces if you let them.
