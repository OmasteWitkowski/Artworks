Ember Tide, Marbled Orange and Plum Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Burnt orange, golden yellow and deep plum-maroon swirl across this horizontal abstract in marbled, liquid-looking shapes. Crimson, dusty rose and cream run through the folds, with small touches of olive green. A bright cream and yellow glow crosses the middle, and thin white glints catch a few edges near the lower center.

The color shifts from cool plum on the left to hot orange on the right. Large translucent shapes overlap on the left and center, while the right opens into a calmer field of orange and gold ribbons. Light seems to come from behind the forms, like embers or molten glass. A fine pebbled grain over the whole surface gives the smooth, fluid shapes a painterly feel.

Look closely at the darker forms. A teardrop loop at upper left and a pointed, hooded shape at center can read as faces or profiles, as if two exposures were layered. Nothing is clearly drawn, so each viewer finds their own figures, or none at all. The piece can also be enjoyed purely for its heat, color and movement.

Its wide, full-bleed format suits a sofa wall, a bedroom, a dining room or an office. The warm right side lifts a space, and the deep plum on the left grounds it. It works well with neutral walls, dark wood or other warm tones.
