Ribbon Blaze - Glossy Swirls in Red, Gold and Blue

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Glossy ribbons and curls of color swirl across the whole surface, looking like liquid glass or thick molten paint. Saturated red, orange-gold, magenta-purple and cobalt blue are touched with emerald green, creamy white and soft lavender-pink. Loops, spirals and small teardrop shapes drift in every direction.

The swirls are smooth and reflective, with thin bright edges and translucent overlaps that make the colors look lit from within. Beneath them runs a fine, regular canvas-like grain across the entire image. Glassy shine over soft texture sets this piece apart from flatter colorful abstracts.

There is no single focal point. Curling tendrils sweep from the lower left toward the warm center and right, so the eye keeps circling. Heavy reds anchor the upper left and lower right, a glowing orange-yellow mass runs through the middle, and cooler blue, violet and green gather at the left and lower center. The mood is lively, playful and celebratory.

Its horizontal, full-bleed format suits a wide wall, such as above a sofa, bed or console, or an open room that needs a warm, high-energy piece. Creamy white passages give the color room to breathe.
