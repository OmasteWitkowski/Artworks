Enchanting Flames

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A horizontal abstract of swirling color in red, orange, yellow, purple and pink, with patches of blue, green and white. Glossy, transparent-looking loops, spirals and droplet shapes flow across the surface. A subtle canvas texture covers the whole image.

A glowing swirl of red, orange and violet, with transparent glass-like loops and droplets that seem lit from within, gives this abstract the depth of a stained-glass window in motion.
