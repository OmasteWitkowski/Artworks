Ribbon Blaze - Glossy Swirls in Red, Gold and Blue

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal abstract made of glossy, swirling ribbons of color that look like liquid glass. Rich reds sit at the upper left and lower right, a glowing orange-yellow mass runs through the middle, and purple, magenta, blue and a patch of green fill the rest. Curls, spirals and small teardrop shapes drift across the surface, which has a fine canvas-like grain. The overall effect is bright, fluid and full of motion.
