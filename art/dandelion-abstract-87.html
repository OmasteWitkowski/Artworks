Quiet Starburst, Glowing Seed Head on Black

Botanical & Flowers · square

A single round seed head sits at the center of a near-black square. Long, slender, pointed filaments fan outward from a pale green, woolly center, and a thin stem is faintly visible at the bottom. The strands run from soft white to pale grey-lavender, with faint periwinkle shadows between them.

What sets it apart is the light. The filaments glow as if lit from within, the brightest whites sit on the outer tips and the longest crossing strands, and the edges blur into soft halos. The look is part botanical close-up and part light drawing, with strands that seem both silky and sharp.

Look longer and the strands curve and cross near the middle in a loose X, which gives the burst a slow swirl. The mood comes from contrast alone: quiet, delicate and still, with a little energy moving outward.

The wide dark margin and square format make it a calm focal piece. It suits a wall where you want one luminous image to stand out against a quiet room.
