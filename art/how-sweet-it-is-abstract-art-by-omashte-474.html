How Sweet It Is Abstract Art by Omashte

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Four large red curving shapes with thick black outlines are mirrored across a navy blue background. Swirled green, orange and brown panels sit inside the curves, and a glowing orange diamond shape marks the center. An orange border frames the piece, which looks like stained glass.

Bold red heart-like forms, outlined in black like stained-glass leading, mirror one another around a glowing orange center, with marbled green and amber panels set into a deep navy field.
