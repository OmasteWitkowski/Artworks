Reflecting Wilderness and Rocky Shorelines Landscape Artwork by

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A shallow creek runs through a forest, its bed covered in smooth gray, white and rust-coloured stones. Still water reflects green trees and the reddish-brown bottom. Evergreen boughs and dark tree trunks frame the right side, and leafy green woods rise behind the creek.

A low, close view of a calm forest creek, where pale river stones rest in water that mirrors green woods and warm rust tones, makes a quiet, immersive scene.
