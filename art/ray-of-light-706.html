Ray of Light

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A tall abstract image with shimmering gold and amber vertical streaks running down the centre like a glowing curtain. Around them, deep blue and purple marbled swirls curl into repeating, mirrored shapes. The whole image is symmetrical left to right, and the gold looks lit from behind.

Mirrored streaks of molten gold glow like a lit curtain against deep indigo and violet swirls, giving a bold, symmetrical abstract with rich colour contrast.
