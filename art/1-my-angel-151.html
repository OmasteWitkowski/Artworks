Ember Mirror, Symmetrical Orange and Blue Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A blazing center of orange, yellow and red sits against deep cobalt blue. Red veins run through it like rising heat, and cream highlights make the shapes look backlit and translucent. On either side, pale blue, green and khaki forms face inward like profiles, and spiral curls close each corner.

The whole image mirrors itself exactly along a central vertical axis. That symmetry lets the glowing center read as wings, a butterfly-like figure or a flame, and the side forms as faces, though none of these is fixed. The surfaces are smooth and glossy, with thin contour lines and layered translucent bands that give a silky, flowing-ink look.

Look slowly and the curves seem to pour outward and downward from the center, so the image keeps a steady pulse. The reading is yours to make: a creature, a flame, a ceremonial figure or just a play of warm light on cool blue. The energy is warm and dramatic, with a touch of the mystical that comes from the symmetry and the glow.

The horizontal, centered format suits a wall that needs one strong focal point, such as above a sofa, bed or console. The cobalt field keeps the warm center from overwhelming a room, and the symmetry sits comfortably in formal or eclectic spaces.
