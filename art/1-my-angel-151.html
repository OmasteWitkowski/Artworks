A Fiery Center That Could Be Wings, Flame or Faces

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ember Mirror is a mirrored digital abstract: a glowing orange, yellow and red center on deep cobalt blue, and what you see in it is up to you.

You know the wall. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and that one space above it still looks unfinished. Whatever you hang there has to be big enough and strong enough to hold the room, and most of what you find looks like everything else.

This is Ember Mirror. A blazing center of orange, yellow and red sits against deep cobalt blue, and the whole image mirrors itself exactly down the middle. Your eye goes straight to the glow.

The first thing you see is heat. The center is luminous yellow and orange, with red veins running through it like rising warmth. Pale cream highlights make it look backlit, as if light is passing through the shapes.

Because the image is a perfect mirror, the center will not settle. Some people see wings or a butterfly-like figure. Others see a flame. On either side, pale blue, green and khaki shapes face inward, and they can read as two profiles looking at the glow. None of these readings is fixed, and I like that. Your response is the only test that counts.

Stay a little longer and the curves seem to pour outward and downward from the center. Spiral curls close each of the four corners, and together they give the image a steady pulse. The surfaces are smooth and glossy, with thin contour lines and layered translucent bands, like silky marbled ink.

The format is horizontal, centered and symmetrical, so it sits naturally above a sofa, a bed, a console or a mantel. The symmetry follows the room's own center line. The warm-on-cool contrast gives that wall one clear focal point.

The mood is warm and dramatic, with a touch of the ceremonial from the glow and the mirroring. The cobalt field keeps it from taking over, so it can sit in a formal room or an eclectic one.

The sizes on offer are shown on the listing. Measure your wall, then mark the space with painter's tape to see the footprint you want. The image is horizontal and centered, so it works best where it has room to be the single focal point. If it doesn't suit the wall, Fine Art America's 30-day money-back guarantee applies.

Screens vary, so I can't promise an exact match. The cobalt blue, the orange and yellow center and the cream highlights are the heart of the image, and the paper, canvas, metal or frame you choose can change how it looks in your room. That is one reason the 30-day money-back guarantee matters.

There isn't a right way. It can be wings, a flame, two faces or just warm light on cool blue, and each of those readings is yours to make. If the image stops you, that is reason enough to want it on your wall.

Picture it centered above your sofa, with the orange glow echoing the room and the cobalt blue holding the wall. The curves pour outward and down, and the corners keep the pulse going.
