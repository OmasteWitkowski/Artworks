Van Gogh Orange Iris

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A close view of an orange iris with ruffled, veined petals, in front of a blurred background of green, yellow and gray-blue. The surface has a textured, painterly finish. A dark red band at the bottom carries a quotation attributed to Vincent Van Gogh about painting despite self-doubt.

A ruffled orange iris glows against a soft green and gold background, and its oil-paint texture makes the flower look painted rather than photographed.
