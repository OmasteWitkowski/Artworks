Emberwing, Stained Glass Style Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A stylized butterfly flies to the right in profile against a clean white background. Its large wing is filled with glowing orange and yellow, swirled like marbled glass, and edged in deep crimson that ends in jagged, flame-like points. A bold black-and-white eye shape sits at the center of the wing. The body is small, with slender black legs and two long, swept-back antennae that curl at the tips.

The eye mark is the focal point. It sits in the luminous marbled fill, and the flat, dark red edge makes that fill look lit from behind. Thin white lines divide the wing into segments, which gives the butterfly a cut-out, leaded-glass look. Hot color, graphic outlines and fluid texture are combined in one figure.

Look closely and the orange and yellow areas show soft internal ripples and olive-gold shadowed patches, while the red stays smooth and flat. The diagonal of the wing, the backswept red points and the long antennae give the butterfly a forward, lifting motion. The black-and-white shape can be read as an eye, a target or a bold graphic mark.

The horizontal format and generous white space let this butterfly work as a single bold accent. It suits a wall that needs a warm focal point, such as a living room, studio, entryway or reading corner, and it pairs well with neutral walls or dark frames.
