Spiral Sun Bloom, Orange Flower on Black

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A round, ruffled orange and yellow flower fills the middle of a dark background. Its surface is made of many small spirals and rounded dabs, like swirled paint, with lighter yellow spots on top and deeper orange in the folds. Curly tendrils trim the outer edge. A thin black mat and a wide, swirl-textured brown border frame the flower.

The story of this pieceA Flower Built From Hundreds of Tiny Spirals, Glowing Like a SunOne round, ruffled orange flower glows against near-black, and every petal is made of tiny spirals.
