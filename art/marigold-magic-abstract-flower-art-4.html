Spiral Sun Bloom, Orange Flower on Black

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A single round, ruffled flower head in saturated orange and golden yellow fills the center of a near-black ground. Its petals are made of hundreds of small spirals and rounded dabs, with pale cream-yellow highlights across the top and right and deeper amber and red-orange settling into the folds. Curly tendrils trim the edge of the round shape.

What sets this piece apart is the swirl. Instead of separate petals, the bloom is one glowing orb of repeating spirals, closer to a whimsical sun or pom-pom than a botanical study. The tendrils flick outward like small flourishes and give the circle a lively edge.

Look closely and the spirals carry a gentle rotating energy across the whole flower, while the dark background pushes the glow forward. The wide brown border carries its own faint swirl pattern. The mood is warm, cheerful and playful, with bold contrast.

A thin black inner mat and a wide brown swirl-textured border give it the look of a matted print. It works as a focal point on a wall that needs a burst of warm color, especially near dark furniture or deep wall tones.
