Cobalt Echo - Concentric Water Ripples

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · panoramic horizontal

Bright cobalt and azure rings spread across the water in a wide oval, each crest traced with a thin bright line and each trough nearly black-blue. A dim brown-grey reflection fills the center of the rings and a band across the top of the image.

The saturated blue against the earthy brown is the center of this piece. A fine, swirling texture of tiny curled strokes covers the water and makes it look painted. The rings run off the left, right and bottom edges, so the pattern feels larger than the frame.

Look closely and the rings do not sit still. They expand from an off-center point and stretch into a long ellipse, while horizontal wobbling streaks in the upper band suggest a gentle stir on the surface. Olive and khaki flecks add small quiet notes to the blue.

The repeating rhythm is calm and a little hypnotic. The pull comes from color contrast and pattern, and you can enjoy it simply as something striking to look at.

Its panoramic shape suits a long wall above a sofa, bed, console or desk. The vivid blue gives a neutral or earth-toned room a strong color anchor.
