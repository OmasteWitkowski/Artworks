Electric Blue Rings Against Earth Brown: Look Closer

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · panoramic horizontal

Cobalt Echo is a panoramic piece of concentric water ripples in saturated blue, with a dim brown reflection and a painterly, brush-like texture.

You know the wall. It's the long one over the sofa, or the bed, or the console. The furniture is settled and the paint is done, and that stretch of wall still looks unfinished.

Now picture bright cobalt rings spreading across it, each crest traced with a thin bright line, each trough nearly black-blue, with a dim brown reflection sitting behind them. That is Cobalt Echo.

The first thing you see is the blue. It is vivid and saturated, not the soft pale blue so many water pictures use. Against it sits a muted brown-grey reflection, filling a band across the top and the center of the rings. The two colors pull against each other, and that tension is the heart of the piece.

Then the surface starts to show. Tiny curled strokes cover the water, most of all in the blue, so it reads as painted. It sits somewhere between a water scene and a graphic pattern, and I leave it to you to decide which it is.

Look closer and the rings don't sit still. They spread from an off-center point and stretch into a long ellipse. They run off the left, right and bottom edges, so the pattern feels bigger than the frame. In the brown band above, horizontal wobbling streaks hint at a gentle stir on the surface. Olive and khaki flecks add a few quiet notes in the blue.

The shape is panoramic and horizontal, with a brown band across the top third and the blue ellipse filling the lower two-thirds. That makes it a natural fit for a long wall above a sofa, bed, console or desk, where a tall piece would feel cramped.

The mood is quiet and contemplative, and the pull comes from color and pattern. You can enjoy it simply as something striking to look at.
