Sky Below, Golden Stone Well from Above

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

Looking straight down into a round stone well, rough golden blocks curve inward and downward in a slow spiral. At the bottom, a disc of still water holds a blue-grey sky and soft clouds. Warm light lights the chipped upper rim in ochre, amber and burnt orange, while the shaft darkens to olive-brown and shadow.

The sky is at the bottom of the picture. The warm stone ring and the cool blue water create a strong contrast, so the eye drops through the shaft to the brightest point. A fallen leaf at the water's edge, a thin blade of grass at upper left and a small dark peg on the right add small details to find.

The curved stone courses and the funnel of the shaft pull the gaze inward, and the rippled, painterly texture across the surface adds a gentle turning motion. The mood is quiet and still. The small patch of sky below suggests depth without needing any story.

The warm palette and centered circular focus suit a living room, entryway, study or bedroom. It can anchor a wall over a sofa or console and sits well with natural wood, stone, leather and cream or earth-toned walls.
