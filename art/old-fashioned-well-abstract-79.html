The Sky Is at the Bottom of This Golden Stone Well

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

"Sky Below" is a painterly digital image looking straight down a round stone well, past glowing gold stone to a small disc of blue sky in the water.

You know the wall. It's the one over the sofa that has looked a little bare since the room was finished. Nothing you've found has been wrong exactly, but nothing has felt like yours either.

Now picture a wide, warm picture there. Gold and amber stone makes a ring, and the ring leads down into shadow. At the very bottom is a small, bright circle of blue sky, and it is below you, not above.

The warm stone on the rim catches your eye first. It is ochre, amber and burnt orange, rough-cut and chipped pale along the edges. The curved courses of stone then lead you inward and downward in a slow spiral.

The shaft darkens to olive-brown and shadow in the middle. Then you reach the water. It is the coolest and brightest part of the picture, and it holds a blue-grey sky and soft clouds. The sky is at the bottom of the picture, and that reversal is why people look twice.

Then there are small things to find. A fallen leaf rests at the left edge of the water. A thin blade of grass hangs near the upper left. A small dark peg sticks out on the right. A rippled, brush-like texture runs across the whole surface, so the view feels slowly turning rather than fixed.

I'm not going to tell you what it means. It's a quiet picture, and what you feel in front of it is yours.
