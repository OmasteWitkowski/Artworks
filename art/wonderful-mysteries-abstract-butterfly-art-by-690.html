Cobalt Jewel Wings, Blue Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A large blue butterfly with open wings is centered on a plain white background. The inner parts of the wings are rich cobalt blue, divided into rounded cells by pale pinkish veins. The outer edges are blush pink and beige, scattered with purple and indigo oval spots and a few olive-green accents near the top. The body is slim and teal-blue, with two thin curved antennae.
