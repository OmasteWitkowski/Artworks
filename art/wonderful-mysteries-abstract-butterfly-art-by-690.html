Wonderful Mysteries - Blue Butterfly Art

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A blue butterfly with open wings is centered on a plain white background. The inner wings are bright cobalt blue, divided into rounded cells by pale pink veins. The outer wing edges are blush-toned with scattered purple spots, and a dark teal body with slender curved antennae sits at the center.

A perfectly symmetrical cobalt-blue butterfly with glossy, stained-glass-like wing cells and violet-spotted blush edges stands out crisply against a clean white ground.
