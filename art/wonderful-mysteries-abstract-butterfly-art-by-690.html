Cobalt Jewel Wings, Blue Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A single butterfly spreads its wings flat, seen from directly above and centered on a clean white background. The inner wings are rich cobalt blue, divided into rounded cells by pale pink veins. The outer borders are blush pink and beige, scattered with purple and indigo oval spots and a few touches of olive green. A slim teal body and two thin curved antennae finish the figure.

What sets this butterfly apart is its jewel-like contrast. The saturated blue panels have a soft inner glow and a mottled, marbled look, like cloudy glass or stone. The pale borders are smooth with a faint pearly sheen. Strict left-right symmetry and plenty of white space keep the image bold but uncluttered.

Look closely and the pale veins branch outward from the dark shading near the body, leading the eye through the wings in a gentle radiating rhythm. The repeating oval spots add a quiet dotted pattern along the edges. The mood is light, cheerful and calm, with the simple charm of a perfectly balanced form.

The horizontal format and bright white ground suit a living room, bedroom, entry or workspace. It works well above a console or bed, or as a colorful accent in a white or neutral room.
