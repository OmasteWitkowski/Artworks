Cobalt Crown: Frilled Blue Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A large cobalt blue form fills the left and center of this horizontal abstract. Its edge ripples into fine frills and sharp serrated points. Inside it sits a paler, crumpled-looking center in light blues and white. A curved band of cream, pale yellow and olive green wraps the right side and bottom, and the corners fall into darker, quieter tones.

What sets this piece apart is the contrast between the saturated, glowing blue and the finely cut rim. The color is intense, yet the edges stay crisp rather than soft. Some viewers see a glowing eye or a blue flower seen from above, though the piece is purely abstract.

Look closely and the movement shows. The forms curl inward toward the center and ripple outward in feathery lines, and long vertical streaks drag down the right side. A fine, even grain runs across the whole surface, and the pale areas read like light passing through glass. The eye keeps traveling from the blue rim to the bright center and back.

The cool, vivid blue gives a room one clear focal point, and the cream and yellow keep it from feeling cold. It suits a living room, studio, office or entry wall where a confident horizontal piece is wanted, and it pairs well with neutral walls and warm woods.
