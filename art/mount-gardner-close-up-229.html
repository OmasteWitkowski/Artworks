Cobalt Summit, Snowy Peaks and Evergreen Slopes

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A snow-covered mountain range rises under a deep, cloudless blue sky, its jagged central peak sitting just left of center. In front of it, ridges of dark evergreen forest and dry tan hillsides overlap in layers, and a sunlit grassy slope fills the lower right.

The contrast is the draw: brilliant white snow against an intensely saturated cobalt sky, set off by dark slopes on both sides. A fine painterly texture covers the whole surface, smoothest in the sky and dense and stippled across the trees, so the scene reads as painted as well as photographic.

Look at the diagonal ridgelines and you can follow them down from the summit to either side. The folded valleys step back in layers and give the wide view real depth. The mood is calm and crisp, with a sense of cold, clear air and open space.

The horizontal format suits a wall that needs width, such as above a sofa, bed or console, or across from a desk. The blue and white carry well across a room, and the green and tan keep the palette grounded.
