Crimson Emblem - Mirrored Red and White Abstract

A symmetrical abstract in glossy red, white and silvery grey, with fluid forms that look like melted glass or liquid metal. The left and right halves mirror each other, creating a central emblem or mask-like shape with two pale ovals around a small red diamond. Thin white lines trace the swirls and form a rounded frame near the edges. Deep crimson and near-black folds contrast sharply with the bright white highlights.

A collector wants this piece for its strict mirror symmetry in glossy red and white. The fluid, liquid-looking forms are organized into a ceremonial central figure: two pale ovals around a small red diamond, under a crown of red curves. Many red abstracts are loose splashes or swirls. This one is ordered, ornate and heraldic, and it still looks molten. Pure visual drama is reason enough to own it.
