Red Heart Of Flowers Fantasy Designs Abstract Holiday Art by Oma

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

A deep red splatter of paint with ragged edges and scattered droplets sits against a white background. Across its center, about three dozen flat white flowers with 5, 6, 8 and pointed petals form a dense cluster. Behind the flowers, the red has a glossy, marbled look with symmetrical amber and gold shapes.

Bright white flower cut-outs float on a glowing, mirrored red splatter, setting graphic floral shapes against raw, energetic paint edges.
