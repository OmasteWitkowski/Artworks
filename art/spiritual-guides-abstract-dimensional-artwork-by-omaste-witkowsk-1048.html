Spiritual Guides Abstract Dimensional Artwork by Omaste Witkowsk

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A wide, symmetrical pattern of pink and lavender curving shapes outlined in green and red, set on a dark band and framed by scalloped pink borders over a teal background. The motifs repeat across the width and mirror top to bottom, with a soft, brushed, painterly texture.

A bold, mirror-symmetrical band of pink, green and teal crescent motifs with a painterly surface and a rhythmic, ornamental repeat that reads as vivid pattern art.
