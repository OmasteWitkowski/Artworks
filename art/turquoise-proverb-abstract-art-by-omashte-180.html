The Doer's Window - Turquoise Chinese Proverb

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

A glowing turquoise oval stands at the center of this square piece, filled with mottled aqua and pale mint color. Dark italic script inside it reads a Chinese proverb: "The person who says it cannot be done should not interrupt the person who is doing it." Below the quote sit a small chocolate-brown flower and a curling tendril. Mirrored aqua teardrops in dark curved frames surround the oval on a deep blue ground.

The oval reads like a lit window or a jewel. A soft blue glow lifts it from the darker surround, and the mirrored shapes around it are in strict balance, both left to right and top to bottom. Bold dark outlines and flat jewel tones give the piece a stained-glass look, while the brushy, daubed texture keeps it warm and hand-made in feel.

Look closely and the color inside the oval shifts between turquoise, mint and cream, like light moving through water or sky. The proverb is about getting on with the work and not stopping to answer doubters. The cool blues and the still, balanced layout give that message a calm, determined tone rather than a loud one.

The centered, symmetrical design suits a wall where you want one clear focal point: above a desk, in a studio or office, along a hallway, or in a quiet corner where the words can be read up close. The turquoise and cobalt pair well with neutral walls and natural wood.
