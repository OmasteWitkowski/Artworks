Sundown Sprinkler, Painterly Mountain Pasture

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A pasture of green and golden grass in the foreground leads back to a line of fencing, a small reddish shed and a sprinkler throwing a thin arc of water. Behind it, forested hills and layered blue mountains rise under a sky of orange, pink and violet clouds. The sun glows near the horizon between the peaks. The whole scene has a soft, swirled, painterly texture.

The story of this pieceThe One Sprinkler Jet Glowing Under a Violet SunsetA warm orange-and-violet sky, cool blue mountains and a single sprinkler jet catching the last light in a quiet pasture.
