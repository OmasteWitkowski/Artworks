Sundown Sprinkler, Painterly Mountain Pasture

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A green pasture runs back to a split-rail fence, a small reddish barn or shed and a sprinkler throwing a thin arc of water. Behind it, forested blue hills and layered mountain ridges rise under long clouds of orange, peach, pink and violet, with the low sun glowing between the peaks.

The warm sky over cool blue mountains is the first thing you notice. The sprinkler jet catching the light is the detail that stays with you, a small, ordinary rural moment in a large landscape. Dark evergreens at the right and a clump of trees at the left frame the view, and the glow sits just left of center.

Small swirling, brush-like strokes cover the sky, hills and trees and soften fine detail into an oil-paint-like surface. The foreground grasses and shrubs keep a little more detail, so the eye moves from a crisp meadow into a dreamier distance.

Long clouds run diagonally toward the horizon and the mountain ridges step back in overlapping bands. The scene feels quiet and unhurried, the end of a day in the countryside with the warm light fading over the valley.

The horizontal format and low horizon suit a wide wall, such as over a sofa, a bed, a console or a desk. The blues and violets sit well in cool rooms, and the orange glow adds warmth to neutral ones.
