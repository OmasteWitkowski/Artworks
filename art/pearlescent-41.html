Golden Daybreak, Mirrored Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A glowing yellow disc with a delicate starburst at its center sits in the middle of a mirror-symmetrical design. Wavy bands of gold spread out to both sides, under a sky-like area of pale blue and white. Cobalt blue flame-like shapes curl across the top, while green, orange and brown swirls fill the bottom and sides.

The yellow disc is the lightest and warmest point, and it makes the whole image seem lit from within. The left and right halves mirror each other exactly, so the eye returns to the center again and again. The darker reds, browns and deep blues at the edges frame that bright middle.

Look closer and the surface reads as glossy and liquid. Translucent layers overlap, and fine contour lines run through the colors like ripples or topographic lines. The movement is strong and rippling, flaring outward and upward from the center.

The mood is warm, energetic and radiant, a brightness that comes straight from the color and the composition.

In a horizontal format with a bright center, it works as a focal point above a sofa, bed or console, and it brings warm yellow and deep blue into the room.
