A Glowing Yellow Sun at the Center of a Perfectly Mirrored Wave

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Golden Daybreak is a mirror-symmetrical digital abstract: a starburst disc of gold held inside flame-like ribbons of cobalt blue, leaf green and burnt orange.

The sofa is in. The paint is done. And the wall above it still looks like nobody lives there yet. Whatever is up there now looks small, or looks like something you nearly bought and saw in a friend's house.

Picture a wide, horizontal piece with one bright point in the middle: a golden disc with a fine starburst at its center. Your eye lands there first, then travels out along the wavy bands of yellow into deep blue, green and orange. That is Golden Daybreak.

The disc is the first thing you see. It is the lightest and warmest spot in the picture, and everything around it seems lit from that middle. Above it, pale blue and white read as open sky. Wavy gold bands spread out to both sides, like light on water or a horizon.

Then you notice that the left and right halves mirror each other exactly. That is why the eye keeps coming back to the center. The edges are darker, in reds, browns and deep blues, so they frame the bright middle the way a doorway frames a lit room.

Look closer and the surface turns glossy and liquid. Translucent layers overlap, and fine contour lines run through the colors like ripples or topographic lines. Cobalt blue shapes curl across the top like flames, and green ones flare up from the bottom.

I can tell you what is in the image. What it means to you is yours to decide. It looks like a sun rising to me, and you may see something else.

The piece is wide, with its bright center sitting just above the middle. That suits a centered spot over a sofa, a bed, a console or a fireplace, where the glowing middle pulls the eye and the darker edges hold it in place.

Its mood is warm, energetic and radiant, and that comes from the color and the composition alone. If your room is calm or neutral, the yellow and deep blue give it one confident point of color.

Screens vary, so I can't promise an exact match. The piece is built on saturated golden yellow, cobalt and royal blue, burnt orange and leaf green, with darker reds and browns at the edges. If it isn't right when it arrives, the 30-day money-back guarantee applies.

I believe there is no good or bad art, and that your own response is the only test. If the glowing center and the mirrored swirls make you want to look longer, that is reason enough. If they don't, it isn't your piece, and that's fine too.

Imagine coming home and seeing that golden disc glowing in the middle of the wall above the sofa, with the blue and orange swirling out on both sides. Imagine a guest asking where you found it.
