Self Portrait

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A large rounded opening of solid amber-brown sits left of center, encircled by translucent, rippled layers in orange, cream and pink. Curved dark and pale bands arc across the top, and a small angular, crystal-like fragment catches light on the right. The edges melt into soft blur and deeper shadow.

A warm amber void is ringed by glowing, ridged translucent layers, so the piece reads like a hidden world seen through light-filled glass.
