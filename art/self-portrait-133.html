Amber Eye - Glowing Macro Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A large, smooth, amber-brown oval opening sits off-center, ringed by translucent, glassy bands in orange, peach and cream. Across the top, dark curved strokes and finely rippled, glittering surfaces sweep from left to right. At the right, a jagged, crystal-like fragment with a frayed tip leans into the frame. Warm light seems to shine through the layers, leaving bright glints and dark shadowed edges.

The story of this pieceAn Amber Eye That Seems to Glow From BehindA smooth amber void glows like a lens, framed by glassy folds and one frayed crystalline shard.
