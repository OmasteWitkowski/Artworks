Finding My Way Abstract Butterfly Art by Omaste Witkowski

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · vertical

A bright orange butterfly with yellow outlines and eye-spot circles on its wings is centered in a vertical blue oval. Above its antennae floats a round orange disc, and the dark purple-blue background has a symmetrical, marbled swirl pattern. The oval sits on a plain white field.

A glowing orange and yellow butterfly with eye-spotted wings sits under an orange sun-like disc, set in a deep indigo marbled oval that makes the colors look lit from within.
