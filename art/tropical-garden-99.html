Tropical Garden

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

A large insect sculpture with long, glassy wings and thin jointed legs stands on a dirt path in a tropical garden. Palm trees, a leafy tree canopy and clusters of red flowers surround it, with green hills and a bright blue sky with clouds behind. The whole scene has a swirling, painted look with intense color.

A towering metallic insect sculpture stands in a saturated tropical garden, and the swirling, oil-paint treatment makes the greens, blues and red blossoms glow.
