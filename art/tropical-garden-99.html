The Garden Visitor, Giant Insect Sculpture Among Palms

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A large silvery-blue insect sculpture stands on a dirt path in a sunny garden. Palm trees and leafy trees frame it, and bushes of red flowers fill the corners. A wooden fence and green hills sit behind it under a bright blue sky with white clouds. Every surface has a swirling, painterly texture and very saturated color.
