A White Glow in Dark Rings That Looks Back at You

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Lantern Eye is an abstract in deep navy, violet and glowing white that reads as an eye or a whirlpool and settles on neither.

You have a wide wall. Maybe it is above the sofa, the bed or a console. The room around it is finished, and that one stretch of wall still feels like something is missing.

Picture a bright white glow in the middle of it, wrapped in dark navy and violet rings. It gives the eye a place to land, and it gives the wall some weight.

The first thing you see is the white teardrop at the center, soft and almost frosty. Dark concentric rings circle it, and they pull your eye inward. Then a dark streak reaches out toward the right and sends your eye back out.

On the right, the surface breaks into clusters of tiny bubbles and beaded highlights, as if the whole field is drifting. On the left, smooth glassy swirls of pale blue and white curve around the center.

The edges of the curves take longer to show themselves. Pink, blue and gold flecks catch the light there, and many people only find them on a second or third look.

Most people see an eye. Some see a whirlpool. It is neither exactly, and I like that it stays open. Art is felt, not seen, so whatever it looks like to you is the right answer.

Lantern Eye is a horizontal piece with a strong center of gravity. That makes it a good fit for a wide wall above a sofa, bed, console or mantel. The dark rings anchor the space, and the white glow gives the eye somewhere to rest.

The deep navy stands out most against pale or warm walls. It would suit a bold room or a quiet one. I find the mood cool, deep and a little watchful.
