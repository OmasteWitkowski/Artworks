A Turquoise Pool Between Pines, Bent Into a Gentle Sweep

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

Still Water Retreat is a wide, painterly landscape of a curving turquoise pool, a timber lodge and tall pines, made for one long wall.

You bought the sofa. The paint is right, the rug is down, and the wall above it is still bare. Or the old picture has begun to look small in a room that has grown around it. That long, wide space asks for something horizontal, and it has to be something you won't tire of.

Picture a turquoise pool curving across the middle of that wall, with pale aqua sky above it and warm brown shingle at the right edge. The chairs are empty and the water is still. This is Still Water Retreat.

I'll tell you what is in the picture, because that is what I know for certain. A curved pool sits on a soft grey stone patio, ringed by a wooden railing. A wood-shingled lodge with many windows and a covered porch stands on the right. Tall pines rise on the left and in the centre, with hazy mountains behind them.

The pool is the brightest thing here. The wide, slightly fisheye view bends its edge and the railing into one gentle sweep. Your eye starts at the lavender-coloured flowers in front, travels across the curve of the water to the lodge, then climbs the pines into a pale pink-lilac sky.

The surface is smooth and painterly, a photo-based treatment, but step closer and the stone, the shingles and the leaves still hold fine detail. This is a piece to look at from across the room and then to walk up to.

I haven't named the place, and I leave that open on purpose. You can decide whether it's a morning or an evening, and what kind of place it is for you.

This is a wide horizontal, so it suits a long wall: over a sofa, a bed, a console or a dining area. The pale aqua sky and turquoise water cool a warm room. The brown wood and shingle tones sit easily with timber, leather and neutral paint.

If you're building a room around blue-green, the turquoise is an easy match. If your room is already warm, it adds one cool, bright point without fighting what is there.
