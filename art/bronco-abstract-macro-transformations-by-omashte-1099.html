Bronco Abstract Macro Transformations by Omashte

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal abstract made of overlapping orange, red and teal oval shapes arranged in a perfectly mirrored pattern. They sit on two pairs of royal blue panels, with a deep red column running down the middle and a red-orange border around the edge. The ovals have thin golden outlines and look glossy and swirled, while the blue areas look mottled and matte.

Glowing orange and red ovals in a mirrored four-way pattern stand out against rich royal blue, giving a bold, high-contrast abstract with a stained-glass feel.
