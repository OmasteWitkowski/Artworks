Wide Blue Quiet, Sunbeams Over a Still Lake

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A wide, still lake lies under a deep cobalt-blue sky filled with scattered white and grey clouds. A low sun at the left horizon sends pale rays fanning across the sky, and the glassy water reflects the glowing gold and peach at the horizon. In the foreground a rocky, rusty-brown shore is dotted with scrubby green plants and a few stones. A dark line of evergreen forest and distant hills runs along the far shore, and a tall conifer stands at the right edge.

The sky is what sets this piece apart. The blue is intense and deepens toward the top and right. Soft, puffy clouds with bright edges and darker undersides sit beside fine, feathery streaks that sweep diagonally across the upper right. The rays radiate from the lower left while the lake below stays completely still.

Look for the contrasts. The smooth water meets the coarse, stony shore, and the cool blues meet the warm rust of the gravel. The low horizon gives the sky about three-quarters of the frame, and the wide, slightly curved view leads the eye from the sun at left across the water to the tall tree at right.

The mood is calm, open and spacious, a quiet moment at the edge of a lake. It needs no more explanation than the light and the sky.

The long horizontal format suits a wide wall above a sofa, bed or desk. The deep blue brings a cool, open space into a room, and the rust and green of the shore keep it from feeling cold.
