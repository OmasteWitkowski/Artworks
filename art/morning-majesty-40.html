Wide Blue Quiet, Sunbeams Over a Still Lake

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A wide view of a still lake at the edge of a forest, under a vivid deep-blue sky. Sunlight breaks from a low sun on the left, casting pale rays through scattered white and grey clouds. In the foreground, a rocky reddish shore is dotted with green scrubby plants, and a tall evergreen stands at the right. Dark tree-covered hills run along the far shore, and the water reflects the glowing horizon.

The story of this pieceA Deep Blue Sky, Sunbeams, and a Lake That Won't MoveThree-quarters sky, one still lake, and pale rays spreading from a low sun across an intense blue.
