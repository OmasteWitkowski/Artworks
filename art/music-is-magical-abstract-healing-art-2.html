Molten Melody, Five Glowing Music Notes

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Five stylized, flagged music notes stand in an evenly spaced row across a dark, swirling field of deep reddish brown, navy and near-black. Each note is translucent, so the liquid marbling shows through its stem, flag and oval head. The note heads move from gold on the left through blue and teal to bright and lime green on the right.

What sets this piece apart is how the notes glow, as if lit from within, against the shadowy ripples and ring-like eddies behind them. A thin yellow-green line encloses the scene, followed by a black band, a dark brown band and a mottled green and brown outer border with a faint canvas-like grain.

Look along the row and the evenly spaced stems set a steady beat, while the shift from warm to cool color carries the eye like a melody moving forward. It can read as sound turned into color, or simply as a rich, dark composition of glass-like shapes on molten marbling.

Its horizontal format suits a music room, studio, den or listening corner, or a long wall above a console, desk or shelf. The dark, framed look pairs well with warm wood and deep jewel tones.
