A Golden Diagonal That Looks Lit From Within

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Lantern Gold, Violet Cell Abstract is a horizontal digital abstract: a glowing golden sweep set against violet-edged, honeycomb-like cells.

You bought the sofa. The paint is settled. And the wall above it still looks bare, or holds something that has started to feel too small for the room.

I'd like you to picture a wide, warm piece there. A broad band of golden yellow runs up on a diagonal, and the yellow and orange seem lit from within. It is the kind of colour that makes a neutral room feel like it has a deep, warm corner.

I'm not going to tell you what this is. I'd rather you look for yourself.

On the right, rounded cells in mauve and violet sit close together like honeycomb. On the left, orange, purple and olive blend into a looser, mottled swirl. The golden diagonal runs between them, from low center toward the top.

Scattered among the shapes are a few small dark hollows. Some people see eyes. Others see openings, or just shadows. None of those readings is wrong.

There is no single focal point. Your eye climbs the gold, the cells press back, and the left side drifts. I believe art is felt before it is explained, and your own response is the only test that counts.

This is a horizontal, full-bleed image, so it suits a wide wall: over a sofa, a bed or a sideboard. The diagonal can travel across the room instead of sitting in one corner.

The mustard, burnt orange and mauve work well in rooms of neutrals, wood tones or earthy colours. They add a deep accent without needing the rest of the room to change. The mood is warm, enveloping and quietly mysterious.
