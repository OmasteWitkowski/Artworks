Emperor with No Clothes

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal abstract in gold, brown and amber, with oval shapes covered in fine grainy texture. They sit among glossy, melted-looking swirls, with a streak of deep red on the right and two pale cream triangular shapes at the bottom. A soft olive-green form curls near the upper center.

Grainy, egg-like ovals appear to float in rivers of molten gold, and two glowing cream shapes interrupt the dark metallic richness with bold contrast.
