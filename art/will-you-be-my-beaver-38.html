Face to Face, Rust Swirl Forms on Turquoise

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Two rust-brown, rounded forms face each other across a field of turquoise and cerulean blue. Each carries a pale cream wedge and a small cream spot that can read as a beak and an eye. A band of cream, tan and soft orange runs along the bottom edge, and the forms are cropped at the sides and base so the image fills the frame.

The surface is built almost entirely from spirals. Tight, dark concentric curls pack the brown shapes, looser rings drift through the blue, and the cream areas open into smooth, buttery strokes. Glossy highlights on the swirl edges give it a lit-from-within, enamel-like look. The warm rust against cool turquoise is what the eye catches first, and the roughly mirrored arrangement gives it a balanced, face-to-face presence.

Look longer and the two forms seem to lean toward each other, as if meeting or squaring off. Whether you see a pair of birds, two creatures or simply two shapes is left to the viewer. The mood is playful, curious and a little whimsical.

The horizontal format and saturated palette suit a wall that needs a confident color accent, such as above a sofa, bed or console. The turquoise holds up well against neutral walls, and the rust brown picks up wood, leather and terracotta.
