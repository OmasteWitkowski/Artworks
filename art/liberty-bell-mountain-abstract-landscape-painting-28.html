Drawn Summit, Outlined Blue Mountain Peaks

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Two jagged mountain peaks rise in slate blue and gray, with a lower ridge between them and soft cream clouds behind. Thick black outlines trace the ridges, the rock details and the clouds. Warm tan and muted green patches show near the base of the slope. The scene sits inside a thin rectangular frame on a wide, shadowy border.

The outlines are the signature of this piece. They wobble like a hand-drawn comic-book contour, and they spill past the inner picture onto the dark border, so the drawing seems to push out of its frame. The color is flattened into posterized patches with a faint canvas-like grain, which keeps the mountain bold and graphic rather than photographic.

The eye climbs the angled slopes to the tall right-hand summit. A lower, rounded peak on the left balances it. Loose black lines in the sky and border add a gentle, restless rhythm to otherwise calm, even light.

The cool blues and charcoal lend a steady, quiet mood, while the heavy line work keeps it from feeling soft. It is a rugged mountain seen in hazy daylight and drawn with confidence.

The strong contrast and horizontal format suit a living room, office or study wall. It works well where a graphic, cool-toned landscape is wanted in place of a realistic view.
