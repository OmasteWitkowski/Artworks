Radical Difference, Amber and Gold Quote Art

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A large pale butter-yellow oval sits at the center, holding three lines of dark red serif text: 'Small, Smart Choices + Consistency + Time = RADICAL DIFFERENCE ~Darren Hardy'. Around it runs a border of overlapping oval and bean-shaped segments in gold, amber and orange, with deep red-maroon filling the outer edge.

The border is what sets this quote card apart. Pale yellow ovals mark the corners, and orange ovals along the edges hold smooth, flowing marbled swirls. Their darker interiors give the amber a polished, stained-glass depth. The whole piece is strictly symmetrical and has a warm, backlit glow.

Look closely and the yellow areas carry a fine, mosaic-like grain with a slight painterly texture. The mirrored repetition of the border keeps the eye moving calmly around the frame and back to the words. The quote is about steady progress, with small choices, consistency and time adding up to a real difference. The warm, ordered design suits that message.

Its horizontal format and readable text suit a home office, desk wall, entryway or hallway, where it adds warm color and a quiet daily reminder. It can also work as a gift for someone starting a new habit or goal.
