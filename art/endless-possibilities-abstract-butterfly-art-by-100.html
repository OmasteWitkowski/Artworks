Glowing Orange Wings, Stained Glass Style Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A large orange butterfly sits at the center of a white panel inside a thick black border, its wings spread symmetrically and seen from above. Black lines divide the wings and body into cells of orange, sky blue, leaf green, yellow and coral pink. Blue spots and scalloped shapes run along the wing edges, with purple at the upper edges. Green stems curl in from the corners, and the antennae curl up into the top border.

The bold black linework and saturated color cells make the butterfly look like a stained-glass window. Each cell is filled with a rippled, marbled, swirling texture that looks translucent and lit from behind, while the white ground keeps the contrast sharp. The look is a visual comparison only; the piece is an illustration, not glass.

Look closer and the swirls mirror one another across the body and the upper wing edges, like a small kaleidoscope. The curved wing outlines and curling vine tips add a gentle sense of motion to an otherwise still, balanced design. The white panel has uneven, torn-looking edges that soften the strict black frame. The overall feeling is bright, playful and full of natural color.

Its horizontal format and strong contrast suit a wall that needs a lively focal point, such as above a sofa, a console or a desk, or in a sunroom or kitchen. The orange gives a warm accent, and the black and white framing keeps it crisp beside other colors.
