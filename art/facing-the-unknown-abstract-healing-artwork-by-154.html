Midnight Medallions: Mirrored Swirl Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Two large cream medallions, each with a small dark maroon bean-shaped center, glow at the heart of this wide abstract. Deep navy diamonds sit between them. Curling ribbons of red, orange, periwinkle and pale aqua swirl outward and repeat across the top and bottom edges.

The image is built on mirrored symmetry, so every curve has a twin and the pattern settles into a steady, hypnotic rhythm. The bright cream centers against the saturated navy and red give it a backlit, glassy glow. The red, orange and blue flame-like forms around the medallions can read almost like faces or masks.

Look closely and the surface is smooth and satiny, with soft marbled gradients and a faint mottled grain in the navy. Motifs along the edges seem to continue beyond the frame, so the eye keeps traveling across the panel. It is an ornate, playful piece, and its appeal is first of all visual.

The wide horizontal format suits a long wall, such as above a sofa, bed or console. The navy and red palette anchors a room, and the cream glow keeps it bright.
