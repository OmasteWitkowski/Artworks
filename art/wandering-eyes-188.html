Amber Gaze - Mirrored Abstract in Jewel Tones

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A tall, perfectly mirrored abstract is divided by a thin dark line down its center. Each half holds a large golden-amber oval with a deep purple and blue hollow at its heart. Bands of emerald green, teal and cobalt blue wrap around the ovals, and a flowing blend of blue, yellow and green fills the lower edge.

The symmetry and the palette set this piece apart. Glowing ambers and yellows push against darker brown and violet centers, while bright cyan edges look backlit. The surface ripples like melted, marbled color, with fine feathery edges that pull toward the central seam.

Look longer and the pair of ovals can read as watchful eyes, a ceremonial mask or the markings of a peacock feather. These are visual impressions rather than a fixed subject, so the image stays open to whatever you see in it. The color and rhythm carry it, and the flowing edges give it a slow, pulsing movement up and down the seam.

Its vertical format and strong symmetry suit an entry, a narrow wall or a spot beside a doorway. The dark center and bright perimeter make it a focal point in a room that can take saturated color.
