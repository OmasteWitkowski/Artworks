Liquid Stones: Swirled Pebbles in Blue and Amber

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A tightly packed bed of smooth, rounded stones fills the whole frame in deep brown, burnt sienna, slate blue and warm tan. Swirling ripples run across them and bend their outlines, so the pebbles look as if they lie beneath moving water. Dark brown gaps between the stones give depth, and the surface has fine curling ridges and glossy, marbled color.

What sets this piece apart is the way a plain stone subject becomes liquid and painterly. A few amber, golden and burnt orange pebbles, with touches of teal, lavender and rust, glow against the cooler blues. The light is soft and even, with no single source and no cast shadows, so the highlights seem to glint along the swirls themselves.

There is no focal point and no horizon. The concentric eddies carry the eye around the frame, and each pass reveals a different stone, a different ripple and a different shift of color. It is a quiet piece to look at slowly, calm and absorbing, with an organic feel that suits its earthy palette.

The horizontal, edge-to-edge format suits a wide wall above a sofa, bed, console or desk. Its warm browns and jewel-like blues work with natural, neutral and earth-toned rooms.
