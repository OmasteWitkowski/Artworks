Plum Ridges Panorama - Purple Mountain Valley

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · panoramic horizontal

A very wide view looks out from a hillside across a mountain valley. Dark evergreen trees frame the foreground, and below them fields, patches of forest and thin winding roads stretch toward rounded mountains. The ridges are shaded in violet and plum with touches of olive and warm tan, under a pale sky that fades from lavender to blue.

The palette is what sets this panorama apart. Cool purples and muted greens replace the usual bright blues and greens of mountain scenes, and the ridges overlap in long horizontal bands that fade into haze. The surface is smooth and painterly, with softened edges and faint brush-like swirls in the sky and hills.

Look slowly and the eye follows the roads across the valley floor and up through each layer of mountains. The light is soft and low in contrast, like the end of the day or an early start, and it gives the scene a quiet, open, contemplative calm.

Its proportions suit a long, low wall, such as above a sofa, bed or sideboard, or along a hallway or office wall. The muted violet and olive tones sit easily with neutral and earthy rooms.
