Plum Ridges Panorama - Purple Mountain Valley

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · panoramic horizontal

A very wide panoramic view looks across a mountain valley from a hillside viewpoint. Dark green evergreen trees stand in the foreground, and below them a valley of fields, forest patches and thin winding roads stretches toward rounded, layered mountains. The hills are shaded in purples and plums with touches of olive and tan. Above them, a pale sky with streaky clouds fades from lavender to blue. The whole scene has a smooth, painterly finish.

The story of this pieceA Mountain Valley in Plum and Olive, Stretched WideA very wide, hushed view of layered violet mountains, dark evergreens and a winding valley, made for a wall that needs width.
