Crimson Curls, Red Sunflower

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A single large red sunflower fills the frame, seen face-on. Pointed crimson petals fan out around a dark brown and purple seed disc, with a small spiral at its very center. The petals shift from lighter orange-red highlights to deep wine-red shadows, and the glowing reds stand out against a pale, airy background of cream, lime green, soft lavender and periwinkle.

What sets this piece apart is its surface. Small curling swirls run through every petal, the seed disc and the background, so the flower looks built from ripples of thick paint. The image sits between realism and abstraction, but the sunflower is never in doubt. The dark red swirled border and thin gold line inside it give the picture a finished, framed look.

Look closely and the curls follow the radiating petals, so the whole flower feels in motion, as if it were slowly turning. The fine, dark texture of the seed center holds the eye, and the pastel background lets the red stay bold and lively without becoming heavy.

This is a strong choice for a wall that needs a warm focal point, such as a living room, dining area or entry. The saturated red pairs well with cream, green and soft neutral surroundings, and the centered, symmetrical flower gives the room a confident anchor.
