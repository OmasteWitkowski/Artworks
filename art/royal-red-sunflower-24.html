Every petal is a ripple of crimson curls

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

Crimson Curls, Red Sunflower is a single red sunflower, seen face-on and built from small curling swirls that give it the feel of thick paint.

You know the wall. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and that one stretch of wall still looks unfinished. You have probably walked past it for months, waiting for the right piece instead of just any piece.

Picture a single red sunflower filling that space. It is deep crimson and wine-red, with a dark seed center, set against a pale, airy background. The red reads from across the room, and when you step closer the surface turns into curls.

I'll tell you what is in the picture, because that is the part I can stand behind. From a distance it is a bold red sunflower, face-on, with pointed petals fanning out from a dark brown and purple center. There is no doubt about the subject.

Then the surface starts to show. Small curling swirls run through every petal, through the seed disc and through the background. The flower looks as though it were carved from rippling paint. The curls follow the petals outward, so the whole image seems to be slowly turning, and a small spiral sits right at the middle of the seed disc.

Behind it, the background is soft cream, lime green, lavender and periwinkle, with touches of peach and golden yellow. That pale field lets the red stay bold without feeling heavy. A dark red swirled border with a thin gold line inside it makes the picture look finished and framed.

It is a digital painterly floral. It sits between realism and abstraction, and my conviction is that art is felt, not seen. You don't need to be an art person to know whether this one stops you.

This is a focal piece, not a quiet accent. The saturated red and the centered, symmetrical flower suit a living room, a dining room, an entry, or any wall that needs a strong warm presence. The image is horizontal, which gives it a natural home over a sofa, a sideboard or a console.

The red pairs well with cream, green and soft neutral surroundings. If your room is calm and settled, this is the piece that gives it a point of view.

You trust your own response. I believe there is no good or bad art, and that the only test is whether a piece holds you. If this red sunflower stopped you, that is reason enough. I can't promise how it will feel in your room, but you are qualified to choose it.

Picture the wall again, this time with a glowing red sunflower in the middle of it, its curls catching your eye each time you walk in. That is the whole idea.
