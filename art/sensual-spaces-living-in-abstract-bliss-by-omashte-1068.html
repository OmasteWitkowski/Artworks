Sensual Spaces Living In Abstract Bliss by Omashte

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Four mirrored panels show black and white curving shapes that read as hearts, each with a small red oval at the center. They sit on a deep red textured background with a soft gray patterned cross between them. The contrast is strong and the layout is perfectly symmetrical.

A high-contrast, mirror-symmetrical abstract in black, white and red, where four heart-like forms each hold a bright red accent around a smoky gray cross.
