Sunburst Wings, Symmetrical Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · vertical

A symmetrical butterfly with outstretched wings is shown against a plain white background. The wing edges are filled with swirling, glossy bands of red, orange, yellow and green. Large white oval and teardrop shapes are cut out of the wings, and a bright yellow band runs across the center near the slim body. Two long thin antennae rise from the top of the body.

The story of this pieceA Butterfly Cut Open With White, Its Wings Full of Liquid ColorA front-facing butterfly in glowing yellow, orange, red and green, with white cutout cells that make it look designed rather than ordinary.
