Sunburst Wings, Symmetrical Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · vertical

A single butterfly faces the viewer with its wings spread, perfectly mirrored left to right on a plain white ground. Glossy, swirling bands of yellow, orange, deep red and green fill the wings, with touches of amber, burgundy, teal and a little purple and blue at the tip of the body. Two long, thin antennae rise up and outward.

What sets it apart is the cutout structure. Large white ovals, long teardrops and ring-like loops open up the wings like veins and cells, so the color becomes crisp, graphic shapes rather than a realistic butterfly. The brightest yellow crosses the middle at the body and anchors the whole design.

Look closely and the color inside the wing edges looks liquid and marbled, with a smooth, polished finish, while the strict symmetry keeps the butterfly still and poised. The pointed wing tips and upswept antennae lead the eye up and out to the corners.

With its white ground and warm palette, it brings cheerful color to a bright wall in a kitchen, sunroom, hallway, studio or child's room, and it works well on its own as a vertical focal point.
