Ember Snowflake in Red and Gold

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A large crimson snowflake sits centered on a clean white background. It has six feathered arms with smaller side branches, each main arm ending in a tiny rounded knob, and a small oval medallion forms its center. The shape is flat and perfectly symmetrical, stretched a little wider than tall.

What sets it apart is the fill. Instead of a solid color, the red is alive with swirling, marbled bands of crimson, burnt orange and amber gold, with traces of maroon, purple and magenta. The gold streaks look luminous against the darker red, so the glow comes from the color itself rather than from any cast light.

Look closer and the surface opens up into fine curved bands and tiny speckles, a kaleidoscopic, stained-glass effect that gives the still, balanced shape a gentle sense of flow. The central oval holds the eye while the pattern moves outward along each arm.

The warm palette makes this a wintry image that feels celebratory rather than cold, and the red and gold read naturally as festive. It is also simply a striking graphic, bold silhouette and rich color, for anyone who likes a snowflake with some fire in it.

The horizontal format and generous white space suit a mantel, console, entry wall or a winter-leaning living space, where the red can be the one confident accent.
