A Heart Made of Marbled Swirls and White Lace

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ember Lace Heart is a mirrored, marbled heart in burnt orange, coral and sage, set in a lattice of fine white-ground spirals and flowers.

Maybe the new sofa went in and the wall above it suddenly looks bare. Or the wall over the bed has been waiting for months, and everything you've found looks like everything else.

This is the piece I'd put in front of that wall. A large heart sits dead center on a bright white field. It is filled with orange, coral, sage and aqua swirls that curl outward from a glowing red-orange diamond. It reads from across the room.

First comes the heart, and then the orange diamond at its center. That is where the eye lands. From there the colors curl outward in matching currents, burnt orange and coral close in, with soft sage, pale yellow-green and dusty aqua resting around them.

The left and right halves mirror each other exactly. The heart looks glossy and fluid, with a soft sheen along its edges and a few pale glints in the swirls.

Then you step closer and the background changes. What looked like plain white is a crisp, thin lattice of spirals, circles, scrolls and flower-like outlines in rust, olive and crimson. It runs off all four edges, inside a thin orange border.

Look closer still and the loops in the lace echo the swirls inside the heart. The heart is a familiar symbol, but here it is built from movement and ornament, so it feels warm and celebratory without turning sentimental.

The format is horizontal and strongly centered, so it suits the wall above a sofa, a bed, a console or a desk, anywhere you want a bright focal point. The white ground stays open and luminous, and the orange and sage palette sits easily with neutral walls, natural wood and soft textiles.

It works as pure decoration, and you don't need a reason beyond liking it. It also works as an affectionate gift for a wedding, an anniversary or a new home, because the heart says what it means without any explanation.
