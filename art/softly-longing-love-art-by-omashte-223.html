Ember Lace Heart, Mirrored Marbled Heart

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A large heart sits at the center of a white field. It is filled with mirrored, swirling marbled shapes in burnt orange, coral red, soft sage green, pale yellow-green and dusty aqua. A red-orange diamond glows at its heart, and the colors curl outward in matching currents on each side. The surface looks glossy and fluid, with a soft sheen along the edges and a few pale glints in the swirls.

What sets this piece apart is the contrast between the heart and its setting. Behind the saturated, flowing color is a crisp, thin lattice of spirals, circles, scrolls and flower-like outlines in rust, olive and crimson. The pattern runs off all four edges, and a thin orange border frames the whole image. The left and right halves mirror each other exactly, which gives the heart a calm, kaleidoscopic order.

Look closely and the swirls inside the heart echo the loops of the lace around it. The warm orange center holds the eye, while the cooler greens and aquas rest around it. The heart is a familiar symbol of affection, and here it is built from movement and ornament. It reads as warm and celebratory without becoming sentimental.

The horizontal format and bright white ground suit a living room, bedroom or entry wall where a cheerful focal point is wanted. Its orange and sage palette sits easily with neutral walls, natural wood and soft textiles.
