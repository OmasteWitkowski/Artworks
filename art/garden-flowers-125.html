Sunmask, Mirrored Yellow Flowers

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A glowing yellow center sits in a dark green-black field, formed by blurred flower shapes mirrored left and right. At its heart the shapes suggest a mask, with a triangular nose, dark eye-like hollows and a wide lower curve. Smaller clusters of yellow and pink blossoms and green leaves repeat along both edges, and teal wave-like bands run across the bottom.

What sets this piece apart is the way strict symmetry turns floral imagery into a single presence. The center is soft and streaked with motion, while the petals and leaves at the margins keep sharper edges. Concentric arcs and fine looping light trails ripple around and below the face, and the yellow forms flare upward like flames.

Look longer and the image shifts between flower garden, totem and creature. The face-like form is an impression rather than a fixed subject, so each viewer can read it differently. Saturated yellow, orange, magenta and red burn against deep teal and forest green, with touches of violet and blue.

The horizontal format and dark surround suit a wall that needs one bright focal point, such as above a sofa, bed or console. It adds energy and color to an eclectic or contemporary room.
