Yin Yang Window, Black Symbol Over Marbled Gold and Blue

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A large black yin-yang fills a tall oval, and a crescent of glowing marbled color shows around it. The oval sits on a black rectangular mat with a thin gold line, inside a wider border of rippled, textured color.

The two small circles of the symbol are cut out as openings, and each frames a different slice of the swirl behind it. Golden yellow and amber orange sweep up from the lower left toward cobalt blue and violet at the upper right, with small teal, magenta and burnt red accents. The color looks lit from within against the flat black, and fine gold speckling traces the outline.

The yin-yang is a familiar symbol of duality, and here the warm gold side and cool blue side make that idea visible without a word. It can be read as balance, or simply enjoyed as a vivid, energetic play of black and color. The swirls curl in S-shaped currents that echo the curve of the symbol, so the eye keeps moving between the dark shape and the light behind it.

Its vertical, symmetrical format and deep black mat make it a strong single focal piece. It suits an entry, living room, office or bedroom wall, and it holds up especially well on a dark or neutral background.
