Yellow goslings, like small lights on steel-blue water

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · panoramic horizontal

Little Suns is a wide, digitally enhanced photograph of two Canada geese and their yellow goslings drifting across rippled blue-green water.

You know the wall. It's wide and low, over the sofa or the bed or a long console, and so far nothing has looked right on it. Too tall, too small, too serious, or too much like everything else.

This is the picture I'd put there. Yellow goslings glow in the middle of a field of blue and teal water, and a Canada goose swims on either side of them. It's a quiet family scene, and it's bright enough to hold a wall.

The goslings come first. Their golden yellow is the warmest thing in the frame, and it sits against steel blue, slate and deep teal. They look like small points of light on the water.

Then the eye starts to travel. There is no horizon and no shoreline, only waves running across the whole panorama, with silvery highlights along the crests. The water has a slightly etched, painterly finish from the way the photograph was enhanced, and the birds seem to move through the pattern rather than sit in front of it.

Last, you notice the adults. They have black necks, white chinstraps, tan and brown bodies and pale breasts, and the striping on their wings is crisp. One is left of center and one is toward the upper right. Everyone is heading the same way, to the right, and the goslings stay close behind the nearest parent.

I won't tell you what it means. Some people see a family out for a swim, and others just love the yellow against the blue. Either is a good reason to want it.

The panoramic shape suits wide, low or shallow spaces: above a sofa, a console or sideboard, or a bed, or along a hallway. It also works in a kitchen or family room, where a bright, friendly picture belongs.

The blue and teal sit easily beside neutral and natural palettes, and the yellow adds one small, cheerful accent. It is calm but colorful, and not dramatic.

Measure the wall first, then compare that to the sizes offered on the listing. The shape is a wide panorama, so check both the width and the height you have room for. If it isn't right when it arrives, the 30-day money-back guarantee applies.

Screens differ, so I can't promise an exact match. The blue, teal and yellow are strong and saturated, which helps them hold up in a room. The guarantee means you can return it if the colors don't suit your space.

You don't need to know anything about art. I believe art is felt, not seen, and that your own response is the only test that counts. If the yellow goslings on the blue water make you want to look again, that's reason enough.

Picture it on that long wall, with the goslings glowing against the blue and the family drifting slowly across the room. It's the first thing you see when you sit down.
