Spiral Lily in Black and White

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A large lily fills the frame, its long pointed petals fanning out from the centre and curling at the tips. Dark speckles and wavy marks run along the inner petals, and round buds and berry-like shapes gather toward the right. The whole scene is in charcoal, gray and soft white against a deep, dark background.

What sets this lily apart is its surface. Small swirling curls cover every petal, bud and background shape, like thick, twisted paint. The petal edges end in tight scrolled ruffles. The result is a rippled, tactile image that sits between a realistic botanical subject and a decorative pattern of spirals.

Look closely and the light does much of the work. The petals are pale and bright at the centre and along their upper edges, then drop into deeper gray where they overlap and curl. The repeating swirls spin outward with the petals, giving the flower a slow, rolling motion. The quieter dark lower left balances the busier buds in the upper and lower right.

The strict black and white makes the piece graphic and sophisticated, and the curling forms add a light, playful note. It suits a wall that needs one confident horizontal image, above a sofa, bed or console. It works especially well in rooms built on neutral, black, white or natural tones.
