Ember Mirror, Symmetrical Abstract in Orange and Violet

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A vertical abstract built from swirling, smoke-like ribbons in fiery orange, red, deep violet and cobalt blue. At the top and bottom, a blue-and-purple oval holds an olive-green band that ends in a glowing orange point. Between them, a soft, hazy rose-mauve field fills the center. Curling gold-orange spirals and thin looping lines trace the edges of every shape.

The whole image is mirrored top to bottom and left to right, so each motif has an exact twin. The ribbon edges glow like embers against the darker blues, purples and reds, and the image looks lit from within. The surface is smooth and glossy, with semi-transparent layers that give it a glass-like depth.

The eye is pulled into the calm central field, then carried back out along the spirals to the busy corners. The matched shapes can read as a kaleidoscope, or as an ornate mask or face, depending on how you look at it. The mood is warm, rich and dreamlike.

Its tall format suits an entry, hallway or the wall beside a doorway. It also works as a focal piece in a room that can handle saturated color, where the orange and violet will stand out against quieter furnishings.
