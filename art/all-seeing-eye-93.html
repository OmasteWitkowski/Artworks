Cobalt Watcher - Abstract Blue Eye of Swirling Color

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A giant eye made of swirling liquid color fills the frame. A bright cobalt-blue oval sits at the center like an iris, with a small dark teardrop shape just below the middle like a pupil. Bands of white, rust-orange, amber, brown and yellow surround it, and their scalloped, flame-like edges suggest lashes or eyelids.

The image is abstract, yet the eye reads at once. Concentric rings lead the viewer inward to the dark pupil, and the blue appears to glow against the near-black navy ring around it. Fine feathery tendrils, small curls and rippled veining give the glossy-looking surface a marbled, flowing detail.

The ripples spiral inward toward the blue while the outer bands flow sideways and the edges flare outward, so the whole image seems to turn slowly. Its mood is intense, hypnotic and watchful, with a dreamlike, otherworldly feel. It can be enjoyed simply for the color and motion, or as a face looking back at you.

The horizontal, centered composition gives a wall one strong focal point. It suits a living room, studio, office or any space that can carry a bold blue accent with warm orange and amber answering it.
