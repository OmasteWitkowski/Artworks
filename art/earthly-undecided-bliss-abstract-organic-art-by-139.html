A Black Center Ringed in Fire: Look Closer

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · horizontal

Ember Eclipse is a symmetrical orange and black digital abstract, with a near-black four-lobed center inside a glowing golden oval.

The sofa is in. The paint is settled. And one wall still looks like it's waiting for something with a strong center.

This is the piece I'd hold up to that wall. A near-black shape sits in the middle of a glowing golden oval, and everything around it seems to burn.

Your eye goes to the dark center first. It is a four-lobed, cross-like shape, almost black, set exactly in the middle of the oval. Then the eye moves outward along the curls of orange, cream and crimson, and it keeps going.

The two sides match, and so do the top and bottom. The symmetry is exact, so the piece feels formal and deliberate. But the surface is smooth, liquid and marbled, with feathery edges and no visible brushwork, and that keeps it warm.

The curls move in two directions. Some flow out from the center. Pointed, flame-like tips curl back in toward it. The design seems to pulse while it stays perfectly balanced.

Look slowly and the image shifts. I see an eclipse, a dark eye, a ring of fire. Those are only impressions, and the piece doesn't depend on any one of them. What you see in it is yours to decide.

The format is horizontal, which suits the wall above a sofa, a bed, a console or a mantel. The exact symmetry works well in balanced, formal arrangements, and the dark center anchors the space.

The orange and amber edges add warmth, and the deep brown corners keep the mood dramatic rather than bright and cheerful.
