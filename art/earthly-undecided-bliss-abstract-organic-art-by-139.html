Ember Eclipse, Symmetrical Orange and Black Abstract

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · horizontal

A near-black, four-lobed form sits at the center of a glowing golden oval. Around it, orange, cream, crimson and olive curls spread outward in perfect mirror image, left to right and top to bottom. The brightest passages are the pale cream and yellow swirls at the left and right. Darker brown marbled corners frame the oval.

The contrast is what sets this piece apart. The center is almost black, while the edges glow, and a thin golden rim holds the oval together. Pointed, flame-like tips curl in toward the void as other curls flow out, so the design pulses even though the symmetry is exact. The surface is smooth, fluid and marbled, with feathery edges and no visible brushwork.

Look slowly and the image shifts. It can read as an eclipse, a dark eye or a ring of fire. These are visual impressions only, and the design does not depend on any one of them. The eye goes first to the dark center, then travels out along the glowing curls and back again.

The horizontal format and the dark, warm palette suit a wall that needs a strong, balanced focal point. Over a sofa, bed or console, the black center anchors the space and the amber and orange edges bring warmth.
