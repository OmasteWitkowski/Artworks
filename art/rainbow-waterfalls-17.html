Ember and Tide, Glowing Glass-Like Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A bold red-orange band curves down the left side beside patches of rough, mottled amber. At the center, a cool blue field is drawn with fine curved lines and scattered with tiny bubble-like dots. On the right, a golden-amber panel carries a faint square grid. The colors look lit from within, as if light passes through translucent glass.

What sets this image apart is the meeting of warm and cool. A thin seam of green, yellow and blue streaks runs between the glowing red-orange and the hazy blue-grey center, like a small rainbow edge. Sharp, bright lines appear where the bands meet.

Look longer and the surface changes from zone to zone. The blue center is smooth and glassy with fine parallel ridges, while the amber at left is rough and mottled. Long arcs sweep from upper center down and to the right, and thin vertical streaks of green and blue pull the eye across the frame. Nothing here is a recognizable object, so the pleasure is in color, light and line.

The horizontal format suits a wide wall above a sofa, bed or console, or a hallway or office that needs a vivid focal point. The warm and cool zones pair well with neutral walls and with rooms that already use blue or amber.
