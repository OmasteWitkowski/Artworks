Ember and Tide, Glowing Glass-Like Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A horizontal abstract image of glowing, glass-like color. A bold red and orange curve sweeps down the left side beside amber tones, while the center is a cool blue field of fine curved lines with tiny bubble-like dots. A thin band of green, yellow and blue streaks separates the warm and cool areas. On the right, a golden-amber panel carries a faint square grid pattern.

The story of this pieceWhere a Red-Orange Glow Meets a Cool Blue Field of BubblesA red-orange band, rough amber and a hazy blue field of fine curves and tiny bubbles, all lit as if from within.
