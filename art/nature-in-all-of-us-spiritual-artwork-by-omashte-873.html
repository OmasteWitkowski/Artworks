Nature In All of Us Spiritual Artwork by Omashte

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A broad, pale gray mountain with patches of snow stands under a clear blue sky, with green spruce and fir trees across the foreground, including a bare white snag at left. Below the photo, a dark green panel carries the word NATURE in large pale blue-gray capitals and a line of smaller white text. The whole image sits inside a teal beveled border with a small signature at lower right of the photo.

A softly painterly mountain-and-conifer landscape paired with bold NATURE lettering and a cool blue, green and silver palette makes this a ready-made statement piece for lovers of wild open country.
