Glowing green threads circling a black center

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

Green Current is a square digital abstract: lime and silvery-white filaments looping around a near-black middle, built to hold a bare wall on contrast alone.

You know the wall. It might be above a console, a desk or the bed. The furniture is settled and the paint is right, but the wall still looks unfinished, and nothing you've looked at has felt like yours.

Picture a square of deep green and near-black with a ring of light in the middle of it. Slender lime and silvery-white strands curve around a dark open center, and a soft green haze glows at the edges. That is Green Current.

I'd rather tell you what is in the image than invent a story for it. The first thing you see is the dark center. The eye goes straight to it, then slips out along the bright arcs and keeps going.

The longer arcs sweep from left to right and loop around the middle. Smaller bristles flick outward in every direction, like threads caught in a current.

The broad strands are soft and smoky. The bristles along them are crisp and hair-fine. That mix of blur and sharpness gives the surface a silky, slightly electric look. It doesn't look like paint or like anything physical.

The strands suggest grass blades, fern fronds or fibers, but nothing in the image is one specific thing. It reads as an imaginary plant world, as if you were looking at it up close. What it is to you is your call.

The strong dark-to-bright contrast suits a single statement position, such as above a console, a desk or a bed. It also suits a quiet corner where the glow can read against a well-lit wall.

The deep green and black edges settle into dark or neutral rooms. It could sit in a study, a modern living space or anywhere that needs one luminous accent. The square format gives you a balanced shape rather than a wide or tall one.
