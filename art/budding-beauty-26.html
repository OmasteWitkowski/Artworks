Green Current, Glowing Filaments Around a Dark Center

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

Slender green and pale silvery-white strands curve across a dark, almost black center. Fine feathery spikes branch off them, and a soft green haze fills the edges and corners. A few strands pick up yellow-gold highlights near the top center and lower right.

The contrast between soft and sharp makes this piece distinctive. The broad strands are smoky and blurred, while the bristles are crisp and hair-fine. Together they give the surface a silky, slightly electric glow that looks neither painterly nor physical.

Long arcs sweep from left to right and loop around the open center, with smaller bristles flicking outward in every direction. The strands suggest grass blades, fern fronds or fibers, but nothing is a specific object. It reads as a glowing, imaginary plant world, seen as if under a microscope.

The square format and dark middle frame the view like a nest or tunnel, and the eye keeps returning to the center before following the bright arcs outward. The mood is calm but energized, and it comes from color and form alone.

The deep green and black ground suits dark or neutral rooms, where the glowing strands stand out as a single focal point. It works well in a study, a modern living space or a quiet corner that needs one luminous accent.
