The Small Pink Bud That Glows Like a Lantern

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

"Lantern Bud, Pink Flower and Blue Glass Vase" is a horizontal, digitally enhanced floral photograph, with one crisp pink-and-cream bud lit from behind by a soft blue window.

You bought the sofa. The paint is right, the lamp is right, and the wall above it still looks like nobody has decided anything. Or the old picture that used to fit now looks small in the room.

Picture a wide, bright image there. A single pink-and-cream bud hangs on a long curving stem, and the sun behind it lights its edges so it glows like a small lantern. Everything around it softens into blue sky, green leaves and rings of pale light.

The bud is the first thing you see, and it is the only thing in sharp focus. It is rounded, pink at the edges and cream toward the middle, with fine detail in the petals. Its long stem sweeps in an arc to the right, and your eye follows it.

Then the rest of the picture opens up. Behind the bud is a sunlit window, but you don't see it clearly. Sky, foliage and golden shapes have dissolved into soft colour, with pale circles of light floating in the blue.

Look to the lower left and there is a cobalt glass vase with tall green stalks rising out of it. The glass holds streaks of blue, red and violet, like a small piece of jewellery set in the corner. A tiny pale bud droops over the rim, and a second one hangs lower on the right.

The surface is smooth and glossy, with a painterly sheen that has been digitally enhanced. A simple close-up ends up feeling like a small still-life painting. I'd rather you stand in front of it than read about it, because what you feel in front of it is the point.

The horizontal format suits a wide wall: above a sofa, a bed, a console or a dining buffet. It also works as a bright accent in a kitchen, sunroom or entry, where the window light in the picture can pick up the light in the room.

The palette is fresh green and cobalt blue with soft sky blue, warmed by blush and gold. It sits easily with whites, wood tones and other blues, and the mood is quiet and bright.
