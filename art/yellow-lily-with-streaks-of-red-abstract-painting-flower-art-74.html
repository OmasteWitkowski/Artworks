Pinwheel Bloom - Swirl-Textured Yellow Flower

Botanical & Flowers · square

A large golden-yellow flower fills the square, its broad petals fanning out from an orange center streaked with red. Small blue flowers, green leaves and pale stones sit behind it. A wide brown border with a thin orange-red inner line frames the scene.

What sets this piece apart is the surface. Every petal, leaf and background shape is built from tiny swirls and curls, which gives the image a thick, painted, tactile look. Red flame-like markings run through the petals, and the flower seems to glow against the cooler blue and green behind it.

Look closely and the petals read as coils of motion that radiate from the center like a pinwheel or a burst of sunlight. The background is a mosaic of small round whorls that keeps the eye moving. The mood is cheerful, energetic and playful.

The warm color and centered form make it a natural focal point in a bright room, a kitchen, a sunroom or an entry. The brown border and blue-green accents help it sit comfortably with natural wood and neutral walls.
