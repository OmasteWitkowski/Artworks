Amber Drift - Red Circle and Blue Dome Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A large golden-amber form fills the center and right of this horizontal abstract. A red circle sits at the upper center, and a cobalt-blue dome pushes up from the bottom edge. Around them, soft pink, lavender and tan fields swirl and marble, and the shapes crop off all four edges.

The three focal shapes are what set this piece apart. Each is outlined by a thin rim of light, so they read like backlit glass or enamel floating in the swirls. There are no cast shadows, only a soft, even glow. The quieter tan side on the left balances the heavy amber mass on the right.

Look closer and small whirlpool curls and pointed, flame-like tendrils appear where the colors meet, across a fine, even surface texture. A pale lilac arc sweeps down the right side and carries the eye around the amber form. The mood is warm, lively and dreamy, with saturated color softened by flowing curves.

The warm amber and strong red and blue give a wide wall a clear focal point. It works well above a sofa, bed or console, in a room that welcomes confident color.
