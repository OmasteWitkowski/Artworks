Amber Eye in Cobalt Glass

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A large amber and brown teardrop floats inside curving bands of deep cobalt blue and bright yellow-green. At its center sits a pale, translucent oval edged with a ring of tiny beads, like an iris and pupil. Fine streaks of amber, cream and teal fan out from it, and small bubbles are scattered through the glassy surface.

The light is what sets this piece apart. It seems to glow from behind and inside the material. The pale center is the brightest point, thin highlights catch along the curves, and the outer blue falls away to near darkness. The saturated cobalt and chartreuse make the amber core feel even warmer.

Look longer and the whole image turns inward. The streaks sweep down through the teardrop, and the blue and yellow bands wrap around it like a slow current. The eye-like center gives the piece a watchful, immersive quality, though it can also be enjoyed simply as vivid color and light.

The wide horizontal format and strong focal point suit a living room sofa wall, a bedroom, a console or an office, anywhere a room needs one saturated, luminous piece to anchor it.
