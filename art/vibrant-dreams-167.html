Amber Eye in Cobalt Glass

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A large teardrop shape with a brown and amber interior sits inside bands of deep cobalt blue and bright yellow-green. At its center is a pale, glowing oval edged with tiny beads, like an eye. Fine streaks of amber, cream and teal fan out from it, and small bubbles are scattered through the glassy surface. The overall look is saturated, translucent and lit from within.
