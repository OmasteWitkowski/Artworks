Is It a Mask, a Totem, or Just Gold Light? Look Closer

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Golden Totem is a mirrored digital abstract of gold, lime and olive ribbons that looks lit from behind, and the face you find in it is yours.

You know the wall. It might be over the sofa, over the bed, or above a console. The furniture is settled and the paint is chosen, and that one stretch of wall still looks unfinished.

Picture a horizontal piece there, glowing gold and green, with a strong line running down its centre. The ribbons on the left and right match, and your eye goes straight to the middle and stays.

The first thing you see is a face. At the top centre a bright cream shape sits above a dark oval, and below it a stacked column of looping lines reads like a totem. On either side, green forms look like masks.

I didn't set out to depict any of that. It is what the eye does with a perfectly balanced shape, and you may see something different from what I see. What holds me here is the symmetry.

Then the face loosens and the piece becomes colour. The ribbons keep folding over one another. The loops at the centre draw your eye down, and the curves carry it back out toward the corners.

What it means is yours to decide. I would rather you stand in front of it than read about it.

This is a horizontal piece, so it suits a wide wall that needs one strong focal point, such as over a sofa, a bed or a console. The central axis anchors the space, and the busy ribbons at the edges keep the sides lively.

The olive and gold sit well with warm woods and neutral walls. The navy and sky-blue touches give it something to play against in cooler rooms.
