Reaching For the Stars And Abstract Patterns

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · square

A large golden eight-pointed star sits in the middle of a dark brown square, with four smaller stars around it. Behind it, a tilted diamond of swirling blue, purple, teal and red shapes is set inside a dark brown frame with a patterned edge.
