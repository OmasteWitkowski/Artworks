Jewel Box Star, Golden Star on a Blue Diamond

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · square

A large golden eight-pointed star glows at the center of a dark chocolate-brown square, with four smaller golden stars placed near the corners of the panel. The square rests on a tilted cobalt-blue diamond swirled with red-orange, magenta-purple, turquoise and olive green. A larger brown field and a blue, purple and turquoise patterned border frame it all.

The design is perfectly symmetrical, and a thin orange line outlines each layer with a soft halo. The calm gold-on-brown center contrasts with the marbled, liquid color of the diamond, whose red-orange forms curl around its corners and mirror each other. It reads as geometric and mandala-like, yet the surface feels fluid and rich.

Look closely at the stars and you will see a faint mottled, flame-like grain inside the gold. The stillness of the radiating points sets off the slow curl of the swirling shapes around them. The dark brown and gold suggest a night sky, while the saturated color gives the piece a festive, jewel-like richness.

The square format and centered design suit a main wall, such as above a sofa, bed or console, or at the end of a hallway. The deep brown and gold sit well in warm rooms, and the cobalt and turquoise add a cooler contrast.
