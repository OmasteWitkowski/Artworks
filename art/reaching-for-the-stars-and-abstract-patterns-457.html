Jewel Box Star, Golden Star on a Blue Diamond

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · square

A large golden star with eight long points sits at the center of a dark brown square, with four smaller golden stars around it. This square rests on a tilted blue diamond swirled with red-orange, purple, turquoise and green shapes. The diamond sits within a larger dark brown field framed by a colorful blue-purple border. Thin glowing orange lines outline each layer, and the whole design is perfectly symmetrical.
