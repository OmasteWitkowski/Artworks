Raspberry Whirl - Berries in a Mirror-Bright Swirling Bowl

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A round, polished metal bowl is seen from above, with a small heap of bright pink-red raspberries resting in its lower half. The bowl's dark interior holds purple, steel-blue and warm tan reflections, and its rim glows copper-orange. Behind it, warm beige at upper left gives way to deep blue-black.

What sets this piece apart is the way the whole scene has been rippled into repeating spirals. The metal looks liquid, as if the bowl were poured rather than made. The raspberries are the most saturated and brightest note in the image, glowing against the mirror-dark interior.

Look closely and the small whorls repeat everywhere: in the background curves, in the reflections across the bowl, and on the soft, plump surfaces of the berries. The curves loop outward and draw the eye back to the center. The mood is playful, vivid and a little dreamlike, built from the contrast between fresh fruit and cool, reflective metal.

The centered round form and dark charcoal, plum and blue palette give a wall a strong focal point with one hot pink-red accent. It suits a kitchen, dining space, bar or any room that needs bold color and a touch of whimsy.
