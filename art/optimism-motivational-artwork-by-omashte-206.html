Four Teal Loops, Glowing Centers, One Word: OPTIMISM

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Optimism is a mirrored pattern of teal almond shapes with glowing yellow centers on a golden-orange ground, with one red word beneath it.

You bought the sofa. The paint is done. And the wall above it still looks like nobody has decided anything yet.

Picture something warm on that wall. A saturated golden orange, deep teal, a little crimson, and one word in large red letters that you can read from across the room. That is this piece.

At first it reads as a pattern: four identical clusters in a tidy grid, mirrored left to right and top to bottom. Then the details come forward. Each cluster is overlapping dark teal almond shapes with a bright yellow center, outlined in thin gold. The centers glow against the teal, and the shapes spiral outward as if each cluster were slowly opening.

Between the clusters, a hazy gray-green diamond lattice pulls your eye toward the middle. Look at the clusters long enough and you may see flowers. You may see watchful eyes. The image doesn't settle it, and I'd rather leave that to you.

The orange ground is mottled and painterly, while the shapes on it are smooth and flat. Under the pattern comes the word OPTIMISM in large red serif capitals, and a short quotation about a state of mind strong enough to keep a person going when it appears too dark to see the sunshine.

This is a horizontal piece with strong color, so it suits a wide wall: above a sofa, over a desk, along a hallway console. The light in the image is flat, even and luminous, with no shadows, and the shapes are bold, so it holds up from a distance as well as up close.

It is warm, upbeat and decorative. It would brighten a kitchen, a studio or an office as easily as a living room, wherever you'd like some color and an encouraging word in view.
