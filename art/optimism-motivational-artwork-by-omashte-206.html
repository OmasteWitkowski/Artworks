Optimism, Mirrored Teal Loops on Golden Orange

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Four mirrored clusters of dark teal almond-shaped loops, each with a glowing golden-yellow center and thin gold outline, sit in a 2x2 grid on a mottled, saturated golden-orange ground. A hazy gray-green diamond lattice runs through the middle. Below the pattern, the word OPTIMISM appears in large red serif capitals, followed by a short quotation in smaller dark and red lettering.

What sets it apart is the pairing of strict mirror symmetry with a warm, flat, luminous palette. The yellow centers glow against the teal, the shapes spiral outward from each cluster, and the central diamond draws the eye inward. The motifs could be read as florals or as watchful eyes, and the image does not settle it.

The quotation speaks of a state of mind strong enough to keep a person going when it appears too dark to see the sunshine. The sunny orange ground and the repeating, steady pattern fit that hopeful message.

The horizontal format suits a wall above a sofa, a desk or a hallway console. It adds a strong shot of warm color and an encouraging word to a home, studio or office.
