A Daisy-Like Flower With Petals That Curl Like Flames

Botanical & Flowers · vertical

Ribbon Sunburst is one large orange flower, seen straight on, with every petal bent into a wavy ribbon against a dark marbled ground.

You bring in the new sofa, the paint is dry, and the wall above it still looks empty. Everything else in the room is settled. That wall is the one thing left that doesn't feel like yours.

This is the piece I would hold up to that wall. One large orange-yellow flower fills the frame, with a dark purple-black center and long petals fanning out like a sunburst. It is warm before it is anything else.

From across the room, you see a flower. It is a daisy-like bloom, close to a black-eyed Susan or coneflower, though I can't promise you the species. It reads at once, which is part of why it is easy to live with.

Step closer and it changes. The petals wave and curl at the tips like flames or ribbons. Inside each one are tiny spiral whorls, like fingerprints, and the surface looks like thick, heavy paint.

Between the petals and in the corners, marbled swirls of navy, teal and violet show through. Deeper red-orange gathers around the center and gives the flower some depth. Every petal pushes outward from that dark middle, so the whole image feels liquid and a little surreal.

I'm not going to tell you what it means. Look at it for a while and see what it says to you.

The vertical shape and the strong orange against dark tones make this a piece that can hold a wall by itself. It suits a living room, an entry, or any neutral or dark-toned room that could use one warm, confident focal point.

The structure is simple and bold: a dark center, a ring of orange, and dark corners. That should read well from across the room as well as up close. I'm not claiming a largest size, so measure your wall and pick the size that feels right to you.

That worry is common with large pieces. Measure the wall first, and mark the space with paper or tape. Then pick a size on the listing. If it doesn't feel right when it arrives, the 30-day money-back guarantee applies.

Screens differ, so I can't promise an exact match. The image is built on saturated orange and yellow against deep purple, navy and teal, and that contrast is what to expect. Paper, canvas and metal each finish color a little differently, so choose with that in mind. The guarantee covers you if it isn't what you hoped for.

I believe there is no good or bad art, only what you feel in front of it. If this flower stops you and you can picture it on your wall, that is the whole test. You don't need to know anything else.

Picture the wall again, with the sofa below it and one orange sunburst above, the dark center drawing the eye in and the petals curling out toward the edges of the frame.

If you can see it there, go to the listing, look at the sizes and finishes, and choose a print.
