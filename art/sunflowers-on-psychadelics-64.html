Ribbon Sunburst – Orange Flower with Dark Center

Botanical & Flowers · vertical

One large orange-yellow flower fills the frame, seen straight on. A dark purple-black center sits just left of middle, and long petals fan out from it in a sunburst, running off the edges of the picture. Between the petals and in the corners, marbled swirls of navy, teal and violet show through.

The petals are what set this flower apart. They wave and curl at the tips like flames or ribbons, and each one is filled with tiny spiral whorls that look like fingerprints. The surface reads as thick, impasto-style paint, and deeper red-orange tones gather around the center to give a soft sense of depth.

Look closely and the flower never quite sits still. Every petal pushes outward from the dark center, and the small eddies inside the petals and background make the whole image feel liquid. It is recognizably a flower, but it has been bent into something more rhythmic and surreal. The mood is warm, energetic and joyful.

The vertical format and strong orange against dark tones give it a sunny, high-impact presence. It suits a wall that needs one confident focal point, such as a living room, entry or a neutral space that could use a burst of warm color.
