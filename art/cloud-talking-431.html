Crimson Bud, Glossy Red and Amber Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A thin amber stalk rises on the left and holds a rounded, glossy crimson bulb that bends to one side, like a drooping bud or a drip caught mid-fall. A curved amber-red form reaches in from the top, and shiny pooled shapes spread along the bottom edge. Soft vertical bands of red and orange blur into a bright cream background.

What sets this piece apart is the single stalk-and-bulb form, which looks like a sprout, a bud or a figure depending on the viewer. Soft backlight makes the translucent shapes glow, and sharp white highlights on their edges give them a wet, polished look. Teal green, cobalt, chartreuse and violet appear in small touches among the crimson and amber.

The forms are non-representational, so the image can be read freely. Look for the calm, pale center that lets the saturated color gather along the bottom and right edges. The crisp foreground separates from the dissolving background through a shallow depth of field. It is a playful, lively piece that works simply as a vivid, glossy study of color.

The horizontal format and bold red and amber accents suit a living room, bedroom, studio or office, especially against neutral walls where the color can be the focal point.
