Crimson Bud, Glossy Red and Amber Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A close-up abstract of glossy, glass-like shapes in red, amber, green and blue. A thin stalk on the left holds up a rounded crimson bulb that curls to one side, and a curved amber form reaches in from the top. Pooled, shiny blobs line the bottom edge, and soft vertical bands of red and orange blur into a bright cream background. Light shines through the shapes and catches sharp highlights on their wet-looking edges.

The story of this pieceA Crimson Bud That Glows: Is It a Sprout or a Drip?One slender stalk, one glossy crimson bulb, and a glow of cream light around them: a small shape you will keep looking at.
