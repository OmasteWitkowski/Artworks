Cloud Talking - Abstract Glass Macro Photography

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Glossy, jewel-toned shapes in red, green, blue and amber look like melted glass. A thin stalk topped with a red-purple bulb rises from a pool of glass at the bottom, and a curved red form hangs above. Soft blurred bands of orange, pink, teal and cream fill the background.

A sharply focused red glass bulb on a slender amber stalk stands out against dreamy, luminous fields of blurred color, which gives the piece a playful, almost sculptural presence.
