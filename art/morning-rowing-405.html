Morning Rowing

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A small red boat with a paddler glides across a still lake in front of a hillside of evergreen trees and purple-toned shrubs. The trees and shoreline reflect in the water below in dark green vertical shapes. The whole scene has a swirled, painterly texture.

A lone red boat on a mirror-still lake anchors this painterly landscape, where lavender shrubs and dark evergreens dissolve into rippling reflections.
