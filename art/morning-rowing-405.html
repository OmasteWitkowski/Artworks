Lilac Stillness, Red Boat on a Mirrored Lake

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A small red boat sits near the center of a still lake. Behind it, a hillside of dark pines and a band of pale lavender shrubs rises in olive and moss green. The water repeats the hillside as a near-mirror image in deeper values, and the shoreline splits the scene almost evenly.

Swirling, brush-like strokes cover every surface, from the slope to the ripples, so the landscape sits between realism and abstraction. The unusual palette of green, lilac and dusty mauve, plus one spot of red and blue at the boat, is what sets it apart from ordinary lake scenes.

Look longer and the tiny boat sets the scale of everything around it. Soft, even light and almost motionless water give the scene a quiet, solitary calm.

The wide horizontal format and muted tones suit a living room, bedroom, study or reading nook, where it adds a settled, restful focal point without a harsh contrast.
