Lilac Stillness, Red Boat on a Mirrored Lake

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A small red boat with a single paddler sits on a still lake below a hillside dotted with evergreen trees. A band of pale purple shrubs runs along the shore, and the slope behind is covered in olive and moss green. The water reflects the trees as dark green vertical shapes. The whole scene is covered in swirling, brush-like strokes that give it a textured, painterly feel.

The story of this pieceOne Tiny Red Boat in a Mirrored Lake of Lilac and GreenA hillside of dark pines and lilac shrubs doubles itself in quiet water, and one small red boat is the only bright note.
