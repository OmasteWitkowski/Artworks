Ember Mirror: Orange and Teal Mandala on Navy

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · square

A glowing circle sits centered on a deep navy square. Its outer ring is made of swirling orange and red forms that look like flames or marbled liquid. At the center, a teal and deep blue field takes the shape of a rounded bowtie. Pairs of claw-like red shapes with green and yellow-green centers face each other above and below it.

The warm and cool colors are sharply opposed. Luminous orange and red wrap around a cool blue-green core, and a soft blue halo lines the circle's edge. The four-way mirror symmetry is exact, and it makes the shapes read as horns, faces or insect-like figures.

Look closely and the surface is smooth but marbled. The orange has a fine cloudy grain, the red carries thin swirling veins, and the blue center is softly streaked. The curling forms move outward around the left and right edges, while the mirrored shapes at top and bottom point inward. The result is energetic movement held in perfect balance.

The square format and dark ground suit a single focal piece. It can hang above a sofa, bed, console or desk, where the orange and red will stand out against calm walls. It also works with other bold, dark-ground work.
