Where the Water Slows, Spring Meadow and Snowy Peak

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A narrow stream spills in white ripples over a low ledge in the foreground, then smooths into a calm blue pool. Beyond it the water winds through a bright green meadow to a dark band of evergreens and a mountain ridge streaked with snow. A cloudless blue sky fills the upper part of the frame, and a pine branch enters at the upper left.

The eye follows the stream from the bottom edge to the peak. The foreground ripple, the mirror-like pool and the snow-dappled ridge make a clear, satisfying path through the picture. Blue, fresh spring green and deep conifer green are set against white water and snow, dusty orange-brown bank soil and pale straw grass in the distance.

Look closer and the textures separate: soft, feathery shrubs and grasses on both banks, the fine vertical lines of the conifers, foam breaking the smooth surface at the weir, and the dry, crumbly bank anchoring the lower left. The light is crisp and even, and the mood is calm, open and fresh.

The wide horizontal format suits a sofa wall, a bedroom, an office or a hallway. The blue sky and greens bring a clear, outdoor brightness to a room.
