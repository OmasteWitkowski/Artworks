The Green Doorway - Sunlit Garden Through Leaves

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A shady, leaf-framed view opens onto a bright green lawn striped with shadows. A low stone wall sits at the left, and a wire fence and patches of flowers lie in the distance. Thick, curving branches with rosy-brown bark arch across the right side, and clusters of white daisies with yellow centers fill the lower right corner. The whole scene has crisp detail and richly saturated greens.
