The Green Doorway - Sunlit Garden Through Leaves

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

Looking out from beneath a canopy of leaves, the eye travels across a bright green lawn striped with long diagonal shadows. A low stone wall rests on the left, with a wire fence and a band of flowers in the distance. Thick, curving branches with rosy-brown bark arch in from the right, and white daisy-like flowers with yellow-orange centers fill the lower right corner.

What sets this garden scene apart is the way the foliage forms an archway around the view. Deep leaf green and olive surround the glowing lime grass, so the bright middle distance pulls you forward. Foreground, middle ground and background are clearly separated, which gives the scene real depth.

The finish is crisp and richly detailed, with sharply outlined leaves, blades of grass and petals and a slightly luminous, painterly quality. Look closely at the ridged, rope-like bark and the pattern of shadow stripes on the lawn.

The mood is calm and sheltered, like a quiet spot in a summer garden where you pause and look out. The curving branches and the path of the lawn make the eye move gently from the lower left to the bright opening.

The horizontal format and saturated greens suit a living room, sunroom, hallway or entry. It can hang above a sofa or console as a fresh, leafy focal point, and the white daisies and gray stone give the green palette some variety.
