Slate Water, Green Wall - River Bend and Pines

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A shallow river curves from the lower left into the distance, breaking into small white rapids over the stones. On the left, a pale gravel bar with a few fallen logs leads to a thick stand of tall evergreens and leafy trees. The right bank is rocky, with green shrubs and dry golden grass. Above it all is a hazy blue sky with thin, streaky clouds.

The river's diagonal S-curve is what holds the picture together. The gravel bar and the rocky bank frame the water, and the eye follows the ripples toward the dark tree line. The palette is deep forest green, slate blue-green water and muted olive, with pale gravel and white rapids as the lightest notes. The surface has a soft, painterly texture.

Look closely and the picture sets movement against stillness. The water ripples and sparkles while the tall trees stand upright and unmoving. Under the even, gentle light there are no harsh shadows, only a quiet, open feeling, like a calm summer day by a river.

This horizontal landscape suits a wide wall above a sofa, a bed or a desk. Its muted greens and blues fit calm, natural interiors in homes, offices and waiting rooms.
