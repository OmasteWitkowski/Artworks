Emberglow - Marbled Orange and Blue Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Swirls of fiery orange, saturated red and golden yellow fill this horizontal abstract edge to edge. Deep cobalt blue frames the top and left edges, and dark navy and burgundy-purple settle into the corners. The color looks liquid and marbled, curling and drifting in every direction. A fine, canvas-like grain lies across the smooth shapes.

What sets it apart is the glow. A bright yellow-orange core sits at the center and lower left, and the cooler edges push it forward. Thin, pale, translucent lines curve across the scene, including one long diagonal and a small vertical form near the middle. They give the color a layered, see-through depth.

The curved lines may remind you of glass or the rim of a lens, but nothing here is a fixed subject. You can follow the diagonal across the swirls or let the warm center pull you in. The mood is energetic and intense, with a feeling of heat and light.

The dense, saturated color suits a wide wall where it can act as the focal point. Over a sofa, a bed or a sideboard it adds a burst of warmth, and the deep blue keeps it balanced.
