Orange Fire, Cobalt Edges, and Lines Like Glass

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Emberglow is a horizontal abstract of liquid-looking orange, red and yellow, crossed by thin translucent lines that make the color look layered.

You bring in the new sofa, step back, and the wall above it looks bare. Or the old picture that fit the old room now looks small. You know the wall needs something. You just haven't seen the right thing yet.

Emberglow is the kind of image that makes a wide wall feel finished. A bright yellow-orange core burns through red swirls, and deep cobalt blue closes in around the edges. It reads from across the room and still has something to give when you walk up to it.

The glow comes first. The brightest yellow-orange sits at the center and lower left, and the cooler blue and dark burgundy at the edges and corners push it forward. The warm color looks lit from inside.

Then the swirls. The color looks liquid and marbled, curling and drifting in every direction. Nothing in it is a fixed subject, so there is no wrong way to look at it.

Then the lines. Thin, pale, translucent curves cross the color, including one long diagonal from upper left to lower center and a small vertical form near the middle. They may remind you of glass or the rim of a lens. I wouldn't tell you they are either. They make the color look layered and see-through, as if you are looking through one sheet of color at another.

Last, the surface. A fine, even grain lies over the smooth swirls, so the liquid shapes have a painterly feel. It is a slow image. The longer you stay with it, the more layers you find.

Emberglow is a horizontal composition, dense and saturated, with a strong warm center. That makes it a focal-point piece rather than a quiet accent. It suits a wide wall: over a sofa, a bed or a sideboard, or in an open entry where it is the first thing you see.

The deep blue and burgundy at the edges keep the heat from running away. The mood is energetic and intense, with a feeling of warmth and light. Its orange, red and yellow suit a room that can use a burst of color, and the blue gives it balance.
