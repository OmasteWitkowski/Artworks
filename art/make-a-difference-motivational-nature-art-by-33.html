The Difference You Choose - Bumblebee on Purple Lupine

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A bumblebee with black, yellow and a touch of orange clings to a tall spike of violet-blue lupine. The petals are velvety and finely veined, and the bee's fur is sharp against a smooth, creamy blur of sage green and faint violet. On the right, a five-line block of deep navy script carries a quote by Jane Goodall.

The bee is the point of sharpest focus, sitting where the flowers meet the open space and the words. The flower spike lifts the eye upward, then the eye moves across the green to the quote. Image and message share one calm, evenly lit frame, so the quote is part of the photograph and not an afterthought.

The quote reads: "What you do makes a difference, and you have to decide what kind of difference you want to make." The bee, small and busy on a single blossom, gives those words a gentle, concrete picture. The mood is quiet, encouraging and bright, and it never turns loud or sentimental.

The soft green and violet palette suits a desk wall, home office, reading nook, sunroom, entry or classroom, anywhere a short, steady reminder is welcome. The horizontal format sits comfortably above a desk, console or shelf.
