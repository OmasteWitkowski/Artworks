Koi Pond Fish - Liquid Delight - by Omaste Witkowski

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A large orange and yellow koi fish swims diagonally across a dark navy background, seen from above. Its scales form a lattice of bright yellow and orange, and pale cream fins fan out on either side. The surface has a soft, painted look with visible brush-like texture.

A luminous orange-and-yellow koi glides diagonally through inky navy water, rendered with a painterly texture that makes the scales and translucent fins glow.
