Green Bend, Forest River With Boulder

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A shallow, clear river with green-tinted water winds through a dense evergreen forest. The left bank is piled with smooth gray and tan stones, and one large rounded boulder rises from the water near the center. A leaning tree arcs across the upper right, and a tangle of dry roots and driftwood sits along the far bank. Small white ripples break across the current in the lower right.
