Green Bend, Forest River With Boulder

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A clear, shallow river winds through a dense evergreen forest. Its water is a translucent green-turquoise, and the pebbled streambed shows through. Smooth gray and tan stones pile along the left bank, and one large rounded boulder stands in the current near the center. Small white ripples break across the water in the lower right.

The glow of the water against the dark conifers makes this image stand out. Deep forest green, dark olive and moss green surround the bright river. Touches of rust brown and orange-red foliage add warmth, and a leaning tree crosses the upper right on a diagonal. Pale boulders catch the light while the canopy stays in shade.

Look closer and the textures separate: rounded river stones, feathery evergreen boughs, and a tangle of dry roots and driftwood on the far bank. The river curves away toward a bend in the valley, so the eye travels from the stony foreground into the distance. The finish is soft and slightly painterly. The mood is calm, quiet and fresh, an unhurried wooded riverside.

The dark, low-key palette gives a wall depth, and the luminous water gives the eye a place to settle. This horizontal landscape suits a living room, bedroom, study or lodge-style space, above a sofa, console or mantel.
