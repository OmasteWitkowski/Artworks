Irises That Look Poured: Gold, Burgundy and a Liquid Swirl

Botanical & Flowers · vertical

Iris Whirl is a tall digital artwork of two ruffled bearded irises ringed with white daisies, with a marbled swirl running through the whole garden.

You know the wall. It might be the hallway, the stretch beside a window, or the space over a console. The paint is settled and the furniture is where it belongs, and that one tall, narrow wall still looks unfinished.

Picture something warm and tall there. Two irises stand one above the other, the tops glowing golden orange and the lower petals falling in deep burgundy, with white daisies gathered around them. The whole garden looks as if it were poured.

I'll tell you what I'd point out if we were standing in front of it. First you see the color: gold-orange against wine-red, set in soft green, with white and yellow daisies at the edges and corners.

Then you notice the texture. A swirling, marbled ripple runs across the ruffled petals and through the green behind them. It is easiest to see on the burgundy. The flowers stay recognizable, but they look as if they were painted in flowing oils.

Stay longer and the picture starts to move. The upper iris sits a little to the right and the lower one a little to the left, so your eye zigzags down the stack while the swirl turns gently around the petals.

Many iris pictures are crisp and still. This one is not. The light is soft and even, with no hard shadows, so the gold tops glow against the darker petals and the shaded green.

This is a vertical statement floral. It suits a hallway, an entry, a stairwell wall, the space beside a window or the wall over a console. The strong warm color and the stacked composition hold attention across a room.

The green keeps it grounded among your furniture, while the gold and burgundy bring the color. The mood is lively and warm, a celebration of garden flowers in bloom.
