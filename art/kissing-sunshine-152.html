Iris Whirl, Gold and Burgundy Irises

Botanical & Flowers · vertical

Two bearded irises stand one above the other in the center of a tall frame. The upper petals glow golden orange, and the lower ones fall in deep burgundy. White daisies with yellow centers and green leaves gather at the edges and corners.

What sets this piece apart is the texture. A swirling, marbled ripple runs across the ruffled petals and the green behind them, most visible on the burgundy, so the garden looks as if it were painted in flowing oils. The irises stay recognizable and rich in color against the soft, out-of-focus background.

Look longer and the composition begins to move. The upper iris sits slightly right and the lower slightly left, and the eye zigzags down the stack while the swirl turns gently around the petals. Soft, even light lets the gold-orange tops glow against the darker burgundy and shaded green.

Its vertical format and warm saturated palette suit a hallway, entry, or a narrow wall beside a window or over a console. The green keeps it grounded in a room, and the gold and burgundy bring color.
