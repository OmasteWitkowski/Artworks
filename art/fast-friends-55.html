Cobalt Ember, Glowing Blue and Amber Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A deep cobalt blue fills the right side of the frame while a glowing band of amber, gold and rust-brown spreads across the left and center. Rounded arches and rings in blue, green and yellow curve through the lower and upper left, and bright white glints run along the edges of the warm area.

The color looks lit from within, as if light were passing through glass or clear resin. The blue absorbs light and reads as dark and velvety, which makes the amber seem to burn brighter beside it.

Look closer and the surface changes from place to place. Small clear bubbles cluster in the upper left. A fine, cracked, net-like pattern shows in the orange and violet center. Long veined streaks in the amber resemble wood grain or agate banding, and ribbed ripples run across the blue.

Horizontal smears sweep through the middle and bend outward to the right, while the curved arches below add a rolling rhythm. The effect is warm and busy on one side and calm and deep on the other. It is a piece to enjoy simply for its color and glow.

Its horizontal format and strong blue-and-gold contrast suit a focal wall above a sofa, bed, console or desk, especially in a room that can use a saturated accent.
