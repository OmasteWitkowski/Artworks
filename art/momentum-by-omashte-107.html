Momentum - Teal Weir Under a Cobalt Sky

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A river drops over a low weir in the foreground, sending white foam across teal water. Beyond it the surface calms into golden and blue reflections of a bank lined with bare trees and dry grass. A few houses sit among the trees on the right, under a deep blue sky streaked with thin clouds. The photo is framed in dark navy with the script word 'Momentum' and a short motivational line below.

The story of this pieceRushing Water and Mirror-Calm, in One FrameChurning teal water, a mirror-still reach upstream and an intensely blue sky meet in one navy-framed print with the word Momentum in script.
