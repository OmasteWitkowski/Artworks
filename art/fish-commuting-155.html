Ruby Drip: Glowing Translucent Color Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Soft rounded shapes in olive green, cobalt blue, violet and glossy red float against a pale grey-white background. The shapes look translucent and glassy, lit from behind so the colours glow. A tall red form with a dripping tip stands at the right, while a blurred purple circle sits in the open center. Some edges are crisp and others are dreamy and out of focus.
