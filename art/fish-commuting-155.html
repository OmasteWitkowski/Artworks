Ruby Drip: Glowing Translucent Color Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Rounded, translucent shapes float against a pale grey-white background: an olive-green form at upper left, a blue oval over green below it, a soft purple circle at the center and a tall glossy red form at the right. Light seems to pass through them, so each color glows from within, with small white highlights along the edges.

What sets this piece apart is the mix of sharp and soft. The red form with its dripping tip and the green shape with its fine hairline creases and tiny droplets are crisp. The purple circle between them melts into a blurred field of color. The colors are saturated, but the pale, open ground keeps the image bright and light.

Look longer and the shapes seem to drift, with a slight downward pull where the red form drips to a point. Pink, teal, amber and lavender-blue notes turn up along the edges. The mood is playful and cheerful, and the colors and glow are enough reason to enjoy it.

The horizontal format and open pale center suit a living room, bedroom, studio or office, where the red and blue can pick up accents in the room while the pale ground keeps the wall calm.
