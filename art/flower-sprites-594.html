Flower Sprites

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A close-up of what looks like a flower, so blurred that it reads as overlapping bands of pink, coral and red. Yellow-green fills the lower left and deep red the right edge. A small area of sharper green and orange detail sits at the top right.

Petals dissolve into glowing, silky ribbons of pink, coral and red, with one small patch of sharp detail that hints at the flower behind the colour.
