Cobalt Crest, Kaleidoscope Abstract in Blue and Crimson

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · horizontal

A crimson mirrored shape sits at the center of a violet and periwinkle field. Around it, cream, peach and coral swirls curl outward, dotted with tiny green-and-teal spirals. A deep royal blue border frames the whole composition, and golden-yellow arches glow at the top and bottom.

The symmetry is exact, left to right and top to bottom, so the eye returns to the dark crimson center again and again. The cream and golden areas look backlit against the saturated blue. The surfaces are smooth, glossy and liquid-looking, with soft feathered edges where colors meet.

Look closely and the center can read as a butterfly, a pair of wings or an ornate mask. That is an impression, not a depicted subject. The scrolled forms in each corner repeat the same curling rhythm, which feels pulsing and radiating yet held in balance.

The warm coral and peach against cool cobalt gives it a rich, jewel-toned energy. It is a piece to enjoy for its color, ornament and symmetry.

Its horizontal format and dark blue frame suit a wall above a sofa, bed or console, or a plain wall that needs one strong focal point.
