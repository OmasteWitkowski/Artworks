The Circle of Life Abstract Mandala Artwork by Omaste Witkowski

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A symmetrical abstract made of mirrored, flowing swirls. A deep red winged shape sits in the center of a blue and lavender core. Glowing cream, peach and orange curls surround it against a rich dark blue background, with small green spirals near the corners.

Rich cobalt blue and glowing peach-gold swirls mirror around a red central form, making a luminous, jewel-like symmetrical abstract with the pull of a kaleidoscope.
