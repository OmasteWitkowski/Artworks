Cobalt Crest, Kaleidoscope Abstract in Blue and Crimson

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · horizontal

A horizontal abstract with perfect mirror symmetry in both directions. A dark crimson, butterfly-like shape sits at the center of a violet and lavender field. Around it, glowing cream, peach and coral swirls with tiny green spiral accents form scrolled shapes in each corner. A deep royal blue border surrounds everything, and golden-yellow arches add a bright glow at the top and bottom.

The story of this pieceThe Crimson Center That Reads as Wings, Then a MaskA dark crimson shape glows at the heart of a mirrored world of cobalt, violet, peach and cream.
