Red Scorpion - Tribal Scorpion Abstract Art

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

A red scorpion drawn in a bold, stylized tribal design, facing the viewer with its claws open and its tail arched high above. The body is made of smooth, curving shapes separated by thin white lines. The red fill is mottled and swirled in deeper reds and touches of orange, all set against a plain white background.

A fierce, graphic scorpion in glowing, marbled red against clean white, where the sharp tribal line work contrasts with the swirling abstract texture inside the shapes.
