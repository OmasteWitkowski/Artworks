Ember Sting, Red Tribal Scorpion

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · square

A red scorpion is drawn head-on as a bold, symmetrical silhouette on a plain white square. Its segmented tail sweeps up and over the body to a sharp stinger, and two large claws and a fan of jointed legs spread across the lower half of the image. Curved, tapering white lines separate the claws, tail segments and body.

The scorpion is a crisp tribal-tattoo shape filled with something that feels almost molten. The red is mottled and swirling, like smoke or marbled stone, moving between deep crimson, dark maroon, bright scarlet and touches of burnt orange. The white outlines and background stay completely flat and smooth, so the glowing fill stands out.

Look at how the white cut-lines draw the anatomy through negative space, and how the S-shaped curve of the tail pulls the eye from the body up to the stinger. The pointed tips and spread claws give the scorpion a coiled, ready-to-strike energy. It is fierce and dangerous in feel, and for anyone drawn to the Scorpio sign it is a natural symbol, though that is a general association and nothing in the image states it.

On a wall, the centered motif and wide white margin make it a clear focal point. It suits a den, office, studio or entry, and it works well in a symmetrical pair or group.
