Ember Sting, Red Tribal Scorpion

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · square

A red scorpion is drawn as a bold, symmetrical, tattoo-style silhouette on a plain white background. Its tail curls high over its body to a sharp point, and its two big claws and several legs spread wide across the lower part of the image. White curving lines separate the body, claws and tail segments. The red fill looks marbled and smoky, shifting between deep crimson, maroon and touches of orange.
