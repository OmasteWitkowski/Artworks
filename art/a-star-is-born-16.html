Tiny Golden Whirlpools Hiding in a Field of Cobalt Blue

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Golden Whirlpools in Cobalt Blue is a glossy blue and orange abstract with spirals you only find when you look closely.

You know the wall. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and that one stretch of wall still looks bare every time you walk past it. Whatever goes there has to be big enough to hold the room, and it has to be a colour you will want to see every day.

This piece is a candidate for that wall. Deep cobalt blue fills the ground and golden orange ribbons curl across it, pooling into small spirals. The surface looks wet and polished, as if the colour has only just stopped moving.

There is no single place to start. The first thing most people see is the colour: saturated gold against deep blue, the two sitting opposite each other so the gold looks lit from within. Then the eye settles on the centre, where a dark navy channel branches out. To me it looks like a star, or like a river delta seen from above. That is how it reads to me, and you may see something else.

Around that channel the gold gathers into clusters of small spirals and eddies, above and below. Look closer and each one is a tiny whirlpool, with fine marbled lines running inside the curls. Rounded blue pools gather along the bottom and on the right, glossy enough to look like they hold the light.

Blue currents run diagonally from the left toward the upper right, while the orange ribbons loop back on themselves. At the right edge there is one black circular opening, and it pulls the eye out of the flow. Then the eye wanders back in and finds another spiral it missed.

I have left out a made-up backstory on purpose. What I can tell you honestly is what is in the image, and the image rewards a longer look.

It is a horizontal, full-bleed image, so it suits a wide wall: above a sofa, a bed, a console or a desk. The blue and gold are strong enough to be the main colour of the room. Cream, warm wood, grey and white walls would all let the colour stand out.

Its mood is energetic, vivid and a little mesmerizing. It is not quiet wallpaper. It is a piece you choose because the colour stopped you, and that is a good enough reason to choose it.
