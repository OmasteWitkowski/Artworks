Golden Whirlpools in Cobalt Blue

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ribbons of golden orange curl into spirals and eddies across a deep cobalt ground. Glossy blue pools gather along the bottom and right, and a dark navy branching channel crosses the center like a star or a river delta. Fine marbled striations run inside the swirls, and small white and pale pink highlights sparkle on the surfaces.

The gold spirals look like tiny whirlpools, and the saturated blue and orange make them glow against the shadows. The surface looks wet, polished and dimensional, with rounded bubble-like pools that appear to hold light. A black circular opening at the right edge draws the eye out of the flow.

There is no single focal point, so the eye keeps traveling. Blue currents run diagonally toward the upper right while the orange ribbons loop back on themselves. The dark central channel anchors the composition, and small details reward a longer look.

Its horizontal, full-bleed format suits a living room, bedroom, office or hospitality space, where the deep blue and warm gold can carry the color of the whole room.
