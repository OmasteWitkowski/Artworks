Yoga Pose

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal abstract image made of mirrored, curving shapes in vivid red, teal, green and orange. Thin glowing neon outlines trace the loops, which surround a dark, nearly black center. A blue band runs across the top and soft pink panels sit at the lower sides.

Mirrored, neon-edged loops in saturated red, teal and green surround a dark central axis, giving the piece a bold, graphic presence and a fluid, glowing depth.
