Ember Burst: Marbled Orange Star in a Dark Oval

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A jagged, many-pointed star glows at the center of a dark navy oval on a black field. Its surface swirls with orange, golden yellow and deep red, with touches of teal, cobalt and pale green toward the tips. Eleven small round shapes circle the star. Each holds a smaller circle that reads as an eye or a crescent moon. Thin dark outlines separate every shape.

The star's points are uneven in length and angle, so the burst feels spontaneous rather than mechanical. The color inside each shape looks poured, flowing in curving ribbons and fine wavy streaks. A hot core fades toward the tips and gives the star a backlit glow against the flat, near-black ground.

Look longer and the small ring shapes shift between bubbles, eyes and moons. They orbit the star with a gentle rhythm while the points push outward. The overall feeling is warm, energetic and celebratory, like a radiant sun or a firework with a playful, cosmic edge.

The dark oval acts as a vignette, so the piece works as a bold focal point on a calm wall. It suits a living room, studio, office or bedroom where a burst of warm color is wanted against quiet or dark surroundings.
