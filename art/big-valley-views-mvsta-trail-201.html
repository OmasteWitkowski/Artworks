Skyward Pines, Sunlit Path

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · vertical

A dirt path starts wide at the bottom of the frame and narrows into the distance between very tall, slender pine trunks. The trunks are reddish-brown and lean slightly as they climb toward a bright blue sky with white clouds. Sun and shadow dapple the pale path and the yellow-green grass along its edges.

The viewpoint is low, so the trees tower overhead and the trunks on both sides frame an opening of sky. A large diagonal trunk cuts across the upper right. Short, curved, brush-like strokes follow the bark, needles, grass and path, giving a realistic scene a swirling, oil-painting surface.

The longer you look, the more the picture moves. The trunks pull the eye upward, the path leads it back into the distance, and the foliage seems to shimmer in the filtered light. The mood is calm, open and fresh, like a quiet walk in the woods.

The vertical format suits a tall wall, an entry or a hallway. Its olive greens, sky blue and warm bark browns work well in living spaces, cabins and offices that want a natural, restful look.
