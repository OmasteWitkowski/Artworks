Moss and Ember, Translucent Green and Coral Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A close-up abstract of smooth, translucent shapes in glowing green and coral-orange. Rounded green fields sit in the upper left and lower right, separated by a curving band of salmon pink. Thin dark lines outline the folds, and a small ringed, bubble-like detail sits near the center. Fine speckles and hairline creases add subtle texture to the glassy surface.

The story of this pieceThe small ringed eye hiding in a glow of green and coralOlive green folds, a backlit-looking ribbon of coral, and one small ringed detail that keeps drawing your eye back.
