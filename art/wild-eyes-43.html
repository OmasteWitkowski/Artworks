Moss and Ember, Translucent Green and Coral Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Rounded fields of olive and moss green meet curving ribbons of coral and salmon in this abstract close-up. The shapes look smooth and translucent, folding over one another with thin dark lines tracing their edges. Fine speckles dot the green areas, and hairline creases cross the glassy surface.

What sets it apart is the glow. The forms seem backlit, warm orange through the pink and cooler green toward the edges of the frame. Near the center, just right of the middle, a small ringed pocket sits like a bubble or a tiny eye.

Look longer and the sweeping diagonals pull the eye from lower left to upper right. The folded shapes seem to press and flow against each other, and the ringed detail gives the eye a place to rest.

The warm, earthy palette suits a living room, bedroom or study, especially beside wood, linen or plants. The horizontal format works well over a sofa, bed or console.
