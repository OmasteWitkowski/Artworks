The red seam that glows like a hot coal

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

Ember Seam is a square abstract in deep red, burnt orange, teal and cobalt, with one bright seam holding it together.

You bought the new sofa, and now the wall above it looks bare. Or it's the console in the entry, where you walk past an empty space every day. The room is finished and the colours are settled. That one wall is still waiting.

Picture something there that is warm and has a glow. This is Ember Seam. Two translucent shapes meet at a red seam in the middle, and your eye goes to that seam first.

I'll start with what is on the surface. Deep red and burnt orange frame the left, right and bottom edges. Above the centre sits a rounded, hourglass-like shape with a bright rim. Below it is a dome of teal green and cobalt blue.

Where the two shapes meet, the seam is red and speckled, and it glows like a hot coal. The thin edges around the shapes carry fringes of yellow, blue and magenta. The centres look lit from within, and the corners fall into dark shadow.

Look closer and the surface keeps changing. Fine scratch-like lines cross the glassy areas. Feathery green brushing fills the dome. Tiny bubble-like droplets gather on the left and along the seam. Crisp edges sit next to soft blur.

The curves seem to stretch and pinch toward the middle, and the streaks in the dome run upward with a ragged, flame-like crest. It moves slowly, like something liquid. What it means is yours to decide. I'd rather you stand in front of it than have me explain it.

Ember Seam is a square, centred, high-colour piece. It suits a wall where you want one warm, saturated accent: above a sofa or console, in an entry, or in an office that could use more colour.

The red and orange bring warmth, and the teal and blue keep it from feeling flat. Colour this strong works best as the one bold thing on the wall, so I'd let it stand alone.
