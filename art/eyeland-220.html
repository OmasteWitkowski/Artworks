Ember Seam, Glowing Red and Teal Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

A square close-up of glowing, glass-like shapes in deep red, burnt orange, teal and cobalt. A rounded, hourglass-like form with a bright outline sits at the upper centre. Below it, a dome of blue and green streaks rises with a ragged, flame-like crest. The two shapes are set against soft, blurred fields of red and orange.

What sets this piece apart is the red, speckled seam where the two forms meet. It glows like a hot coal, and thin edges of yellow, blue and magenta light fringe the outlines. The cool teal core against the warm frame pulls the eye straight to the lower centre.

Look closer and the surfaces change. Fine scratch-like lines cross the glassy areas, feathery green brushing fills the dome, and tiny bubble-like droplets gather on the left and along the seam. Crisp edges sit beside soft blur, and the corners fall into dark shadow. The effect is a slow, liquid movement, with the curves seeming to stretch and pinch toward the middle.

The mood is vivid and warm, with a calm glow at the centre. Its strong colour is the main attraction.

The square format and saturated palette let it hold a wall as a single focal point. It suits a living room, entry or office that wants a warm, jewel-toned accent.
