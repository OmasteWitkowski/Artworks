Ember Drift, Glowing Red Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A bold red, leaf-like shape slants across the center of this horizontal abstract. It looks translucent and lit from behind, set among soft fields of golden yellow and amber orange. A cobalt blue orb sits at upper left, a folded maroon and amber form at lower left, and a golden teardrop shape below the red.

What sets it apart is the glow. Bright yellow-white light pools at the upper left and center, and darker, saturated edges outline each form, so the image has a stained-glass luminosity. The small blue orb is the only strong cool note in an otherwise warm composition.

Look closer and the surfaces are smooth and glossy, scattered with tiny bubbles and crossed by hairline curves. The red form pulls the eye along a diagonal from upper left to lower right, and the swirling color fields around it keep the gaze drifting. There is no horizon and no fixed scale, so the shapes can read as leaf, fish, flame or pure color.

This piece is meant to be enjoyed for its color and light. It suits a living room, studio, bedroom or entry that needs one warm, energetic focal point, and its red and gold pair well with neutral walls, dark wood or cool blue accents.
