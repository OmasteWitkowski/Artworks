A Red Shape That Glows Like Stained Glass From Behind

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Ember Drift is a horizontal abstract photograph in which one saturated red form, lit from within, slants across fields of gold and amber.

You know the wall. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and that one stretch of wall still looks unfinished. Nothing you have found so far has felt like it belonged there.

Now picture a single bold red shape glowing against warm gold, as if light were coming through it. That is Ember Drift. It does not blend into a room. It gives the room something to look at.

At first you see color: saturated red, golden yellow, amber orange. Then your eye settles on the red form, tilted from upper left to lower right. It looks see-through, as though it were lit from behind.

Next you notice the small things. A cobalt blue orb sits at upper left and is the only strong cool note in the warm picture. A folded maroon and amber shape sits at lower left, and a golden teardrop floats below the red.

Look closer and the surfaces are smooth and glossy. Tiny bubbles are scattered through them, and hairline curves cross the color. There is no horizon and no fixed scale, so the red shape can read as a leaf, a fish, a flame or just color. I would rather leave that to you. Art is felt, and what you see in it is yours to decide.

Ember Drift is a horizontal piece with one clear focal shape, so it suits a wall that needs a single warm statement. Think of the space above a sofa, a console or a bed, or the end of a hallway.

Its red and gold sit well against neutral walls and dark wood, and the blue orb picks up cool accents nearby. It is a good fit for a living room, studio, bedroom or entry.
