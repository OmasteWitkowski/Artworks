Glass and Lace - Stained Glass Butterfly

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

A single butterfly spreads its wings at the center of a horizontal canvas, perfectly mirrored left to right. Its body and wings are built from glossy, translucent-looking cells in brick red, seafoam teal, cobalt blue, golden yellow and amber, much like backlit colored glass. Small cobalt dots mark the lower wings.

What sets this piece apart is the white web laid over the glass. Crisp, angular lines fan out in triangles and rays and make the butterfly look like glass and lace at once. A hand-drawn-looking white frame of line clusters surrounds it, inside a thin red-brown border. Red, white and blue keep a very busy pattern clear, so the butterfly always stays the focal point.

Look closely and the surfaces change. The wing cells have soft gradients inside hard outlines, and a faint grain runs through the red areas. The white lines stay bright and flat, like cut paper. The lines radiate outward while the wings hold still, which gives the piece a lively, festive energy.

Its bold red field and strong white linework suit a living room, dining area, bedroom or entry. It works best where a horizontal, graphic piece is meant to be the color anchor of the wall.
