A Butterfly That Looks Like Glass and Lace at Once

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

Glass and Lace is my stained glass butterfly, a digital illustration in brick red, white and cobalt blue, available as a print made to order.

You bought the sofa. The paint is done. And the wall above it is still the one thing in the room that looks unfinished. Maybe the old picture looks small now, or maybe you just keep walking past that empty space.

This is the piece I would put there if you want the wall to have a color and a center. A single butterfly sits in the middle with its wings open, in deep brick red, crisp white and cobalt blue. It reads from across the room, and it still has plenty to show you up close.

The first thing you see is the butterfly, perfectly mirrored left to right. Then you notice what it is made of. Its body and wings are built from small glossy cells in red, seafoam teal, golden yellow and amber, and they look like colored glass with light behind it.

Then comes the second discovery. A web of crisp white lines is laid over the glass. The lines fan out in triangles and rays, like cut paper or lace. The butterfly looks like glass and lace at the same time, and I can't make my eye settle on just one.

Look closer and the surfaces change. Each wing cell has a soft gradient inside a hard outline. A faint grain runs through the red. Small cobalt dots mark the lower wings. The white lines stay bright and flat while the wings hold perfectly still, so the whole piece feels lively without being noisy.

A thin red-brown border frames it, and inside that border a hand-drawn-looking white frame of angular line clusters surrounds the butterfly. The pattern is busy, but the limited palette keeps the butterfly clear as the focal point.

Glass and Lace is a horizontal piece, so it suits the wide wall spaces: above a sofa, a bed, a console or a desk. The deep red field and bright white linework let it hold a wall by itself, and it works best where you want one graphic piece to be the color anchor.

The mood is lively, ornate, playful and festive. It fits a living room, dining area, bedroom or entry where you want some bold decorative energy. It is a piece to enjoy for how it looks, and that is reason enough to want it.
