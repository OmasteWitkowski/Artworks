Amber Current - Radiating Blue and Gold Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal abstract of swirling, glossy color in amber, burnt orange and gold, cut through with bright cobalt and electric blue. Streaks radiate outward from a smooth brown center, where a curved blue-white sweep with a pointed tip stands out. Curling ripples and feathery wave patterns drift along the left and right sides. The whole surface looks fluid and lit from within, like molten glass or liquid in motion.

The story of this pieceThe Blue Sweep That Cuts Through a Glowing Amber SwirlWarm amber and cool cobalt meet in a glossy, radiating swirl that keeps the eye moving around one bright blue sweep.
