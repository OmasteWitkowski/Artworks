Amber Current - Radiating Blue and Gold Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Streaks of amber, burnt orange and golden yellow radiate outward from a smooth, glossy brown center, cut through with bands of cobalt and electric blue. A bright blue arc crosses the top of the horizontal frame. Small curling ripples and feathery wave patterns drift along the left and right sides.

What sets this piece apart is the contrast between warm and cool. A curved blue-white sweep with a pointed tip stands out against the amber and brown at the center. The colors look lit from within, with a glassy sheen, as though liquid were caught mid-motion.

Look closely and the surface rewards it: fine combed streaks in the radiating bands, tiny droplet-like beads near the top center, and a small patch of coarse grain at the right edge. The central sweep may suggest a wing or a beak, but nothing is depicted. It is simply color in motion, and the pleasure is visual.

Its saturated warmth and strong central focus suit a living room, studio, office or entry. It works well as a single statement piece on a wall that needs energy, and it pairs naturally with neutral or deep navy surroundings.
