Cobalt Ember - Rippled Blue and Gold Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Tall vertical bands of cobalt blue, bright sky blue, golden yellow, amber orange and pink ripple down a horizontal frame. Cool blue and white fill the left, deep cobalt fills the right, and a warm glowing section sits between them. Thin dark jagged lines separate the color areas, and a dark maroon curve runs along the bottom edge.

The color and surface make this piece distinctive. Pale white and pink highlights stand out against deep blue and near-black navy, and the colors look luminous, almost like light through stained glass. A fine, wavy, swirled texture covers the whole image. Small curling squiggles trace the edges of the bands, and the blue at the upper right gathers into a gentle spiral.

There is no single focal point. The eye moves across the color transitions, from cool to warm and back to cool. The bands flow downward in a flame-like, dripping rhythm, and the dark curve at the bottom holds the movement in place. The mood is energetic and lively, a warm glow set against cool depth.

The wide format suits a sofa wall, a bedroom headboard wall, a sideboard or a hallway. The blues give it calm structure, and the warm center gives the room a bright point of color.
