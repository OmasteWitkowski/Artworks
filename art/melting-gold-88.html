Ember Currents, Marbled Orange and Red Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ribbons of orange, gold and deep red swirl across the whole image like molten glass or marbled liquid. Bright cream highlights trace the folds, while darker brown and olive-black areas gather in the upper right. Fine vertical streaks and a grainy, scuffed texture overlay the smooth curves. The effect is glowing, fluid and energetic, with no recognizable objects.
