Ember Currents, Marbled Orange and Red Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ribbons of fiery orange, amber gold and deep crimson swirl across the whole canvas, folding and doubling back on themselves like liquid marble. Bright cream-yellow highlights trace the edges of each fold, while dark brown pockets and a patch of olive and charcoal gather in the upper right. Small flecks of teal and touches of rust appear in the flow.

What sets this piece apart is the glow. The colors seem lit from within, and the smooth glossy bands are crossed by fine vertical streaks and a grainy, scuffed speckle, as if light were passing through weathered glass. The warm reds and golds of the left and center are balanced by the darker, cooler upper right, so the image feels dramatic rather than uniform.

Look longer and the eye travels the currents as they sweep up from the left toward the upper center and then to the right, circling through eddies and whirlpools. There is no single focal point and no recognizable subject, only warmth, energy and intensity in color and motion. The straight vertical streaks along both edges set up a counter-rhythm to the curves.

The horizontal, edge-to-edge format suits a wall that needs a strong warm presence, such as above a sofa, a bed or a console. In a neutral, dark or wood-toned room, the glowing reds and golds become the focus of the space.
