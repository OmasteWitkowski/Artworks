Paddle Pause - Colorful Kayaks on a Rust Red Shore

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A row of colorful kayaks in yellow, teal, pink, red, blue and white rests on a reddish-brown pebble shore beside a calm lake. Tall evergreens line the left side and a low forest edge runs along the far shore, with a small peak on the horizon. A deep blue sky with thin white clouds fills the top of the frame. The colors are rich and saturated, and the surfaces have a slightly painterly finish.

The story of this pieceWhy Do These Kayaks Glow Against a Rust-Red Shore?Yellow, teal, hot pink, red, blue and white kayaks wait on a rust-red shore beneath a deep blue sky.
