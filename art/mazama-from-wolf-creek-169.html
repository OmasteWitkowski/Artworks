Lavender Hush: Snowy Blue Ridges Panorama

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · panoramic horizontal

A wide landscape shows a meadow of purple-gray grasses and scrubby bushes in the foreground, with a tall dry shrub standing out at left of center. Dark green evergreens line the middle distance, and layered blue mountains with snow on their upper slopes rise behind them. A pale blue sky with a soft, textured look fills the top of the image. The whole scene has a swirling, painted-looking texture and a cool blue and lavender palette with small touches of fresh green.

The story of this pieceA Lavender Meadow Under Snow-Dusted Blue RidgesA mauve meadow meets cool blue, snow-dusted peaks in a long, quiet panorama with a soft, brush-like surface.
