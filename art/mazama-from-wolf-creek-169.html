Lavender Hush: Snowy Blue Ridges Panorama

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · panoramic horizontal

A wide mountain panorama opens over a meadow of dusty lavender-gray and mauve grasses. A tall dry bush stands just left of center, a line of dark evergreens runs through the middle distance, and layered blue ridges with snow on their upper slopes rise beneath a pale, hazy sky.

The color is what sets this scene apart. The mauve meadow and deep indigo ridges give it a cool, soft palette, lifted by small patches of fresh spring yellow-green. A fine, swirling, brush-like texture runs across the whole surface, with short curved strokes in the grasses and a faint canvas-weave look in the sky.

Look longer and the ridgeline dips in a gentle V at the center, guiding the eye across the horizon. The snow on the peaks catches the brightest light while the distant ridges fall into blue shadow. The even daylight gives the scene a calm, open, quiet feeling of high-country space.

The long horizontal format suits a wide wall above a sofa, bed or sideboard, or along a hallway or office wall. Its cool, muted palette works in rooms that call for something restful.
