Emerald Wedge - Luminous Abstract Glass Close-Up

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Angular and curving planes of translucent, glass-like color fill the frame edge to edge. Emerald and cobalt dominate the top. Burnt orange, golden yellow and dusty rose warm the lower half, with milky white and clear passages between. Light seems to pass through the surfaces, so the colors glow, and bright white highlights run along the edges and creases.

A pointed green wedge at upper left cuts through the blue and orange fields and pulls the eye across and down the frame. Crisp, sharp edges sit beside zones that dissolve into soft color. Small bubbles or flecks in the yellow and clear areas keep the surface looking wet, polished and slightly rippled.

There is no recognizable object or figure, so the image works as pure color, light and structure. Look closely at the folds, where deeper greens and blues gather, and at the blue and orange bands on the right, which add a sense of flow.

Its horizontal, full-bleed format suits a wall that needs a vivid focal point, such as over a sofa, a console or a desk. The cool upper half and warm lower half hold up well against neutral walls.
