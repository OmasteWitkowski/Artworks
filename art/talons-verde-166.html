Emerald Wedge - Luminous Abstract Glass Close-Up

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A close-up abstract of what looks like colored glass, filled edge to edge with angular and curved translucent planes. Rich greens and cobalt blues dominate the top, while orange, yellow and dusty rose warm the lower half. Light glows through the surfaces and flashes white along the edges. Small bubbles and flecks are visible in some areas, while others blur softly.
