Where to Look Next, Abstract with Jonas Salk Quote

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Flowing, overlapping bands of royal blue, violet and forest green sweep across a horizontal canvas. Thin white and pale lavender filaments gather at a bright point just right of centre, then spread out toward the right in curving lines. Faint smoky wisps drift over the colour, and a fine, canvas-like texture covers the whole image.

The centre of the piece is the glowing convergence where the green, blue and purple fields meet. The white light seems to come from behind the colour, so the bands look almost translucent against the deeper navy and violet. Touches of magenta and yellow-green add warmth to the cool palette.

Below the image, a solid deep blue strip carries two centred lines of light serif type: 'Intuition will tell the thinking mind where to look next', attributed to Jonas Salk. The quote suits the image's sweeping movement toward a single bright point, and it asks for a slower, more reflective look.

The horizontal format and the calm colour make it a good fit above a sofa, console or desk, or in a study or studio. The glow holds attention from across a room, and the quote rewards a closer look.
