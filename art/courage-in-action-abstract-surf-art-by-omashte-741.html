Courage In Action Abstract Surf Art by Omashte

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

A blue silhouette of a person lying in a backbend pose on a wave stretches across a white oval, with two palm trees at left and a large sun made of orange and yellow dots behind. Curving bands of blue, green and yellow suggest water and hills, and an italic quote about courage by Bethany Hamilton sits beneath the scene. The oval is surrounded by a rainbow-toned border covered in black interlacing organic shapes.

A tropical beach scene with a yoga-posed figure on a wave beneath a dotted sun, set inside a bold, tangled black-and-rainbow frame and paired with a courage quote.
