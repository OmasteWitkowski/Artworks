Smiley Face

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A horizontal abstract of flowing, liquid-looking shapes in hot red, orange and yellow with deep purple. A big yellow crescent with cream swirls sits at the center, outlined by a dark purple curve, while wavy orange forms fill the left and a red area with dark star-like patterns sits at lower right.

A glossy, high-contrast fluid abstract where a glowing yellow crescent and deep purple voids pull against waves of fiery red and orange.
