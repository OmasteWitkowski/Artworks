Amber Meets Blue Reach: Fluid Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

On the left, a glossy, layered form in olive, bronze, amber and orange rises out of a warm yellow-orange glow. Its outline is rough and rippled, and a small speckled patch sits near its right side. On the right, flat slate-blue shapes spread into branching, finger-like arms, each edged with a thin red line. A pale beige field lies between them.

What sets this piece apart is the contrast of surfaces. The left form looks polished and reflective, with fine contour ripples like poured enamel or wood grain. The blue shapes and the beige ground are smooth and matte. Thin red outlines run through both sides and connect them, and red pools along the bottom edge.

Let your eye start at the detailed left form, cross the quiet beige center and follow the blue shapes as they reach toward the lower right. The forms stretch, swirl and drip as if currents were bending around each other. The left form might read as a head or a mask, or as nothing in particular. The mood is energetic, warm and a little playful.

The horizontal format suits a wall that can take color, such as above a sofa, a bed or a console. The beige center keeps the saturated oranges and the single blue from crowding the room.
