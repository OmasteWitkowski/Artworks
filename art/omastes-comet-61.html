Amber Comet, Glowing Abstract in Cobalt Blue

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A glowing amber and gold teardrop sweeps diagonally across a deep cobalt blue field. It ends in a dark brown-ringed oval with a pale, star-like cream burst at its centre. Layers of green, yellow-green and teal form a halo around it, and tiny bubbles and specks scatter through the blue.

What sets this piece apart is its single, clear focal point. The bright centre of the oval draws the eye, and the amber streak leads you to it. The form is cropped at the upper left, so it seems to carry on beyond the frame. Depending on the viewer, it reads as a comet, an eye or a luminous drop.

Look closer and fine, curved, hair-like lines run through the green halo and wrap around the oval like a wake. Soft streaks follow the length of the amber form. A few larger bubbles carry bright highlights, and the surface looks smooth, glassy and backlit.

The blue and gold contrast is strong and the colours are fully saturated. Energy and motion are the main feeling here, and the pleasure is chiefly visual.

In a horizontal format, it suits a wall where one bold, colourful piece is wanted, such as above a sofa, a bed or a console. The deep blue corners frame the glowing centre and give the room a rich colour anchor.
