The Quiet Weight of Clouds, Snowy Mountain Valley

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A snow-covered mountain range rises behind a wide white valley, with thick clouds piled above the peaks. In the lower left, a red-brown building with a brown metal roof sits in the snow. Bare shrubs and a small evergreen stand at the lower right, and dark conifers dot the valley and foothills.

The warm cream and peach light in the clouds and snowfields meets deep slate-blue shadow on the slopes, and sunlight breaks through the cloud banks at upper right. The surface is painterly and slightly oily, with rippled, brushlike patterning in the sky and snow, while the foreground brush stays finely detailed. The result sits somewhere between photograph and painting.

The image is built in three layers: rolling clouds above, a band of jagged ridgelines stepping back toward the right, and an open snowy plain below. The angled roof leads the eye toward the peaks, and the small building gives the whole scene its scale. The mood is quiet, remote and still.

Its horizontal format suits a wide wall above a sofa, bed or console. The taupe, slate and cream palette fits calm, neutral rooms, and the rust-red building adds one small warm accent.
