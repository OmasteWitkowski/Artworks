The Quiet Weight of Clouds, Snowy Mountain Valley

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A snow-covered mountain range rises behind a wide white valley, with thick clouds piled above the peaks and catching warm cream light. In the lower left, a wooden building with a brown metal roof and red-brown walls sits in the snow. Bare brush and a small evergreen stand in the lower right foreground, and dark stands of conifers dot the valley and foothills. The surface has a painterly texture, with cool blue shadows against warm, pale highlights.

The story of this pieceWarm Clouds Pressing on Cold Blue Peaks, One Red Building BelowWarm, glowing clouds settle over cool blue snowy peaks, and a small red-brown building shows how big the valley is.
