Fairytale Colors

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A horizontal abstract filled with swirling ribbons and small spiral curls in fiery orange, red, gold and olive green. Long vertical streaks run down the left side, while a bright cream and gold swirl bursts across the upper centre and right. The surface looks glossy and liquid, with fine lines and tiny ring shapes throughout.

Glowing orange, gold and olive swirls with fine spiral patterns flow across the frame like molten glass, giving a rich, luminous surface that rewards close looking.
