Ember Current, Orange and Amber Swirls

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ribbons of burnt orange, deep amber and olive green swirl across the whole frame, with dark brown shadows between them. Long glossy streaks run down the left side. On the right the shapes open into larger loops and a pale burst of cream and yellow. Small spirals, fine bright edges and tiny bubble-like circles repeat across the surface.

What sets this piece apart is the way it looks like light passing through molten glass or swirled liquid. The warm palette stays rich and glowing, and small flecks of red-orange, violet, magenta and electric blue add sparks of contrast. The bright flare near the upper right gives the busy surface a clear point of focus.

Look closer and the movement becomes the subject. The streaks sweep downward on the left, curved forms rush toward a bright spiral near the upper center, and small whorls roll across the image like turbulence. There is no horizon and no single subject, so the eye is free to travel. Warm color and motion are reason enough to enjoy it.

The horizontal format and warm palette suit a living room, dining area, bedroom or office wall. The dark brown and olive tones anchor the orange, so it adds energy and glow without overwhelming the room.
