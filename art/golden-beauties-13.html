Spun Gold Lily, Swirled Yellow Bloom on Green

Botanical & Flowers · vertical

A large golden-yellow lily fills the center of a vertical frame, shown close up. Its orange-red centers, speckled throat and long stamens with dark anthers sit at the heart of pointed petals that radiate in a star shape. A second lily and unopened green buds crowd the upper and left areas, and a dark gray-green border surrounds the image.

Every petal edge curls into a spiral or flame-like tendril, and the deep olive-green background spins in loops and eddies around the flower. The lily looks as if it were made of glowing, swirling gold, and it is warm and ornamental rather than quiet.

Look closely and the whole surface is covered in small swirls and brush-like ridges, so it feels thick and painterly with a canvas-like grain. The eye lands at the bright center, travels out along the star-shaped petals, and is carried around the green by the spiral motion. The bright yellows and oranges against the shadowy ground give the flower a glow that seems to come from within.

The vertical format and dark ground suit a wall where you want a warm, high-contrast accent. The gold and orange stand out against neutral walls and pair well with deep greens or dark wood.
