Lacework Heart - Symmetrical Blue and Gold Pattern

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A single large heart sits at the center of a soft, mottled peach background, outlined with a thin dark edge and a pale glow. Inside it, bright royal blue ovals and golden yellow loops spread across a deep plum and burnt-orange web of fine, lace-like lines. Small flower-like rosettes sit among them, and curved yellow and red stripes sweep out toward the left and right edges.

The heart is built as an exact mirror image around a central vertical axis. A cluster of yellow loops sits at the center, four large blue ovals frame it above and below, and two yellow flowers rest near the heart's lower point. The blue and yellow read brightly against the darker plum. The result is a heart that rewards close looking as much as a first glance.

The pattern looks digitally made, with crisp symmetry and smooth gradients.

Look closely and the interior becomes a field of detail: the fine web lines, the repeated loops and the small rosettes that echo across both halves. The mood is warm, cheerful and ornamental. The heart suggests affection in a simple, open way, and the pattern carries the rest.

The horizontal format and generous peach margins suit a bedroom, a living room wall or a gift for someone who loves color and pattern. The warm ground and strong blue and gold accents work well with neutral walls, and the piece can stand alone or sit in a group of ornamental work.
