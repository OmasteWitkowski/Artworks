Bloom Masquerade - Mirrored Flower Abstract

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A pale pink and cream flower with a red heart and a golden-yellow cluster of stamens glows at the center of this horizontal composition. Thick, translucent ribbons of coral and crimson curl outward on both sides in looping spirals, over a deep plum and olive-green base.

The image is perfectly mirrored along a central vertical axis, and the flower looks lit from within while the edges sink into darker, richer tones. The stamens are finely detailed and slightly granular, while the ribbons look smooth, glossy and liquid. Together they give the piece a stained-glass luminosity.

Look longer and the symmetry changes what you see. The flower can read as a face, a mask or a winged figure, with the scrolls as plumes or wings and a dark, heart-like plum shape anchoring the bottom. The eye moves into the bloom and is then swept outward along the paired curls.

The warm, saturated palette suits a wall that needs one confident focal point, such as above a sofa, a bed or a console. The strict symmetry sits easily in a formal, centred arrangement, and the deep plum tones pair well with dark or neutral rooms.
