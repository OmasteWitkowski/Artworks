One Red Roof, Pink Walls, and a Sky That Won't Sit Still

Travel / Specialty / Projects · horizontal

Red Roof Reverie is a half-timbered house with a saturated red roof, pink-lilac walls and a swirling blue sky, all in a thick oil-paint style texture.

You know the wall. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and that one stretch of wall still looks unfinished. Maybe the room is comfortable but a little quiet, and it needs one strong note of colour.

This is the picture I would hold up to that wall: a steep red roof, soft pink and lilac walls, and a sky of blue and pink clouds moving behind them. It is a house, but the colour is what stops you first.

The red roof comes first. It is the most saturated shape in the picture, and it sits against plaster walls that pick up dusty pink and lilac. Those two colours are close enough to feel warm together and different enough to make each other glow.

Then the dark timber beams show up. They are drawn in crisp lines across the walls, and their diagonals repeat the slope of the roof. Your eye climbs the roofline to the gable peak, and the clouds swirl loosely around it. The weathered picket fence leads you in from the lower left, past a second red-roofed building at the edge of the frame.

Last, you notice the surface. The same swirling, wavy relief runs through the sky, walls, roof and grass, like thick oil-paint impasto. A plain house portrait becomes a storybook scene with some weather in it. I've listed the medium as Digital Art with a painterly, oil-paint style treatment, and that is the label I stand behind. I haven't named the house or its location here, because the picture doesn't need that to work.

The image is horizontal, with the house filling the centre and right and the sky across the upper third. That makes it a natural fit over a sofa, a dining table, a desk or an entry bench, anywhere you want a wide, warm focal piece.

The bold shapes and heavy texture read well from a distance. The red works as a strong accent next to neutrals, wood tones or deep blue. It sits comfortably in rustic, traditional or eclectic rooms.
