Mirrored Lantern, Golden Symmetrical Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A tall golden-yellow oval fills most of the frame, set against a muted olive-green border. Ribbons and curls of yellow, amber and chartreuse swirl outward in mirrored pairs from a vertical center line. At the heart sits a glowing magenta-pink diamond, surrounded by clusters of teal and turquoise curls.

The piece is distinctive for its exact center. The pink diamond is the brightest point, and everything else balances around it. Orange-red fan shapes at the top and bottom close the oval, and the border repeats the swirls in fainter echoes.

Up close, the surface is smooth and liquid, with soft transparent overlaps and fine pale lines. The layering makes the oval look lit from behind, and a faint pinkish halo traces its edge. The eye lands on the pink center, then follows the curls toward the top and bottom points.

The warm palette and strict symmetry make it a natural focal piece for a hallway, entry, or the wall above a console or bed. It sits comfortably beside neutral or olive-toned rooms and adds a bright accent of teal and pink.
