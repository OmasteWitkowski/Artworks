Heavenly Bliss Abstract Healing Artwork by Omaste Witkowski

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A vertical abstract built from swirling yellow, orange and gold shapes inside a large oval. The pattern is mirrored left to right and top to bottom. A bright pink diamond sits at the center, with teal-blue curls on either side, all on an olive-green background.

A warm, glowing oval of mirrored golden swirls with a hot-pink center and teal accents gives this symmetrical abstract the presence of a mandala or icon.
