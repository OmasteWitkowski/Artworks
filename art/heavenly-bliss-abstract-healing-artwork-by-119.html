Mirrored Lantern, Golden Symmetrical Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A tall, symmetrical abstract in which a golden-yellow oval fills most of the frame against an olive-green border. Swirling, liquid-looking ribbons of yellow, orange and chartreuse repeat in mirrored pairs on both sides of a vertical center line. A bright magenta diamond glows at the middle, surrounded by clusters of teal and turquoise curls. The overall effect is warm, luminous and ornate, like a kaleidoscope or a glowing egg-shaped emblem.
