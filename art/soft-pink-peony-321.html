Rose Aglow, Ruffled Magenta Rose

Botanical & Flowers · vertical

A single magenta-pink rose sits just above the middle of a tall frame, seen from slightly above. Its petals are layered and ruffled, and they swirl inward toward the centre. Green leaves and stems surround it, softly blurred, with small touches of teal, deep red and lavender in the shadows.

What holds me here is the glow. The light is soft and diffused, and it lifts the petals out of a cool, deep green background. Pale highlights run along the petal edges. The blooms are blended and painterly, like brushwork, and a fine canvas-like grain runs over the whole image.

There is a lot of quiet movement in a flower that is standing still. Follow the petals inward and your eye keeps turning toward the centre. The edges stay soft and the colour stays saturated, so it is easy to keep looking.

I don't think a rose needs to mean anything in particular. What you feel in front of it is yours.

The vertical format suits a tall, narrow wall: beside a bed, in an entry, along a hallway or up a stairwell. The deep green sits well against neutral walls and dark wood, and the magenta gives the wall a clear point of colour.
