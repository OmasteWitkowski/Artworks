Three Small Hearts Lead You Into One That Glows Like Embers

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

Ember Heart is a digital artwork of one large marbled heart, filled with spiraling red ribbons over gold, set on solid black.

Picture the wall in your home that has bothered you for months. The sofa is in, the paint is done, and that one stretch of wall still looks unfinished. Quiet colours around it make the gap feel bigger.

Now put a black square there, with a heart in the middle that looks like it is glowing. Red ribbons fan out from its center over a golden, marbled ground. That is Ember Heart.

I can't tell you a made-up story about this piece, so here is what is actually in it. Start at the top. Three small hearts are stacked in a line, each a little smaller than the one below. They work like a path, and your eye follows them down into the large heart.

Inside, the red bands spiral out from a swirling point near the center, like a sunburst. Finer waves of marbling run underneath in a slower second flow. There is no real light source in the image. The glow comes from deep red, golden yellow, orange and amber set against flat black, so the heart looks lit from within.

Then look at the edge. The wide border is made of the same red and yellow marbling, with a thin pale line and a thin dark line inside it. That makes the whole piece feel like one continuous design, framed like a matted print.

You can read it as a symbol of love and warmth. You can also read it as a bold graphic study in red, gold and black. Either answer is fine.

The saturated heart and the flat black field make this a statement piece. It suits a single-statement wall, a bedroom, an entry, or a spot where you want one warm, graphic accent against quieter surroundings. It can also sit in a gallery grouping as the bold note.
