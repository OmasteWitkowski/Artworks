Ember Heart - Red and Gold Marbled Heart on Black

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

A large heart fills most of a black square, packed with red ribbon-like bands that fan out from a swirling center point over a golden, marbled yellow ground. Three smaller hearts rise in a vertical line above its top cleft, shrinking slightly toward the top.

The red bands spiral from a vortex near the heart's center, and finer waves of marbling add a second, slower flow. Deep red, golden yellow, orange and amber glow against flat black, so the heart looks lit from within even though there is no real light source. A wide border of the same red and yellow marbling, with thin pale and dark lines inside it, frames the black panel like a matted print.

Look closely and the small hearts work as a path that leads the eye down into the large heart and its spiral. The border repeats the heart's own pattern, so the whole piece feels like one continuous design. It can be read as a symbol of love and warmth, or simply as a bold graphic study in red, gold and black.

The strong contrast and symmetry make it a clear focal point on a single wall. It suits a bedroom, an entry or a spot where a warm, graphic accent is wanted against quieter surroundings.
