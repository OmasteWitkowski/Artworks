Paper Flames, Red Bougainvillea and Turquoise Sky

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

Red bougainvillea bracts fan out from a branch seen from below, against a saturated turquoise sky. The bracts are thin and papery, with a few bright green leaves and dark stems rising from the bottom centre. Behind them the foliage is soft and streaked with gold and green.

What holds me is the light. The sun comes from behind the flowers, so the red glows and seems to let light through. Thin bright edges outline each bract. The surface has a painted look with a fine canvas grain, and it makes the flowers look like enamel or stained glass.

The colour is the whole story here. Hot red and deep teal sit opposite each other, and the green and the touches of orange and yellow keep the two from fighting. Nothing in the picture is quiet, and nothing is cluttered.

Look for the way the bracts flutter like paper, and how the background swirls loosely behind them. The longer you stay with it, the more the red and the blue seem to push against each other. What you take from that is yours to decide.

The horizontal frame suits a wide wall, such as over a sofa, a bed or a sideboard. It works well in a room with calm walls and furniture, where one confident piece can carry the colour.
