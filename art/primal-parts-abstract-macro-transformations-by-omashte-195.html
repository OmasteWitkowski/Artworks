Four heart-like crimson folds meeting at one diamond

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Heartfold is a mirrored digital abstract in crimson, plum and glowing lilac, with every curl turning toward a feathery diamond at the center.

You've got the sofa, the paint and the lamp. The wall above them is still bare, and you've looked at it long enough to know that the usual prints won't fix it.

Picture a deep burgundy field across that wall. In each corner a crimson curl sits wrapped in plum and ringed in lilac. All four lean gently toward the middle, where a small diamond of pale light holds them together.

I'll tell you what is in the image, because that is what I know for certain. Four large, curling shapes in deep crimson sit in a mirrored grid, one in each corner. They read as hearts without being a picture of a heart. That impression is a visual one, and I'd rather you decide what they are.

Each shape is wrapped in a thick plum band with a mottled, patchwork-like grain. Around that runs a smooth, marbled ring of lilac and pink that looks like smoke or silk and seems to glow. The crimson and plum stay matte and dark, so the rings stand out as the light.

The four quadrants mirror each other exactly, so the eye is pulled inward to the diamond at the center. Its edges are feathery and pale. Some people will see a symmetrical flower there, and others a butterfly. Heartfold is a digital piece, and I am not claiming more about how it was made than that.

Look closer and the surfaces change as you move across it, from blocky plum to finely grainy crimson to smooth, smoky lilac.

Heartfold is horizontal, which suits the wide wall above a sofa, a bed, a console or a sideboard. The dark, saturated palette carries color and depth into a room, and the symmetry gives it a strong, orderly focal point.

It is rich and warm without being busy. Many red and purple abstracts are loose or chaotic, and this one is composed and steady, so it can anchor a wall without competing with the furniture below it.
