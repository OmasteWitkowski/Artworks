Into the Pale Grove, Woodland Path Under Blue Sky

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A tall view of a forest on a bright day, with thick brown pine trunks on the left, a dark trunk on the right, and a grove of slender white trees in the middle. A faint path of fallen leaves leads from the bottom center into the trees. Bare branches make a tangled web against a blue sky with a few white clouds, and some green foliage shows at the sides. Every surface is drawn in swirling, outlined strokes that give the scene a painted, textured look.

The story of this pieceA Forest Where Every Twig Becomes Flowing LineA faint leaf-covered path, a grove of pale trunks and a bright blue sky, all turned into swirling, etched-looking line.
