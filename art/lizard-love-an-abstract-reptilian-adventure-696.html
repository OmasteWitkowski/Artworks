Mirror Crawlers: Kaleidoscope Lizard Triptych

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

Three vertical panels sit side by side on a white ground, each with a lizard seen from above. Its feet are splayed and clawed, and its long tail curls into a spiral. The outer two lizards are rich mirror images, filled with swirling patterns of crimson red, teal green and golden yellow. Thin navy lines divide the panels.

What sets the piece apart is the contrast between the panels. The two outer lizards are fully saturated and glow against the white. The center panel holds two pale, see-through lizards whose bodies cross in an X and whose tails interlock in spirals at the bottom. The silhouette edges are crisp and smooth, while the interiors are busy with mirrored, kaleidoscopic marbling.

Look closely and the patterns repeat and reflect inside each body, so each lizard reads as both a clear shape and a field of detail. The S-curved bodies and spiral tails make the figures seem to crawl upward in a rhythmic, dancing symmetry. The mood is playful and lively, and the bold color gives it a warm, decorative energy.

The wide, three-part format suits a long wall above a sofa, bed, console or desk. It works as a strong color accent in a room with white or neutral walls.
