Mirror Crawlers: Kaleidoscope Lizard Triptych

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · horizontal

Three side-by-side panels each show a lizard with splayed clawed feet and a long tail that ends in a tight spiral. The outer two lizards are rich, saturated mirror images filled with swirling patterns of crimson, teal and golden yellow. The center panel overlaps two pale, see-through lizards whose bodies cross and whose tails curl around each other. Everything sits on a clean white background, separated by thin navy lines.
