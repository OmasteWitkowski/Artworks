Ember Curl, Glossy Burgundy and Orange Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Smooth, glossy shapes in deep burgundy, crimson and burnt orange crowd across a horizontal canvas. A dark, inky mass sits at lower left, a large red rounded form fills the right of center, and an orange sphere-like shape rises at top. A mustard-yellow diagonal and a ribbon of blue and lilac run across the lower part of the image.

The forms look both liquid and sculptural. Soft highlights give them a luminous, glassy shine, and a bright peach-white glow runs along the edge of the central dark shape. Against the pale background on the right, the saturated colors look vivid and the composition has room to breathe. At upper right, an orange tendril curls into a small spiral and draws the eye out of the frame.

Look closer and the shapes seem to fold into each other, sometimes like petals, though nothing is literally identifiable. The left edge is a band of marbled swirls in olive and bronze that ripples like veined stone and frames the glossy forms beside it. The mood is rich, warm and a little playful.

The warm, dense palette and wide format suit a focal wall above a sofa, bed or sideboard. The blue ribbon and pale space give the strong reds and oranges some contrast.
