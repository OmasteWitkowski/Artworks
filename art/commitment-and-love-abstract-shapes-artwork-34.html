A Heart Cut Out of the Dark, Lit From Behind

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

Ember Heart in a Leaf Wreath is a vertical digital artwork: a glowing heart-shaped window held inside a mirrored wreath of leaves, set in a field of swirling orange.

You know the wall. It's above the console, the bed or the mantel, and the room around it is finished. The furniture is where you want it and the paint is settled. That one space still looks like nobody decided anything.

Now picture a tall, near-black oval there, with a heart cut out of it like a lit window. Amber, olive green and violet glow inside the heart, and the whole thing sits in a field of ember orange. I made this piece to hold a wall the way a warm lamp holds a corner.

The first thing you see is the glow. The heart looks lit from behind, and a thin gold halo traces the edge of the oval. The leaves seem to burn against the dark silhouette.

Then the leaves. Two mirrored branches of pointed leaves curve upward on either side, like a laurel wreath, and meet in a small bow-like tie at the base. The symmetry is exact, left to right, and it gives the image a formal, steady feel. The brush-like marks in the orange field swirl around it, so it never feels cold.

Then the center of the heart. Look closely and a faint mirrored form appears there. Some people see a face, some a flame, some a vase. I'm not going to tell you which is right. That part is yours to decide.

A heart inside a wreath is an old, familiar symbol of love, celebration or honor. Here it is dark, warm and jewel-lit instead of soft and sweet.

This is a vertical piece with a dark center and a warm surround, so it works as a focal point. Over a console, a bed or a mantel, the dark oval anchors the space and the orange pulls the eye in. Because it is symmetrical, it also sits easily in the middle of a wall, or as one of a mirrored pair.

The deep black-brown, burnt orange and crimson add a strong warm note to rooms with dark wood, black accents or neutral walls. The color does the work, so you don't need to match it to anything else.

Measure the space first, then compare it with the sizes shown on the listing. This is a vertical piece, so check the height as well as the width. If it doesn't feel right once it's in your room, Fine Art America's 30-day money-back guarantee applies.

Screens vary, so I can't promise an exact match. The colors to look for are deep black-brown, burnt orange, fiery amber and crimson, with accents of green, magenta and violet. The material you pick also changes how the print looks. The money-back guarantee covers you if it isn't what you pictured.

You don't need to know anything. Your own response is the only test that counts. If the glow stopped you, that is a perfectly good reason. The face, flame or vase in the heart is there if you want a second look, and you can leave it if you don't.

Picture it on that wall. The dark oval sits in the middle and the orange holds the room's warm tones. The heart glows, and a guest wanders over to ask what that shape in the center is.
