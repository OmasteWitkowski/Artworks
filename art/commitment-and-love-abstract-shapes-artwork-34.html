Ember Heart in a Leaf Wreath

Spiritual, Mandala & Symbolic · vertical

A tall near-black oval holds a heart-shaped window of glowing amber, olive green, magenta and violet. Two mirrored branches of pointed leaves curve upward around it like a laurel wreath and meet in a small bow-like tie at the base. Around the oval, swirling orange and crimson brushwork fills the frame, edged by a thin black border.

The heart looks lit from behind. A fine gold halo traces the edge of the oval, and the leaves seem to burn against the dark silhouette. Strict left-right symmetry gives the image a formal, emblem-like steadiness, while the brush-like marks in the orange field and the leaves contrast with the smooth, flat oval and border.

Look closely at the center of the heart, where a faint mirrored form appears. It can read as a face, a flame or a vase. The heart inside a wreath is a familiar symbol of love, celebration or honor, and here it is rendered in rich, jewel-like color.

The vertical format and the warm, dark-framed center suit a focal wall, such as above a console, a bed or a mantel. It also works as half of a mirrored pair, and it adds a strong orange note to rooms with dark wood, black accents or neutral walls.
