Jewel Pool, Glowing Translucent Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Rounded, translucent shapes in teal, ruby red, violet, cobalt blue and amber overlap and blend across the whole frame. Golden-yellow highlights run along their curves, and small white glints sit on glossy surfaces that look like backlit glass or liquid droplets.

What sets it apart is the light. The shapes seem to glow from within, and the glowing violet and pink form near the center, with a small green shape at its upper left, anchors the picture. Tiny bubble-like specks in the blue and purple areas add fine detail.

Look longer and the movement appears. Teardrop and lobed forms drift and pool, and thin tapering tails, such as the pointed sliver at lower right, suggest slow liquid motion. Soft, out-of-focus edges set off the sharper detail near the center, and cool teals and greens on the left balance the warm reds and oranges on the right.

This is a horizontal piece with rich color and strong contrast. It can carry a wall over a sofa, bed or console, and it suits rooms that want a bold, jewel-toned focal point.
