Jewel Pool, Glowing Translucent Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A horizontal abstract filled with glossy, translucent shapes in teal, ruby red, violet, cobalt blue and amber. Golden highlights trace the curved edges, and the shapes glow as if lit from behind. A pink and purple form sits near the center, with a small green shape beside it, while soft blurred areas surround the sharper detail. Tiny bubble-like specks and smooth gradients give the surface a glassy, liquid feel.
