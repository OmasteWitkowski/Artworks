Carved in Light - White Plumeria

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

White plumeria blooms fill the frame, their rounded, overlapping petals curling toward soft yellow centers. Green stems cross the middle left, and the background melts into golden-amber bokeh with touches of rust red and pale lime green.

What sets this piece apart is the petal surface. Fine, swirling strokes give the petals a rippled, paint-like texture, so they look almost carved or enameled. Warm light gathers on the pale petals and sets them against the darker foliage.

Look closely and the petals spiral gently, drawing the eye in a loose circle around each bloom and across the cluster from upper left to lower right. The tactile petals contrast with the smooth, out-of-focus background and give the image depth. The overall feel is warm and lush, with a quiet richness.

The deep green and olive tones with warm amber suit living rooms, bedrooms, entryways and dining areas where a glowing floral can be the main piece on the wall.
