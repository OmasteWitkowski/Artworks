White Petals That Look Carved From Light

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

Carved in Light is a close-up of white plumeria, its petals swirled like thick paint and glowing against deep green and amber.

You know the wall. It might be above the sofa, the bed or the console table. The furniture is right and the paint is settled, but the wall still looks unfinished, and it has for months.

Picture white flowers on that wall, so close that the petals fill the frame. They are pale and glowing against a dark green ground, with warm amber light melting behind them. That is Carved in Light.

From across the room you see white plumeria, soft yellow at the center of each bloom, with green stems crossing behind. It reads as a flower first, and that is enough.

Step closer and the petals change. Fine, swirling strokes ripple across them, so they look almost carved or enameled, like something shaped by hand. The background is the opposite: smooth, out-of-focus golden circles. Rough against smooth is what gives the image its depth.

The petals also curl and overlap in a gentle spiral. Your eye travels around each bloom and drifts across the cluster from upper left to lower right. Around the yellow petal bases there is a faint inner glow, as if the flowers were lit from within.

I'm not going to tell you what it means. That part is yours.

This is a horizontal piece, so it suits the wide, low wall above a sofa, a bed or a console. It can be the main piece there. The dark green and olive ground with warm amber behind it works well in rooms with warm or earthy palettes, such as a living room, bedroom, entry or dining space.

The mood is warm and lush, with a quiet richness. The glow comes from the light on the petals, not from anything I'm asking you to feel.
