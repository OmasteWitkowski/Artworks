Golden Meander, Marbled Abstract on Navy

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ribbons of golden yellow, amber and ochre swirl into two large, winding shapes. Fine contour lines repeat inside every fold, like marbling or wood grain. A flat deep navy field frames the gold and runs between the two masses as a snaking channel. Pockets of teal, turquoise, crimson and cobalt blue glow within the gold, gathering toward the lower right and center.

The gold looks molten and glossy, as if light were running along the folds. Against the dark ground, the cooler passages shimmer like iridescent, oil-slick color. The maze-like layout and the dense line detail set it apart from the usual gold swirl.

Look closely and the contour lines read like topographic maps or growth rings. The edges ripple, and the whorls and eddies lead the eye through the navy channels, so there is always another turn to follow. The rich, energetic warmth is reason enough to want it.

Its horizontal format and strong contrast suit a focal wall above a sofa, bed, console or mantel. It works especially well in rooms with deep blue, charcoal or warm neutral tones, where the gold can glow.
