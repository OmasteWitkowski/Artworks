Ember Seam - Mirrored Crimson Swirl Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

A deep burgundy form fills the center of this square abstract, split by a dark vertical seam and mirrored left to right. Blue-violet and grey-lavender bands run out to each side. Golden yellow curls and a burnt orange field fill the corners and flank the center.

What sets it apart is the discipline of the symmetry. Liquid, marbled swirls are held in a balanced, ornamental arrangement, and the central form reads like an emblem or a mask without becoming a literal image. Thin curls and tendrils spiral outward toward the corners, so the image feels in motion yet settled.

There is no directional light. The glow comes from color alone: bright yellow against dark maroon, with orange adding a fiery warmth at the edges. A fine, canvas-like grain runs across the smooth color, and the delicate swirled lines reward a closer look.

It works as a bold focal piece in a living room, entry, dining space or bedroom. The square format and centered symmetry suit formal arrangements, and the warm palette sits well with neutral walls, dark wood or deep blue furnishings.
