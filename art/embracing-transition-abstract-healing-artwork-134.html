Embracing Transition Abstract Healing Artwork

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

A square abstract in warm red, orange and yellow, mirrored across the center. Two large maroon forms face each other, with blue-grey and purple shapes inside them and purple bands reaching out to the sides. Yellow curls and orange swirls fill the corners and edges.

A vivid, mirror-symmetrical abstract in which fiery yellows and oranges surround deep maroon and violet forms, creating a bold, ornamental pattern with a painterly surface.
