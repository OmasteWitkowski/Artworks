Ember Seam - Mirrored Crimson Swirl Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

A square abstract image built from swirling, liquid-looking shapes mirrored left to right around a dark center line. A large dark red form sits in the middle, with grey-lavender and blue-violet bands extending sideways. Bright yellow curls and a burnt orange field surround the center and fill the corners. The overall effect is warm, glowing and ornamental, with a fine canvas-like surface texture.

The story of this pieceThe Dark Seam That Turns Liquid Swirls Into an EmblemA deep crimson form, split by a dark seam and mirrored edge to edge, glows against golden curls and burnt orange.
