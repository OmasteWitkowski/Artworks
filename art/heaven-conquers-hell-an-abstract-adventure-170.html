Ember Current - Red and Yellow Glass-Like Swirls

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ribbons and curling loops of saturated red, orange and golden yellow swirl across the image like translucent colored liquid. Cream-white light glows behind them, thin bright edges catch like highlights, and a patch of teal-green sits low on the left. The abstract is centered in a wide black mat and surrounded by a soft, blurred halo of the same colors.

What sets this piece apart is its glass-like depth. The overlapping translucent circles and glossy surfaces look as if light is passing through them, and the black mat makes the reds and yellows glow. The single cool teal patch gives all that heat a counterpoint.

Look closely and the eye travels in curves. Red ribbons twist through the upper corners, yellow and orange billow through the center, and small circular eddies pull you around the image. The mat has a fine, faintly woven texture that contrasts with the smooth, glossy swirls inside. The mood is warm, energetic and flowing, and the appeal is simply the color and motion.

The horizontal format and built-in black mat give the image a finished, gallery-like presence. It suits a neutral or dark wall, or a wide space above a sofa, console or bed, where the warm colors can act as the focal point of the room.
