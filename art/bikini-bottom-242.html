Warm Coral Cells Glowing Against a Cool Turquoise Field

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Ember Pools is a macro abstract of red-orange and coral shapes pressed against turquoise, with thin glassy lines and tiny bubbles at the seams.

You know the wall. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, and that one stretch of wall still looks unfinished. You have probably looked at it for months, and nothing you have found has felt like yours.

This is the piece I would hold up to it. A warm glow of red-orange, coral and salmon-peach on one side, a cool turquoise field on the other, in a wide horizontal frame that suits a space above a sofa, bed, console or desk.

From across the room, you see color. Soft coral and red cells press together in the center and right, and turquoise and pale aqua fill the left. The warm shapes look lit from within, as if light is passing through them.

Step closer and the picture changes. Thin, sharp-edged glassy lines run between the soft color fields and meet at a small point just left of center. Tiny bubble-like spots dot the blue, a few larger ones sit in the coral, and small bright glints catch on them. Along the edges there are hints of violet and green.

Nothing in it is recognizable, so I leave it open. You might see pools, cells or fragments of glass. The red wedges point in different directions and pull your eye across the frame. What you see is yours to decide, and I would rather you stand in front of it than read about it.

The warm center and right carry the energy, and the turquoise side keeps the whole thing balanced. That makes it a good single focal piece for a living room, bedroom, studio or office where the furniture is already chosen and the wall needs one strong, colorful answer.
