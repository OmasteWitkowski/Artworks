Ember Pools, Coral Cells on Turquoise

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

Soft, translucent shapes in red-orange, coral and salmon-peach press together against a cool turquoise and pale aqua field. Thin glassy lines mark the edges between the cells and meet at a small point just left of center. Tiny bubble-like spots dot the blue area, and a few larger ones appear in the coral shapes.

The warm cells glow as if light is passing through them, while the turquoise stays cool and calm. Small bright glints catch on the bubbles and along the fine ridges. Red wedges point in different directions and draw the eye across the horizontal frame.

Look closely and the surface changes from soft color fields to crisp, sharp-edged lines, with hints of violet and green along the edges. Nothing in the image is recognizable, so the shapes can read as pools, cells or fragments of glass. The appeal is in the color and the light.

The warm center and right bring energy to a wall, and the turquoise side keeps it balanced. It suits a living room, bedroom, studio or office that wants one vivid, calm focal piece.
