A Tangle of Pink and Turquoise Ribbons, and One Word

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Intuition is a horizontal digital abstract of looping turquoise and bubblegum-pink shapes on a pale pink ground, with its one-word theme printed along the bottom.

You know the wall. The sofa is in, the paint is settled, the room is finished, and that one stretch of wall still looks plain. Maybe it has looked that way for months.

This is the piece I would picture there. Turquoise and bubblegum-pink ribbons loop and curl across a pale pink ground, and the colour reaches the other side of the room before you do.

The colour comes first. The pale pink background glows, and the turquoise and pink shapes are the brightest passages. Peach, olive green, teal and dusty mauve fill the spaces between them.

Then the shapes. Spirals, ovals, curled ribbons and petal-like forms overlap from edge to edge. Each one is filled with a marbled colour that looks like soft liquid or watercolor, and thin dark outlines hold them in place.

Then look for the dark olive-green coil near the centre. The ribbons circle it, and your eye follows one curve into the next spiral and then the next.

Along the bottom, a teal band carries the word INTUITION in large blue capitals, with a short definition in white about the subconscious and the conscious mind. The word names a theme. What the piece means to you is your own call.

It is a horizontal piece, so it suits a wide wall: over a sofa, a bed, a console or a desk. It works best in a room that is finished but a little plain, where you want colour to take the lead.
