One Cloud Catches the Last Light. The Rest Stays in Shadow

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Last Light Tower is a wide, painterly evening sky: a cream-gold cloud crown over heavy violet cloud banks and a salmon-pink horizon.

You know the wall. It is the long one above the sofa, or behind the bed, or over the console. The furniture is settled and the paint is chosen, but the wall still looks bare, and it has looked that way for months.

Picture a wide evening sky filling that space. Most of the frame is cool steel blue and slate violet. Near the middle, one tall cloud holds the last warm light at its top. That small glow is what you see first from across the room.

I'll tell you what I see in this piece, because it is what made me want to offer it. The warm light touches only two places: the crown of the tallest cloud and the thin band of peach and salmon near the horizon. Everything between them sits in cool violet and charcoal shadow.

That shadow is what gives the glow its weight. Many sunset skies are one orange gradient. Here the dark middle makes the small bright crown feel like an event.

Then there is the surface. A swirling, brushstroke-like texture runs across the whole image, so the clouds and the low hill of small evergreens look sculpted from thick paint. Up close, the grain rewards a longer look. The clouds billow upward and then stretch sideways in long bands, and a few thin streaks in the blue add a gentle drift.

I don't tell you what it means. It is an evening sky as one cloud catches the last warm light, and what you feel in front of it is yours.

This is a horizontal piece, and the sky fills about 85 percent of the frame. That makes it a natural fit for a wide wall: above a sofa, a bed, a console or a mantel. The cool violets and blues sit comfortably in a living room, bedroom or entry, and the gold and pink add one warm focal point.

If your room already has its own colours, the blues and purples are the calm base, and the warm notes are small enough to sit alongside what you have.
