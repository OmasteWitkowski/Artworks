Float Like a Butterfly: Swallowtail on Pink Blossoms

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · vertical

A yellow-and-black striped swallowtail rests with its wings spread on a cluster of dusty pink and orchid-purple star-shaped flowers. Its antennae are lifted, and small flashes of blue and orange show on the hindwings. Behind it, a soft blur of olive and moss green carries patches of light and shadow, and a dark leaf frames the lower left corner.

The butterfly's bold stripes and pale, softly glowing wing bands stand out against the quiet greens and pinks. Below the image, a dark olive band carries the line 'Float like a butterfly, sting like a bee. Muhammad Ali' in cream serif lettering. The quote turns a gentle nature portrait into something playful and a little fierce.

Look at the diagonal sweep of the wings, which suggests lightness and a hint of motion in an otherwise still moment. A fine, even, embossed texture runs across the butterfly, the blossoms and the background. It gives the photograph a painterly, tactile surface. The delicate subject and the boxing saying give the picture both grace and spirit.

Its vertical shape and dark text band suit a desk wall, hallway, den, gym or reading corner. The green, black and cream palette sits well with natural wood, and the pink flowers add a soft contrast.
