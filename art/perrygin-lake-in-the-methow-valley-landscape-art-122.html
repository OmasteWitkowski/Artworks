Blue Ribbon Valley - Painterly Mountain Landscape

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A wide view over a dry, shrubby hillside looks down into a mountain valley. A modern house with a rust-red roof sits on the left slope beside tall pines. A bright blue river bends through the middle of the valley, and rows of blue-green ridges rise behind it to snow-capped peaks under a deep blue sky. A fine textured surface gives the whole scene a painted look.

The story of this pieceOne Blue River Cuts Through an Olive-Brown Mountain ValleyA wide, high-vantage mountain valley where one bright blue river is the only saturated note in an earthy, open view.
