Blue Ribbon Valley - Painterly Mountain Landscape

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A wide view from a dry, scrubby hillside looks down into a mountain valley. A bright blue river bends through the middle distance, and rows of blue-green ridges climb behind it to snow-capped peaks under a deep blue sky. On the left slope, a modern house with a rust-red roof sits beside a tall pine.

The vivid cobalt-to-cerulean water is the one saturated note against the olive, khaki and sage hills, and the small red roof answers it as a warm accent. A fine, even texture runs across the entire scene, including the sky, so the view reads like a painted surface with photographic detail. The foreground shrubs and dry grasses are busy and bristly, which sets off the smooth water beyond.

The eye travels from the brush in the foreground to the blue water just below center, then back along the receding ridges to the snow. A faint dirt road curves through the right middle ground. Distant ridges sit in cool blue shadow while the nearer slopes catch warmer, even light, which gives the scene its depth and its calm, spacious feeling.

Its horizontal format and clear layers suit a living room, bedroom, office or hallway where a long, open view is wanted. The blue-and-earth palette pairs easily with neutral walls, natural wood and warm textiles.
