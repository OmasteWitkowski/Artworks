A Red Starburst That Glows Beside a Glossy, Marbled Tangle

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

Ember Garden is a horizontal digital abstract: a soft red starburst on cream, a wet-looking brown and gold tangle at the centre, and emerald and teal above.

You know the wall. It is wide, the sofa or bed beneath it is settled, the paint is settled, and the room is pleasant but quiet. Nothing in it is the first thing a guest looks at.

This is what I picture there. On the left edge, a soft red starburst glows, its long thin rays reaching across a pale cream ground. It is warm and it reads from across the room.

I can't hand you a backstory for this piece, and I won't make one up. What I can tell you is what is there, because it rewards a slow look.

Two surfaces meet. The red starburst and the cobalt blue patch in the lower left are soft and blurred at the edges. The centre and the upper right look wet and liquid, finely marbled, with tiny speckles, thin curling lines and feathery, fern-like edges.

At the centre sits a dark reddish-brown tangle of curling shapes, edged in gold and white highlights. A band of emerald and teal runs across the top. Follow the dark, spiky diagonal toward the upper right and the colour thins into faint ripples on the cream.

The shapes feel like sun, flame and growing things, but none of them is literal. I would rather you decide what it is than have me tell you.

Ember Garden is horizontal, with its weight on the left and a calm cream right side. That suits a wide wall: over a sofa, a bed, a sideboard or a console. The red does the work of an accent in a room that is otherwise quiet.

The cream ground helps it sit on a light or neutral wall. The mood is energetic, vivid and warm, and the main reason to choose it is that you like looking at it.
