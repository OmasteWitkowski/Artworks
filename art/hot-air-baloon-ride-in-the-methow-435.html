Hot Air Baloon Ride in the Methow

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A wide mountain landscape with snow-covered peaks under a sky of layered white and grey clouds over blue. In the foreground, dry golden-brown grassy hills slope across the lower half, with a faint dark track running through them. A single small hot air balloon floats at the left. The whole scene has a textured, painted look.

A small hot air balloon drifts across a big painterly sky above snowy peaks and glowing golden hills, a cool-and-warm contrast rendered in rich, swirling texture.
