Exploring Life's Mysteries Abstract Pattern Artwork by Omaste Wi

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · square

A square abstract with mirrored, glossy swirls of blue, olive green, yellow and red arranged symmetrically around a teal-green diamond shape at the centre. Pale white and lavender curls fill the space between the colourful forms, and a pink glow shows at the left and right midpoints. The overall effect is ornate, fluid and balanced.

Mirrored liquid swirls of cobalt blue, olive, gold and red frame a glowing green star, making a bright, ornate abstract with strong symmetry and a glossy, marbled feel.
