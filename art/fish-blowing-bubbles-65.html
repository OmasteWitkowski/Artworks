Ember Lantern, Glowing Orange Abstract with Bubbles

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A rounded, shield-like shape in orange, amber and green sits just left of center, lit from behind so it glows. Overlapping crimson and cream shapes surround it, and small round bubble-like spots are scattered across its surface. One larger bubble sits at its lower left edge.

The piece is distinctive for its light. Bright luminous edges rim every shape, and the reds deepen to near-burgundy where they overlap. The central form looks like a fragment of colored glass, studded with bubbles, set against saturated red. Small accents of teal, cobalt blue and dusty violet show at the edges.

Look closely and you find fine streaks of green, orange and pink running diagonally through the central form. The red shapes press in from the top and right, while pale cream and peach space opens at the lower left. The movement is slow and flowing, and the tight crop leaves no horizon or reference point. It is simply color, glow and glossy surface.

The warm palette and strong focal shape suit a living room, dining area, studio or office where you want a bright, energetic horizontal piece. The cream areas help it sit comfortably beside neutral walls and wood tones.
