Ember Lantern, Glowing Orange Abstract with Bubbles

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A close-up view of translucent, glass-like shapes glowing with backlight. A rounded orange and amber form with green streaks sits near the center, dotted with small bubbles and one larger bubble at its lower edge. Deep red shapes frame the top and right, and soft cream and peach areas fill the lower left. Small teal and blue accents peek in at the edges.

The story of this pieceThe Glowing Orange Shape That Looks Like a Glass LanternA rounded orange and green shape glows from behind like a lantern, set against deep crimson and soft cream.
