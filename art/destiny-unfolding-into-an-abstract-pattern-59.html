Ember Quadrants - Mirrored Orange and Navy Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · horizontal

A deep navy cross-shaped space fills the center of this horizontal abstract. In each corner, mirrored swirls of orange, gold and yellow curl around glowing cyan and yellow orbs. Lavender and blue marbled shapes rise toward pointed peaks at the top and bottom edges, and a wavy turquoise-green border with fine swirling lines frames the whole composition.

What sets it apart is the contrast. Saturated orange and gold glow against the dark navy center, and cream highlights and bright orbs give a backlit, stained-glass effect. The strict mirror symmetry makes the four corner clusters match exactly, so the image feels both energetic and ordered.

Look slowly and the shapes curl outward from the center. The eye travels around the four corners and returns to the dark middle. The smooth, glossy blends in the orange and violet areas contrast with the thin marbled lines in the green border.

The strong blue-and-orange palette suits a living room, office or entry as a bold horizontal focal point, especially where a wall needs a burst of color and a clear center.
