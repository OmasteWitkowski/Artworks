Ember Wings - Mirrored Red and Gold Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

Deep crimson ribbons curl in from the left and right edges like flames or folded wings. Between them, a golden yellow core of heart shapes and fine lattice runs down the center of the vertical canvas. Pale peach and apricot lobes glow at the top and bottom, and a thin dark border frames the whole design.

The composition is mirrored left to right and top to bottom. That strict symmetry gives the swirling forms an ornamental, almost creature-like presence. The pale areas look backlit and translucent against the darker red, so the center seems to radiate warmth.

Look closer and the surface is soft and mottled, with a fine speckled, marbled grain. Thin overlapping contour lines give the ribbons a silky, liquid depth. The eye travels inward along the red sweeps, rests on the bright core, then drifts out again to the top and bottom lobes. The hearts, wings and flames are impressions the forms suggest, not fixed subjects, so each viewer can find their own.

The burnt orange, crimson and gold make a strong focal point in a living room, entry or bedroom. The vertical format suits a narrow wall, a spot above a console, or a bold pair of mirrored panels.
