Ember Wings - Mirrored Red and Gold Abstract

Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media · vertical

A vertical abstract in fiery red, orange and golden yellow, built from swirling ribbon-like shapes mirrored left to right and top to bottom. Pale peach and yellow lobes glow at the top and bottom, while a yellow heart-like, lattice-patterned core runs down the middle. Dark crimson, flame- or wing-like swirls fill the left and right sides, and a thin dark border frames the whole design. The surface looks soft, mottled and translucent, with fine overlapping contour lines.
