A Pink Lily That Looks Sculpted From Whipped Frosting

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

Frosted Star Lily is a hot pink lily on a dark olive-brown ground, every petal covered in tiny curling swirls.

You have the sofa, the paint and the rug. The wall above it is still empty, and every time you walk in, it shows. A neutral room can feel finished and flat at once.

This is the piece I would hold up to that wall. A large pink lily opens in the middle of the frame, seen from above, and it glows against a dark olive-brown ground. You can read it from across the room. Then you walk closer and find the petals are covered in tiny curls.

Start in the center. Six pointed petals radiate out from a golden-orange core with small dark speckles. The tips are pale blush pink. Toward the middle the color deepens to coral, then red and magenta.

Now look at the surface. Every petal is covered in small, curling spirals, so the lily looks piped from whipped paint or swirled frosting. The curls follow the shape of each petal and catch the soft glow of the light.

Behind the flower the same curling turns into pattern. Dark, paisley-like shapes in rust and chocolate loop and eddy around the bloom. A second pink lily enters at the upper right, cropped by the edge, so the picture doesn't sit still.

I made this one to be enjoyed for its color and its surface. If it stops you, that is reason enough.

The horizontal format suits the wide, low wall: over a sofa, a bed, a console or a dining buffet. The hot pink is a bold accent in a neutral room.

The dark olive-brown ground also sits comfortably with deep brown, olive or charcoal tones, so it works in a darker room as well as a pale one. The mood is playful, lush and celebratory.
