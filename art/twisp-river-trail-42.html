The Shimmering Path - Forest Trail with Two Walkers

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A dirt trail winds through a sunlit forest of tall, slender conifer trunks. Two small figures in red and pink walk away from the viewer along the path in the distance. Moss and green undergrowth cover the ground on either side, and tangled branches fill the background with swirling lines. Patches of blue sky show at the top and dappled light falls across the trail.

The story of this pieceTwo Tiny Walkers, One Shimmering Forest PathA dirt trail curves through tall conifer trunks toward two small walkers in red and pink, and the branches around them shimmer with etched, swirling line.
