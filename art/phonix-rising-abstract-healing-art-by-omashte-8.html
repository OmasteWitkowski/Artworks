Ember Wings, Orange Phoenix with Peacock-Eye Feathers

Butterflies, Birds & Wildlife · vertical

A bright orange bird stands head-on at the center of a tall, symmetrical image, its wings raised high in long curved feather shapes and its tail pointing straight down. Rows of small teardrop, peacock-eye feather motifs cover the chest, with a small heart-shaped outline near the center. Behind it, cream-white swirls and concentric ripples spiral out on both sides over a yellow and orange ground. A solid deep navy border frames the whole piece.

The figure is a flat, flame-colored silhouette with only a faint mottling, and it glows against the pale ground with no cast shadows. The fine, flowing background lines, which look like marbling or zebra stripes, mirror the bird's symmetry. The result is part emblem, part pattern study. Navy, red-orange and touches of olive green at the edges deepen the warm palette.

The bird can be read as a phoenix, a firebird or a stylized peacock. The raised wings and flame-like color give it an upward, celebratory energy, and you can bring your own meaning to it.

Its vertical format and strict symmetry suit a centered position, such as an entryway, a narrow wall or above a console or desk. The orange gives a neutral or cool-toned room a strong warm accent, and the navy border keeps the image contained and framed.
