Barefoot at the Blue River, Boy on a Rocky Bank

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A young boy with short blond hair stands barefoot on a flat stone at the edge of a rocky riverbank, looking down at the ground or water. A wide blue river curves away to the left toward tree-covered banks and distant hazy hills under a cloudy blue sky. Slender pale-trunked trees and bright green shrubs and grasses fill the right side of the scene. The whole image has a vivid, sharply detailed, slightly painterly look.
