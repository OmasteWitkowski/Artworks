Barefoot at the Blue River, Boy on a Rocky Bank

Pacific Northwest Landscapes · horizontal

A young blond boy in a gray camouflage-patterned shirt and shorts stands barefoot on a pale flat stone at the edge of a rocky riverbank. He looks down toward the water. A wide blue river curves away to the left toward tree-lined banks and hazy hills under a sky of scattered clouds. Tall, slender white-barked trees and dense green shrubs fill the right side of the scene.

What sets this piece apart is the contrast between the tiny, quiet figure and the intensity of everything around him. Cobalt and teal water meets deep forest green, dark charcoal shadow and bright lime highlights. Leaves, grass, wet stones and ripples all have crisp, glowing edges and a slight oil-paint sheen, so the scene looks both real and heightened.

The composition splits on a diagonal. The open river sweeps through the left and lower left, while the stony shoreline leads the eye from the foreground to the child. The tall trunks lean slightly upward, and small ripples add motion, while the boy stays absorbed and still. The mood is one of quiet curiosity and the unhurried calm of a summer day by the water, with a gentle, nostalgic feel.

The horizontal format and saturated color give it presence on a living room, family room, hallway or entry wall. The blue and green suit a calm, natural palette, and the detailed surface rewards a closer look.
