Orange Tulip

Botanical & Flowers · horizontal

A close view of one orange tulip with overlapping, slightly open petals. The outer petals are marked with deep red-purple streaks near the base. Tall green grass blades and leaves fill the blurred background, with tiny blue and pink spots of other flowers. A subtle brushed texture covers the whole scene.

A sunlit orange tulip with burgundy-streaked petals glows against deep green, and a fine painterly texture gives the close-up a tactile, canvas-like feel.
