Ember Heart, Glowing Red and Purple Abstract

Abstract Glass Macro · horizontal

A heart-like shape formed by overlapping deep purple and bright red planes fills the center of the image. Around it, soft blurred areas of amber, pink, pale blue and cream melt into one another. Thin glowing curved lines and a few tiny bubble-like droplets add detail near the top. The whole piece feels translucent and luminous, like light passing through colored glass.
