The Discount Club

Somewhere there is a room that is nearly finished. The furniture is in, the paint is dry, and one wall is still waiting.

It might be a client's living room, an office lobby, or a guest room at your inn. It might be your own house. Either way you know the feeling: the room works, but it does not yet feel like anyone's.

The part of the job nobody talks about

If you choose art for rooms, you know how the search goes. You scroll. You save a few. Then you start again for the next room, and the one after that.

Somewhere past the thousandth picture they all begin to look alike, and the safe choice starts to look like the only one: the print everybody has, the one that could hang in any room because it belongs to none of them.

The budget is set and the deadline is close, and a wall cannot be finished with "nearly right".

What I learned photographing other people's rooms

Before I made art, I was an architectural photographer. My job was to make a finished room look the way it feels to stand in it, and that work appeared in Architectural Digest, Fine Homebuilding and Green Building.

The aim has not changed since my years photographing rooms: an image should complement the space it hangs in, and make it better.

Then one long winter day I pointed the camera at something very small: light passing through a handblown glass ornament, from so close that the object itself disappeared. What came back was colour and light I had not seen before, and no two frames were alike. Everything I make now began there.

In 2012 I founded Artworks and The Art Drop Shop Gallery, to help people find art that suits the room it will live in. That is still the whole idea.

Why there is a Discount Club

The Club is for anyone who chooses art for rooms, from one wall to a whole building. It gives you 15% to 25% off my prints, so you have a steady source of art that is distinctive, well made and affordable, without starting the search from nothing each time.

The questions that come up

Will it be the right size?

Every piece on this site can be seen on a wall at its real size: over a sofa, a bed, a sideboard or a desk, in photos of real rooms, or in a photo of your own wall. Each one shows the largest size it prints at well. My prints hang in homes in 17 states and the UK, some of them six feet wide.

Will the pieces work together?

Ask the Gallery for several pieces that belong together, and it pulls work that shares a colour, a subject or a shape. The Rooms pages group pieces by the room they suit.

Has the work hung in rooms like mine?

It has hung in galleries, corporate offices, hotels, rental properties and private homes, including the Hotel Rio Vista in Winthrop. My friend Garth Mudge, who blows the glass I photograph, hangs my prints in his shop at Glassworks in Winthrop and took almost twenty pieces for his home, office and store.

The day the wall is finished

Picture the room again, finished. The wall holds something chosen for that room. And when someone asks where it came from, there is a story to tell: a piece of handblown glass, photographed from a thumb's width away, or a river you can stand beside in the Methow Valley.

The Club is how I make that easier, for the people who do this for a living and for everyone who simply loves art on their walls.
