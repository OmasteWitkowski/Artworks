Botanical & Flowers

Flowers, gardens and botanical details, seen up close.

Flowers, gardens and botanical details, photographed up close so the petals and the light become the subject.

There are 252 pieces here. Most are horizontal. The colours that come up most are green, orange and pink. They suit rooms like the living room, the entry & hallway and the bedroom. Narrow them by colour and shape below, and see any of them on a wall at its real size.
