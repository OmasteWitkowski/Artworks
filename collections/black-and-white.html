Black & White

Light and shadow with every colour taken away: black, white and the greys between.

Every piece here is made only of black, white and the greys between. Each one also belongs to its own collection; this is where they hang together.

There are 23 pieces here. Most are square. The colours that come up most are black, white and gray. They suit rooms like the living room, the entry & hallway and the home office. Narrow them by colour and shape below, and see any of them on a wall at its real size.
