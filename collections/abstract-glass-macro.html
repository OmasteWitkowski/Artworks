Abstract Glass Macro

Handblown glass photographed from a thumb's width away, so close that the object disappears and only light and colour remain.

Each piece in this collection is a photograph of handblown glass, taken from so close that the object itself disappears. What you see is light passing through the glass. The glass is blown by my friend Garth Mudge at Glassworks in Winthrop, Washington.

For each image I take seven photographs, from light to dark, and combine them so the whole range of light and colour sits in one picture. The colour and depth suit metal and acrylic prints especially well.

There are 186 pieces here. Most are horizontal. The colours that come up most are orange, red and blue. They suit rooms like the living room and the home office. Narrow them by colour and shape below, and see any of them on a wall at its real size.
