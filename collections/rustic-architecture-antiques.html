Rustic Architecture & Antiques

A 1700s German farm, an Ulster forge, a spinning wheel by the window: timber, stone and things made by hand to last.

Old buildings and the things kept inside them: half-timbered farmhouses from the 1600s and 1700s, an Ulster forge, a log cabin door, a spinning wheel and a stone well.

Warm wood, weathered stone and window light, for a room that wants a sense of history.

There are 13 pieces here. Most are horizontal. The colours that come up most are orange, brown and gold. They suit rooms like the living room, the entry & hallway and the dining room. Narrow them by colour and shape below, and see any of them on a wall at its real size.
