Contemporary Abstract & Mixed Media

Layered abstracts built by hand in digital and mixed media, where shape and colour do all the talking.

Hand-painted digital abstracts, built in layers. Nothing here is a picture of anything: shape, layer and colour do the work, and each piece sets the mood of the wall it hangs on.

Many are large-format works, made to hold a wide wall.

There are 587 pieces here. Most are horizontal. The colours that come up most are orange, blue and red. They suit rooms like the living room, the entry & hallway and the dining room. Narrow them by colour and shape below, and see any of them on a wall at its real size.

By place: Methow Valley · North Cascades · Winthrop
