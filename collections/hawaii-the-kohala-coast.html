Hawaii & the Kohala Coast

Hapuna Beach, the Ala Kahakai Trail and the gardens of Pua Mau Place: the Kohala Coast of Hawaii's Big Island, to hang at home.

Photographs from the Kohala Coast on Hawaii's Big Island: Hapuna Beach, the old shoreline path of the Ala Kahakai Trail, and the gardens of Pua Mau Place.

Beaches and coastline, flowers and gardens, and the trees and creatures along the way. If you have walked that coast, you may know the spot.

There are 36 pieces here. Most are horizontal. The colours that come up most are green, blue and brown. They suit rooms like the living room, the entry & hallway and the home office. Narrow them by colour and shape below, and see any of them on a wall at its real size.
